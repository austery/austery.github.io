Seatbelt on. Everyone will argue about Greenland tariffs and Fed independence this week. Important, yes. But the picture you’re looking at is the bigger tell. OpenAI CFO’s blog just did something rare: it put a hard spotlight on the real engine of the AI boom. Electricity. Compute. The meter. Strip away the hype and you see the uncomfortable shape of the business: an “AI company” that behaves like a power-hungry utility with a software logo on top. Training is a furnace you can’t turn off. Inference can get cheaper per token while the total bill still goes up, because usage explodes. Cheaper unit price, bigger volume, higher total cost. That’s how margin dreams die quietly. And that’s why the monetization debate is back with teeth. Ads, take-rate, enterprise agents paid on outcomes, maybe. But none of that is proven at scale yet. Until it is, the market has to price a simple risk: a compute burn rate chasing a revenue model that’s still being invented. Now stack the macro on top. Greenland tariffs and the US–EU trade fight are a risk premium machine. Policy uncertainty taxes multiples. Then the Fed “The Lisa Cook case “ adds another layer of noise: legal drama and independence anxiety make the referee look pressured, and markets hate that feeling. Put it together and you get the same outcome: higher volatility, thinner patience, and less forgiveness for story stocks. One takeaway: this week can punish size. If you don’t have a clear “I’m out” level and a max loss you can live with, you’re not trading, you’re gambling. Smaller bets, tighter rules, and no ego. Survive the chop, then swing when the dust settles. I’ll keep the public post clean. The positioning playbook and Intel $INTC checklist go to subscribers via the link in my profile.

今年过节不收礼，收礼只收DeepSeek的“大红包” 人家过节发钱，DS梁文峰发论文。这不是小修小补修个函数算法，这次是又一次震撼性的系统级的训练方面大升级 我尽可能浅显易懂讲清楚拆解出核心的对于26年AI训练和半导体意味什么？搞研究搞投资必读啊 以前训练大模型，有点像修一条很长的高架。 每一层高架只有一个接口，一辆车一路往前开，稳定是稳定，但你能做的路况调度很有限。 论文呢想法很激进： 把每一层高架改成多层立交，直接开四个车道，允许车在不同车道之间换道分流。信息流动更自由，理论上模型更聪明。 但自由是要付代价的。 立交越复杂，越容易“失控”：有的层把车流放大成拥堵，有的层把车流抽干成断流，层层叠加，训练就容易崩。 这篇论文最关键的价值，是给这套“多车道立交”加了一套硬规则：每次分流只能按比例分配，不能凭空把车变多，也不能把车搞没。结果就是两件事同时成立：训练更稳，效果也更好。 更重要的是，它没有停在“理论很美”那一步。这么搞整体训练成本只多了大约 6.7%，但换来的，是可训练性和性能上的提升。 这篇论文背后的信号是什么： 首先模型训练的竞赛远没有停滞。推理更重要但训练还在继续抬高上限可能性。 算法的时代结束了吗？没有。算法敢不敢设计得更激进，工程能不能把它撑住。谁能把“想法”变成“跑得起来的训练吞吐”，谁就拿到下一轮领先。 再次验证了一个老结论：今天最大的瓶颈依旧不是算力本身，而是带宽和内存。 对应到现实，就是高带宽内存、互联、通信、内核优化这些硬东西。 所以，这篇论文并不会让 HBM 需求消失。相反，它在告诉你：架构越进化，带宽和内存越成为核心约束，软件和硬件会继续互相逼迫、互相推动，形成滚动式升级。 把它当成 2026 的第一份开年礼就行。 它预示的不是“又一个模型更聪明了”，而是更大的趋势： AI 的天花板还在上移，而且这次上移的方式是系统工程把算法托上去。 新年第一份论文红包，收下。 今年 AI 还会继续把高度往上拔。

People keep turning $NVDA Groq’s SRAM story into a meme: “More SRAM = no more HBM.” - Wrong, Why? Quick question: have you ever cooked and wondered why you’re “slow”? I t’s rarely the stove. It’s the constant trips to the fridge. SRAM is the prep counter next to the stove. Put the hot ingredients and tools within arm’s reach, and the workflow becomes smoother, more predictable. That’s the real Groq signal: not “less thinking”, but less waiting inside the system. But a bigger prep counter doesn’t eliminate the fridge. HBM is the fridge + pantry: the capacity and bandwidth you need to serve many customers, long contexts, and high concurrency. So no, SRAM doesn’t “kill” HBM. SRAM buys determinism. HBM buys scale. In the inference era, if anything, scaling real deployments can make HBM even more important. $MU I turned this into a simple visual. Link in first reply.
