九月重磅：戏剧、小说与AI时代思考

十月风云：村上春树、重磅回忆录与历史叙事

朱马娜·哈提卜: 进入10月，我们迎来了一位文学巨匠的新作——村上春树（Haruki Murakami）的《小城与不确定之墙》（The City and Its Uncertain Walls）。

对于村上春树的忠实读者来说，这个书名并不陌生。它源自他1980年在杂志上发表的一篇未单独出版的中篇小说，也是他代表作《世界尽头与冷酷仙境》（Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World）的雏形。如今，在相隔40多年后，村上春树重新回到这个故事母题，将其扩写为一部完整的长篇小说。

故事讲述主人公在年轻时爱上了一位少女，少女告诉他，自己真正的自我生活在一座被高墙环绕的神秘小城里，在那里，人们没有影子，小城里还生活着独角兽，而图书管理员的工作是“读取梦境”。多年后，主人公终于踏入了这座神秘小城。全书充满了村上标志性的超现实主义意象、对失去与追寻的冥想、孤独感以及对现实与潜意识边界的探索。

Original English

Jumana Khatib: Moving into October, we have a major new novel from Haruki Murakami, titled The City and Its Uncertain Walls. For devoted Murakami readers, this title might ring a bell. It's based on a novella he published in a literary magazine back in 1980, which he never released as a standalone book, but which later served as the foundation for Hard-Boiled Wonderland and the End of the World. Now, more than four decades later, he has returned to that foundational story and expanded it into a full-length novel. It follows a protagonist whose teenage love told him that her true self lives in a walled city where shadows are separated from people, unicorns roam, and the library holds dreams instead of books. Years later, he finds his way into this city. It's classic Murakami—full of magical realism, meditation on loss, solitude, and the blurred lines between reality and the subconscious.
