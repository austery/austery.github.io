社交媒体的“大烟草时刻”：科技巨头面临法律责任

Ryan Mack: 我是《纽约时报》的 Ryan Mack。我正坐在洛杉矶一家法院的台阶外，这里刚刚结束了一场审判，审判内容主要关注社交媒体应用的成瘾性。在昨天一个里程碑式的案件中，Meta 和 YouTube 被认定在平台设计上存在疏忽，而这些平台已成为现代生活的重要组成部分。陪审团裁定，作为 Instagram 和 YouTube 母公司的 Meta，对其平台设计给原告造成的精神健康困扰负有责任。此案由一名年轻女性提起，她表示自己大约从六岁开始几乎不间断地使用社交媒体，这导致了她出现焦虑、抑郁和自残念头。她作证说，诸如无限滚动和算法不断推荐新内容等功能让她沉迷其中，她说：“我就是觉得我得一直盯着它。如果我不看，我就会错过什么。” 陪审团同意了她的说法。Ryan 表示，虽然她最终获得的 $6 million 赔偿对涉事的万亿美元公司来说只是九牛一毛，但这一刻的意义远不止于此。这只是数千起由青少年、学区和州政府提起的针对社交媒体平台的诉讼之一。此判决可能为更多诉讼铺平道路，潜在地使这些互联网巨头面临更大的经济处罚，甚至被迫改变其产品。这一判决实际上验证了一个新颖的法律理论，即社交媒体可能像香烟一样令人上瘾且有害。这被称为“社交媒体的‘大烟草时刻’”。这让人联想到 Philip Morris 等烟草公司曾被指控隐瞒吸烟危害信息的时期。那些公司最终达成了超过 2000 亿美元的和解。此后，许多烟草法规出台，吸烟率也随之下降。

Original English

Ryan Mack: This is Ryan Mack with the New York Times. I'm actually sitting outside on the steps of a Los Angeles courthouse where a trial just wrapped up essentially looking at the addictive nature of social media apps. In a landmark case yesterday, Meta and YouTube were found to be negligent in how they've built the platforms that have become a huge part of modern life. A jury found that Meta, the parent company of Instagram and YouTube, were essentially liable for the mental health distress caused to a plaintiff because of the design of their apps. The case was brought by a young woman who said that she started using social media almost constantly around age six and that it led to anxiety, depression, and thoughts of self harm. She testified that features like infinite scroll and constant new recommendations from algorithms kept her hooked, saying, quote, "I just felt like I wanted to be on it all the time. If I wasn't on it, I was going to miss out on something." The jury agreed. And Ryan says while the damages she was ultimately awarded of $6 million are obviously just a drop in the bucket for the trillion dollar companies involved, the moment is bigger than that. This is just one of thousands of lawsuits filed by teenagers, school districts, and states against social media platforms. And it could pave the way for more, potentially exposing the internet giants to bigger financial penalties and even forcing them to change their products. The verdict has essentially validated a novel legal theory that social media can be addictive and as harmful as products like cigarettes. That's led to this being called social media's big tobacco moment. A call back to when companies like Philip Morris were accused of hiding information about the harms of smoking. Those companies ultimately reached a 200 plus billion dollar settlement. After that, many tobacco regulations went into place and smoking declined.

OnlyFans 创始人逝世：一位科技行业巨头的陨落

Correspondent: 最后，那位帮助在线色情产业飞速发展、将Only Fans网站打造成全球巨头的Leonard Rvinsky，年仅 43 岁便已去世。Rvinsky 在不到十年前购买了该网站的股份。当时，该网站每年收入约 200 万美元，连接成人内容创作者与订阅者。到去年，得益于他的一些创新，Only Fans 年收入估计达到 70 亿美元。他通过鼓励创作者在社交媒体上发布内容来提高可见度，essentially 将色情内容与网红文化相结合，并邀请名人开设账户，从而帮助壮大了该网站。流行歌手 Lily Allen 曾表示，她一度在 Only Fans 上卖脚的照片赚的钱比音乐收入还多。Radinsky 是一个非常低调的人。他的照片似乎只有几张存在，关于他生平的细节也很少。他出生在乌克兰敖德萨，当时该国仍是苏联的一部分，他随家人移民到伊利诺伊州时大约六岁。高中时，他已经创办了一家销售成人域名的公司。他年纪太小了，以至于他的母亲签署了公司注册文件。有趣的是，多年来，Only Fans 与其以露骨内容闻名的核心业务之间一直存在一种复杂的关系。它曾一度禁止此类内容，尽管显然并未坚持下去。尽管如此，该网站仍坚称提供包括烹饪和越野摩托车比赛在内的各种内容。Ravinsky 因癌症去世，在过去一年里一直在寻求出售 Only Fans，该网站的估值约为 80 亿美元。

Correspondent: And finally, the man who helped supercharge the online porn industry, building the site Only Fans into a global juggernaut, has died at 43. Leonard Rvinsky bought a stake in the site a little less than a decade ago. At that point, it was making about $2 million a year, connecting adult content creators with subscribers. By high school, he'd already started a business that sold adultoriented domain names. He was so young that his mom signed the incorporation documents. Over the years, interestingly, Only Fans has actually had kind of a complicated relationship with the very thing that it's known for, explicit content. It has at some points banned that though obviously that didn't stick. Still, the site insists it offers a wide range of content including cooking and dirt bike racing. Ravinsky, who died of cancer, had been looking to sell Only Fans over the last year, which has been valued at about $8 billion.
