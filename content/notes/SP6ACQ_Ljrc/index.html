OpenAI 面临质询

主持人: 今天在加拿大，运营 Chat GPT 的公司 OpenAI 将面临关于 18 岁的枪击案凶手如何使用其平台的质询。该国人工智能部长表示，他对有关该公司在袭击前可能知道什么的报道“深感不安”。本月早些时候，这名枪手在家中杀害了两名家人，随后在一所附近的学校杀害了五名儿童、一名教育工作者以及她自己。

Original English

>>: In Canada today, Open AAI, the company that runs Chat GPT, will face questions about how the 18-year-old behind a recent mass shooting used its platform. The country's Minister of Artificial Intelligence has said he is quote deeply disturbed by reports about what the company may have known before the attack. Earlier this month, the shooter killed two family members at home before killing five children, one educator, and herself at a nearby school.

主持人: 《纽约时报》对她社交媒体账户的回顾发现，她多年来一直与心理健康问题作斗争，并日益迷恋武器和极端暴力。去年 6 月，她发送给 Chat GPT 的信息引起了 OpenAI 内部的警觉，她因此被禁止使用该平台。据该公司称，他们曾考虑就她的账户通知执法部门，但最终决定不这样做，因为他们认为她没有可信的袭击计划。《华尔街日报》报道称，这一决定当时让公司的一些员工感到不满。事发地不列颠哥伦比亚省（British Columbia）的最高政府官员表示，OpenAI 未能分享他所说的“相关情报”，这一点“非常令人不安”。

Original English

>>: A Times review of her social media accounts found she had a yearslong struggle with mental health issues and a growing fascination with weapons and extreme violence. Last June, messages she sent to ChatGpt raised flags internally at OpenAI and she was banned from the platform. According to the company, it had considered informing law enforcement about her account, but ultimately decided not to since it determined she had no credible plan for an attack. The Wall Street Journal has reported that decision upset some of the company's employees at the time. Original English

>>: Now, Canadian officials will be asking for explanations about OpenAI's safety protocols and its threshold for when information is shared with the police. For its part, OpenAI says it did contact Canadian authorities after the attack and that it tries to balance public safety with protecting the privacy of individual users.

伊朗监控与抗议

主持人: 在伊朗，过去几天，主要是学生的反政府抗议者走上街头，这是自今年早些时候严厉镇压抗议活动以来该国首次出现大规模示威。该政权正高度戒备，防范美国可能对其核计划发动的袭击，但同时也在努力压制威胁要从内部破坏政府稳定的广泛不满情绪。据多个权利组织称，1 月份，安全部队杀害了数千名抗议者，此后，他们逮捕了约 40,000 人。我的同事们一直在报道伊朗今年早些时候用于针对示威者的“高科技监控工具”。

Original English

In January, security forces killed thousands of protesters. And since then, they've arrested around 40,000 people, according to several rights groups.
