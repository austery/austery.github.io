如何教机器人跳舞：从仿真模拟到动作编排工具

关键字 robotics motion-control computer-vision motion-generation

摘要

机器人娱乐初创公司 UFB 的 David Dobáš 展示了通过直接身体控制与动作模型训练机器人的方法。团队从原本在仿真环境中耗时从零训练单个动作，转向利用大量动作数据训练单一模型，使用户只需拍摄一段视频即可快速生成机器人动作并像剪辑视频一样完成动作编排。UFB 宣布在官网公开发布该套工具。

英文原文 BS: Can I do a kick? This is clearly how you do your robot fighting. Can you explain to the audience what’s going on here?

DD: 好的。这被称为直接身体控制。这个摄像头拍摄你的视频，然后估算你做了什么动作，比如出拳和踢腿。接着机器人就会接受训练去做出类似的动作。

英文原文 DD: Yeah. So this is called direct body control. This camera takes a video of you and then estimates what motions you did, like punches and kicks. And then the robots are trained to do similar motions.

BS: 你刚才说“训练去做类似的动作”。你实际上是怎么训练这些机器人做新动作的呢？

英文原文 BS: You said “trained to do similar motions.” How do you actually train these robots to do new motions?

DD: 好的。我在 UFB 工作，这是一家机器人娱乐初创公司。我的目标是教机器人做新动作，比如跳舞、出拳、搞笑动作。所以我通常会把成千上万个机器人放入仿真环境中。它看起来就像这样。然后发生的第一件事是，机器人们甚至不知道如何保持自身平衡，对吧？它们就像婴儿一样。你必须从零开始教它们，但你必须坚持下去。于是你继续训练。看着它们自我改进真的很有趣。随着时间的推移，它们会变得有点摇晃，但开始能够保持平衡了。如果你训练得足够长，它们基本上就能完美地掌握这个动作。

英文原文 DD: Yeah. So I work at UFB, [a] robotics entertainment startup. So my goal is to teach robots new moves, like dances, punches, funny moves. So what I would do, I would take thousands of robots and put them in simulation. So that looks like this. And then the first thing [that] happens, the robots don't even know how to balance themselves, right? They're like babies. You have to teach them from scratch, but you have to keep going. So you continue training. It's really fun watching them improve themselves. Over time, they get a bit wobbly, but start, you know, balancing. And if you train long enough, they basically learn the motion perfectly.

从单独训练到通用动作模型

DD: 是的，而且过去确实需要做大量工作。但幸运的是情况正在发生改变，因为大家意识到，与其像我刚才展示的那样从零开始训练每一个动作，不如获取大量的动作数据、不同的动作，并训练一个单一的模型来完成所有不同类型的动作，这样不是更好吗？这改变了一切，因为这样你真的只需要拍摄一段自己的视频，几分钟之内，你就能看到机器人做那个动作会是什么样子，以及它在仿真中看起来如何。然后你就可以用实体机器人进行尝试了。

英文原文 DD: Yeah, and it used to be a lot of work. But luckily things are changing because people realized that instead of training each motion from scratch, like as I showed you, how about we rather take a lot of motion data, different motions, and train one single model to do all different types of motions? And that changes everything because then you can literally just take a video of yourself, and in a couple of minutes, you can see how the robot would look like doing that motion and how it would look in simulation. And then you can try [it] with the real robot.

BS: 这真的很酷。所以现在你只需要拿一段视频片段，拖进你做的这个工具里，然后模型就会搞清楚如何让机器人做出正确的动作。你不需要经历那成千上万步的训练了。

英文原文 BS: This is really cool. So now you can just take a video clip, drop it into this tool that you made, and then the model figures out how to get the robot to do the right move. You don't need those thousands of steps of training.

DD: 没错。关键就在这里。所以我们在这里教机器人一些新动作玩得很开心。这是我的同事 Emerson。这是我在教一些出拳动作。但这并没有结束，因为借助这些模型，你可以直接开始把动作组合在一起，创造出复杂的编舞。我们制作了这个工具，让它变得像剪辑视频一样简单。你拖放不同的动作，将它们组合在一起，就能得到一整套编舞。

英文原文 DD: Exactly. That's the thing. So we had a lot of fun here teaching our robot some new moves. This is my colleague Emerson. This is me teaching some punches. But it doesn't end here because with these models, you can just start combining motions together and create complex choreographies. We made this tool which makes it as easy as just video editing. You drag and drop different motions, combine them together, and you get whole choreographies.

英文原文 BS: I love this. So now suddenly, if you want to choreograph your robot, you basically take a bunch of video clips, drag it into this timeline, and you're off to the races. What does this mean for the folks that are watching and listening?
