加拿大移民系统是否失灵？深度解析 TVO Today 2026-03-24

领域: society-thinking

分类: politics-society

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-03-24

摘要:

本次讨论深入剖析了加拿大移民系统的运作及其面临的挑战。首先阐述了移民的初衷——为国家社会经济发展、家庭团聚及提供人道主义援助。随后，探讨了人口增长、劳动力需求及医疗保健系统对移民的依赖性。接着，聚焦于系统近期的剧烈变化：包括政府的政策调整、疫情期间的临时措施、乌克兰战争引发的人道主义工作许可，以及国际学生数量的激增。最后，分析了“部长指令”对经济类移民项目带来的不确定性和对申请人的影响，并指出当前永久居民的高技能门槛。

加拿大移民系统：目标与现状

Clearly, we have volumes in this country that are unprecedented, of immigration, and I think that's a good thing. Today, we're announcing we will reduce the number of immigrants we bring in. Mr. Speaker, we are taking back control over the immigration system.

Original English

Clearly, we have volumes in this country that are unprecedented, of immigration, and I think that's a good thing. Today, we're announcing we will reduce the number of immigrants we bring in. Mr. Speaker, we are taking back control over the immigration system.

SANGEETA: 长期以来，加拿大一直欢迎新移民，但现在这个门似乎正在关闭。在短短几年内，我们来了个一百八十度大转弯。这对国家意味着什么？系统是否已经失灵？让我们来解开加拿大的移民系统。首先，我们看看移民的初衷是什么。

Original English

SANGEETA: For a long time, Canada has been welcoming to newcomers. But now that door seems to be closing. We've done an about-face in just a couple of years. What does that mean for the country? Is the system broken? Let's unravel the Canadian immigration system. Let's start with a look at why we have immigration in the first place.

SANGEETA: 《移民法案》列出了几项目标，但主要有三个。第一个是丰富我们国家的社会经济结构。人们的筛选依据是他们将如何根据自己的背景和才能为国家做出贡献。第二个是家庭团聚。我们希望允许加拿大人和永久居民与其近亲属团聚。第三个是难民保护以及出于人道主义和同情心的考量。我们是一个希望为有需要的人提供庇护和保护的国家。

Original English

SANGEETA: The Immigration Act lays out several objectives, but there are three main ones. The first is to add to the socio-economic fabric of our country. People are selected based on what they would contribute to our country, based on their background, based on their talents. The second is for family reunification. We want to allow Canadians and permanent residents to be reunified with their close family members. And the third is for refugee protection and for humanitarian and compassionate grounds. We are a country where we want offer sanctuary and protection to those who would need it.

加拿大的人口需求与移民重要性

SANGEETA: 那么，这些理念如何直接关系到加拿大的需求呢？

Original English

SANGEETA: So how do those ideas tie directly to Canada's needs?

ELIZABETH: 显而易见，我们国家自身的出生率不足以维持我们所需的人口增长。如果没有任何移民，我们的医疗保健系统将变得极其昂贵。此外，我们需要人们不仅作为工人，更要作为消费者。没有消费者，我们就没有对商品和服务的需求。所以，我们的人口必须增长。我们正在寻找人们加入我们的社区。这就是移民系统的作用。

Original English

ELIZABETH: It is very clear that our own birthrates in this country is not gonna be enough to sustain our population growth that we need it to sustain. If we didn't have any immigration, our health care systems would be prohibitively expensive. Also, we need people not only as workers, but even as consumers. Without consumers, we don't have a need for services and goods. So our population needs to grow. We are then looking for people to join our communities. So that's what the immigration system is for.

移民系统的复杂性与不稳定性

ELIZABETH: 但它是如何运作的呢？移民部长制定规则。

Original English

But how's it work? Well, the Immigration Minister makes the rules.

ELIZABETH: 任何移民部长的工作都非常复杂，他们必须平衡所有这些利益。我们希望履行我们的使命，帮助那些有需要的人，但我们不能轻易地向全世界敞开大门，让所有需要帮助的人都来到这个国家。所有这些相互竞争的利益使得我们的移民系统非常复杂且引人入胜。而且，移民系统一直在努力实现这些目标。但最近，它经历了很多变化。

Original English

ELIZABETH: The job of any Immigration Minister is very complicated, and they have to balance all of these interests. We want to serve our mandate, where we want to help those who are in need, but we can't necessarily open our doors to the world and let everybody who needs help come to the country. So all of these competing interests make our immigration system very complex and fascinating. And our immigration system has worked to achieve those goals. But recently, it's seen a lot of change.

ELIZABETH: 自 24 年前，即 2001 年《法案》生效以来，我们已经有了十五位移民部长。平均每位部长任期大约一年。这是一个大问题。大多数移民部长上任时可能根本没有多少移民方面的经验。而这是一个加拿大最复杂的法律体系之一。它在欺诈方面存在巨大问题，并且需要平衡不同的利益。它也是影响我们以及我们国家未来最重要的体系之一。

Original English

ELIZABETH: Since the Act came into place 24 years ago, 2001, we have had fifteen Immigration Ministers. Each Minister, on average, stays around a year. That's a big problem. Most Immigration Ministers who come into their role probably don't have much immigration experience at all. And this is one of the most complex legal systems in Canada. It has a huge issue with fraud and balancing different interests. And it is one of the most important systems that will affect us and our future in this country.

疫情与政策调整：冲击与应对

ELIZABETH: 这种部长职位的频繁变动与 2020 年的疫情相撞。首先，我们将拒绝非加拿大公民或永久居民进入加拿大。

Original English

This ministerial upheaval then collided with the pandemic in 2020. First, we will be denying entry to Canada to people who are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents.

ELIZABETH: 突然之间，我们不得不关闭边境。当时的部长决定让已经在国家境内的人留在国内。尤其重要的是，人们认识到，我们不仅需要所谓的“高技能工人”，许多关键的必需品工人是那些在杂货店、餐馆和医疗保健领域工作的人。因此，部长当时决定允许几乎所有在移民池中的人移民。在短短几个月内，加拿大接纳了永久居民配额，不仅是一年的，而是两年的。

Original English

ELIZABETH: All of a sudden, we had to close our borders. And the Minister in charge then decided that people who are in country, we should let them stay in the country. And especially, there was a recognition, not only do we need what's called "high-skilled workers", many of the essential workers were people who were working in grocery stores, who were working in restaurants, in health care. So what the Minister decided to do at that point is to allow virtually everyone who was in a pool to immigrate. In the space of a few months, Canada let in all of the quotas for not just one year, but two years of permanent residents.

SANGEETA: 这意味着几乎没有空间允许更多的移民发生。

Original English

SANGEETA: That means there was no room to allow for more immigration to happen.

ELIZABETH: 当时的想法是允许人们继续持有工作许可。

Original English

ELIZABETH: The idea then was to allow people to continue their work permits.

SANGEETA: 这样我们就能留住这些高技能工人，并让他们有理由继续来加拿大。

Original English

SANGEETA: So we can keep these high-skilled workers on the hook, and give them a reason to still come to Canada.

乌克兰战争与人道主义工作许可

ELIZABETH: 接着，我们又更换了移民部长。新任部长上任时正值乌克兰战争爆发。加拿大想帮助乌克兰人，但……

Original English

Then, we shuffled the Immigration Minister again. And this new Minister was in place for the start of the Ukraine war. Canada wanted to help Ukrainians, but...

ELIZABETH: 我们不希望他们都以难民申请人的身份前来。所以新任移民部长提出了一个全新的想法：人道主义工作许可。

Original English

ELIZABETH: Well, we didn't want to have all of them come as refugee claimants. So the new Immigration Minister comes up with a brand-new idea. Humanitarian work permits.

ELIZABETH: 在大多数情况下，这以前从未发生过。这是我们首次大规模提供人道主义工作许可。然后人们开始质疑：“那为什么在阿富汗局势发生时，你们不允许阿富汗人获得工作许可？或者，你知道的，来自世界其他地方的人呢？为什么突然间来自欧洲某地的人就能获得这种人道主义工作许可的巨大好处？”

Original English

ELIZABETH: For the most part, this was never done before. For the first time, we had humanitarian work permits en masse offered to people. And then people started to question, "Well, why it is that when the Afghanistan situation occurred, "you didn't allow people from Afghanistan "to get work permits? "Or, you know, from other parts of the world. "Why is it all of a sudden "somewhere from Europe that you're allowing "this great benefit of humanitarian work permits?"

SANGEETA: 因此，新计划扩展到允许来自其他冲突地区的人们通过这些新的人道主义工作许可来到加拿大。

Original English

SANGEETA: So the new program expanded to allow people from other conflict zones to come to Canada under these new humanitarian work permits.

疫情后配额耗尽与学生涌入

SANGEETA: 好的，我们来回顾一下。我们在疫情期间用完了两年的新永久居民配额，将它们给了那些已经在国内的人，以填补我们的劳动力缺口。边境开放后，我们希望新的、高技能的永久居民来加拿大，但配额已经用完了。所以我们给了这些人临时工作许可，让他们仍然可以来到这个国家。然后乌克兰战争爆发，我们为受那场战争和其他冲突地区影响的人们创建了新的人道主义工作许可。这是一个我们未曾预料到的大量人口涌入加拿大。

Original English

SANGEETA: Okay, let's recap this for a sec. We burned through our two-year quota of new permanent residents during the pandemic, handing them out to people who were already in the country to fill our labour gaps. After the borders opened up, we wanted new, high-skilled permanent residents to come to Canada, but the quota was used up. So we gave these people temporary work permits, so they could still come to the country. Then the Ukraine war happened, and we created new humanitarian work permits for people caught in that war and other conflict zones. That's a big influx of people coming to Canada that we hadn't accounted for.

ELIZABETH: 而且，我们还有另外第三类人——国际学生数量惊人且不受控制的增长。疫情开始前，学生人数约为 400,000 到 500,000。到 2023 年，这一数字在一年内增长到约 900,000。结果，我们的人口激增。国际学生不仅受到我们政府的追捧，美国也同样如此。他们年轻，带来巨额学费收入，并且在毕业时，他们受过良好教育，能够进入劳动力市场。然而，当人口看到国际工人和学生的巨大增长时，人们突然开始思考：“这里发生了什么？”公众的看法正在转变。以前，我们一直非常欢迎移民。我们为此感到自豪，但突然间，故事变成了“这个国家的人太多了。”

Original English

ELIZABETH: And on top of that, we have another third group of people, the tremendous unchecked growth of international students. Before the pandemic started, the number of students were around 400,000, 500,000 or so. By 2023, those numbers had grown to around 900,000 in one year. And as a result, our population shot up. And international students have been very sought-after, not just by our government, but also the US. They're young, they bring in huge tuition dollars, and by the time they graduate, they're well-educated and able to enter the labour market. However, when the population saw this huge growth of international workers and students, suddenly people thought, "Oh, what's happening here?" The public's perception was turning. Before, we were always very welcoming for immigrants. We were very proud to do that, and all of a sudden, the story became "We have too many people in the country."

SANGEETA: 因此，联邦政府再次改变了规则，限制了可以来加拿大的国际学生数量。

Original English

SANGEETA: So the federal government changed the rule again, and capped the number of international students who could come to Canada.

“部长指令”带来的不确定性

SANGEETA: 那么，所有这些对移民系统的改变带来了什么后果呢？还记得我们之前谈到的移民部长制定规则吗？嗯，还有另一部分导致了我们现在的情况，那就是所谓的“部长指令”。

Original English

SANGEETA: So what's the consequence of all of this change to the immigration system? Remember earlier where we talked about the Immigration Minister making the rules? Well, there's another part of that that's led us here, and it's because of something called "ministerial instructions."

SANGEETA: 这些是部长可以在没有议会任何意见或协商的情况下对移民项目所做的更改。结果是，移民项目，尤其是加拿大的经济项目，大多在没有警告、没有咨询专家和利益相关者的情况下发生变化。这成为了一个巨大的问题。这是一个非常复杂的系统。这是一个人们需要花多年时间准备来获得移民资格的系统。因此，了解如何获得资格对任何申请人来说都非常非常重要。由于这些非常快速的变化，这不仅会让那些试图申请的申请人感到极度沮丧，也会让想雇佣他们的雇主感到沮丧。

Original English

SANGEETA: Those are changes a minister can make to an immigration program without any Parliamentary input or consultation. As a result, immigration programs, especially economic programs in Canada, change mostly without warning, mostly without consultation of experts and stakeholders. This becomes a huge problem. This is a very complex system. It's a system where people prepare for years to try to qualify to immigrate. And so therefore, knowing what you have to do to qualify is very, very important to any applicants. Because of these very fast changes, it can be extremely frustrating, not only to the applicants who are trying to apply, but also to their employers who want to hire them.

当前永久居民的高门槛

ELIZABETH: 我们看到加拿大有很多临时居民。但如今，永久居民身份需要非常高水平的技能，以至于大多数以前移民过来的人今天可能都无法满足条件。

Original English

ELIZABETH: We see a lot of people in Canada who are temporary residents. But permanent residency, these days, requires such a high level of skills that most people who had immigrated before wouldn't be able to do so today. ♪
