匈牙利大选：欧洲MAGA支持者竞选失败

记者报道: 在昨天匈牙利的选举中，欧洲最杰出的右翼民粹主义者 Victor Orban 承认了落选。这是对特朗普政府的重大打击，因为 Orban 曾是某种 MAGA 的标志，也是对克里姆林宫的打击，两者都支持他的竞选。创纪录数量的匈牙利人前往投票。注册选民中几乎 80%。当结果出来时，主要是年轻人的人群冲到街上庆祝。Orban 已经执政 16 年，他消除了政府中的许多制衡机制，控制了法院，并掌控了大多数新闻媒体。他现在有望被 Peter Magyar 取代，一位保守派政治家，他分享了 Orban 的一些政治观点，包括警告所谓"觉醒"意识形态的危险。但 Magyar 通过承诺在 Orban 的家人和朋友自我充实多年后结束该国的广泛腐败，在一定程度上赢得了选民。Magyar 也预计对欧盟的敌意较少，对俄罗斯的友好也较少。Orban 的政府长期以来被视为安全风险，因为他的政府成员据称与克里姆林宫共享欧盟会议的信息。Orban 的政党还曾阻止了一笔 100 亿美元的欧盟贷款来帮助乌克兰。该贷款现在预计将获得批准。

Original English

Reporter: In elections in Hungary yesterday, Victor Orban, Europe's most prominent right-wing populist, conceded the race. It's a major blow to the Trump administration as Orban had been something of a MAGA icon and to the Kremlin, both of which backed his campaign. A record-breaking number of Hungarians went to the polls. Almost 80% of registered voters there. And as the results came in, crowds of mostly young people rushed into the streets to celebrate. Orban has been in power for 16 years, and he's eliminated many checks and balances in the government, stacked the courts, and taken control of most news media outlets. He's now expected to be replaced by Peter Magyar, a conservative politician who shares some of Orban's politics, including warning about the dangers of so-called woke ideology. But Magyar managed to win over voters in part by promising to end widespread corruption in the country after years of Orban's family and friends enriching themselves. Magyar is also expected to be less hostile towards the EU and less friendly to Russia. Orban's administration had long been viewed as a security risk because members of his government reportedly shared information from EU meetings with the Kremlin. Orban's party had also held up a $100 billion EU loan to help Ukraine. That loan is now expected to be approved.

变性军人驱逐政策的实际影响

记者报道: 一年多前，特朗普政府宣布他们将把所有变性军人赶出军队。他们说这是必要的举措，以保持军队效率并省钱。但根据我的报道，一年后，似乎恰恰相反已经发生了。

Original English

Reporter: A little over a year ago, the Trump administration announced that they were going to force all transgender service members out of the military. It was a move that they said was needed to keep the military efficient and to save money. But what I found in my reporting is that a year later, it seems like the opposite has happened.

Pentagon 记者 Dave Phillips: 军队进入这项新政策时没有计划如何执行。因此，我的报道显示，各种高度熟练的人在军队中，比如医生、律师、飞行员，政府大量投资支付其非常昂贵的教育的人，已经被迅速解雇，没有明显的替代品。即使他们不再从事这些工作，他们仍在名单上。他们中的许多人，实际上，大多数人，在付费行政假期中度过了数月，领取薪水，但无法做他们的工作。我采访了一位名叫 Parker Moore 的海军中士，海军花费多年训练他成为航空母舰上的核裂变技术员。从本质上讲，他们的工作是监督整个团队，使航空母舰的心脏运转。但一旦宣布了规则，他们很快就被告知要休假。现在该航空母舰的司令官说"不，我们需要你"。但海军还是让他们休假。那艘航母亚伯拉罕·林肯号没有 Moore 中士就离开了，现在在中东没有一位真正关键的核反应堆监督员。

Original English

Pentagon Reporter Dave Phillips: The military went into this new policy with no plan of how to carry it out. And so my reporting showed that all sorts of highly skilled people in the military, people like doctors, lawyers, pilots, people that the government invested heavily in paying for their very expensive education have been quickly dismissed without an obvious replacement. And even though they're not in their jobs anymore, they're still on the books. Many of them, actually, most of them, spent months and months on paid administrative leave, collecting a paycheck, but unable to do their jobs. I spoke to one Navy chief named Parker Moore, who the Navy had spent years training to be a nuclear fission technician on an aircraft carrier. Essentially, their job was to oversee a whole team of people that made the heart of an aircraft carrier run. But very quickly once the rules were announced, they were told to be put on leave. Now the admiral in charge of this aircraft carrier said, "No, we need you." But the Navy put them on leave anyway. And that carrier, the Abraham Lincoln, left without Chief Moore and is now in the Middle East without a really critical nuclear reactor supervisor.

Pentagon 记者 Dave Phillips: 总的来说，我在报道这个故事时采访了十多个人，他们说这是对人才的浪费、对培训经费的浪费、对多年经验的浪费，但成本也远不止金钱，对吧？这些人想为他们的国家服务，现在他们被迫离开，这让他们感到不仅他们让战友失望，而且他们献身于的政府也在背叛他们。

Original English

Pentagon Reporter Dave Phillips: In all, I talked to more than a dozen people while reporting this story and they said that yeah, this is a waste of talent, a waste of training dollars, a waste of years of experience, but also the cost goes much deeper than just money, right? These are people that wanted to serve their country and now they're being forced to leave, which makes them feel like not only they're letting their comrades down, but that the government that they committed themselves to is betraying them.

记者 Eli Tan: 我的同事 Eli Tan 一直在报道圣昆汀监狱的棒球队——加州圣昆汀监狱的棒球队。有些人曾在大学或职业球队中打过球，而其他人自小学以来就没有拿过球棒。这些比赛在娱乐时间进行。所以，有数百名男性在垒球外举重、理发。队员们告诉我，这是他们在圣昆汀生活中最重要的事情之一，它给了他们一种怀旧的感觉，让他们想起了他们最后一次自由人的时光。Eli 说这项棒球项目花费多年时间建立，之所以值得注意，是因为囚犯对抗轮流出现的外部球队，比如涉足社区的球队，他们来到圣昆汀参加比赛。监狱长告诉 Eli，这项计划对囚犯的改造和康复与强制性治疗课程等措施一样有帮助。自从 Eli 去年秋天首次报道圣昆汀巨人队以来，他说他听到了来自全国各地想要帮助和寄送设备的人的声音。他还听到了一位加州州议员的声音，他现在正在推动一项法案，以扩大全州监狱的体育项目。下个月，Eli 将回到圣昆汀，不是作为记者，而是作为一名球员。他自己的男子球队将与巨人队对抗。

Original English

Reporter Eli Tan: My colleague, Eli Tan, has been covering the San Quentin Giants, the baseball team at California's San Quentin prison. Next month, Eli is going back to San Quentin, not as a reporter, but as a player. His own men's team is going to play against the Giants.
