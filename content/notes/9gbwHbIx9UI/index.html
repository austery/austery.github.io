小米 CFO 林世伟谈‘人车家全生态’：从智能手机到智能汽车与物理 AI 的跨越 Norges Bank Investment Management 2026-04-22

领域: tech-engineering

分类: ai-application

主讲人: Norges Bank Investment Management

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-04-22

摘要:

在本期访谈中，小米 CFO 林世伟与挪威主权财富基金 CEO 深度对话，回顾了小米从 OS 起家到成为全球技术巨头的历程。重点探讨了小米如何以‘中国速度’在三年内实现跨界造车，解析了‘人车家全生态’战略背后的软硬件集成与供应链优势。此外，林世伟还分享了小米在仿人机器人、自研大模型以及‘物理 AI’领域的战略布局，并揭示了创始人雷军极度专注的产品精神如何塑造了小米的企业文化。

小米的成长历程与“人车家”愿景

Nicolai Tangen: 大家好，欢迎来到《In Good Company》。我是挪威主权财富基金的 CEO Nicolai Tangen。今天，我非常荣幸能邀请到小米公司的 CFO 林世伟。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Hi everybody and welcome to In Good Company. I'm Nicolai Tangen, the CEO of the Norwegian Sovereign Wealth Fund, and today I'm in a particularly good company with the CFO of Xiaomi, Alain Lam.

林世伟: 你好，Nicolai，感谢你的邀请。

Original English

Alain Lam: Hello Nicola, thanks for having me.

Nicolai Tangen: 小米是一家令人惊叹的公司，业务涵盖手机、汽车，甚至机器人。作为持有公司超过 1% 股份的股东，我们很想听听，你的工作是什么？小米是如何走到今天的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Now Alain, Xiaomi is just an incredible company with phones, cars, humanoids. You know, we are the lucky owner of more than 1% of the company. So just tell us, what do you do and how did it all happen?

林世伟: 谢谢，Nicolai。首先，小米还是一家非常年轻的公司，刚满 16 岁（注：原文表述为 turned 16，指公司成立于 2010 年）。我们成立于 2010 年 4 月 6 日。自那时起，我们取得了巨大的成就。但从本质上讲，我们认为自己是一家全球科技公司。除了广为人知的智能手机和智能设备，我们最近还推出了智能电动汽车并进入了制造领域。

支撑这一切的是我们的底层技术，无论是操作系统、半导体还是 AI。我们构建了一个非常全面的生态系统，我们称之为“人车家全生态”（Human x Car x Home），涵盖了从个人设备到家庭设备，再到现在的移动出行方案。去年，我们的营收超过了 4500 亿人民币，并保持了良好的盈利水平。

Original English

Alain Lam: Well, thanks, Nicola. Thanks for the question. And good to be here. So, first of all, Xiaomi is still a very young company. We just turned 16. We founded in April 6, 2010. And since then, we have done a lot as you mentioned. But ultimately, I think we think of ourselves as a global technology company. Obviously we have smartphones, we have a lot of smart devices. Plus recently we have smart electric vehicles and we are into manufacturing. But underlying all of these is our technology whether it is our operating system, our semiconductors, our AI. I think those are the stuff that we built underneath all of our ecosystem, right? I think we built a pretty comprehensive what we call a human x car x home ecosystem ranging from all your personal devices to all your home devices and now with your mobility solutions. So that's where we are but you know we've achieved quite a bit over the last 15 years. Obviously last year we have over 450 billion RMB in terms of sales and we have achieved pretty good profitability.

从操作系统起家与早期战略

Nicolai Tangen: 这是一个非常精彩的故事。让我们按时间顺序来回顾一下，你们的第一款产品是如何诞生的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: It's just such an incredible story so I'm not quite sure even where to start but why don't we just go through it chronologically. The first product you had, just how did it start?

林世伟: 2010 年公司成立时，我们最初是为智能手机开发操作系统。我们想在 Android 生态系统之上做一个操作系统并提供给手机使用。但最终，我们决定亲自下场造手机。我们的第一款手机 小米手机 1 在中国以 1999 元的价格销售，获得了巨大成功。因为在当时，市场上充斥着大量低质量、高价格的产品。

Original English

Alain Lam: We actually started off building the operating system for smartphones and that was in 2010 when we founded the company. We want to do a operating system on top of the Android ecosystem and then try to offer it to smartphones. But eventually we decide to get into the smartphone ourselves, right? And our first smartphone which we call Xiaomi 1, we sold it at 1999 RMB in China. And that become a huge success because I mean as you remember back in the days there were a lot of products out there that were low quality but high prices.

Nicolai Tangen: 开发那款手机花了多长时间？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How long time did it take you to develop the telephone?

林世伟: 我们在 2011 年发布了第一款手机，也就是公司成立一年后。

Original English

Alain Lam: So we launched our first smartphone in 2011. So one year after we...

Nicolai Tangen: 如此短的研发周期是怎么做到的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How is it possible to have such a short cycle time?

林世伟: 那时中国的供应链已经相当先进了。当时市场的问题是，昂贵的产品质量很差。我们的理念是：能否制造出一款质量更好，且价格让用户更易负担的智能手机？这种“高品质、高性价比”的理念贯穿了小米产品的整个发展史。

Original English

Alain Lam: Yeah, because at that time I think the supply chain is obviously quite advanced in China but the problem in the market for that at that time was there was a lot of expensive products with very low quality right. And the philosophy for the company is that can we do a smartphone that is much better quality and we can offer to users at much more affordable prices. And that's something that you see permeated in the history of our products, right? We try to offer very high quality solutions to our users at affordable prices.

供应链本地化与跨代创新

Nicolai Tangen: 你如何看待你们的供应链？其中有多少是中国本土采购的，有多少是来自国外的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How do you think about your supply chain? How much of that is Chinese sourced and how much is sourced outside?

林世伟: 在初期，我们确实对国外技术有很大依赖。但经过 15 年的发展，对于智能手机来说，坦率地说，大部分内容现在已经实现了本土化。

由于可以从本地采购，产品开发的速度大大加快。国外的产品很多是标准化的现货方案，而本地合作伙伴更愿意与你合作，开发更符合我们需求的定制化方案。这不仅让速度更快，也让我们有能力提供更契合智能手机需求的定制化体验，从而更好地满足消费者的愿望。

Original English

Alain Lam: Well, I mean, at the beginning, I think there's still a lot of reliance on foreign technologies. But over the last 15 years, for a smartphone, frankly, most of the content now is localized. You can find local solutions for that in most of our component. It is important because the local, frankly speaking, if you think about the foreign products a lot of them are more standardized solutions off the shelf right whereas the local partners are willing to work with you developing products that are more customized to what we want. And as a result it's not just faster but it's also our ability to offer much more customized solutions that fits better into our smartphone, that fits better into what we want and as a result, we are able to offer products to our customers that offer something that they really want.

Nicolai Tangen: 你们现在的旗舰型号是 17（注：指对应发布年份的系列名）。你们在 16 系列上似乎经历了一个特殊的阶段，发生了什么？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: So, you are now at model 17. Um, you've aligned with the Apple kind of numbers. You had a setback in 16. What happened then?

林世伟: 实际上，我们认为从 15 到 17 的跨越非常巨大，在改进程度上已经超出了单一世代的范畴，因此我们直接命名为小米 17。这项创新在于，大多数手机只有正面面板，而我们提供了背面面板，用户可以更有效地使用双面。这被证明是非常具有突破性的，深受用户喜爱。

Original English

Alain Lam: Well, I think there was such a big leap between 15 and 17 that we think that it's beyond one generation in terms of improvement from our Xiaomi 15 to our Xiaomi 17 and hence we name it Xiaomi 17. The innovation comes when you look at the smartphone, it offered a back panel. So instead of having a front panel like most smartphones do we actually offer the back panel as well so you can use both sides effectively and that's something that has proven to be very groundbreaking and a lot of our users like it.

智能家居与 IoT 的互联体验

Nicolai Tangen: 物联网（IoT） 以及与家电的互联对你们有多重要？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How important has the internet of things or your connectivity with home appliances been?

林世伟: 非常重要。现代用户喜欢智能化的解决方案。像空调、洗衣机、冰箱这些技术已经存在了三五十年，很久没有改变了。我们提供的是智能方案，你可以像手机一样对这些设备进行 OTA（越空）软件升级，而不需要购买新机器。

此外，通过将一切连接在一起，整个生活方式会变得更聪明。智能设备不仅仅是能用手机远程控制，而是能理解用户、理解用户的行为，并每天提供定制化的智能方案。

例如，智能门锁。它不仅能让你看到门外的情况，提供专门的密码以保护隐私，如果你将它与其他设备互联，当你打开门锁时，空调会自动开启。这就是我们认为用户需要的智能解决方案。

Original English

Alain Lam: It is important because I think in this world a lot of our users like intelligent solutions right. Think about air conditioners, washing machines, refrigerators. The technology have existed for 30-50 years but that hasn't changed for a long time. What we offer to our users is a smart solutions. Now you can upgrade your software over the air in your devices as opposed to buying a new machines. Plus the fact is can you make your whole lifestyle, whole ecosystem much smarter by connecting everything together so that they can do things for you. Not just using your smartphone as a remote control but smart intelligence solutions that they understand who you are they understand your behavior and they can try to offer customized solutions for you every day. For example, smart lock. Not just you can look at who's outside of your door, you can offer specialized password for people to enter your apartment so that you don't leak your secrecy. But also, if you interconnect it with other devices at home, for example, when you open your smart lock, your AC will automatically be switched on, right? And those are the smart solutions that we like to think that our users need.

小米汽车：三年造车与“中国速度”

Nicolai Tangen: 然后你们决定开始造车。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: And then you decided to make a car.

林世伟: 是的。我们在 2021 年决定进入汽车行业，2024 年发布了第一款车。

Original English

Alain Lam: Correct. We decided to get into the car industry in 2021 and then we launched our first car in 2024.

Nicolai Tangen: 这怎么可能？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How is that possible?

林世伟: 在不到三年的时间里，我们完成了设计并建造了一座工厂。这就是“中国速度”。

小米在进入造车领域之前，已经投资了一些中国的电动汽车厂商，有观察和帮助他们发展的经验。当我们决定自己下场时，我们得出的结论是：如果你相信电动汽车（EV）将成为另一件消费电子产品，那么我们在软硬件集成、供应链管理方面的丰富经验就派上用场了。虽然汽车在安全性等方面有所不同，但中国的电动汽车供应链已经非常成熟。

我们的重要理念是：投入 10 倍的精力来打造第一款车。我们没有盲目地同时开发多款车型，而是倾尽全力打造这一款单品。

Original English

Alain Lam: So a little bit over less than three years we launched our first car, we designed it and then we built a factory as well. So China speed. At the beginning the car industry to us is not that foreign. Xiaomi as a company has already invested in some of the EV manufacturers in China before. So we have an experience of seeing them develop. But then we decided to do it ourselves. The ultimate conclusion is that if you believe that a car, a EV is going to be another piece of consumer electronics and that's something that we have a lot of experience in whether it is managing the software hardware integration or managing the supply chain. Obviously car is a little bit of a different animal because you need to consider about safety but again the supply chain in China has been relatively well-developed on the EV industry. What we said was, can we spend 10 times more investments to build the first car. Instead of making multiple cars we decided to spend all of our efforts on one single car.

Nicolai Tangen: 而且这是一辆非常快的车，去年你们在纽博格林（Nürburgring）赛道创造了最快圈速纪录。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: And it's a pretty fast car right because now you set the record for the quickest round on Nürburgring last year.

林世伟: 是的。在电动汽车领域，我们坚持要拥有并自主研发核心技术，比如电动机、电池包封装技术、智能驾驶和辅助驾驶等。至于其他部分，我们可以依靠行业内的供应商提供成熟方案。在研发初期，我们投入了大约 3000 人，这是同行的 10 倍，因为我们的董事长雷军希望在初期通过巨大的投入来快速赶超。

Original English

Alain Lam: Yeah. As we look into this EV industry, we think that there are certain things that are core to the EV technology that we want to own and develop ourselves. Things like the electric motors, things like how do you pack the battery, things like smart driving, assisted driving, etc. Those are the stuff that we spend real time on developing our proprietary technology. The rest I think we can rely on other people. We have around 3,000 people to develop the first car which is probably 10 times more than other peers because our chairman has the philosophy that we want to spend a lot of effort initially to catch up with our peers.

品牌忠诚度与小米使命

Nicolai Tangen: 你们在 30 分钟内就卖出了 5 万辆车。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Now you sold 50,000 cars in 30 minutes.

林世伟: 是的。虽然在汽车行业我们是新兵，但小米在中国拥有非常强大的品牌和忠诚的用户群。中国有超过 1.8 亿人每天在使用小米手机。当初宣布造车时，很多用户就说：“只要小米造车，我们就买。”首批买家中，有超过 20% 的人甚至没见过实车、没试驾过就直接下单了，这是对我们长期建立的品牌信任的最好证明。

Original English

Alain Lam: Yeah. Obviously we are very new entrant to the EV business but we already have a very established brand in China. We have over 180 million people in China that are using our smartphones every day. At the beginning when we decided to develop a EV a lot of our users saying that if Xiaomi wants to make a car we'll buy it. 20 plus% of our initial buyers haven't even test driven the car or seen the car in real life. They just see us launching the car and they put an order in. That's a testament to the loyal users that we've built up.

Nicolai Tangen: 你们是如何赢得人们喜爱的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How do you get people to love you?

林世伟: 这回到了我们的愿景：坚持做“感动人心、价格厚道”的好产品，让全球每个人都能享受科技带来的美好生活。我们通过创新的技术和我们所谓的“厚道的价格”来运营全球业务。我们和用户做朋友，保持接触并倾听他们的反馈，根据反馈调整产品。用户知道，无论走进哪家小米店，他们都能获得最好的性价比。

Original English

Alain Lam: It goes back to our philosophy, right? If you look at our mission, we want to build amazing products with honest prices to let everyone in the world enjoy a better life through innovative technology. We want to build amazing products at honest prices and we want to do a global business and be innovative. We make the coolest products, we make a lot of friends with our users, we stay engaged and listen to the feedback. So those are the stuff that we built a very good rapport with our users over time. They know that whenever they walk into our store, they are getting the best pricing possible.

手机与汽车制造的异同

Nicolai Tangen: 造手机和造车有哪些相似之处？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: What are the similarities between making a phone and a car?

林世伟: 有很多不同。在 2024 年之前，我们没有自己的手机工厂，而是外包给富士康或 BYD。我们负责设计、核心技术和营销。但对于汽车业务，我们在北京建设了一座全新的大规模现代化工厂，这是我们首次亲身管理大规模制造。

相似之处在于，软件定义硬件。在电动汽车中，软件的重要性日益增加，就像手机一样。在手机里，我们将大量芯片堆叠在极小的空间；在车里，我们也需要管理大量组件，尤其是管理电池效能和驾驶软件，这与手机业务非常相似。此外，两者的供应链管理也有很多重叠，许多供应商是共通的。

Original English

Alain Lam: Some similarities, a lot of differences obviously. In the phone industry before 2024 we did not have a smartphone factory. We were using outsourcing it to the Foxconn of the world, the BYD of the world. For the car business we undertook the task of building an entire new factory in Beijing. That's our first real large scale factory. But there's a lot of similarities because increasingly the software drive the hardware in the EV just like they're in the smartphone. In smartphone we stack up a lot of chipset into a very small form factor, in the car is similar we have to stack up a lot of components. Especially in the EV world the software becomes very important. How do you manage battery power? How do you manage the software to drive the car in a safe manner? Managing the supply chain is also very important. We have a lot of suppliers that are the same between the smartphone business as well as the EV business.

中欧 EV 市场的竞争与协作

Nicolai Tangen: 在电动汽车方面，欧洲落后了多少？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How far behind is Europe when it comes to EVs?

林世伟: 我们仍然非常仰慕欧洲的传统 OEM 厂商，在品牌建设、动力传动系统和驾驶体验方面，有很多值得学习的地方。我们认为他们目前缺乏的是两点：第一是汽车的“聪明程度”（Smartness），他们在智能功能实现上落后于中国；第二是对 NEV（新能源汽车） 的定义。有些厂商太专注于“电动”，而我们定义小米汽车是“智能电动汽车”。

小米的优势在于能将汽车与整个生态系统连接：手机、IoT 设备等，创造出超越汽车本身的智能生活生态。

Original English

Alain Lam: We still look up to a lot of the traditional OEMs. There's a lot of things to learn from them—the way that they build the brand, the drivetrain, the driving experience. What we think that they are lacking are number one, the smartness of the car. I think in that sense they are probably behind what we are seeing in China right now. And second is the new energy vehicle development. We call our EV a smart electric vehicle. Some OEMs may focus too much on electric, but let's focus on smart. How do I link it with all of our ecosystem products? I can create not just a EV but also a smart life ecosystem beyond the EV.

Nicolai Tangen: 你认为西方品牌需要多长时间才能赶上？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: And how long time do you think it would take the western brands to catch up on that particular element?

林世伟: 很多人都在学习。许多欧洲厂商来访问我们，进行经验分享。我们也看到了一些欧洲公司与中国选手的合作，这有利于推动整个行业向前发展。听说福特（Ford）的 CEO 试驾了我们的 SU7 六个月后，都舍不得把它还回去。

Original English

Alain Lam: A lot of people are learning and studying how the Chinese EV development has become so advanced. We have received quite a lot of European OEMs who come to visit us for experience sharing. I think you already seen a couple of the European collaboration with the Chinese players as well. I think it was the CEO of Ford who used your SU7 for a little while. He said he couldn't get rid of it after 6 months of driving it because he loved it too much.

仿人机器人与“物理 AI”的拐点

Nicolai Tangen: 让我们谈谈机器人和仿人机器人。我看到了你们最新的模型，简直令人难以置信。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Let's move on to robotics and humanoids. I've just seen your latest models and it's pretty mind-boggling.

林世伟: 我们投入机器人领域已经五六年了。现在我们开始看到市场的拐点：硬件和软件（即驱动机器人大脑的 AI）都在变好。

目前，小米开发仿人机器人的主要目的是提升我们自身的制造能力和效率。我们还没有推出面向消费者的（2C）机器人。视频里展示了两个机器人可以连续工作三小时，误差极低。

但未来还有很长的路要走。现在的 AI 主要学习文本和语音数据，而物理数据的积累和解释还非常落后。开发“物理 AI”（Physical AI）的大模型，目前最缺乏的是尚未存在的物理交互数据。

Original English

Alain Lam: We've been doing robotics for five six years now. I think we are beginning to see the inflection point in this market. A combination of the hardware getting better as well as the software getting better i.e. AI that is driving the brain of the robots. The way that Xiaomi internally is thinking about it is we are making these humanoid robots to enhance our own manufacturing capability and efficiency. We haven't launched any 2C robots. All the humanoid robots we are developing right now are used in our own manufacturing scenario. But still I think that's a long way to go because the AI right now is mostly focusing on learning from the text data and voice data, but then the physical data are still quite a way behind in terms of the collection and the interpretation. When you develop the large language model for physical AI right now, what we still lacking is a lot of data that don't exist, right? So you have to stimulate it.

Nicolai Tangen: 能说说最新模型的进展吗？比如手部的抓握功能。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Can you tell us about the improvement in the grip functionality in your latest model?

林世伟: 它的手部尺寸现在已经非常接近人类，且拥有更高的自由度。另一个挑战是散热。就像智能手机或任何机器一样，长时间运行会发热，性能就会下降。我们引入了散热系统来进行手部的热管理。

至于何时推向消费者，这目前不是小米的重点。我们首先要用它来重塑整个制造过程。

Original English

Alain Lam: The hand is very similar to a human hand size now, and also the degree of freedom that it offer. The second problem that we're trying to solve is the cooling. Once it get heated, the performance will degrade. So we're trying to introduce a cooling system. When do you think you'll have this for consumers? That's not the focus of Xiaomi right now. We want these humanoid robots to be able to improve our manufacturing efficiency first and maybe to recreate our entire manufacturing processes.

AI 战略与物理 AI 的未来

Nicolai Tangen: 下一步是什么？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: What's next?
