伊朗战争专家紧急警告：最危险的‘第四阶段’已经开启

关键字 geopolitics military-strategy nuclear-proliferation energy-security diplomatic-crisis

摘要

芝加哥大学教授 Robert Pape 深度解析伊朗局势。他指出，美国的轰炸行动反而增强了伊朗的政治凝聚力，使其意识到美国无法通过空袭彻底击败它。随着伊朗控制霍尔木兹海峡，并可能在一年内拥有核武器，中俄伊三方协作正以前所未有的速度重塑全球权力平衡，美国正面临由于过度依赖军事手段而陷入的战略陷阱。

军事行动的误区：为什么轰炸无法击败伊朗

史蒂芬: 罗伯特·佩普教授，很高兴再次见到你。自我们上次讨论以来已经过去了四周，战争的进展速度惊人。你当时的许多预测已经成真，还有一些正在演变。现在外界众说纷纭，但我感觉在伊朗、以色列、特朗普和美国各自的表述之下，隐藏着某种真相。教授，首先请告诉大家，你是谁，为什么你能在这个话题上有发言权？

英文原文

Stephen: Professor Robert Pape, good to see you again. It's been 4 weeks since we sat down and talked about everything that was happening in the war and it's all moved at light speed. You made some predictions then. Many of them have come true already and many of them still unfolding. But I wanted to get you back to talk about what the hell is going on. And I think that's kind of how I started last conversation. But there's so much that's being said and I get the sense that there's a truth that sits underneath there somewhere... My first question to you, professor, is who are you and who are you to speak on this subject matter?

佩普教授: 我是芝加哥大学的教授，在那教书快27年了。在此之前，我曾在美国空军任教，教授常规目标打击。我最初是想了解我们为什么会输掉越南战争，这促成了我后来的著作**《轰炸致胜》**（Bombing to Win）。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: I am a professor at the University of Chicago. I have been there for 26 years almost 27 years and before that I was a professor who taught for the US Air Force. I taught conventional targeting and I thought I was going to go into the foreign service. I wanted to understand how we lost the Vietnam War and this became the origins of Bombing to Win.

史蒂芬: 你所谓的“空中战役”具体指什么？

英文原文

Stephen: When you say air campaigns, for someone that knows nothing about military conflict, what do you mean by air campaigns?

佩普教授: “空中战役”是指军用飞机不仅是进行一天的单次袭击，而是持续数天、数周、数月，甚至像越南那样持续数年的行动。我研究的是，为什么有些国家在军事实力如此强大的情况下，进行了深思熟虑的空中战役，结果却依然是失败者。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: What I mean with an air campaign is when you have military aircraft who were not just doing a single raid bombing one target one day, but doing a campaign over days, weeks, months... Why is it that when a strong power really gets its act together, it's not careless, it's really thinking hard, it then applies this force, a campaign overtime and comes out a loser.

美国轰炸伊朗的模拟结果：无法触及的‘核心’

史蒂芬: 你花了21年时间模拟美国与伊朗之间的战争。在你的模型中，美国是如何走出这种局面的？

英文原文

Stephen: And you modeled for 20 years a war with Iran versus the United States... how did America come out of this situation?

佩普教授: 调查结果非常一致：我们的轰炸机总能摧毁工业设施，但无论在哪一年模拟，我们都无法摧毁已经浓缩的铀材料。就像你在河里淘金，我们可以摧毁你的淘金盘，甚至摧毁整条河，但金子还在那里。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: There was a consistent set of findings you just couldn't ignore, Stephen, which is our bombers would always be able to destroy the target, the industrial facility that was enriching the uranium. The problem always was... you wouldn't be able to destroy the enriched material, the actual gold.

史蒂芬: 也就是说，轰炸只能把浓缩铀埋在废墟下，这只是在拖延时间，因为他们随时可以回来把它挖出来继续进程。

英文原文

Stephen: So they could bomb these sites where they're making the enriched uranium, but it wouldn't destroy the enriched uranium. It would just put it underneath a bunch of rubble... you're basically just kicking the can down the road.

佩普教授: 没错。而且他们可能会预判轰炸的到来并提前分散物资。去年底的“午夜之锤”行动（Operation Midnight Hammer），美国出动了 B2 轰炸机和巨型钻地弹（MOAB），一切都和我三周前给学生们演示的模型一模一样。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: That's right... And at the end of last year they did operation midnight hammer where they bombed the sides with these incredible... exactly as we did in class... almost exactly the platforms... every single thing we talked about unfolded just as we had modeled in class.

战争催生民族主义：伊朗反而更强大了

佩普教授: 我真正的专长是军事行动与政治的互动。你打击的不只是工业目标，这个国家的民众和政权会在政治上做出反应。我在研究越战时发现，民众的政治反应往往会压倒战术上的军事效果。轰炸反而会激发民众更努力地克服损失。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: My real specialty, Stephen, is the interaction of military action and politics. You're not just hitting an industrial target. The people in the country, the population, the regime, they're reacting to that politically... the political reactions by the population often are overwhelming the tactical military effects.

佩普教授: 现在的情况是，我们并没有削弱伊朗，反而增强了伊朗。伊朗已经意识到我们无法击败他们。我们可以炸毁他们地面上的发射器，但他们已经把无人机和导弹库深埋在地下。尽管美国释放了巨大的空中力量，技术领先，但我们无法阻止他们对霍尔木兹海峡船只的袭击。他们深知这一点，并正在极大地利用这一优势。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: We have strengthened Iran... Iran has figured out that we can't beat them... We can see anything that's above ground... But what they've been doing is they have been not just deeply burying their industrial enrichment facilities, they've been deeply burying their arsenals of drones, deeply burying their arsenals of missiles... we can't stop their drone attacks against the ships in the straight of Hormuz. They know it.

特朗普与“三个中心的博弈”

佩普教授: 我们现在正处于一个分叉路口。没有人能回到2月27日（战前状态）了。摆在我们面前的只有两条路：要么进行一场全面的地面战争，要么接受伊朗成为世界权力的第四个中心。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: We're at a branch, a fork in the road. There's no way to go back to February 27... either we go through with the ground war or Iran becomes an emerging not right away fourth center of world power.

史蒂芬: 请解释一下这个“权力中心”的概念。

英文原文

Stephen: Explain that to me.

佩普教授: 伊朗正在通过选择性封锁霍尔木兹海峡来产生政治权力，迫使其他国家屈服。亚洲80%到90%的石油经过那里。印度现在没有站在美国这边，而是保持中立甚至向伊朗靠拢。日本也在疏远美国。伊朗想要的正是让美国的亚洲盟友重新定向。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: The shipping that goes through the straight of Hormuz, 80 to 90% of it is going right to Asia. The power that comes with that is with say India. India is not siding with the United States... Japan... She's distancing herself from the United States. That's exactly what Iran wants out of America's Asian allies.

佩普教授: 此外，特朗普正在要求盟友自掏腰包加强防御，这实际上是拆掉了美军作为“安全保证人”的地位。在波斯湾，中东国家正在分裂成三个阵营：伊拉克在疏远我们；阿曼和卡塔尔在观望；沙特和阿联酋则开始寻求巴基斯坦作为安全保障。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: President Trump is not even willing to do much to actually defend our own bases... What he's telling them is you go out there and start defending yourself. Well, that's not a guaranter of security... All of this coalition, it's not all siding with Iran right now. It's fragmenting. And that's weakening America.

能源武器化：俄伊中的协作

佩普教授: 一旦我们撤退，伊朗的权力会迅速增长。它与俄罗斯和中国的关系将更加紧密。俄罗斯几乎立即向伊朗提供了瞄准美国军舰的各种信息。如果伊朗（拥有20%世界石油储备）和俄罗斯（11%）进行协作，将30%的石油撤出全球市场，让中国吸收，这将给美国和欧洲带来巨大的经济灾难。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: Russia almost immediately offered Iran military targeting information to target US ships... Iran has control now of 20% of the world's oil. Russia has 11%... That means there can be either formal or tacic cooperation to take 30% of the world's oil off the global market. Let China soak up a whole lot of that. And that can truly produce mega economic consequences for America, for Europe.

关于以色列的“外交搅局者”角色

史蒂芬: 观众中有很多关于以色列角色的问题。有人怀疑我们的情报受以色列左右。

英文原文

Stephen: One of them was about Israel's role in this... there are some people that are skeptical that the intelligence is coming from Israel and that therefore that it might not be as accurate...

佩普教授: 我会说以色列一直充当着“外交搅局者”的角色。去年六月，特朗普打算与一组伊朗人谈判，结果第二天以色列空军就炸死了那些谈判代表。今年2月28日的轰炸最初也是由以色列发起的，美国随后才跟进。国务卿卢比奥解释说，以色列把我们逼到了墙角。以色列在向外界描绘伊朗是“纸老虎”，只需最后一击就能垮台。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: I would say Israel has been playing the role of diplomatic spoiler... Donald Trump said he was going to negotiate with a certain set of Iranians and literally the next day... Israeli air power killed them... The public description is that Iran is simply a paper tiger... That has been Prime Minister Netanyahu's language.

“文明消亡”论与 9200 万伊朗民众

佩普教授: 特朗普最近在社交媒体上威胁要“让一整个文明在今晚消亡”。这不是一个醉汉在酒吧胡言乱语，这是一个掌握着数千枚核武器的人。这在美国总统历史上是最露骨的种族灭绝意图声明。这种言论只会让 9200 万伊朗人感到生存威胁，迫使他们支持政府研发核武器。即使是伊朗国内的亲民主运动，现在也不得不向政府寻求保护。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: That statement that President Trump said that he will end an entire civilization in one night... this is the most declared statement of genocidal intent we've ever seen from an American president... what President Trump has done by making those statements is he's persuading all 92 million Iranians that he is willing to kill them... President Trump is bonding them together like never before.

第三与第四阶段的陷阱

史蒂芬: 你还是认为最可能的结果是特朗普派出地面部队去夺取铀材料和保护海峡吗？

英文原文

Stephen: So, you still think the most plausible probable outcome is that Trump ends up sending ground troops in specifically to do two things... to go and get the uranium... and to defend the straight of Hormuz?

佩普教授: 是的，我认为有70%的机会开始地面行动。这不是因为特朗普想打仗，而是因为他掉进了陷阱。他愿意成为那个在任期内见证伊朗引爆核武器的总统吗？他的一系列威胁和轰炸已经让伊朗人不仅想要核威慑，更想要报复。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: I still say there's a 70% chance that we're going to start a ground operation... It's because there's a trap... You have 92 million people desperate now for not just getting a nuclear weapon for deterrence, but for payback.

佩普教授: 特朗普面临的是几个极其糟糕的选项。如果他不采取那种极端的“军事遏制以色列”来换取和平的离岸方案（这需要以色列加入《不扩散核武器条约》并接受核查，这在政治上几乎不可能），那么他就会像当年的林登·约翰逊（LBJ）一样，在战争的泥潭中失去权力，不仅是国际上的，也是国内的。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: He is stuck. He's facing several bad options... he's going to become LBJ if he doesn't take a deal soon... It is an unstable balance here between three and four.

史蒂芬: 谢谢你，罗伯特。这是一个令人困惑且沉重的局面。

英文原文

Stephen: Thank you, Robin... I am confused about which path is productive and most beneficial to society and humanity.

佩普教授: 让我们希望陷阱没我画的那么深。但如果真的如此，我们就不能再自欺欺人地认为这一切能很快解决。我们正在走向一条非常沉重的道路。

英文原文

Professor Robert Pape: And let's hope it's not as much of a trap as I'm painting it. But if it is, then it's really even more important that... we don't mislead ourselves into thinking that this is just temporary.
