寿命与基因研究

Tracy Mumford: 最后，关于长寿的关键，我指的是活到 90 岁甚至 100 岁，一直以来都有研究、争论和理论。专家说，你需要活动身体。多吃水果蔬菜。保证充足睡眠。不吸烟。不过量饮酒。确保与朋友见面。然而，本周发表在《科学》（Science）杂志上的一项新研究，又确定了另一个因素：你的基因。根据这项新研究，你潜在的寿命就写在你的 DNA 里。健康的生活方式当然有帮助，但基本上，如果你想知道自己是否能活到 100 岁，你需要赢得长寿的基因彩票。研究人员查看了瑞典双胞胎的数据。他们还查看了超过 400 位活到 100 岁美国人及其所有兄弟姐妹的数据。他们排除了事故或感染等外部因素，发现就寿命差异而言，最大的单一因素似乎是基因。一位未参与该研究的公共卫生教授告诉《泰晤士报》，这项研究有一个强有力的信息：你并没有你想象的那么大的控制力。他说，基本上，结果是有些人开着梅赛德斯，而有些人则没那么幸运。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: And finally, the key to living a long life, I'm talking cracking 90 or even 100 years old, has been studied, debated, theorized about. Experts say you got to move your body. Eat your fruits and vegetables. Get enough sleep. Don't smoke. Don't drink too much. Make sure you see friends. A new study out this week in the journal Science, though pins down another factor, your genes. According to the new research, your potential lifespan is written in your DNA. A healthy lifestyle can help, of course, but basically if you want to know if you could live to be 100, you need to have won the genetic lottery for longevity. The researchers looked at data from sets of Swedish twins. They also looked at data from over 400 Americans who lived to be a 100 and what happened to all of their siblings. They controlled for outside factors like accidents or infections and found that the biggest single factor when it comes to differences in lifespans seems to be genes. One public health professor who was not involved in the study told the Times that the research has a powerful message. You don't have as much control as you think. He said, basically, it turns out some of us are driving a Mercedes and some of us aren't quite that lucky.
