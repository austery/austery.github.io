第二件事是重置价值（replacement value）。如果你图便宜只买一份最基础的精简保单，你可能无法获得损失资产的全部重置赔付，包括房屋主体结构以及屋内的所有物品。我们一次又一次听到这样的事情：人们为了省钱而削减保额，结果购买的保险无法保障全额重置成本。正如格雷格提到的，在当今高通胀的时代背景下，这一点至关重要。重置一座价值80万加元的住宅绝不是一笔小数目，重置里面的所有物品同样花费不菲。

我认为第三点就是免赔额（deductible）。大家通常试图通过提高免赔额来省钱，这本身无可厚非，但你必须做好万一出险时自掏腰包支付该免赔额的准备。免赔额可以是500或1000加元，但也可能是2000、5000加元甚至更高。提高免赔额确实能拉低每月的保费支出，但在遭受重大损失时，你必须具备承担这笔免赔金额的财务承受力。

英文原文 >> We're not combing through the fine print. As a matter of fact, my wife's aunt and uncle had a basement flood in in the 2013 flooding in Toronto, and I was reading their policy, and I've been that in that game for 35 years. I had trouble understanding their policy. It took me a good half hour to determine if they had coverage or not. So I'm having trouble. Your average person is going to have more trouble. So a couple of things to watch out for. There's been umpteen polls and surveys which indicate that Canadians think that they're covered for flood automatically, and they're not. Okay. So either whether we're talking about sewer backup or whether we're talking about overland flooding, those are add ons to your policy. You have to ensure that you have those included in your policy. So talk to your insurance provider about that. And you could even call them up and make sure you have it. These days, some companies are folding the sewer backup and the overland flood into a single product. So you have to see if you have some sort of Cadillac version, high end version of water damage coverage. The other thing is replacement value. If you get a bare bones policy and try to go cheap, cheap, cheap, you may not get full replacement value for what you've lost. That includes the home, the structure, and everything in it. We hear this time and time again where people have tried to skimp, tried to save money, and they got insurance that did not guarantee full replacement value. And as Greg mentioned, in this day and age of really high inflation, that's really, really important. Replacing a $800,000 home is not a cheap exercise in replacing everything in. It is not a cheap exercise either. And I think then the third thing is the deductible. People try to save money by getting having a high deductible, which is fine, but you have to be prepared to pay that deductible if something happens. So, you know, you can have a deductible of $500 or $1000, but you could also have one of $2,000, 5000 or even higher than that. Again, it gets your monthly payment down, but you have to be prepared to pay that deductible in the event that that you get a lot of damage.
