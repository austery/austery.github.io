中东情报：伊朗化学武器疑云

现在播报一条来自中东的最新消息。《纽约时报》获悉，以色列已向美国通报了一份情报，称伊朗正在考虑使用化学武器，可能是将其安装在导弹或无人机上。但美国官员在对待这份情报的严肃程度上存在分歧。一些美国官员认为该情报令人警惕。美国最近一直在监测来自伊朗的大量威胁，特别是在破获了一起伊朗企图袭击美军战机在英国所使用空军基地的阴谋之后。然而，其他美国官员深表怀疑。他们怀疑的不是伊朗是否在研发化学武器，而是它是否准备使用它们。这些官员认为，以色列共享的情报是本雅明·内塔尼亚胡总理试图推动特朗普总统重启针对伊朗的大规模作战行动的一部分。对一些官员来说，关于伊朗化学武器威胁的讨论甚至让人联想起2000年代初关于伊拉克是否拥有大规模杀伤性武器的有缺陷的美国情报，而该情报当时被援引为美国入侵的理由。就伊朗自身而言，它承认几十年前曾发展过化学武器能力，但表示后来已将其拆解。

英文原文 Now, an update from the Middle East. The Times has learned that Israel has shared intelligence with the US that Iran is considering the use of chemical weapons, possibly by putting them on a missile or drone. But American officials are divided on how seriously to take that intel. Some US officials believe the intel is cause for alarm. The US has been monitoring a lot of threats from Iran recently, especially since it uncovered an Iranian plot to target an air base used by American war plananes in Britain. Other US officials, however, are deeply skeptical. Not that Iran is developing chemical weapons, but that it's ready to use them. Those officials see Israel's shared intelligence as part of an effort by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to try and push President Trump toward restarting major combat operations against Iran. For some officials, the talk of a chemical weapons threat from Iran has even evoked the flawed US intelligence from the early 2000s about whether Iraq had weapons of mass destruction, which was cited as a reason for the US to invade. For its part, Iran has acknowledged it developed chemical weapons capabilities decades ago, but has said it later dismantled them.
