当编舞遇上病毒式传播：Sean Bankhead 的创作哲学

关键字 choreography viral-marketing pop-culture cultural-influence inspiration

摘要

著名编舞师 Sean Bankhead 分享了他为 Victoria Monét 的歌曲《On My Mama》创作病毒式传播舞蹈的理念。他强调了融合 HBCU 文化、黑人文化、滑板文化和网络迷因文化等多元灵感的重要性。Bankhead 认为，将个人热情与真诚投入到作品中，以及包含易于模仿的“妈妈舞步”，是舞蹈视频走红的关键。他还致敬了 Michael Jackson、Janet Jackson 等偶像，并展望了新一代舞者和编舞师的潜力。

英文原文

SB: Woo! I'm going to catch my breath. Thank you. So myself and Victoria knew that we wanted to pay homage to so many different things that have inspired us in pop culture: HBCU culture, Black culture, college culture, skate culture, even meme culture and things that you see on Twitter that are just really funny and memorable. And we decided to make a kind of a mixing pot of a lot of different, really cool things that have inspired us. And that’s how “On My Mama” came about.

病毒式传播的秘诀

Helen Walters: 那么，我们应该明确一点，那个视频在网上火爆到不行。你认为为什么有些舞蹈视频会走红（病毒式传播）呢？

英文原文

HW: So we should be clear, that video went super bonkers online. So why do you think that some dance videos go viral?

Sean Bankhead: 有几方面原因。第一，我认为是运气或者神，谢谢。继续创作热门内容。有一点，我认为，我总是把百分之 1000 的心血、时间和精力投入到我创作的任何东西中。所以，如果我开始一个项目，我想要做到极致。我想要把我百分之 1000 的自己投入到这个项目中。人们能感受到心意，能感受到热情。我认为这能通过屏幕传达。还有一点，说来也 odd（奇怪），这首歌叫《On My Mama》，我总是喜欢在视频里加入至少一个我妈妈也能跳的动作或者节奏。

英文原文

SB: There's a couple things. One, I think luck or God, thank you. Still making things that are hot. One thing, I think, I always put 1,000 percent of my heart and time and energy into anything I create. So if I'm stepping into a project, I want to kill it. I want to put 1,000 percent of myself into the project. And people can feel heart, they can feel passion. And I think it translates through the screen. And I think another thing is, oddly enough, the song is called "On My Mama," and I always love having at least one move or one groove that my mama can do, in a video.
