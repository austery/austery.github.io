开票现象与“选举幻象”

随着开票结果开始出炉，需要牢记的一件非常重要的事是：你看到的首批选举结果可能无法代表最终的计票情况。一名候选人在最初的开票报告中可能遥遥领先，但随着夜色加深和更多选票被统计，这种领先优势可能会缩小甚至反转。这种现象被称为“幻象”。

英文原文 >> One really important thing to keep in mind as the results start coming in is that the first election results that you see may not be representative of the final vote tallies. A candidate could be way ahead in the initial vote reports, but that lead could shrink or even reverse as the night goes on and more votes are counted. This phenomenon is known as a mirage.

克里斯汀·张（Christine Jang）为《纽约时报》报道选举数据。她表示，幻象可能由多种原因引起。

英文原文 >> Christine Jang covers election data for the times. She says mirages can for a number of reasons.

最常见的原因可能是该县或州在不同时间处理不同类型的选票。例如，一些州会提前预处理邮寄选票、缺席选票和提前投票，而这些类型的提前选票往往倾向于民主党。所以，在这种情况下你可能会看到“蓝色幻象”。可能发生这种情况的另一个例子，是某个州内的某些特定区域率先汇报。因此，在某些州，较小、偏乡村的县最先报告，这就造成了一定程度的“红色幻象”，因为全美大部分地区的乡村县都倾向于共和党。

这可能会引起混淆或招致对计票的怀疑，但必须记住，取决于具体的州或辖区，类似这样的幻象相当普遍，而且在一定程度上是可以预测的。

英文原文 >> The most common reason could be that the county or state processes different types of ballots at different times. For example, some states pre-process mail and absentee and early ballots and those types of early votes tend to lean Democratic. So, you'd see a blue mirage in this case. Another example of when this might happen is when certain kind of areas in a state report first. So, in some states the smaller, more rural counties are the first ones to report and that creates somewhat of a red mirage because rural counties in most of the country leans Republican.

This might cause confusion or invite suspicion about the vote count, but it's important to keep in mind that mirages like this are quite common and somewhat predictable depending on the state or jurisdiction.

男性健身新趋势：普拉提的普及

最后，不知何故，在这个选举季，男性的身体比我想大多数人预见到的更成为一个话题。但是，说到这一点，这里有一个应景的故事：越来越多的男性正在练普拉提。

这项侧重于核心力量和缓慢、受控动作的全身锻炼，长期以来一直更多被视为女性的锻炼项目。但男子体育界的大牌人物已经转向了它：汤姆·布雷迪（Tom Brady）、勒布朗·詹姆斯（LeBron James），而且不仅仅是他们。根据行业组织健康与健身协会（Health and Fitness Association）的数据，在过去5年中，美国参加普拉提课程的男性人数翻了一番，从200万增加到400万。

尽管与成套的两件式运动服紧密相关，普拉提从来都不是一项针对特定性别的运动。它的创始人约瑟夫·普拉提（Joseph Pilates）是一名德国马戏团表演者和拳击手，他在第一次世界大战期间被拘留在英国时，开始向狱友传授他的方法。

从那里，他移居到纽约，许多舞者（其中许多是女性）在一间工作室参加他的课程，随后这一运动不断传播扩散。

据健康与健身协会主席称，现在有更多男性尝试普拉提（无论是在垫子上还是在核心床器械上），这一转变与更广泛的健身趋势转变相一致。她说：“总体而言，现在健身房里的方式减少了性别界限。”并表示，引用原话：“你现在能在力量训练室看到女性，也能在普拉提课上看到男性。”

英文原文 >> And finally, somehow men's bodies became more of a theme this election season than I think most people saw coming. But, on that note, here's an apropos story. More men are doing Pilates.

The full-body workout that focuses on core strength and slow, controlled movements has long been seen as more of a women's workout. But, big names in men's sports have turned to it. Tom Brady, LeBron James, and it's not just them. According to the Health and Fitness Association, an industry group, the number of men taking Pilates classes in the US has doubled in the last 5 years, from 2 million to 4 million.

Despite being closely associated with matching two pieces, Pilates was never intended as a gendered exercise. It's founder, Joseph Pilates, was a German circus performer and boxer, who, when he was being held in the UK during World War I, started teaching his methods to his fellow inmates.

From there, he moved to New York, where a lot of dancers, many of them women, took his classes at a studio, and it spread and spread.

The shift now, with more men trying out Pilates, either on a mat or on a reformer machine, is in line with a shift in exercise trends more broadly, according to the president of the Health and Fitness Association. She said, "Overall, there's now a less gendered approach [music] at the gym." Saying, quote, "You see women in the weight room now, and you see men in [music] Pilates."
