白宫人事更迭：麦克纳尼简报首秀与驻希腊大使引咎辞职

特朗普政府近期迎来了人事变动的一进一出。曾活跃于福克斯新闻（Fox News）与 Newsmax 的保守派评论员凯莱·麦克纳尼（Kayleigh McEnany: 前白宫新闻秘书及媒体评论员）正式接替今年夏天离职的卡罗琳·莱维特（Caroline Leavitt），主持了她上任后的首场白宫新闻简报会。在简报中，麦克纳尼向特朗普总统的信任表达感谢，并强调本届政府始终在为美国人民谋福祉。不过整场简报仅持续了短短14分钟，麦克纳尼大部分时间都在照本宣科，仅点名了来自福克斯新闻、《每日邮报》（The Daily Mail）和布赖特巴特新闻网（Breitbart）的三名记者。当被问及美国是否已停止与乌克兰共享情报时，她仅表示后续会再作答复；她还宣称总统本人就是最好的发言人，记者可以直接向特朗普提问。此外，针对麦克纳尼的职业背景此前引发的争议——特朗普称其为律师且麦克纳尼本人拥有法学学位，但在《纽约时报》此前询问其在哪一州通过律师资格考试（Bar Exam: 美国各州执业律师资格考试）时，她曾给出“当我搬到中国时”这一含糊回答，白宫官员随后也拒绝对其究竟是否或在何处通过律考作出明确说明。

与此同时，麦克纳尼在简报中证实了一则重要离职通报：特朗普已实质上解雇了美国驻希腊大使金伯利·吉尔福伊尔（Kimberly Guilfoyle: 前福克斯新闻主持人、检察官及前外交官），白宫已要求其辞职。吉尔福伊尔的去职源于一系列负面报道发酵。此前《华尔街日报》披露，她在去年夏天参议院批准其任命前夕，曾请求一名共和党捐款人替她偿还10万美元的信用卡账单。根据捐款人向《纽约时报》提供的通讯记录，吉尔福伊尔曾在短信中写道：“亲爱的，求你了，我今天就需要你帮我办妥这件事”，并许诺只要汇款就会在特朗普阵营中充当对方的铁杆支持者。此外，《华尔街日报》还曝光了她在能源交易中涉嫌潜在的利益冲突。针对此事，特朗普总统表示吉尔福伊尔“此前工作干得不错，但我们不喜欢所看到的新闻报道”，并试图淡化与这位前福克斯新闻主持人、曾与其长子小唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump Jr.）订婚的前检察官的关系，直言“她不是我的家族成员”。在出任驻希腊大使的短暂任期内，吉尔福伊尔高调行事，不仅登上《Vogue》希腊版封面，还在与希腊总统互动时开玩笑称自己来这里是为了寻找新丈夫，并频频流连于雅典夜店，声称活跃的社交生活展现了她对工作的投入。在确认辞职的社交媒体声明中，吉尔福伊尔坚称将有力捍卫自己的名誉与清白。

From The New York Times, it's The Headlines. I'm Tracy Mumford. Today is Wednesday, October 14th. Here's what we're covering.

To start, two updates on the Trump administration, one arrival and one departure. First, good morning. Kayleigh McEnany held her first briefing yesterday as the new White House press secretary.

"I want to thank President Trump for his trust that he has placed in me. I have wanted to work for him for a very long time."

The conservative commentator who used to appear on Fox News and Newsmax is taking over for Caroline Leavitt, who left the job this summer.

"This is a president and administration that is looking out for you, the American people, and I'm thrilled to officially be part of this incredible journey together. And with that, I will take your questions."

McEnany spent most of her time yesterday reading from a script and only called on three reporters, from Fox News, The Daily Mail, and Breitbart.

"Has the US stopped sharing intel with Ukraine?"

"I'll have to get back to you on that."

In all, the whole thing was very short, just 14 minutes, during which McEnany said the president was his own best spokesperson and that reporters could ask him their questions directly. Since McEnany was announced as press secretary, there have been questions about her background. Trump has called her an attorney. She identifies herself as a lawyer and has a law degree, but when The Times asked which state she had passed the bar exam in, she replied, quote, "When I moved to China." Asked later for clarification, White House officials would not say whether or where she passed the bar.

Now, when it comes to the departure from the administration:

"We have asked Ambassador Guilfoyle to resign. She has been a wonderful asset to the team and we wish her well."

Zacharias did break one piece of news in her first briefing, announcing that Trump had effectively fired Kimberly Guilfoyle, the US ambassador to Greece. Guilfoyle's resignation comes after a string of damaging reports, including one from the Wall Street Journal that she had asked a Republican donor to pay off her $100,000 credit card bill last summer, right before she was confirmed by the Senate. According to messages the donor provided to the Times, Guilfoyle asked him, quote, "Honey, please, I need you to get this done for me today." and promised that she would serve as his ride or die in Trump world if he sent her the money. The Journal also reported on her potential conflicts of interest over energy deals.

Yesterday, President Trump said, quote, "She was doing a good job, but we didn't like what we were reading." He also tried to downplay his connection to Guilfoyle, who is a former Fox News host and former prosecutor. Trump nominated her as one of his first ambassadors when he returned to office, and she was once engaged to his son, Donald Trump Jr. But the president said, quote, "She wasn't a member of my family." During her brief time as ambassador in Greece, Guilfoyle made a splash, appearing on the cover of Vogue Greece, joking with the Greek president that she was there to find a new husband, and staying out late at Athens nightclubs, as she said her active social life showed her commitment to the job. In a social media post confirming her resignation, Guilfoyle wrote that she plans to quote, "Vigorously defend my name, my reputation, and my integrity."

教皇利奥确诊早期肺癌与康奈尔大学性侵诉讼风波

在国际与社会议题方面，梵蒂冈官方正式通报，71岁的教皇利奥（Pope Leo）在外科医生切除其右肺下叶后，被确诊患有肺腺癌（Lung Adenocarcinoma: 肺部非小细胞癌中最常见的一种病理分型）。此前周日，教皇在圣伯多禄广场向聚集祈祷的数万信众透露肺部发现微小占位病变并将接受手术。尽管梵蒂冈拒绝评论教皇是否有吸烟史，但医学专家依据公开通报评估认为该病症处于早期阶段；早期病变通常以手术切除为主，术后生存率极高。这一诊断也引发了外界对这位领导全球14亿天主教徒的领袖后续如何应对繁重圣职的关注。教皇近期刚结束对西班牙和法国的牧灵访问，目前其仍计划按原定行程于下月出访拉美。

美国高校安全机制同样面临风暴。康奈尔大学（Cornell University）校方宣布在整个学年内全面禁止兄弟会派对（Frat Parties: 校园男性兄弟会组织举办的大型派对），并在校警系统中增配经验丰富的性侵案件调查员。这一举措旨在平息学生、教职员及校友对一起民事诉讼所暴露问题的强烈不满。在该起诉讼中，一名代号为“简·多伊”（Jane Doe: 法律上指代未具名女性原告的标准称谓）的女学生指控其于2024年秋季遭到多名兄弟会成员轮奸。事发当时地方检察官并未提出刑事指控，但随着当事人对校方及7名涉案男子（均坚称无罪）正式提起民事诉讼，该案已被重新立案启动调查。康奈尔大学校长在宣布整改措施的信函中承认校方在履行安全职责上存在短板，并在接受《纽约时报》专访时表达了个人歉意。校长透露，受害者最初报案时他便知晓指控，但在随后的数月间既未主动跟进该学生的情况，亦不知道其已退学，同时并未追踪案件处理进展或涉案人员是否受罚。

At the Vatican, officials announced that Pope Leo has been diagnosed with lung cancer after surgeons removed a lower lobe of his right lung. The official diagnosis is lung adenocarcinoma, a common form of the disease. The Vatican declined to comment on whether Leo was ever a smoker, but according to one medical expert the time spoke with, the information the Vatican has released so far seems to indicate it's early stage, which is typically treated with surgery and tends to have a very high survival rate.

The Pope, who is 71, had announced on Sunday he was about to have the surgery, sharing the news with thousands of people who had gathered in St. Peter's Square to hear his weekly prayer. He said at that point doctors had identified a small growth in his lung. His cancer diagnosis raises questions about how he'll handle his demanding role moving forward leading the world's 1.4 billion Catholics. He just recently traveled to Spain and France, and for now he has said he will keep his plan to travel to Latin America next month.

At Cornell University, administrators have now banned frat parties for the rest of the school year and say they will add experienced sexual assault investigators to work with the campus police force. The updates come as the university is trying to reassure students, faculty, and alumni that it is addressing the concerns highlighted in a student's recent lawsuit in which she said she was raped by multiple frat brothers in the fall of 2024. At the time, the local prosecutor did not bring charges, but the case has since been reopened after she sued the school and seven men she accused of being involved, all of whom have denied wrongdoing.

The president of Cornell said in a letter announcing the changes that quote, "We must meet this moment by acknowledging where we have fallen short." In an interview with the Times, the president said he was personally sorry for how the university handled the accusations. He said he'd learned about Jane Doe's allegations when she first came forward, but in the months after he did not follow up with her or know that she ended up withdrawing from the school. He also didn't seek to find out what happened with the case or whether any of the accused men had been punished.

物联网供应链黑客袭击与传统苹果品种复兴

在工商业与网络安全领域，法国遭遇了一起前所未有的新型网络攻击。黑客将攻击矛头指向了许多人未曾意识到连入互联网的终端——超市冷藏系统。全法超过400家部署了远程温度控制软件的连锁超市受到波及。法国电视媒体报道显示，黑客控制了后台管理系统，在部分门店甚至将冷柜温度恶意调高至华氏120度（约合摄氏48.9度）以上，导致大量新鲜肉类与冷冻食品彻底腐坏，迫使超市倾倒成吨物资。法国一家大型超市连锁高管在广播采访中直呼前所未见，斥责这起事件是对国家食品供应体系的蓄意破坏。该高管指出事故损失巨大，单体小型杂货店因冷藏瘫痪导致的经济损失达数万美元，大型超市的直接损失则超过20万美元。目前尚无黑客组织宣布对此负责，巴黎检察官办公室已正式立案调查攻击来源并核实确切损失规模。

在消费文化与农业生态方面，当前正值苹果收获季。虽然现代商超中以蜜脆（Honeycrisp）、粉红佳人（Pink Lady）或红富士等主流品种占据主导，产业数据显示全美仅五大常用品种就占据了零售货架一半以上的总产量，但美国历史上曾拥有极高的生物多样性——一百多年前美国农业部（USDA）曾登记过本土种植的约1.4万种苹果。历经全美各地的“苹果侦探”耗费长达半个世纪翻阅古旧地籍图、复古苗圃目录和县博览会记录，一系列濒临绝种的传家宝苹果（Heirloom Apples: 经由几十年或上百年人工培育并代代保存的古老传统农作物种质）正重回大众视野。诸如康涅狄格州、威斯康星州和佛蒙特州的果园开始重新栽种曾受查尔斯·狄更斯喜爱的多汁品种“里布斯顿皮平”（Ribston Pippins）、外皮深紫适宜制作苹果泥的18世纪名品“黑牛津”（Black Oxfords）、带有蜂蜜与花香调的“康沃尔金盏花”（Cornish Gillyflower），以及“埃索珀斯斯皮岑堡”（Esopus Spitzenburg）与“哈伯德斯顿无双”（Hubbardston Nonesuch）等。佛蒙特州果园管理者表示，这类古老品种外形虽不如工业化商超苹果光鲜平整，但其风味层次远更为复杂独特，往往带有丁香或香槟的气息，甚至如罕见的英国品种“诺布罗塞特”（Knob Russet），外表虽粗糙宛如缩小的头颅，尝起来却宛如香蕉面包般醇厚香甜。

In France this week, business leaders say hackers carried out an unprecedented cyber attack that targeted systems many people may not even realize are connected to the internet, supermarket refrigerators. The attack hit more than 400 grocery stores across the country, which had been using software that allowed the supermarkets to remotely control the fridges. A French television network reported that the hackers took over those systems and in some cases jacked up the temperatures to more than 120° Fahrenheit, forcing workers to throw out tons of fresh and frozen food.

In a radio interview, one executive of a French supermarket chain said he'd never seen anything like it, calling it an attack on the country's food supply. The executive said the costs could be huge, explaining that a failing refrigeration system could put a small grocery store out tens of thousands of dollars. And for a big one, he said the losses could reach more than $200,000. No one has claimed responsibility for the attack yet, and prosecutors in Paris say they're working to identify the culprits and establish the full extent of the damage.

And finally, it is apple season and everybody's got an opinion. Maybe you're team Honeycrisp or a Pink Lady lover or maybe, somehow, you're out there defending Red Delicious. Whatever your pick, chances are you've only ever tasted a fraction of what's actually out there.

"The apple is red on the inside. That's crazy. This is called a Mount Rose apple."

"Mount Rose apple."

"What?"

That's because in the US today, just five varieties, the usual suspects, account for more than half of the apple production headed for grocery store shelves, according to an industry group. But, 100-plus years ago, the USDA cataloged something like 14,000 varieties that had been growing in the country.

"I got two heritage apples. This one's an Esopus Spitzenburg and this one's an Ashmead's Kernel."

And recently, some of these heirloom apple varieties have been making a comeback.

"This apple is called the Cornish Gillyflower, and it's known for rich, sweet, sharp, perfumed flavors with honeyed and floral nuances."

Orchards in Connecticut, Wisconsin, Vermont, and elsewhere are planting Ribston Pippins and Black Oxfords. The first being a juicy variety Charles Dickens apparently liked. The second, a 1700s specimen with deep purple skin that apparently makes beautiful applesauce. Just a side note here, they really knew how to name an apple back in the day. Who doesn't want a Hubbardston Nonesuch? These varieties are making a comeback thanks to 50 years of work by apple detectives around the country who poured over old property maps, vintage nursery catalogs, and county fair records, all in a quest to identify antique apples and prevent them from disappearing.

One thing to know if you are interested in antique apples, their looks are decidedly more homely than the pristine orbs piled up in the grocery store that you're used to. But, what they lack in beauty they often make up for in taste, mixing in a hint of clove or champagne. Consider the knob russet, for example, a rare British heirloom variety. As one orchard manager in Vermont told the times:

"it might look a little bit like a shrunken head, but her son thinks it tastes like banana bread."
