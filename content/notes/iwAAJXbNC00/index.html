吉尔伯特·克鲁兹: 跟我讲讲这本书的开篇吧，然后我想深入聊聊弗兰岑本人。

奥普拉风波、文学纯粹性与名声的代价

吉尔伯特·克鲁兹: 接下来我们必须谈谈那起著名的事件，尽管最后一切都和解了——那就是奥普拉读书俱乐部事件。

奥普拉选选中了这部小说作为她读书俱乐部的推荐书目。弗兰岑当时感到非常矛盾。他在几次采访中公开表示，对封面印上奥普拉的标志感到不适，觉得那太具商业企业色彩，而且他担心男性读者会因为认为奥普拉俱乐部推荐的书都是写给女性看的而远离这部小说。最终，奥普拉完全有权取消了邀请他上节目的决定。

虽然九年后当奥普拉选中他的下一部小说《自由》（Freedom）时，两人公开和解了。但那起事件在很长一段时间里都成为了弗兰岑身上撕不掉的标签。

Original English

Gilbert Cruz: And then we have to talk about this even though it ended up all being fine in the end. And then the Oprah thing happened, right? Oprah selected the novel for her book club. Franzen was conflicted. He said in a couple of interviews he was uncomfortable with having her seal on the cover. It sort of struck him as being overly corporate and he had the feeling that men maybe would stay away from the book because they associated the Oprah book club with books that were written for women.

And then eventually, totally her right, Oprah rescinded her invitation for him to appear on her show and discuss the book. They eventually ended up making up nine years later when she picked Freedom. But this incident has followed him.

塔菲·布罗德瑟-阿克纳: 人们对这件事情的记忆往往是存在偏差的。这件事之所以长久地附着在他身上，是因为它触及了我们讨论严肃文学时最深层的焦虑：什么是纯文学？什么是通俗流行虚构？一个严肃作家对于资本主义商业推广体系究竟负有什么责任？你可以在多大程度上追求商业上的巨大成功而不被视作“向商业出卖灵魂”（selling out）？

这也带有强烈的 X 世代（Gen X）文化心理烙印。在后来的岁月里，无论弗兰岑接受了多少次采访或长篇特写，他始终给人一种与现代流行文化格格不入的印象。他不使用社交媒体，坚守着传统文学阵地。我曾当面问过他，是不是因为内心过于脆弱而不敢直接在社交网络上面对大众读者的审视。

Original English

Taffy Brodesser-Akner: People remember it wrong. There's a part of it that exposes something about how we talk about literature, which is about like what is literature? What is popular fiction? What is the responsibility of an author to the capitalist infrastructure? How much should you be invested in your own success?

I think there's also like a Gen X "sellout" conversation happening here. I think a lot of that stuff underlies why it has stuck to him. Because he is also someone who in the years since, regardless of the fact that he has done many interviews, he still seems like someone who's against modern culture in some way. He doesn't participate in social media, and I pedaled to him the theory that he is too fragile to interact with the audience.

文学创作中的模仿与焦虑：《长岛妥协》背后的回响

吉尔伯特·克鲁兹: 在你创作自己的第二部小说《长岛妥协》时，《纠正》对你产生了怎样的影响？

Original English

Gilbert Cruz: How did The Corrections influence your own novel, Long Island Compromise?

塔菲·布罗德瑟-阿克纳: 当时我想写一个关于童年创伤如何贯穿家庭一生的故事。但我写着写着，发现写出来的东西感觉就像是廉价打折店版的《纠正》。我抗争了很久，试图把兄弟姐妹各自的章节缩短，后来我意识到我之所以缩短篇幅，仅仅是因为我害怕别人说我在抄袭《纠正》。

直到后来一位朋友对我说了一句让我释怀的话：“难道文学史不就是这样演进的吗？一部作品接着一部作品的借鉴与模仿，而你只是在倾尽全力做到最好。”

这也让我想起我们的前同事A.O. 斯科特（A.O. Scott）在他的《批评之死与艺术之生》（Better Living Through Criticism）一书中写到的：所有的艺术创作本质上都是试图去复印和重构失去的“伊甸园”。我们虽然明知自己永远无法完美复制它，但在这场全力以赴的努力中所蕴含的价值是无可替代的。每次我交稿时，我都会重读那段话，因为它提醒我即使未能达到神作的高度，这种追求本身就具有神圣的意义。

Original English

Taffy Brodesser-Akner: When I sat down to write about childhood trauma, it kept coming out as a dime-store Corrections. I fought it for so long. I tried to make the sections with the siblings shorter, and then I realized I was only doing that so that it wouldn't seem like I was copying The Corrections. And then I realized there is no copying The Corrections.

And then a friend said to me something that got me through, which is: "Don't you think this is how literature goes? It is imitation upon imitation and you are doing your best." And I think very often of our friend and colleague A.O. Scott and his book Better Living Through Criticism. The way it starts is saying that all art is trying to photocopy and recreate the Garden of Eden. And we mostly accept that we can't do that, but there is so much value in the effort.

影视改编的诅咒与梅丽尔·斯特里普加盟的新剧

吉尔伯特·克鲁兹: 《纠正》曾多次尝试被改编为影视作品。早年诺亚·鲍姆巴赫（Noah Baumbach）曾为 HBO 拍摄过一部由安东尼·霍普金斯（Anthony Hopkins）和黛安·韦斯特（Dianne Wiest）主演的试播集，但最终被砍掉了。如今，Netflix 又宣布了一部由科德·杰斐逊（Cord Jefferson）执导、梅丽尔·斯特里普（Meryl Streep）主演的全新剧集改编计划。你对此怎么看？

Original English

Gilbert Cruz: The Corrections has had a long history of attempted adaptations. Noah Baumbach shot an HBO pilot with Anthony Hopkins and Dianne Wiest that didn't go forward. Now Netflix has announced an adaptation directed by Cord Jefferson, who directed American Fiction, with Meryl Streep attached. Is Meryl Streep the perfect Enid?

塔菲·布罗德瑟-阿克纳: 梅丽尔·斯特里普演任何角色都是完美的。弗兰岑曾对我说过，他在内心深处其实总是暗自希望这些影视改编无法成功，因为他是“小说的狂热拥护者”。他非常清醒地认识到，当今的大众已经全面转向了电视和流媒体媒介，书籍的销量规模也早已无法与他当年出版《纠正》时同日而语。

但他同样明白，一部伟大小说之所以立足于世，正是因为它的文学性本身就是无法被影视镜头完全替代的。伊妮德这个角色极其复杂——在平庸琐碎与深沉的悲剧性之间，她承载了整部小说的灵魂重量。

Original English

Taffy Brodesser-Akner: She's the perfect anything. Franzen told me once, "I am sort of always rooting for these things to not work out because I am a partisan of the novel." He very much understands that the audience is a TV audience now. The numbers on books all around are different than they were when he published The Corrections.

But he also understands that a novel makes its case for itself by being a thing you can't truly adapt. Enid is a hard character to convey because beneath all her Midwestern domestic cliches is the emotional engine of the entire American post-war dream coming undone.
