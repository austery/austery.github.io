灰色地带博弈：俄罗斯对欧破坏升级与北约防御困境

过去几年间，欧洲多国接连发生针对关键基础设施的蓄意破坏事件。在波兰东南部，连接华沙的一条铁路干线因周末发生的爆炸而受损；西方安全官员指出，这是俄罗斯（Russia）策划并执行的一系列愈演愈烈、戏剧性破坏行动的一部分。情报显示，俄方的全盘计划甚至包括通过商业货运航空公司递送燃烧装置，这些危险包裹存在流入美国的潜在风险。此类破坏活动早在俄乌全面战争爆发初期便已展开，是俄方将战火外溢至西方世界的战略手段之一。近期，一架装载军用级炸药的无人机直接撞击了机场停机坪上的飞机机翼；多国情报与安全部门向《纽约时报》证实，这类行动的频率与烈度正在显著上升。仅在过去一周内，德国政府便披露俄罗斯是一起夏季机场未遂无人机袭击的幕后黑手；波兰官方证实一家无人机制造厂的火灾极可能是破坏所致；而丹麦情报机构亦警告，俄方正在该国招募人员企图对国防军工企业发动袭击。

尽管截至目前此类袭击尚未造成重大人员伤亡，但随着发生频次的增加，恶性后果的风险急剧攀升。全球安全机制正面临结构性考验：北大西洋公约组织（NATO）的建立初衷是为了威慑并应对大规模地面入侵，而非这种零敲碎打的渐进式破坏（Incremental Sabotage: 介于和平与全面战争之间的低烈度敌对行动）。俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京（Vladimir Putin）极其审慎地将行动烈度控制在传统战争门槛以及北约第五条（Article 5: 集体防御条款，规定对任一成员国的攻击视同对全体的攻击）的触发红线之下。面对这一局面，北约传统的驱逐外交官和经济制裁手段收效甚微。为打破被动僵局，部分西方国家开始尝试反制措施：例如德国政府在 8 月份自二战以来首次授权其情报机构对俄展开所谓的积极防御行动（Active Measures: 包括破坏行动与网络攻击等主动干预手段）。然而安全专家与官员普遍认为，即便采取更具攻击性的反制手段，短期内仍难以彻底遏制俄方的破坏势头。

From the New York Times, it's the headlines. I'm Will Jarvis in for Tracy Mumford. Today's Friday, September 4th. Here's what we're covering.

Over the past few years across Europe, a railway line linking Warsaw to southeastern Poland was damaged by a weekend explosion. Officials have been sounding the alarm about a dramatic and escalating series of sabotage attacks that they say are being carried out by Russia. It's part of a wider plot where Russia scheme to send the incendiary devices through a commercial carrier on planes that would potentially end up in the United States.

The attacks began just after the full-scale invasion of Ukraine as part of Russia's attempts to bring the war to the West more broadly. One of the drones equipped with military-grade explosives bounced off the wing of a plane on the tarmac. But security officials tell the Times that they're now becoming more intense and more frequent. In just the past week, German officials announced that Russia was behind a failed drone attack this summer at an airport. Polish officials said a fire at a drone factory was most likely caused by sabotage. And Denmark said Russia is recruiting people there to attack defense companies.

So, for the moment, nobody's been killed or seriously injured in these attacks, but the more frequently these things occur, the more that becomes a possibility. My colleague Michael Schwarz covers global security issues. He says the attacks pose a real challenge for NATO specifically because the alliance was formed to deter or respond to a major land invasion and not this kind of incremental sabotage.

Russian President Vladimir Putin has been very careful to pursue a campaign that seems to fall well below the threshold of a war, well short of Article 5, which is this central tenant of NATO, that an attack on one is attack against all. And this has left NATO struggling to respond. They've kicked out diplomats. They've imposed sanctions, but they've realized that this is really doing little to blunt this Russian campaign. And so they are starting to experiment with some new ideas. Recently in August, in fact, the German government authorized its spy services for the first time since World War II to carry out what are called active measures. So this is sabotage, cyber attacks, things like that directed at Russia. But experts and officials are telling me that even these kinds of more aggressive measures are not going to be enough. And so for the time being, they expect these attacks simply are going to continue.

特朗普行政动向：地缘定调、选民审查与争议性工程

在白宫内政与防务动态方面，现任副总统JD·万斯（JD Vance）在数周以来白宫简报室的首场官员发布会上，试图淡化当前与伊朗的军事冲突。尽管美伊双方在过去一周互相发动空袭并打破了长达近一个月的停火态势，万斯依然拒绝将当前对抗定义为“全面战争”，并表示在缺乏持续地面交火的情况下无法给出冲突结束的具体时间表，将局势缓和的责任归于伊方。与此同时，白宫新闻秘书卡罗琳·莱维特（Karoline Leavitt）近期离任，官方尚未任命继任人选。

在内政管理与行政权力运作上，美国国土安全部（Department of Homeland Security: DHS）已抽调数百名联邦特工，脱离其打击人口贩卖和反恐的核心本职，转而排查全国范围内的非公民选民登记情况。这一由唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump）强力推动的行政审查引发了巨大争议：历史数据显示非公民投票极其罕见（保守派智库统计过去四分之一世纪仅记录约 100 起），但官方依然坚持该行动以维护选举安全为由展开，招致了批评人士关于“政治钓鱼与选民恐吓”的严厉指责。此外，特朗普力推的一座高 250 英尺、顶部镀金的首都凯旋门（Triumphal Arch）工程已进入破土动工阶段。尽管该项目尚未获得联邦专门委员会及《国家历史保护法案》的法定审查批准，且面临越战退伍军人团体关于其破坏阿灵顿国家公墓（Arlington National Cemetery）历史景观的司法诉讼，白宫仍采取加速推进策略，试图在司法审查落地前形成既定事实，这一模式此前同样体现在拆除白宫东翼改建大型宴会厅的争议之中。

Now, three updates on the Trump administration. First, at the White House yesterday, Vice President JD Vance tried to downplay the war in Iran, saying he doesn't currently consider it a war at all. His comments came as the US and Iran have both launched air strikes over the past week. Attacks that shattered a nearly month-long pause in active fighting. When asked when will this end, the Vice President said he did not know the answer, and did not have a timeline for how long the war will stretch on, suggesting that was up to Iran. Vance spoke from the White House briefing room, the first administration official to do that in weeks. White House press secretary Caroline Levitt recently stepped down from the role and the White House has not announced anyone to replace her.

Also, the Times has learned that hundreds of federal agents from the Department of Homeland Security have been reassigned to scour the country's voter roles for people who aren't citizens. The administration has launched the intensive operation despite the fact that there's no evidence of widespread voter fraud. A database from a conservative think tank lists only about 100 instances of non-citizens voting over the past quarter century. But President Trump has fixated on the issue, claiming without evidence that tens of thousands of non-citizens are registered to vote. Now the administration has pulled DHS agents away from their normal duties like investigating trafficking and terrorism. It's not clear what they will find in the voter roles or what would be done with that information. One former immigration official called the effort a political fishing expedition meant to intimidate people from exercising their right to vote. In a statement, DHS said that ensuring the integrity and security of American elections is one of its top priorities.

And President Trump is about to break ground on construction of the 250-foot tall gold-topped arch he's been talking about for months. It's moving forward even before a federal commission has approved the project and before it's been reviewed under the National Historic Preservation Act. Both of those are legally required. The project is also facing a challenge in the courts. A group of Vietnam veterans has sued to stop the plan construction, arguing that the massive arch would disrupt historic views from Arlington National Cemetery and that it should have been approved by Congress. It's just the latest major construction project Trump has rushed to get underway, including his demolition of the White House East Wing to build a new ballroom. Critics say they see a pattern of Trump trying to basically outrun any scrutiny by the courts by building as quickly as possible.

政治资本运作与极端灾害救援：中期选举金援与尼泊尔矿难

随着美国中期选举临近，全球首富埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）重新开启其庞大的政治资金库，计划斥资高达 1.2 亿美元协助共和党赢得选战。最新的竞选财务报告显示，马斯克旗下的超级政治行动委员会（Super PAC: 独立开支政治委员会，可无限额募集并投放政治广告）已在得克萨斯州、密歇根州和爱荷华州等全美十余个关键选区展开首轮资金投放，在 Facebook 与 Google 等平台集中投放聚焦选民登记并对民主党进行意识形态攻击的数字广告。在 2024 年大选期间，马斯克曾以近 3 亿美元的开支成为单笔最大捐助者，而此次重新大规模注资，也平息了此前因其与特朗普公开分歧及自建政党言论而引发的政治参与度疑虑。

在南亚地区，尼泊尔（Nepal）发生了一起由毁灭性山洪引发的重大矿山地质灾害。这场灾难已造成超过 1,300 人丧生，另有数千人失踪，其中包括大量参与该地区水利枢纽工程建设的工人。今晨，救援人员成功在一座水电设施的引水隧道中救出被困逾一周的 2 名幸存者。受灾地下设施曾被厚重的泥沙与碎石彻底填埋，救援队伍日夜不休地钻孔掘进并向地下泵送新鲜空气。据现场指挥部门透露，目前隧道深处仍有多达数十名工人被困，救援人员已在作业断面探测并听到了部分受困人员的声音，搜救行动仍在全面展开。

In Nepal this morning, officials said that two people were rescued from a tunnel at a hydro power facility after being trapped there more than a week by a sudden devastating flood. The flood killed more than 1,300 people and thousands more are still missing, including many who had been working on the multiple energy projects in the region. Rescuers have spent days working around the clock to try and bore into tunnels and dig through a dense mix of sludge and mud trying to access the underground facilities. 中美 AI 发展路径分流：全面场景渗透与前沿模型竞争

在人工智能产业的发展逻辑与社会落地形态上，中国与美国呈现出截然不同的演进范式。行走在北京街头，公众会被无处不在的 AI 元素所包围：商场中自主巡检的清洁机器人、宣称具备行人违章过马路人脸识别与罚款追踪功能的街头摄像头、指导垃圾分类的智能回收箱，乃至尝试递送咖啡的具身人形机器人。尽管技术的大规模应用同样引发了关于隐私侵犯、就业替代等敏感议题的讨论，但中国各级政府与科技企业依然坚定推进全场景普及战略。

与美国科技公司将主要资源与资本倾斜于研发最前沿的基础大模型（Cutting-edge Frontier Models）不同，中国的产业策略侧重于以最快速度将 AI 技术下沉并嵌入实体生活网络，重点覆盖课堂教学、工厂制造与医院诊疗等垂直场景，力求让公众直观感知其实用价值。这种路径差异也映射在社会心理层面：民调数据显示，仅有约三分之一的美国公众认为人工智能的收益大于风险；而在中国，对 AI 持积极乐观态度的受访者比例高达三分之二。公众对技术普惠的高度期待，成为驱动其全场景落地的关键社会土壤。

And finally, if you were to walk down the street in Beijing right now, you would just be bombarded by AI. There are cleaning robots just roaming around malls. There is a camera that claims to use facial recognition to tell if you were jaywalking and identify who you were so that potentially you could be fined. There are trash cans that claim to be AI powered to help you sort garbage or humanoid robots that try to sell you coffee but really have trouble grabbing onto anything. There's just advertising for AI absolutely everywhere.

Vivian Wong covers China for the Times and she's part of a team that's been looking at how the country is going all-in on artificial intelligence. The technology has sparked growing concerns about privacy, unemployment, and other hot button issues, but Vivian says China is pushing ahead. This proliferation of AI really speaks to a few different things in China. So while in the US AI companies are really focused on building the most cutting edge models, Chinese companies and the Chinese government have really emphasized the importance of just putting AI everywhere as quickly as possible. They want it to be shaping people's lives so that they're seeing it in classrooms, in factories, in hospitals. They really want people to feel that AI is useful to their lives practically.

The other thing it shows is that people in China are just generally much more optimistic about AI than people in the US. One survey recently showed that about 1/3 of Americans think that the benefits of AI outweigh the risks. But in China, that's actually about 2/3 of people. So there's just a lot more sense that AI is going to be exciting or at least bring something good to people's lives.

每周新闻测验：林肯号靠港休整、Shein 上市受挫与公告牌纪录

在节目尾声的每周新闻知识竞答环节中，回顾了本周发生的数起重大国际事件与文化新闻：

  1. 林肯号航空母舰休整地：美国海军林肯号航空母舰（USS Abraham Lincoln）在结束长达 280 余天超期中东部署的回国途中，停靠泰国（Thailand）芭堤雅（Pattaya）港口进行休整。军方明确对官兵颁布了禁止帆伞运动、禁止摩托艇滑水以及严禁吸食当地已合法化的大麻等纪律禁令。

Now, time for the quiz. First up: the United States aircraft carrier, the USS Abraham Lincoln, made its way into port this week. The crew of the USS Lincoln got their first time off in more than 280 days after their deployment to the Middle East dragged on way longer than expected. Now on their way back to the US, they're getting some overdue time off in a beach town known for its nightlife and freewheeling atmosphere. Where has the ship docked? The answer: Thailand. The USS Lincoln is docked near the beach town of Pattaya, which has been an R&R spot for US soldiers going back decades to the Vietnam War. The ship is set to leave on Sunday when it'll head back toward its home port of San Diego.

Next question: This week, a major fashion giant went public in a closely watched stock market debut that turned out to be a flop. Shares falling by about 2% at the open and then those losses extending to as much as 10%. That would have been almost unthinkable just a few years ago when the company was valued at $100 billion. What company is it? The answer: The fast fashion brand Shein, which revolutionized the clothing industry. At one point, the company was creating over 4,500 new styles a day, in part by using algorithms to predict what would be a hit with shoppers. But when the White House cracked down on trade loopholes that had let Shein ship mountains of cheap clothes from China, it was forced to raise prices. Revenues dropped and investors say the company has not yet laid out a clear plan for how it will bounce back.

And last question: This week, the Billboard Hot 100 announced that Choosen Texas, Ella Langley's twangy tale of a Texas-Tennessee love triangle broke the record for the most weeks at the top of the chart. Choosen Texas has been in the number one spot for a whopping 20 weeks, edging out two other country-infused mega hits which had been tied for the record: Lil Nas X's Old Town Road and A Bar Song (Tipsy) by Shaboozey. The asterisk: they held the record for non-holiday hit.
