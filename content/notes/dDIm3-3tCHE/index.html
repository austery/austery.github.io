俄罗斯《新报》被迫停业

本周早些时候，我人在莫斯科市中心俄罗斯最大胆的报纸《新报》（Novaya Gazeta）的办公室里。他们正在给纸箱打包封口、缠塑料薄膜，向这个作为他们数十年家园的办公室道别。

英文原文 >> Earlier this week, I was in downtown Moscow at the offices of Novaya Gazeta, Russia's most daring newspaper. They were doing the final packing of boxes, plastic wrapping, and saying farewell to an office that had been their home for decades.

瓦莱丽·霍普金斯（Valerie Hopkins）是《纽约时报》莫斯科分社的记者。她一直在跟踪报道这家传奇俄罗斯报纸《新报》的最后岁月。

数十年来，该报调查腐败、揭露政治阴谋，并顶着政府日益严苛的审查制度努力报道乌克兰战争。有时这意味着即便在出版时，报纸头版上的“战争”一词也不得不被模糊处理。

然而在克里姆林宫对全国控制愈加收紧的情况下，该报如今被迫关闭。

英文原文 >> Valerie Hopkins is a reporter in the Times Moscow Bureau. She's been covering the last days of the legendary Russian newspaper, Novaya Gazeta.

For decades, the paper investigated corruption, shined a light on political machinations, and worked to cover the war in Ukraine despite increasingly strict government censorship. Sometimes that meant even publishing front pages with the word war blurred out.

But now the paper has been forced to close amid the Kremlin's ever tightening grip over the country.

在其成立30多年的历程中，《新报》经历了六名同事被谋杀。他们的印刷机被扣押，媒体执照被吊销。联合创始人德米特里·穆拉托夫（Dmitry Muratov）曾因捍卫新闻自由荣获2021年诺贝尔和平奖，后来也遭到了残酷的酸液袭击。这是一家见证并经历了一切磨难的报纸。《新报》曾代表着俄罗斯自由与独立言论最后的堡垒之一。随着它的消失，对俄罗斯社会是一个巨大的损失，对任何真正想要尝试理解俄罗斯内部正在发生什么的人来说，也是一个巨大的损失。

英文原文 >> Over the more than 30 years of its existence, Novaya Gazeta faced the murder of six of its colleagues. Their printing presses were seized. Their media license was revoked. The co-founder Dmitry Muratov, who won the Nobel Prize in 2021 for defending press freedom, he was violently attacked with acid. This is a newspaper that has seen and lived through so much. And the Novaya Gazeta represented one of the last bastions of free and independent speech in Russia. 该报联合创始人称这是“蓄意摧毁”。
