因此，试图通过移民系统去人为硬性充实那些正在持续流失人口的偏远社区，是极难奏效的。那些地方之所以流失人口是有其深层经济原因的。你无法通过移民政策强行逆转这一趋势，因为宪法赋予了每个人迁徙的自由，你无法强迫任何人永远留在特定地区。

Original English

Tony Keller: So one of the challenges with that argument is, in Canada, we have labour mobility. So you can say to someone, hey, we're going to help you or give you a special benefit if you immigrate to a certain small town or a certain area, but you can move. People have been leaving those areas precisely because there are economic opportunities elsewhere. They are drawn to those economic opportunities. That's a good thing. So it is challenging to use the immigration system specifically to try to bulk up local communities in areas that are losing people. They're losing people for a reason.
