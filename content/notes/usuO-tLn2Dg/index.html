联合国的意义：从多边主义到全球治理的困境 TVO Today 2026-08-27

领域: geopolitics

分类: geopolitics

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-08-27

摘要:

本文深入探讨了联合国的核心职能、运作机制及其面临的批评。从每年九月的大会到遍布全球的专门机构，联合国既是多边外交的论坛，也是提供人道援助的关键平台。然而，庞大的官僚体系、安理会否决权机制以及大国博弈，使其在解决冲突和达成共识方面屡屡受挫。文章通过分析联合国的成就与局限，反思了其在当代国际关系中的不可替代性。

多边主义的舞台：联合国大会的象征与现实

每年九月，193个国家的领导人和外交官齐聚纽约，参加联合国大会的开幕。这是世界上最大的论坛，供人们建立关系、谈判协议和解决问题。这正是多边主义（Multilateralism: 通过多国合作和平解决共同问题的理念）的目标。然而，这样的世界领导人聚会并非没有批评——有人认为联合国不仅没有解决它应该解决的问题，反而常常制造新的问题。

那么，这些批评准确吗？联合国的作用是什么？如果它消失了会发生什么？要回答"联合国做什么"这个问题，几乎可以说"有什么是它不做的"。纽约总部大楼是一个外交论坛，是国家及其代表面对面交谈的场所。它通过其各机构提供对话得以进行的程序。但联合国不仅仅是世界领导人会面和讨论目标的场所，它还有许多在全球范围内提供关键资源的机构。

Original English

You may have seen this room before. Every September, the leaders and diplomats of 193 countries gather in New York for the opening of the United Nations General Assembly. It's the biggest forum in the world for people to come together to build relationships, negotiate deals, and solve problems. That's the goal of multilateralism, to peacefully solve common issues through the cooperation of many countries, but a gathering of world leaders like this is not without criticism. Not only is the UN not solving the problems it should, too often it's actually creating new problems for us to solve.

But are those criticisms accurate? What purpose does the UN serve? And what would happen if it went away? Let's unravel the role of the United Nations. So, we've seen the general assembly meet, but what does the UN actually do? It's little bit difficult to answer the question "What does the United Nations do?" One might almost say, "What do they not do?" But what happens in the building in New York? It's a forum for diplomacy, it's a forum for states and state representatives to talk to one another in person. It provides, through its bodies, processes through which these conversations can happen. But it's not just a place for world leaders to meet and discuss goals. The UN has many bodies that provide vital resources globally.

生命线机构：从难民署到世界粮食计划署

联合国向世界各地的人们提供大量维持生命的援助，包括食物、住所和药品。许多专门机构分布在全球各地，例如联合国难民署（UNHCR: 负责保护难民和解决难民问题的机构）、人权理事会、联合国儿童基金会（UNICEF: 联合国儿童基金，致力于儿童权利和福祉）以及世界粮食计划署。这些组织的规模和范围揭示了联合国有多么庞大，其工作从一开始就雄心勃勃。

联合国的核心宗旨体现在《联合国宪章》的开篇之词——"欲免后世再遭今代人类两度身历惨不堪言之战祸"。联合国成立于1945年，背景是两次世界大战和数千万平民的死亡。这解释了为什么宪章的开篇条款中明确绝对禁止使用武力。经过80多年的国家建设和资源共享，联合国需要大量资金和人员来推动各项工作，这本身也带来了一系列问题。

Original English

It also provides a lot of life-giving aid to people around the world. This includes food, shelter, medicines. There are a number of specialized agencies that are kind of scattered throughout the world. Things like the High Commissioner for Refugees, the UNHCR, the Human Rights Council, UNICEF, which is the UN Children's Fund, the World Food Program. Those are all organizations whose size and scope reveal just how big the United Nations is and the work that it does has been ambitious since the beginning.

One big purpose is the opening words of the United Nations Charter, which is "to save succeeding generations from the scourge of war." The UN was founded in 1945, against the backdrop of two World Wars and the deaths of tens of millions of civilians. That explains why, in the opening provisions of the charter also, there is an absolute prohibition against the use of force. With those more than 80 years of nation-building and resource sharing, there's a need for both a lot of funding to make things happen and the people to do the work, which brings its own set of issues.

官僚体系与安理会否决权：联合国的结构性困境

对联合国的批评有很多是合理的。它是一个庞大的官僚机构，在庞大的官僚体系中，资金很容易被滥用。最常见的批评之一是联合国浪费金钱——它花费大量资金维持全球存在、资助维和行动和援助项目，这些都非常昂贵。官僚体系本身就是一支庞大的公务员队伍，为所有这些雇员提供资金也一直受到批评。

此外，联合国安理会（UN Security Council: 负责维护国际和平与安全的主要机构）存在一个持续的问题——五个常任理事国可以对提交给它们的任何问题行使否决权。这种本意是确保各国参与安理会的安全机制，反而成为其最大的拐杖之一，也是联合国无法达成共识、真正提出解决方案的原因之一。联合国秘书长本人也认同这些担忧——正是成员国的分裂，使联合国无法正常运作。

Original English

There are a lot of valid criticisms of the UN. It is a huge bureaucracy, and in a huge bureaucracy it is very easy to mis-spend funds. And one of the most common criticisms of the UN is that it wastes money, and it uses a lot of money to maintain presence all over the world, to fund peacekeeping operations, assistance programs that are very expensive. That bureaucracy also is itself its own massive civil service, and funding all of those employees has also been the subject of criticism.

On top of that, there's the ongoing issue of the UN Security Council... where there are five permanent members of the council who can veto any issue brought before them. And so, that fail-safe, which was a way to ensure participation in the Security Council, is also one of its biggest crutches. It's also one of the reasons why the UN is unable to reach consensus and actually come up with real solutions to solve problems. The UN Secretary himself echoes those concerns. It's the member states that, divided, do not allow the UN to work properly.

大国的转向：美国退出国际协议的影响

五个常任理事国之一是美国，其在特朗普政府领导下，国际关系方针转向了新方向。美国退出了多项国际协议，不仅包括《巴黎协定》，还包括联合国气候变化框架公约本身。这意味着美国发出信号——它甚至不再有兴趣坐在谈判桌前。这意义重大，因为美国在谈判桌上的席位就像驾驶座，拥有油门、刹车和一切，对该机制如何向前推进产生了重大影响。

面对这些批评和这个国际机构中最大参与者之一的转变，你可能会问：联合国在实现自身目标方面有多成功？这是一个复杂的问题。联合国的目标之一是防止第三次世界大战的爆发，在这一点上联合国是成功的——自联合国成立以来，我们没有发生过第三次世界大战。但在缓和冲突方面，它有效吗？当然没有，战争不断爆发，并且仍在继续。联合国还努力在冲突升级时充当国家之间的调解人，但往往无法阻止任何一个国家走向战争。

Original English

One of those permanent members is the United States, whose approach to international relations has headed in a new direction under the Trump Administration. The US withdrew from a number of international agreements. They include not just the Paris Agreement, but also the underlying UN framework convention on climate change. That means that the United States signals that it is no longer interested in even being at the negotiating table, which is significant because the seat at the table in the case of the United States has been kind of a driver's seat with gas pedal, brakes, everything that has really had significant influence on how that regime moved forward.

With this criticism and this change in one of the biggest players in this international body, you might ask how successful has the UN been in meeting its own goals? It's a complex question as to whether the UN is effective. One of the goals of the UN was to prevent the outbreak of a third World War, and on that score the UN has been successful. We have not had a third World War since the founding of the United Nations. Now, has it been effective in de-escalating conflict? Of course not, and wars have broken out and continue to break out. The UN also strives to be a mediator between states in the escalation of a conflict, and oftentimes it is unable to pull back a single state from going to war.

不可想象的世界：联合国的不可替代性

联合国并非自古就有。维护国际和平的努力长期以来一直是国际外交的目标。如果我们生活在一个没有联合国的世界里，我们会找到其他方式来协调多边主义和合作。但很难想象一个没有联合国的世界——它与许多维持国际关系正常运转所必需的事物如此紧密地交织在一起，以至于很难设想我们会直接切断这一切。

Original English

The UN didn't exist forever. The effort to maintain international peace, that has been a goal of international diplomacy for a long time. If we lived in a world in which there was no UN, we would find other ways to coordinate multilateralism and to work together. It's difficult for me to imagine a world without the United Nations. It is so deeply intertwined with so many things that are necessary for functioning international relations that it seems difficult to contemplate that we would just pull the plug on all of that.
