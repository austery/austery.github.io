温德尔的LGBTQ+历史谁在拯救？

中文翻译：温德尔（Windsor）是加拿大最知名的城市，也是加拿大的自豪首都。它位于德州、密歇根和人们经常与它的历史典故联系在一起的地方。它正在蓬勃发展，跨越了文化界并充满活力。但除了那些描绘了早期移民、拓荒者以及塑造LGBTQ+人群生活的宏大叙事之外，加拿大这里还有一些人致力于将这些故事从被遗忘中拯救出来。

英文原文 Windsor, Ontario, is best known as Canada's autonomous capital and its southern most city. It's located just across from Detroit, Michigan, and people here often associate it with its historic distilleries. It's rum running lore, it's cross-border culture and its vibrant waterfront. But beyond those images lies a rich queer history filled with firsts, trailblazers, and stories that shaped the lives of LGBTQ+ people. Here in Canada.
