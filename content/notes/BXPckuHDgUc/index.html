摘要:

本期访谈深入探讨了伊朗在当前冲突中的核心战略——通过打击地区能源基础设施来恢复“威慑力”。专家 Greg Brew 分析了霍尔木兹海峡封锁对全球油价的长期冲击、伊朗国内政权的韧性，以及美国在面对地理限制和不对称战争时的战略误判。内容涵盖了从哈尔克岛的战略地位到特朗普政府面临的经济与外交双重挑战。

伊朗国内政局：政权韧性与民间鸿沟

Joe Weisenthal: 西方评论家常有一种愿景：伊朗社会大部分是渴望西方自由生活的自由主义者，只是被一小撮宗教强硬派压迫。只要除掉那些教士，大家就能过上西式生活。这种看法离现实有多远？

Original English

Joe Weisenthal: But I have this sense that Western commentators... have this vision of Iranian society as one in which there is a very broad mass of people that are like deep down liberals who are dying to cast off their hijabs... and that there is this very small group of religious hardliners oppressing them... Greg Brew: 伊朗人民与政权之间确实存在巨大鸿沟，大多数伊朗人不赞成严厉的社会政策或对地区代理人的巨额投入。但政权仍拥有 10% 到 20% 的硬核支持者，这足以维持统治。更重要的是，长期的镇压意味着国内没有有组织的政治反对派。**伊斯兰革命卫队（IRGC）**等机构的存在完全是为了保卫政权，而且他们手里有枪。就像其他威权政府一样，虽然大部分人不满意，但改变的障碍极高。

Original English

Greg Brew: There is a huge gap between the Iranian people and the Iranian regime... The regime can probably count on the support of a hard core of maybe ten to 15 to 20% of the population. Okay. That's probably enough for them to stay in power. The other aspect of this... What that means is that there's really no organized political opposition inside Iran... The other aspect of it is there are whole institutions that are built into the fabric of the Islamic Republic, the IRGC... who exist solely to preserve the regime from internal and external threats. They also happen to have all of the guns.

哈尔克岛：伊朗的能源命门与“灰色市场”

Joe Weisenthal: 给我讲讲哈尔克岛（Kharg Island），我一周前还没听过这个地方。

Original English

Joe Weisenthal: Greg, tell us about Kharg Island. I'd never heard of it before a week ago.

Greg Brew: 哈尔克岛是伊朗主要的石油出口码头，处理伊朗 80% 到 90% 的原油出口。它能停靠巨型油轮（VLCC），且靠近主要油田。美国有一种理论认为，只要占领或摧毁哈尔克岛，就能切断伊朗的财政命脉，迫使其屈服。但我认为这个理论是错的。伊朗还有其他出口终端，且由于长期受制裁，他们已经建立了成熟的灰色市场，通过铁路运往俄罗斯或中国，或者通过伊拉克走私石油。

Original English

Greg Brew: So Kharg Island is a very small island... It handles around 80 to 90% of Iran's crude exports... It's designed to handle VLCCs, the biggest oil tankers... there is a belief, there is a theory that if you see Kharg Island... then you have sufficient leverage over Iran that you can get them to do what you want... The problem I have with that theory is that I don't think it's right. Iran has other means of exporting oil... Iran still has a limited capability of exporting oil by rail... oil gets smuggled out, particularly through Iraq.

Tracy Alloway: 这种压力是否反而会让灰色市场变得更强大？

Original English

Tracy Alloway: would you expect that grey market system to start to break or would you expect to see it sort of like, I guess maybe formalized to an extent or grow even stronger in the face of U.S. pressure like on Carr Island?

Greg Brew: 没错。当你试图通过军事力量封锁出口时，只会激励伊朗转向替代路线。同时，这会在市场层面产生巨大的涨价压力。高油价反而进一步激励了走私活动，因为利润变得更丰厚了。

Original English

Greg Brew: I think the upshot of trying to cut off the flow of oil... only incentivizes the Iranians to turn to these alternative routes. At the same time, what you're doing on a market level is you're putting more upward pressure on prices... which only further incentivize incentivizes grey and black market activity because now the profits are even better.

结语：地理的局限与长期的阴影

Joe Weisenthal: 最后一个问题。这场战争最让你惊讶的是什么？

Joe Weisenthal: Is there anything about the situation that has surprised you as it's been playing out?

Original English

Greg Brew: how little the Iranians have needed to do to Hormuz to keep it shut... The threat was enough to keep it closed, which I thought was surprising. The second surprising point... my understanding was that this was a scenario the U.S. Navy had prepared for... And the U.S. response has been insufficient. It's not only that they haven't done enough to open the strait, it's that they didn't seem to have expected this at all.
