国际刑事法院深陷正当性危机：美国严厉制裁与国际司法困境 TVO Today TVO Today 2026-09-25

来源: TVO Today

发布时间: 2026-09-25

摘要:

本期节目深入探讨了国际刑事法院（ICC）当前面临的严峻正当性危机。从内外交困的丑闻与退约潮，到特朗普政府发起定点制裁以维护国家主权，详尽梳理了ICC的历史沿革、管辖权限及缺乏强制执行力等制度困境。

正当性危机与各方围攻

解说: 国际刑事法院（ICC）在一连串争议之后，正面临着严重的正当性危机。多国相继宣布退出该法院；其首席检察官已被停职；同时，它还正遭受来自特朗普政府的正面攻击。

Original English

Narrator: The International Criminal Court is facing a legitimacy crisis following a slew of controversies. Countries are withdrawing from the court. Its chief prosecutor has been suspended and it's facing an attack from the Trump administration.

美方代表: 本届政府绝不会坐视国际刑事法院及其盟友企图威胁我们的人民。如果他们以为可以剥夺我们的国家主权，我们将让他们彻底领教美国决心的全部力量。

Original English

U.S. Official: This administration will not sit by as the ICC and its allies seek to threaten our people. If they believe they can deprive us of our sovereignty, we will teach them the full meaning of American resolve.

历史渊源与管辖权范围

解说: 那么，这个法院究竟为何而存在？它又为何会遭遇对其合法性的种种质疑？

国际刑事法院依据一项名为《罗马规约》的条约于 2002 年正式成立。截至 2026 年，大约有 120 个国家成为该法院的成员国，其中包括加拿大。建立该法院的构想源自第二次世界大战的暴行，并在 20 世纪 90 年代发生波斯尼亚种族灭绝和卢旺达种族灭绝之后得以彻底确立。在那些大屠杀惨案发生后，联合国设立了特设法庭，以起诉在这些地区犯下滔天罪行的人，并为受害者伸张正义。

尽管这些特设法庭促成了一些定罪判决，但批评人士指出，它们办案程序既缓慢又耗资巨大。因此，各国认为需要建立一个集权化的常设法院，专门向被指控犯有最严重国际罪行的人追责，而不是根据具体情况设立临时法庭。于是，《罗马规约》创立了国际刑事法院，并赋予其起诉被指控犯有四项罪行之一或其组合罪行个人的权力：灭绝种族罪、危害人类罪、战争罪，以及自 2010 年起纳入的侵略罪。

Original English

Narrator: So why does this court exist at all? and why is it facing questions about its legitimacy?

The ICC was established in 2002 through a treaty called the Rome Statute. As of 2026, around 120 countries are members of the court, including Canada. The idea for the court emerged out of the atrocities of World War II and solidified after the Bosnian and Rwanda genocides in the 1990s. In the aftermath of those genocides, the UN had set up tribunals to prosecute people responsible for crimes committed in those regions and to bring justice to the victims.

While the tribunals resulted in some convictions, critics said they were both slow and expensive. So, countries decided a centralized court was needed to hold people accused of the worst international crimes responsible instead of creating new tribunals on a casebycase basis. And so the Rome Statute created the ICC and gave it the power to prosecute people accused of any one or a combination of four crimes. Genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes, and as of 2010, the crime of aggression.

机构独立性与运作机制

解说: 这里正是人们经常产生混淆的地方。国际刑事法院是一个独立机构，它并不属于联合国，尽管它经常与联合国开展合作。它也完全独立于国际法院（ICJ），后者是联合国的正式组成机构，专门解决国与国之间的争端。

再次明确，国际刑事法院的职责是审判个人，无论他们是政治领袖、国家元首、军人，乃至普通平民。但它将工作重点放在所谓“对所起诉罪行负有最大责任的人”身上。然而，国际刑事法院并非对所有地方的所有人都享有必然的管辖权。它被设计为一个最后诉求法院，只有在相关当事国的国内法院无法或不愿亲自起诉罪犯时才会介入，并且它仅在特定条件下才拥有管辖权。

这些条件包括：如果你是成员国的公民或居民；如果你在成员国的领土内实施了犯罪行为；或者联合国安理会正式将案件移交至国际刑事法院。

Original English

Narrator: Here's where people often get things muddled. The ICC is an independent body that's not part of the United Nations, though it does regularly cooperate with it. It's also completely separate from the International Court of Justice or the ICJ which is a part of the UN and settles disputes between countries.

So again, the ICC's job is to try individuals, whether they're political leaders, heads of state, soldiers, or even civilians. But it focuses its efforts on quote those most responsible for the crimes it prosecutes. But the ICC doesn't have guaranteed authority everywhere over everyone. It's meant to be a court of last resort where national courts cannot or will not prosecute criminals themselves and it only has jurisdiction in certain conditions.

Those conditions are if you are a citizen or resident of a member country, if you commit a crime in a territory of a member country, or if the UN Security Council refers a case to the ICC.

缺乏执行力与内部丑闻

解说: 作为该法院成员的国家，根据《罗马规约》有义务配合其调查和起诉工作。这包括执行国际刑事法院所签发的逮捕令。近期，菲律宾前总统罗德里戈·杜特尔特在马尼拉因涉嫌在国内犯下危害人类罪，依国际刑事法院的逮捕令被捕，审判日期定于 2026 年 11 月。

国际刑事法院别无选择，只能在抓捕等事项上依赖成员国，因为它自身并没有任何强制执行机制。它没有警察部队；尽管许多国家都是该法院的成员，但包括俄罗斯、中国和美国在内的几个大国并不是其成员。

这就把我们带回到了争议的核心。在内部，法院深陷一场调查风波，导致其首席检察官卡里姆·汗因涉嫌性行为不端而被免职，不过他本人对此予以坚决否认。

Original English

Narrator: Countries that are members of the court are obligated by the Rome Statute to cooperate with its investigations and prosecutions. This includes carrying out arrest warrants that the ICC issues. Recently, former president of the Philippines, Rodrigo Duterte, was arrested in Manila on an ICC warrant for crimes against humanity committed in the country with a trial date of November 2026.

The ICC has no choice but to rely on member countries for things like arrests because it doesn't have an enforcement mechanism of its own. There's no police force and while many countries are members of the court, several, including Russia, China, and the US are not.

Which brings us back to the controversies. Internally, the court was embroiled in an investigation that led to the removal of its chief prosecutor, Karim Khan, over allegations of sexual misconduct, which he denies.

外部批评与地缘博弈

解说: 在外部，长期以来一直存在针对该法院的批评声音，特别是认为它不成比例地专门针对全球南方国家，尤其是非洲。截至 2026 年，已有许多国家以此种针对失衡为由，宣布将退出该法院。

最近，该法院通过对诸如俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京以及以色列总理本雅明·内塔尼亚胡等极具权势的政治领袖签发逮捕令，释放出其关注视野正超越全球南方的信号。但是，这两名领导人均未被捕。实际上，对内塔尼亚胡签发的逮捕令，直接促使特朗普政府对国际刑事法院及其部分工作人员实施了制裁。特朗普政府随后发起了一场彻底瓦解国际刑事法院的运动，声称该机构严重威胁到了美国的主权。

Original English

Narrator: Externally, there are longstanding criticisms against the court, namely that it disproportionately targets the global south, specifically Africa. As of 2026, a number of countries said that they would withdraw from the court, citing this disproportionality.

Recently, the court signaled it was looking beyond the global south with the warrants for the arrests of powerful political leaders such as Russia's Vladimir Putin and Israel's Benjamin Netanyahu. But neither of these men have been arrested. In fact, the warrant for Netanyahu's arrest prompted the Trump administration to issue sanctions against the court and some of its staff. The Trump administration that launched a campaign to completely dismantle the ICC, claiming that it threatens American sovereignty.

美方代表: 此时此刻，国际刑事法院及其盟友正在对我们的国家发动一场战争——不是用子弹或导弹，而是用法规、公约以及所谓的国际法强制力。

Original English

U.S. Official: As we speak, the ICC and its friends are waging a war against our country, not with bullets or missiles, but with statutes and compacts and the force of so-called international law.

制裁升级与盟国立场

解说: 美国对该法院的制裁措施包括将数名国际刑事法院法官列入特别指定国民名单（SDN名单），该名单通常是专门用于列入恐怖分子和毒贩的。在这些被制裁的法官中，包括加拿大籍法官金伯利·普罗斯特，她曾参与调查美军在国际刑事法院成员国阿富汗境内的涉嫌犯罪行为。面对美国的攻击，加拿大重申了对该法院及其各项工作的坚定支持。

Original English

Narrator: The American sanctions of the court have included putting several ICC judges on the specially designated nationals list, a list typically reserved for terrorists and narcotics traffickers. Among those judges is Canadian Kimberly Prost who worked on an investigation into actions of American troops in Afghanistan, a member country of the ICC. In the face of the American attack, Canada has reaffirmed its support of the court and its work.

加拿大官方代表: 加拿大是国际刑事法院自豪的成员国。我们对国际刑事法院的支持是毫不动摇、坚定不移的。

Original English

Canadian Representative: Canada is a proud member of the International Criminal Court. Our support for the ICC is unequivocal.
