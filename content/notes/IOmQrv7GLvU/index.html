摘要:

沙滩男孩乐队 Brian Wilson 的女儿 Carnie Wilson 坦诚分享她如何在摇滚巨星家庭的创伤阴影下成长，通过食物缓解焦虑与面对校园欺凌，以及 Wilson Phillips 组合的辉煌与重组。她详述了胃旁路手术后的迷失、酗酒危机与戒酒心路，最终通过烹饪、音乐和重塑家庭晚餐实现自我救赎与历史的改写。

摇滚家庭的童年阴影

卡尼: 嗯，我是在加利福尼亚州的贝莱尔（Bel Air）长大的。我的父亲是沙滩男孩乐队（The Beach Boys）的布莱恩·威尔逊（Brian Wilson）。我们家里经常发生的只有一件事，那就是音乐。要么是音响里放着音乐，要么是钢琴在弹奏。我的确记得和爸爸一起坐在钢琴旁，他教我弹琴，我弹给他听。但这并不是一个典型的家庭。我是说，发生过一些很疯狂的事情。所有人整天都会来我们家。都是些歌手，主要是音乐人。**艾尔顿·约翰（Elton John）**会过来弹钢琴。**保罗·麦卡特尼（Paul McCartney）带着他的孩子们过来，还有肖恩·卡西迪（Shaun Cassidy）**也来过，我们还拍了照片，照片里我的双脸通红。看，他就在那里。但我们当时永远不知道谁会突然出现在家里。这就是摇滚乐式的生活方式。我知道自己接触到了很多极其美好的事物，但也有很多时候我感到非常没有安全感，目睹了一些很糟糕的事情，这些创伤留下了疤痕。我没有很多我认为本可以帮我获得安全感的东西，比如大家坐在餐桌旁吃晚饭、聊聊我们的一天之类的。我当时没有这些。

Original English

Carnie: Well, I grew up in Bel Air, California. My father is Brian Wilson from The Beach Boys. What was always happening was just music. Whether it something was on the stereo or piano was playing. I mean, I have memories of sitting at the piano with my dad, him teaching me and playing for him. But it wasn't a typical household. I mean, there were some crazy things that happened. Everybody would come over the house all the time. It was singers, mostly musicians. Elton John would come over, play the piano. Paul McCartney came over with his kids and Sean Cassidy came over and we got a picture of it and my cheeks are bright red. There he is. But we didn't know who was gonna show up. It's like that rock'n'roll lifestyle. I know that I was exposed to so many great things, but there were days and times when I felt really unsafe and saw some pretty bad things, traumatic and have left scars. And I did not have a lot of things that I think would have helped me feel more safe, like dinners at the table, discussing our days, and stuff like that. I didn't have that.

食物、欺凌与和声救赎

伊丽莎白: 在你小的时候，有什么事或物能让你感到安全吗？

Original English

Elizabeth: Was there anything that did make you feel safe when you were young?

卡尼: 小时候的安全感绝对来自于甜甜圈、蛋糕和食物。我以前是那种扮演保护者角色的人。那是我的天性，你知道吗？我妹妹非常安静，非常害羞。所以我总觉得自己想要力量，你懂吗？我想让自己感觉强大。但我就是无法控制自己对食物的需求。我做不到。我必须得到我的食物，这绝对与寻求安全感有关。但在我自己的皮囊里我感觉很不正常，因为我总是超重。所以我被非常严重地嘲笑和欺负。甚至到了每天在校车上都会情绪崩溃、歇斯底里大哭的地步。那简直是折磨。那就是折磨。你知道的，我感受到了那种悲伤和羞耻，觉得自己不够好，对吧？但尽管对这些有很多消极的感受，我还是非常热爱表演。我会想，我有这件事要做。我在赞美艺术。我在歌唱，我在表演。我和观众之间建立了这种联系。这不仅是因为我是布莱恩·威尔逊的女儿。更多的是，我共享着对和声的热爱。我的生活需要它。我当时痴迷于把朋友们聚在一起唱和声。我们开始一周唱大概三天。**琴娜（Chynna）**会到我家来，我们就一起唱和声。所以这里有卡尼、温迪、琴娜，三个独立的声音。但当我们把声音融为一体时，它创造出了这第四个声音。♪ 宝贝，你必须放开我 ♪

Original English

Carnie: Safety as a little girl was definitely like donuts, cake, food. I was just the sort of the protective one. That's my nature, you know? My sister was very quiet, very shy. So I always felt like I wanted to feel powerful, you know? I wanted to feel strong. I just couldn't get a hold of my need to eat. I couldn't do it. I had to have my food, and it was definitely about safety. But I didn't feel normal in my own skin because I was always overweight. So I was really, really teased and bullied. Like to the point of just breaking down, hysterically crying every day on the bus. And it was torture. It was torture. You know, I felt that sadness and shame and like, I'm not good enough, right? But despite all the feelings about this, I had such a love of performing. I'd be like, I have this to do. I'm celebrating the arts. I'm singing, I'm performing. I have this relationship with the audience. And it wasn't just like being the daughter of Brian Wilson. It was more like, I share the love of harmony. And I needed that in my life. And I was obsessed with getting my friends together, harmonizing. We started singing like three days a week. Chynna would just come over and we would just harmonize. So here's Carnie, Wendy, Chynna, three individual voices. But when we put our voices together, it created this fourth voice. ♪ Baby you've just got to release me ♪

体重骤减与酗酒危机

主持人: 这是她自蜕变以来首次在公开活动中亮相。请欢迎，卡尼·威尔逊。

Original English

Host: This is her first appearance at the public event since the transformation. Please welcome, Carnie Wilson.

卡尼 (历史录音): 谢谢！呼！

Original English

Carnie (Archived): Thank you! Whew!

卡尼: 当我减掉大量体重时，我做了胃旁路手术，天哪，我在16个月内减掉了160磅。我的身体变化得太快了，以至于我在镜子里看到自己时甚至都认不出自己。我感到很不舒服。我想我只是感到很脆弱。我至今仍不太理解。你知道，我想我还不习惯待在一个小一号的身体外壳里。虽然我有食物，但我不想把瘦身成果搞砸。所以我开始喝酒。是的。我无法应对那种状态。我无法应对自己内心的感受，你懂吗？我只想把它屏蔽掉。当你来自某些创伤或者来自那个摇滚世界时，你无法再忍受沉溺于“自我、自我、自我”的纠结感受中。关于我的酒精依赖症，我所学到的是，当我喝酒、药物成瘾、这样对待我的身体和心灵时，我是在疏远自己。而这与我与他人建立连接是背道而驰的。我加入了一个**“十二步戒酒计划”（12-step program）**。它帮助了我。虽然它是匿名的，但如果不付出那些努力，我根本不可能保持清醒。感谢上帝。

Original English

Carnie: When I lost a bunch of weight, I had a gastric bypass surgery and I lost, oh my god, I lost 160 pounds in 16 months. I was changing so rapidly that I didn't recognize myself when I looked in the mirror. And I did not feel comfortable. I guess I just felt vulnerable. I still really don't understand it. You know, I think I wasn't used to being in a small shell of a body and I didn't have, I mean, I had food, but I didn't wanna mess it up. So I started drinking. Yeah. I could not handle it. I couldn't handle how I felt inside, you know? I just wanted to shut it out. When you come from some trauma or you come from, you know, that world, I can't sit in the me, me, me, me, me feelings anymore. And what I've learned about my alcoholism is that when I'm drinking, when I'm using, when I'm doing that to my body and my mind, I'm alienating myself. And that's the opposite of me connecting with others. I'm in a 12-step program. That's what helps me. And it is anonymous, but there's no way I could keep my sobriety without that work. Thank god.

伊丽莎白: 那么除了匿名戒酒会，现在是什么在帮助你面对那种令人恐惧的感觉？因为你不再通过做过去的那些事情来麻痹和慰藉自己了。现在是什么在支撑着你？

Original English

Elizabeth: So besides AA, what helps you attend to that terrifying feeling now? 'Cause you don't do all the things that you used to do to numb and comfort. So what helps you now?

卡尼: 是的。我是说，我发现了对烘焙和烹饪的热爱。我喜欢烘焙和做饭，这对我帮助很大。但同时，我相信音乐能够治愈心灵。我坚信音乐的治愈力量。而和声，对我来说，能带来平静。那是唱给人们听，然后看到他们脸上的情感波动时的那种体验。我将这些情感吸入内心，这就是治愈。我也在和女儿合唱时体会到了这一点。她会在浴缸里，我们一起唱歌。实际上，当她还是个婴儿的时候，我就在教她唱和声了。我们的家庭纽带，你知道，这对我、**罗伯（Rob）**和孩子们来说是一件大事，我们一起吃晚饭、聊天、共同度过难关，无论是起起落落还是各种挑战，你懂吗？我对此心存感激。我也很感激我父亲能以他所能及的方式陪伴我的孩子们。当然，这种互动永远觉得不够。

Original English

Carnie: Yeah. I mean, I found the love of baking, cooking. I love to bake and cook, and that helps me a lot. But also, I think music heals. I just believe music heals. And harmony, for me, brings serenity. It's that experience of like, singing to them and then seeing emotion in their faces. I take that in, and it's healing. I've been experiencing it with singing with my daughter. She'd be in the bathtub and we'd be singing. And I actually was teaching her harmony when she was just a little baby. Our family unit, you know, that's a big thing with Rob and I and the kids, having dinner together, talking, going through it, the ups and the downs, the challenges, you know? I'm so grateful for that. And I'm also grateful that my dad got to experience my kids in the way that he was able to. It wasn't, you know, like, this interaction, it would never be enough.

伊丽莎白: 好的。

Original English

Elizabeth: Okay.

卡尼: 这永远不够。但我非常高兴我们之间建立了连接，我能够为他唱歌，基本上一直唱到他去世的那天。当我年轻的时候，我人生的主旋律大概是“我只想感觉好起来，想在自己的身体里感到安全和舒服”。而现在，我的人生是“我的生活里已经拥有了能让我感到满足的一切。我不需要再继续寻觅了”。

Original English

Carnie: It'll never be enough. But I'm so happy that we got to connect and that I got to sing to him until basically the day he died. When I was young, I think more of the narrative was like, I just wanna feel okay and like, safe and good and inside my body. And now it is that I already have everything I need in my life to feel fulfilled. I don't need to keep searching.

重构历史与被爱的权利

伊丽莎白: 你知道吗，这真的很有意思。当你还年轻的时候，你极度渴望这种家庭晚餐，然后你亲手给了你现在的家庭这种晚餐。跟我聊聊这个吧。

Original English

Elizabeth: You know, it's really interesting. When you were young, you craved these family dinners, and then you gave your family the family dinners. Tell me about that.

卡尼: 这很奇妙，因为我必须承认，有好几次我坐在餐桌旁。我知道说出来可能会让人觉得奇怪，但有什么关系呢？我们今天在这里敞开心扉。我会坐在餐桌旁环顾四周，看着露西（Lucy），看着洛拉（Lola），看着罗伯，我会在心里想：“天啊，我们是一个家庭，我们一起坐在这个餐桌旁，我们是一家人，我们彼此相爱，我们就在这里。” 这简直是世界上最美丽的事情。我体内有一部分其实还不太理解这种状态，无法与它产生共鸣，但情况正在改变。这在改变历史。这在改变我自己。一切都在改变。这让那些感受变得真实而美好，不再令人害怕，不再是负面的。我努力去适应并吸收这些爱，并告诉自己，他们值得我们所有人团聚在一起，我也配得上拥有这份爱。而且我认为我们永远都会拥有这样的羁绊。我总是会想到我的“核心四人组”——那就是我、我妹妹、我妈妈和我爸爸，以及我和罗伯、洛拉和露西。

Original English

Carnie: It's weird because I have to admit, there have been a few times when I've been sitting at the dinner table. I know this is gonna sound weird to say, but why not? We're letting it out here today. I would sit at the table and I'd look around and I'd look at Lucy, look at Lola, look at Rob, and I would think to myself, "Oh my god, we're a family and we're here around this dinner table, and we're a family and we love each other and we're here." And it's like, it's the most beautiful thing. And like, there's a part of me that doesn't really understand that, that can't connect to that, but it's like, changing. It's changing history. It's changing myself. It's changing. It's allowing those feelings to be real and beautiful, and not scary, not negative. I try to relate to it and take it in and be like, they deserve for all of us to be together. I deserve to feel this love. And I just think that we'll always have like that, I'll always think of the core four, which is me, my sister, my mom and my dad, and me and Rob, and Lola and Lucy.
