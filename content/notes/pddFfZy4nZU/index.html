国防工业的万亿级机遇：自主化、商业化与美国工业基地的重建 a16z 2026-05-19

摘要:

在 a16z 美国动力峰会上，Seronics 创始人 Dino 与五角大楼官员 Honorable Duffy 探讨了国防工业的转型。核心议题包括通过软件自主化降低造船成本、借鉴 IKEA 模式重建劳动力队伍，以及“商业优先”战略如何通过规模效应增强国防供应链的韧性。

国防工业的代际机遇

Dino: 这里存在一个真正的代际机会，去建设这个国家未来 100 年所需要的东西。我们需要更多的创始人，需要更多的建设者，也需要政府内部有更多的人在推动变革。

Original English

Dino: There's a real generational opportunity to build what this country needs for the next 100 years and we need more founders. We need more builders and we need more folks in government pushing for change.

主持人: 在传统的国防工业基地 (DIB) 内部，我们经常会遇到非常令人沮丧的脆弱性，因为我们可能只有一个并不怎么盈利的唯一供应商，而这个供应商是专门为国防工业“量身定制”的。突然之间，我们就给自己制造了一系列的漏洞。这个房间里坐满了来自五角大楼各处的项目执行官 (PEO) 和项目经理 (PM)。当我们展望 2026 年共同建设并加快建设速度时，你想向这个群体传达的一个关键信息是什么？

Original English

Host: It's really frustrating how much fragility we encounter within the traditional defense industrial base because we have a sole supplier that's not really that profitable that was bespoke for the defense industry and all of a sudden we've really created our own set of vulnerabilities here. This room is filled with PEOs and PMs from across the Pentagon. What's one key message you'd like to send this group as we look to build together and build faster in 2026?

自主化技术对造船业的颠覆

主持人: 你是如何将 Seronics 的自主化技术与海军“不抛弃任何一个伙伴”的信念转化为合同的？自主化技术的突破又是如何让你为海军生产出更多、更快的产品的？

Original English

Host: How have you translated this alignment between Seronics's autonomy and the Navy's no man left behind ethos into contracts? and and how does how do autonomy breakthroughs actually allow you to build more faster products for the Navy?

Dino: 这个问题我会再回过头来详细说。我坚信，自主化开启的是以前从未存在过的能力。当然，战争在某些环节仍然需要人类的参与，但我亲眼见过，如果你有能力派机器人去，你就永远不应该派人去。你就是不应该这么做。所以，让我们利用机器人技术，在可以的地方保护人类生命。

自主化真正的开启钥匙是速度和规模。如果我坐在这里告诉部长，我们将建造过去 50 年一直在建的那种船，或者建造和中国一样的船，只是做得更好、更快、更便宜，那他应该礼貌地请我离开。你必须弯曲经济成本曲线。你必须造船更便宜，造船更快速。

你唯一能拨动的两个杠杆是材料成本和劳动力成本。我们买钢材的价格不可能比中国便宜，所以你必须对船只进行首要原则 (First Principles) 的重新设计，从根本上减少钢材的使用。你怎么做到的？你通过软件自主化和数字化来进行建造。

劳动力工时也是一样。我们获得的每小时人工费率不可能比中国低。所以，让我们把造船速度提得更快。例如，我们的第一艘 Marauder 船大约消耗了 5 万个工时。为了让大家对数量级有个概念，我先声明这有点像苹果和橘子的类比，但一艘驱逐舰需要 700 万到 900 万个工时。

这就是自主化所开启的规模和速度。因为你可以重新设计整个平台，使其建造起来简单得多。然后你就拥有了所需的规模化能力，从而让尽可能多的人远离战斗场景。

Original English

Dino: So, that's one I'll come back to. What autonomy enables is, and I'm a I'm a huge believer in this, we're unlocking capability that didn't exist before. Like, warfare will require humans involvement at some point, but I've seen it. And you should never send a human if you have the ability to send a robot. you just shouldn't do it. So, let's leverage robotics and protect human life where we can. The real unlock to autonomy is speed and scale. Right? If I was sitting up here telling the secretary how we're going to build the same ships that we've been building for the last 50 years or the same ships that they're building in China, we're just going to do it better, faster, and cheaper. I mean, he should just politely ask me to leave. You have to bend the economic cost curve. You have to build ships cheaper. You have to build ships faster. The only two levers you have to pull are material costs and labor costs. We're not going to buy steel cheaper than they're buying it in China. So, you have to take a first principles design to the ship and just fundamentally use less steel. How do you do that? You build for software autonomy and digitization. Same thing on labor hours. We're not going to acquire a per hour labor rate cheaper than they're paying in China. So, let's build ships much faster. For example, Marauders using is about a 50,000 labor hour on our first ship. Just for order of magnitude, put it in frame of reference. Apples and oranges comparison here. I'm going to acknowledge that up front, but a destroyer is 7 to 9 million labor hours. So that's the scale and speed that autonomy unlocks because you can redesign the whole platform and make it much simpler to build and then you build the then you have the capability at the scale that you need to keep as many people as possible out of combat scenarios.

Original English

Dino: I mean, the workforce is critical, right? The secretary and I were talking about the workforce earlier. We have to rebuild the workforce to support the maritime industrial base, right? You can't create people with 15 years of experience overnight. So it goes back to the first principles approach design of the ship. Design the ship so you actually don't need 15 years of welding experience to build it in the first place. Then you can actually rebuild and retrain the workforce. So, it's rebuilding and recreating the workforce and changing the culture, what it actually means to work at a shipyard again, making it cool, building for the future, giving people a mission, and giving people job security behind that.

拥有商业市场绝对关键。因为归根结底，我们的目标是在和平时期为国防部提供战时生产能力。如果我们不能以经济可行的方式进入商业市场，那么我们就会一直盯着政府，等待下一个合同工具和下一张支票来支撑公司的寿命。也许是 5 年、10 年、20 年、30 年，但最终我们可能会回到起点。这就是为什么商业市场如此重要。你会看到《海事行动计划》的一个重点就是美国的商业航运业。
