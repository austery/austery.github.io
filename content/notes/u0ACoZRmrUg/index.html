摘要:

盈透证券（Interactive Brokers）创始人 Thomas Peterffy 在访谈中探讨了预测市场从散户投机转向机构化工具的必然趋势。他分享了 IBKR 推出 Forecast Trader 的战略意图，并针对内幕交易提出了极具争议的观点：主张取消限制以加速信息流动。此外，他还回顾了 70 年代手工计算期权定价的往事，并将 AI 视为一种新型的高级自然语言编程工具。

内幕交易与信息透明度

Thomas Peterffy: 在我职业生涯的早期，我曾因为内幕交易损失了一半的初始资本。所以我对它能造成的破坏非常熟悉。但另一方面，尽管如此，我依然赞成不设立任何针对内幕交易的规则。我希望所有的信息一旦产生，就立即公开。

Original English

Thomas Peterffy: Early on when I started my career, I left. I lost half my initial capital based on inside the three. So I'm, I'm extremely familiar with with the damage it can cause. But on the other hand, and in spite of that, I'm in favor of not having any, rules against insider trading. I would like all the information out there as soon as it's available.

Thomas Peterffy: 因为，听着，作为一个社会，我们越早知道任何可感知的信息，处境就越好。为什么我们要无休止地等待？首先，当你面临并购情况时——这也是大部分内幕交易发生的地方——秘书、律师，每个人都知道。他们每天回家，消息总会过滤出去。所以这几乎是无法避免的，监管起来非常困难且繁琐。为什么我们不干脆废除这些限制，让信息尽快释放出来呢？

Original English

Thomas Peterffy: Because, look, as a society, we're better off knowing as soon as possible anything that is knowable. Right. So why why do we have to wait forever? First of all, when you when you are faced with a merger or acquisition situation where there most of the insider trading is happening, right? They, they, you know, this the secretary's the lawyers, everybody knows about it. They go home day to day... So eventually always filters out. So it's almost impossible to avoid. It's very, very difficult and cumbersome. Why don't we just do away with it and let the information come out as soon as possible?

预测市场的潜力与流动性

Joe Weisenthal: 你认为最终机构投资者会使用预测市场工具来交易诸如“2026 年是否会出现衰退”之类的问题吗？

Thomas Peterffy: 绝对如此。我对此坚信不疑。股市让我们投资于不同公司的未来，但这些公司的表现很大程度上取决于未来的经济和社会环境。预测市场为专家们提供了一个机会，让他们不惧于将资金投入其中，表达观点并收集共识意见。这可能比我们个人能想出的主意更好。

Original English

Joe Weisenthal: Do you think that eventually institutional investors will use prediction market like instruments to trade things like will there be a recession in the year 2026?

Thomas Peterffy: Definitely. I'm I'm absolutely convinced that, so the stock market gives us, a venue to, to invest in the future, basically in the future, of different companies. But, you know, some of the how those companies are actually going to end up trading in the future has a lot to do with the economic environment and social environment in the future... So the prediction markets gives them an opportunity to to gather experts around who are not afraid to put their money on the line and, and, express what they think and to collect a consensus opinion...

Tracy Alloway: 目前预测市场的一个大问题是流动性极低。当你在推广你的 Forecast Trader 平台时，阻碍机构投资者全力以赴的大障碍是什么？

Thomas Peterffy: 这和任何市场刚开始时都非常相似，流动性始终是个大问题。如果你回顾已经存在了 54 年的期权市场，最初流动性也非常小，当时一次交易超过 20 或 30 张合约都很困难。现在你可以交易成千上万甚至上百万张。期权是一个比预测合约复杂得多的概念。这就是为什么我们试图想出比期权更简单的东西。理解预测合约不会像理解期权合约那样耗时，所以它不需要像期权那样花五十年时间来发展流动性。

Original English

Tracy Alloway: A lot of the prediction markets, one of the problems that they currently have is that volumes are still pretty low, right? The markets are very illiquid... what are the big hurdles for them?

Thomas Peterffy: Well, at this point, just it's very similar to any other market when when it starts, liquidity is always a big question... If you look at, for example, the options markets that have been around for 54 years, I think initially there was very little liquidity... And since the stock market is basically similar... it is not going to be as complex for people to understand a prediction contract as it is to understand an options contract. So, I think that they do not take equally long time to, to, develop sufficient liquidity.

Thomas Peterffy: 别忘了我们是非常不同的公司。盈透证券拥有大量成功的、管理着大规模投资组合的机构投资者。对我们来说，预测市场是一个补充。我们的客户有能力评估衰退等问题。而 Kalshi 除了预测市场的投资者外，基本上没有其他投资者。对他们来说，很难仅靠预测市场维持生计直到它真正起飞，所以他们必须增加体育博彩等内容作为支柱。我们更愿意专注于与客户投资相关的严肃问题，不想用体育博彩来分散他们的注意力。

预测市场的监管与未来

Joe Weisenthal: 以前在盈透上交易微软，你是通过建立好的交易所进行的。但在 Forecast Trader 中，你既是经纪商又是交易场所。这种角色的融合在历史上并不常见，这是否让你们处于一种全新的地位？

Thomas Peterffy: 你说得对。虽然我们过去曾推出过一些指数和期货合约，但预测市场是一个全新的领域，以前从未存在过类似的东西。

Original English

Joe Weisenthal: Whereas with Forecast Trader, you are the market, you're the broker and the venue at the same time... Is this new for you?

Thomas Peterffy: ...Yeah. It's it's you are correct. It's the first market that where nothing have has existed like it actually.

Joe Weisenthal: 为什么我们没有更早拥有预测市场？我们十年前就可以押注全球变暖了。

Thomas Peterffy: 我们其实在十年前就开始开发预测市场了。当时我们正在申请银行牌照，顾问告诉我们：“如果你推出这个预测市场，你永远拿不到银行牌照，SEC 不会善待你的。”所以我们最初只推出了一个使用虚拟货币（phantom money）的系统。结果因为虚拟货币没有吸引力，这个项目逐渐平息了。后来 Kalshi 拿到了 CFTC 的牌照。我当时很生气，但对他们来说很容易，因为他们一无所有，没有什么可失去的。我们最终在去年，也就是 2024 年大选前也拿到了牌照。

AI：一种更高级的自然语言

Tracy Alloway: 作为一个程序员，你怎么看现在的 AI？这只是另一次技术改进，还是某种本质上全新的东西？

Thomas Peterffy: 它是技术发展的巨大飞跃。但我认为它本质上是一种新的、更高级别的编程语言，比之前的任何东西都强大得多。就像我们从机器语言发展到汇编，再到 Fortran、COBOL 和 C 语言一样。AI 是一种自然语言级别的编程，而且它可以利用世界上存在的所有数据。

Original English

Tracy Alloway: When you look at AI and compare it to the early days of electronic trading, what similar and what what feels different to you now... or is this something fundamentally new in your mind?

Thomas Peterffy: Well, it's a it's a huge leap in technological development. But but it's basically as a computer programmer, I look at it basically as as a new, higher level of language that is much, much, much, much more powerful than anything that came before... this is a natural language and, and so it that that AI is basically a higher level language, which is a natural language. And it also has the it available, all the data that exists in the world.

Joe Weisenthal: 你提到 AI 具有概率性，而不是确定性的。这对它在金融领域的应用有什么影响？

Thomas Peterffy: 期权价格是概率性的。我开发预测市场的初衷之一就是教人们概率学，因为人们倾向于不以概率的方式思考。他们倾向于认为要么发生，要么不发生，或者认为某人知道结果。但事实并非如此，任何事情的发生都有一个概率，没有人真正知道会发生什么。

Original English

Joe Weisenthal: In fact, frequently you won't, even with the exact same query... does the non deterministic element of AI... have implications for its use in finance?

Thomas Peterffy: Well option prices are probabilistic. Prediction markets are not probabilistic. That's what that's what my initial impetus was for for prediction markets to teach people probabilities. Because people don't don't tend to not think of probability probability probabilistically. They tend to think either this will happen or will not happen... There is a a there is a probability for something to happen. And nobody knows what really will happen. Right.
