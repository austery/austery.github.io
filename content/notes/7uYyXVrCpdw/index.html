油价为何居高不下：从加油站微观困境到全球原油博弈 New York Times Podcasts New York Times Podcasts 2026-09-17

领域: finance-wealth

分类: investment-assets

主讲人: New York Times Podcasts

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-09-17

摘要:

本期《The Daily》通过佛罗里达州独立加油站店主Cam Judy的亲身经历与《纽约时报》能源记者Clifford Krauss的专业解读，深度剖析了俄乌冲突及中东局势下美国汽油价格暴涨背后的多重因素：加油站薄弱的利润空间、炼油产能瓶颈、石油巨头的资本约束以及全球能源供应链的结构性困境。

佛罗里达加油站的隔夜涨价风波

Rachel Abrams: 这里是《纽约时报》的 Rachel Abrams，欢迎收听《The Daily》。大约在战事爆发一个月后，当时油价还在每加仑 3.99 美元左右，《The Daily》制作人 Anna Foley 前往佛罗里达州，与一位加油站老板探讨油价对该加油站以及顾客产生的影响。

Original English

Rachel Abrams: From the New York Times, I'm Rachel Abrams and this is the Daily. About a month into the war, back when gas was at $3.99 a gallon, daily producer Anna Foley headed to Florida to talk to a gas station owner about how prices at the pump were feeling both for him and his customers.

Cam Judy: 我真心希望这能在下周结束，油价能回到每加仑 2.79 美元。如果能那样就太棒了。我无比期望这是暂时的，不会持续太久。但是，我确实很担心高油价会长期持续。

Original English

Cam Judy: I would love for this to be over next week and I go back to 2.79 a gallon. That'd be awesome. I hope beyond hope that it is temporary and it does not last long. But yeah, I do worry about the longevity of high prices.

Rachel Abrams: 那是在今年三月份。但就在最近，有些出乎意料的是，那位名叫 Cam Judy 的加油站老板再次联系了我们。

Original English

Rachel Abrams: That was back in March. But recently, and somewhat unexpectedly, that gas station owner, Cam Judy, reached out to us again.

Cam Judy: 喂，你好。

Original English

Cam Judy: Hello.

Anna Foley: 嘿，Cam。

Original English

Anna Foley: Hey, Cam.

Cam Judy: 嘿，Anna。你最近怎么样？

Original English

Cam Judy: Hey, Anna. How you doing?

Anna Foley: 他带来了一个最新情况。几天前你给我发了短信，因为你看到油价发生了一些不可思议的事情。我很想知道究竟发生了什么。跟我讲讲这件事吧。

Original English

Anna Foley: He had an update. So, a few days ago, you texted me because of this kind of wild thing you were seeing happen at your pumps. So, I just want to know what happened. Tell me the story.

Cam Judy: 好的。那天我查了油价，我记得应该是周三。我一直工作到下午三点左右，随时关注油价动向，当时一切看起来还挺正常的。

Original English

Cam Judy: Yeah. So, I checked the gas prices. I believe it was Wednesday. I was working until around 3:00 in the afternoon just checking the prices and see what was going on and everything seemed normal.

Anna Foley: 你是在哪里查的？

Original English

Anna Foley: Where were you checking them?

Cam Judy: 我是在 GasBuddy 上查的，看看周围各个加油站的定价是多少。当时我的普通无铅汽油价格依然维持在每加仑 3.99 美元。

Original English

Cam Judy: I was checking them on GasBuddy, the different gas stations, see what everybody was priced at. I was sitting at $3.99 a gallon for regular gas.

Cam Judy: 第二天一早我开门营业，正跟我的啤酒供货商聊天。他问我：“Cam，你现在油价多少？”我说：“我这儿还是 3.99 美元。”他惊讶地说：“哇，我今早一路开过来路过的其他所有加油站，全都在今天早晨涨到了 4.19 美元。”我当时觉得：“绝不可能，昨天明明都还是 3.99 美元。”

Original English

Cam Judy: And the next day I opened the gas station and I was talking to my beer distributor and he was like, "What's your gas at, Cam?" And I said, "I'm still at 3.99." And he's like, "Wow, all the other gas stations I passed by on the way in here all went to 4.19 this morning." And I was like, "There's no way. There's no way. It was 3.99 yesterday."

Cam Judy: 哇，一夜之间每加仑暴涨 20 美分，这太疯狂、太令人震惊了。我一开始不相信他，直到我清了清嗓子，再次拿出手机查了周围所有的加油站。果不其然，每一家都涨到了 4.19 美元，无一例外。

Original English

Cam Judy: Wow. That's a 20 cent jump in price overnight. That's pretty crazy. It's pretty shocking. And I didn't believe him until I again checked the gas stations in and around me. And sure enough, every single one of them was at 4.19.

加油站前的拥堵长队与断供危机

Cam Judy: 紧接着，我的加油站开始排起了长队。人们像疯了一样涌过来加油，因为相比周围其他地方，我这里的油价简直是白菜价。我一下子忙得不可开交。那天下班时我心想：“天哪，明天得涨价了，因为进货成本肯定也大幅上涨了。”

Original English

Cam Judy: And then I started to get lines at the gas station. People were just going crazy trying to get gas, because it was a steal compared to what everybody else was. And so, I was super busy. I left that day and I was like, "Man, I got to go up tomorrow, because my cost must have gone way up."

Cam Judy: 到了周五早上，我查了一下进货成本，果不其然涨了。所以我把零售价调高到了 4.15 美元，故意比周围所有竞争对手便宜 4 美分，心想这应该能保持竞争力。

Original English

Cam Judy: And then Friday morning, I looked at my cost. Sure enough, it went up. And so, I went up to 4.15, 4 cents below all my competitors around me, and I was like, "All right. That'll be good."

Anna Foley: 嗯。

Original English

Anna Foley: Mm-hmm.

Cam Judy: 结果那天上午大概 11 点左右，我又顺便看了一眼 GasBuddy，差点吓掉下巴。周围所有加油站又全线涨到了 4.39 美元。不到 24 小时内又涨了 20 美分！那一刻我整个人都懵了，我做加油站这行已经八九年了，从来没见过油价以这种速度暴涨。

Original English

Cam Judy: And then around 11:00 that day, I just happened to look at GasBuddy again, and all the gas stations around me had gone to 4.39. Another 20-cent jump in under 24 hours. And at that point, I had my head in my hands. I've been doing this for eight or nine years, and I've never seen prices jump like that.

Anna Foley: 所以你的油价又成了全镇最便宜的。

Original English

Anna Foley: So you were the cheapest again.

Cam Judy: 没错，我又成了最便宜的。而且这一次，排队的车龙直接从油泵一路甩到了马路上。由于附近一处立交桥正在施工，加上这些排队等加油的车辆，整条街的交通彻底瘫痪了。

Original English

Cam Judy: Yeah. And this time the line went from the pumps out into the street. And with the overpass construction they're doing down the street and all the people waiting to get gas, the whole street was blocked.

Anna Foley: 哇。

Original English

Anna Foley: Wow.

Cam Judy: 顾客们开始在大街上狂按喇叭，场面完全失控。更有甚者，有些人直接把车斜横在我的停车场里堵死通道，司机们互相破口大骂，只为了抢一个油枪位置。

Original English

Cam Judy: People were honking. And then on top of that, people were parking sideways in my parking lot, blocking people in. People were cussing at each other.

Anna Foley: 他们当时是什么情绪状态？

Original English

Anna Foley: What was their mood like?

Cam Judy: 他们非常愤怒、极度焦虑，并且完全被恐慌情绪笼罩。

Original English

Cam Judy: Angry, anxious, frantic.

Anna Foley: 这么多人抢着加油，最后油够用吗？

Original English

Anna Foley: And with that many people, did you run out of gas?

Cam Judy: 是的，到了下午 1 点左右，我们的普通无铅汽油彻底见底了。由于我的油价比别人便宜太多，原本打算去别处加油的人全挤到了我这里，两个地下大油罐直接被抽空。

Original English

Cam Judy: Yeah, around 1:00 PM we ran out of regular unleaded. Because I was priced so much cheaper, everybody who would normally go elsewhere came to me, and we just emptied both main underground tanks.

Anna Foley: 卖光了所有汽油，而且销量如此惊人，作为加油站老板，你那两天是不是赚翻了？

Original English

Anna Foley: Since you sold out completely and had such massive volume, as an owner, didn't you make a ton of money?

Cam Judy: 完全相反。大众普遍有一种误解，以为油价上涨或者销量大增时加油站就能大发横财。事实上，零售汽油的利润极其微薄。当我们以 3.99 美元甚至 4.15 美元卖出时，进货批发价早已飙升，扣除信用卡刷卡手续费（通常在 2.5% 到 3% 左右）以及运营电费后，我们在每加仑汽油上实际只赚几美分，甚至在倒贴亏钱。更糟糕的是，当我们用光库存后，必须以更高昂的批发价重新补货进油。

Original English

Cam Judy: Completely the opposite. There's a common misconception that when gas prices go up or volume surges, the station makes a fortune. In reality, the margin on retail gasoline is razor-thin. When we sold at 3.99 or 4.15, the replacement wholesale price had already skyrocketed. After factoring in credit card transaction fees—which are around 2.5% to 3%—and electricity costs, we make only a few pennies per gallon or even lose money. Worse yet, once empty, we have to replenish the tanks at much higher wholesale prices.

能源专家的深度解析：全球石油困局

Rachel Abrams: 为了搞清楚为什么加油站会出现如此剧烈的价格波动，以及为什么全美乃至全球的油价都在持续攀升，我连线了《纽约时报》资深能源记者 Clifford Krauss。

Original English

Rachel Abrams: To understand why gas stations are experiencing such wild price swings and why fuel prices continue to surge across the nation and the world, I spoke with Clifford Krauss, a senior energy reporter for The New York Times.

Rachel Abrams: Cliff，感谢你能来。

Original English

Rachel Abrams: Cliff, thanks for joining us.

Clifford Krauss: 很高兴能与你们交流。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: It's great to be with you.

Rachel Abrams: Cliff，我们刚才听了 Cam 的遭遇。油价在一夜之间跳涨 20 美分，紧接着第二天又涨 20 美分。普通消费者和加油站老板都被打得措手不及。这到底是怎么回事？为什么油价会涨得如此凶猛？

Original English

Rachel Abrams: Cliff, we just heard what Cam went through. Gas prices jumped 20 cents overnight and then another 20 cents the next day. Regular consumers and station owners are caught completely off guard. What is going on here? Why are prices soaring so aggressively?

Clifford Krauss: 要理解这个问题，我们需要将视线从加油站终端拉回到全球原油与炼油供应链。当前的油价危机并非由单一因素造成，而是多重结构性危机叠加的完美风暴。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: To understand this, we have to look past the local pump to the global crude and refining supply chain. The current price crisis isn't driven by a single factor, but rather a perfect storm of multiple structural crises converging at once.

Clifford Krauss: 首先是原油供应端的剧烈震荡。俄乌冲突爆发后，西方国家对俄罗斯石油实施了严厉的制裁与禁运。俄罗斯原本是全球第二大原油出口国，每天供应数百万桶原油。当这部分原油退出或受阻于西方主流市场时，全球现货市场立刻陷入了恐慌性的供应缺口。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: First is the violent shock on the crude supply side. Following the outbreak of the war in Ukraine, Western nations imposed severe sanctions and embargoes on Russian oil. Russia was the world's second-largest crude exporter, supplying millions of barrels daily. When that supply was disrupted or shunned by Western markets, the global spot market immediately faced a panic-driven deficit.

Rachel Abrams: 那么其他产油国不能立刻增产来弥补这个缺口吗？比如 OPEC（石油输出国组织）或者美国本土的页岩油生产商？

Original English

Rachel Abrams: Can't other oil producers just pump more to fill the gap? Like OPEC or American shale producers?

Clifford Krauss: 理论上可以，但现实中存在巨大的阻碍。首先看 OPEC+，以沙特阿拉伯和阿联酋为核心的产油国集团并不愿意轻易打破既定的增产配额，部分原因是他们享受着高油价带来的丰厚财政收入，另一部分原因是多数成员国自身的剩余产能已经消耗殆尽，根本无力大幅增产。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: In theory, yes, but in reality there are massive obstacles. First, looking at OPEC+, the producer group led by Saudi Arabia and the UAE is reluctant to abandon its disciplined production targets. Part of it is enjoying the windfall revenue from high prices, but more importantly, most member nations are already pumping at near-peak capacity and lack the spare capacity to ramp up significantly.

页岩油企业的资本约束与炼油瓶颈

Rachel Abrams: 那美国本土的石油公司呢？比如 ExxonMobil 或 Chevron，他们为什么不全力开采？

Original English

Rachel Abrams: What about American oil companies like ExxonMobil or Chevron? Why aren't they drilling full throttle?

Clifford Krauss: 这涉及到了华尔街投资逻辑的根本转变。在过去十年的页岩油繁荣期，美国石油勘探公司大举借债扩张产能，导致市场供应过剩，油价暴跌，股东们蒙受了巨额亏损。如今，华尔街投资者对石油巨头下达了铁律：实行资本约束（Capital Discipline）。投资者不再奖励盲目扩大产出的钻探行为，而是要求企业将巨额利润优先用于股票回购与发放股息。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: That comes down to a fundamental shift in Wall Street's investment mindset. During the previous decade's shale boom, U.S. exploration companies borrowed heavily to expand production, which eventually caused market gluts, crashing prices, and massive shareholder losses. Today, Wall Street investors enforce strict capital discipline. Investors no longer reward drilling for production growth; they demand companies return bumper profits via share buybacks and dividends.

Clifford Krauss: 此外，还有一个公众经常忽略的关键环节——炼油产能（Refining Capacity）。人们往往以为把原油从地下抽出来就能直接加进油箱，但原油必须经过炼油厂的复杂裂解催化才能变成汽油和柴油。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: Furthermore, there is another critical bottleneck that the public often overlooks—refining capacity. People tend to think extracting crude from the ground means you immediately have fuel, but crude must be processed through complex refining facilities to become gasoline and diesel.

Rachel Abrams: 炼油环节目前面临着什么问题？

Original English

Rachel Abrams: What challenges are refineries facing right now?

Clifford Krauss: 在过去几年特别是疫情期间，由于全球出行需求骤降，美国及全球关闭了大量老旧、低效的炼油厂。全美失去了每天超过 100 万桶的炼油产能。而且在绿色能源转型的宏观背景下，没有任何能源公司愿意投资数十亿美元去建设一座生命周期长达数十年的新传统炼油厂。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: Over the past few years, especially during the pandemic when global mobility plummeted, numerous aging and inefficient refineries across the U.S. and globally were permanently shut down. The U.S. lost over a million barrels per day of refining capacity. Moreover, amid the long-term green energy transition, no energy company wants to sink billions of dollars into building brand-new conventional refineries that take decades to pay off.

Clifford Krauss: 结果就是，现存的炼油厂几乎在以 95% 以上的极限负荷运转，但依然无法满足疫情后报复性反弹的出行需求。哪怕某一座炼油厂因为例行维护或火灾事故出现短暂停工，都会瞬间引发区域性甚至全国性的成品油价格暴涨。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: Consequently, surviving refineries are operating at near 95% utilization rates, yet they still struggle to satisfy the post-pandemic rebound in travel demand. Any unplanned outage, routine maintenance, or fire at a major refinery immediately triggers violent regional or nationwide spikes in retail fuel prices.

未来展望与通胀传导

Rachel Abrams: 这么说来，短期内油价回落的希望非常渺茫？

Original English

Rachel Abrams: So the prospect of gas prices dropping back down in the short term looks very slim?

Clifford Krauss: 确实不容乐观。只要地缘政治冲突持续、炼油瓶颈未解、供需基本面维持紧平衡，高油价就会成为一种新常态。而高油价不仅影响私家车主，柴油价格的飙升更会直接推高全社会的物流运输成本，进一步加剧全方位的通胀压力。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: It is indeed sobering. As long as geopolitical conflicts persist, refining bottlenecks remain unresolved, and supply-demand fundamentals stay tight, elevated fuel prices will remain the new normal. High gas prices don't just affect passenger cars; skyrocketing diesel prices directly inflate logistics and freight costs throughout the entire economy, intensifying broader inflationary pressures.

Rachel Abrams: 感谢 Cliff 为我们带来的深入解读。

Original English

Rachel Abrams: Thank you, Cliff, for breaking this all down for us.

Clifford Krauss: 不客气。

Original English

Clifford Krauss: You're welcome.
