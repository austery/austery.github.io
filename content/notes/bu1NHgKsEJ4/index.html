科学发现：乌鸦的惊人认知能力与决策速度

[Tracy Mumford]: 最后，人类欠鸟类一个道歉。长期以来，研究鸟类的学者看着它们小小的脑袋，便得出它们并不是特别聪明的结论。因此有了“鸟脑子”（bird brain）这个词，这可不是什么夸赞。但越来越多的证据表明，它们脑子里发生的事情远比人们认为的要多得多。例如，某些物种能够区分癌细胞与非癌细胞，另一些物种能认出镜子里的自己、解决抽象问题，甚至为未来做规划。现在，发表在《科学》（Science）杂志上的新研究表明，乌鸦在决策速度上甚至可以快于人类。该研究涉及将乌鸦置于屏幕上一团旋转的点阵前，并奖励它们判断这些点是在向左还是向右移动。乌鸦准确作答的速度是恒河猴或人类的两倍，而且是在没有牺牲准确率的前提下做到的。该研究的一位作者表示，他甚至让自己实验室里的所有人——包括本科生、博士生和博士后——与这些鸟类同台竞技，结果他们在乌鸦面前都太慢了。这项研究突显了鸟类大脑尽管体积较小，但却是为了快速处理信息而调优的。研究团队推测，这在很大程度上与飞行的风险性有关：为了在空中生存，你必须非常迅速地做出大量决策。

英文原文 And finally, humans owe birds an apology. For a long time, scholars studying birds looked at their small brains and concluded they weren't all that bright. Hence the term bird brain, not a compliment. But evidence has been increasingly piling up that there's a lot more happening in there than anyone was giving birds credit for. For example, some species are capable of distinguishing between cancerous and non-cancerous cells, and others can recognize their own image, solve abstract problems, even plan for the future. And now, new research published in the journal Science shows that crows specifically can make decisions faster than humans. The research involved placing crows in front of a swirling mass of dots on a screen and rewarding them for answering whether the dots were moving to the left or the right. The crows reliably pecked their answers twice as fast as Reese's monkeys or humans and they did it without compromising accuracy. One of the authors of the study said he even put all of the people in his lab up against the birds, undergrads, PhDs, postocs, and they were all too slow for the crows. The research underscores how the aven brain, despite being small, is tuned for rapid processing. The research team suspects it has a lot to do with how risky flying is. In order to stay alive up there, you got to make a lot of decisions really quick.
