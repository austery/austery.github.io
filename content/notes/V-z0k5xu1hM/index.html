UFO大揭秘：CIA物理学家与纪录片导演证实外星生命及政府掩盖真相 The Diary Of A CEO 2026-05-14

领域: society-thinking

分类: politics-society

主讲人: The Diary Of A CEO

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-05-14

摘要:

本集访谈深入探讨了美国政府对‘非人类智能生命’长达80年的掩盖真相。纪录片导演丹与前CIA物理学家哈尔·普特霍夫博士揭露了关于UFO坠毁回收、反重力推进技术以及著名的‘星门计划’远程遥视实验的惊人内幕，并分析了特朗普政府启动解密进程对全球科技与意识形态的潜在冲击。

80年的掩盖与UAP证据发布

[丹]: 美国政府内部的部分势力一直在掩盖非人类智能生命存在的真相，这种掩盖已经持续了80年。但在刚刚过去的周五，第一批证据已经正式向公众发布。

Original English

[Dan]: There has been an 80-year cover up of the existence of non-human intelligent life covered up by elements of the US government. But this past Friday, the first trunch of evidence was released to the public.

[哈尔·普特霍夫]: 证据绝对清晰地表明，确实存在某种拥有先进技术的生命形式。它们无处不在。

Original English

[Hal Puthoff]: And the evidence is absolutely clear that there is some form of life with advanced technology. They're all over the place.

[丹]: 但那些负责把守这些信息的人认为公众无法承受真相。许多人的生命受到了威胁，很多人害怕站出来告诉白宫他们所知道的情况。甚至连在任总统都被排除在知情范围之外。我采访过高级情报官员和政府官员，多年来发生过多次 UAP 坠毁事件，在某些情况下，坠毁的飞行器中还有非人类的尸体。

Original English

[Dan]: But the people involved in gatekeeping this information don't think the public can handle the truth. People have had their lives threatened. A lot of them are afraid to come forward and tell the White House what they know. And this has been kept from even sitting presidents and I've interviewed highle intelligence officials and government officials and there have been UAP crashes over the years and in some cases the crashed crafts had the bodies of nonhumans in.

[史蒂文·巴特利特]: 那么这份报告里到底有什么？我们有这么多目击记录，甚至接触到了实物材料。

Original English

[Steven Bartlett]: So what exactly is inside this report? We have so many sightings, even access to materials.

[丹]: 大量的文件、报告、视频和静态图像已经被解密。你可能熟悉 NASA 的报告，他们基本上说不相信这些 UAP 是外星人。为什么 NASA 要撒谎？为什么这些东西没被 iPhone 拍到？你信任特朗普政府会发布所有可用信息吗？我认为最终我们会迎来电影中才见过的时刻：现任总统走向麦克风，告诉全世界我们在宇宙中并不孤单。

Original English

[Dan]: And there's a number of files, reports, video, and still images that were declassified... You're probably familiar with this NASA report. They essentially say that they didn't believe that these UAPs are aliens. Why would NASA be lying? Is there a reason why this stuff hasn't been captured on like an iPhone? Are they currently living amongst us? And then do you trust the Trump administration to release all of the available information? I think eventually we'll get to that moment that we've all only seen in movies where sitting president steps to a microphone and tells the world we're not alone in the universe.

纪录片《揭秘时代》与幕后真相

[史蒂文·巴特利特]: 丹，你的背景是什么？关于 UAP，你认为大多数人不知道或不理解的是什么？

Original English

[Steven Bartlett]: Dan, what is your background and as it relates to this subject of UAPs, what is it that you believe that most people don't know or understand?

[丹]: 我对这个话题的兴趣始于童年。我一直希望有人能制作一部严肃、可信、冷静的纪录片，只采访那些因为为美国政府工作而直接了解该话题的人。于是我制作了电影**《揭秘时代》**（The Age of Disclosure）。

我学到的核心信息是：美国政府自40年代后期以来一直在掩盖非人类智能生命的存在。更重要的是，那些把守机密的人一直在与中国、俄罗斯等竞争对手进行一场高风险的秘密冷战竞赛，试图对这些非人类起源的技术进行逆向工程。这部影片促使特朗普总统在2月中旬发布指令，要求联邦机构解密相关证据。

Original English

[Dan]: My interest in this topic comes from my childhood... I always wish someone had made a super serious, credible, sober documentary that only interviews people who have direct knowledge of this topic as a result of working for the US government... I made this movie, The Age of Disclosure, in secrecy over three and a half years... there has in fact been an 80-year cover up of the existence of non-human intelligent life... the people who within the US government that have been gatekeeping this, they've also been involved in a highstakes secret cold war race with adversarial nations like China and Russia to reverse engineer this technology of non-human origin... it led to President Trump issuing this directive in the middle of February.

科学顾问哈尔·普特霍夫的证词

[史蒂文·巴特利特]: 哈尔，你的背景是什么？你凭什么能就 UAP 和 UFO 发表意见？

Original English

[Steven Bartlett]: Hal... What is your background and why what reference points are you drawing on to speak on the subject of UAPs and UFOs etc?

[哈尔·普特霍夫]: 我是一名量子物理学家，曾为 NSA 以及 CIA 等情报机构工作。我还担任过比格洛航天公司（Bigelow Aerospace）老板罗伯特·比格洛的首席科学顾问。当时国防情报局（DIA）找到我们，希望弄清楚 UAP 领域到底发生了什么。

Original English

[Hal Puthoff]: I'm a quantum physicist worked for the National Security Agency for various uh organizations in the intelligence community like CIA and so on. And so as part of my technical work uh I was also a consultant uh chief science adviser to Robert Bigalow of Bigalow Aerospace... the Defense Intelligence Agency came forward and said, uh you know, we need to find out uh really what's going on in the so-called UAP area.

[丹]: 有一天比格洛的一位同事给他看了一段视频：一个三角形 UAP 盘旋在核武器基地上空。那位同事问：“这是我们的尖端技术吗？”比格洛直觉知道那不是。他开始寻找情报界处理 UFO 的办公室，结果发现存在一个深藏不露的**“遗留项目”**。这个项目脱离了国会和白宫的监管。

Original English

[Dan]: Simply put, one day, one of his colleagues... shows him a video that Air Force security guards took over a nuclear weapons site. It was a triangle UAP hovering over a nuclear weapon site. this colleague said, "Please tell me this is one of ours... some advanced cutting edge technology that's ours." And he knew instinctively it it was not... turns out there was a deeply hidden program referred to as the legacy program... outside of congressional oversight, outside of the oversight of the White House.

超越现代工程学的物理原理

[哈尔·普特霍夫]: 飞行员看到飞行器从海中飞出，以 6G 的过载做直角转弯。这超越了我们的物理学，但如果你能根据广义相对论操纵时空度规，就能实现这些效果。我们掌握了科学原理，只是还没有工程实现能力。我 100% 相信非人类智能生命已经在这里很久了，它们甚至能关停我们的核导弹。

Original English

[Hal Puthoff]: These pilots are out there and they suddenly see craft coming out of the ocean and making right angle turns at 6G or whatever... it's way beyond our physics... but turns out if you could engineer those (Einstein's equations) you would actually get the same effects... we don't have the engineering to do it... I 100% believe that non-human intelligent life is here and has been here for a long time... UAP have come over nuclear uh missile sites and actually turned off the missiles.

CIA的“星门计划”与远程遥视

[史蒂文·巴特利特]: 我们聊聊 CIA 的远程遥视项目，到底什么是远程遥视？

Original English

[Steven Bartlett]: Remote remote viewing. That was that CIA project. What is remote viewing?

[哈尔·普特霍夫]: 70 年代 CIA 担心苏联研究 ESP（超感官知觉），于是让我建立了一个项目来调查这是否真实，这就是**“星门计划”（Stargate）。我们发现远程遥视**（用“心眼”观察遥远位置的信息）是一种像艺术天赋一样的潜能。

具体的例子：一架苏联飞机在非洲坠毁，CIA 无法定位。我们的远程遥视者在地图上画了个叉，准确标出了三英里以内的位置。吉米·卡特总统后来也证实了这个故事。我们甚至做过预测白银期货的实验，30 天内赚了 26 万美元。这似乎是人类本性的一部分，与量子纠缠有关。

Original English

[Hal Puthoff]: Remote viewing... CIA suddenly got concerned because they saw that the Soviets were spending millions... to investigate the possible use of quote ESP... I was at Stanford Research Institute... It grew into a two decade program... Stargate... remote viewing, this ability to sit in a location and pick up information from someplace far away, uh, is a talent... A Soviet plane... went down somewhere in Africa... We got two of our best remote viewers... they put an X on the map that was in three miles of where the plane went down... there's an audio recording of President Jimmy Carter telling that story... we set up a little program on a challenge to predict silver futures... made $260,000 in the 30 days... quantum theory and quantum entanglement...

意识控制与技术革命

[哈尔·普特霍夫]: 很多坠毁飞行器的报告称舱内没有控制面板，只有座椅，这暗示飞行器可能是通过思维连接来控制的。

Original English

[Hal Puthoff]: The reports from people involved say that a lot of them don't have any control panels in them. Like they're basically empty other than seats, which suggests that maybe there's some sort of mind connection controlling these craft.

[丹]: 如果解密真的实现，将引领一场技术爆炸，解决能源危机并实现星际旅行。这还会产生巨大的心理影响，让我们意识到宇宙充满了生命。

Original English

[Dan]: I think it will lead to a giant technology boom... revolutionizing the way we live... lead to new energy sources. Solve the energy crisis overnight... it would have a great psychological effect... this is a universe full of life.

[史蒂文·巴特利特]: 特朗普已经发布了第一轮报告。这个话题未完待续。

Original English

[Steven Bartlett]: Trump has released the first round of the UAP reports. Um so I guess in many respects, this conversation is to be continued.
