《纽约时报》头条新闻：中东战事扩大风险与红海航运威胁

在中东地区，随着也门胡塞武装发表最新声明，各界普遍担忧战争可能会开启一条全新的战线。作为伊朗“抵抗之弧”网络中的关键代理人力量，也门胡塞武装宣布将切断该地区的一条核心航道。胡塞武装明确表示，将阻止所有来自美国重要盟友沙特阿拉伯的船运通行红海特定水域。由于霍尔木兹海峡此前一直处于战火威胁之下，红海已成为全球石油运输至关重要的替代通道。

尽管胡塞武装多年来一直在也门与沙特支持的政府军作战，但此前在很大程度上并未直接介入美伊冲突。然而，这一最新的封锁威胁不仅可能将其全面拖入更大规模的区域冲突，还将给全球能源供给带来更严峻的压力。此前在加沙冲突高潮时期便有先例：胡塞武装为了声援哈马斯，在红海频繁袭击并劫持商船，迫使各大航运公司不得不绕道非洲南端的好望角，航程增加数千英里，直接导致全球海运成本大幅飙升。

From The New York Times, it's the headlines. I'm Tracy Mumford. Today is Tuesday, July 21st. Here's what we're covering.

In the Middle East, there are fears that a new front in the war could be opening. Yesterday, one of Iran's key proxy forces, the Houthi militia in Yemen, declared that it will choke off a key waterway in the region. It said it would stop all ships from Saudi Arabia, a major US ally, from passing through a portion of the Red Sea, which has become a critical alternate path for oil shipments as the Strait of Hormuz has been under fire.

The Houthis, which are part of the so-called axis of resistance led by Iran, have been fighting the Saudi-backed government in Yemen for years. They've largely stayed out of the US-Iran war so far, but the new threat could bring them fully into the broader conflict in the region. It could also put more strain on global energy supplies. There is some precedent for what that could look like. During the height of the war in Gaza, the Houthis attacked and hijacked ships in the Red Sea in solidarity with Hamas. That forced shipping companies to divert their cargo thousands of miles south around the tip of Africa, sending shipping costs soaring.

国会山博弈：数百亿美元战争预算与行政权力边界

与此同时，在国会山，国防部长皮特·海格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）与总统首席军事顾问丹·凯恩通用将军（General Dan Cain）于今日出席国会听证会，试图说服立法者批准数十亿美元的新增战争拨款。众议院议长麦克·约翰逊（Mike Johnson）已明确信号将全力推进该预算请求通过。然而，许多议员（包括部分共和党人）提出强烈质疑，指责特朗普总统在未获得国会授权的情况下私自发动战争。

此前，参众两院曾表决通过决议，要求总统在开展军事行动前必须取得国会批准，但特朗普对此予以忽视。民主党人则拒绝为该拨款请求背书，强调行政当局必须公开更多关于这场已持续近5个月且在美国民众中极不受欢迎的冲突的具体战略细节。

特朗普行政动向：对加关税升级、空军一号改造与泄密调查

在特朗普行政当局的最新动态中，总统昨日签署行政命令，决定从下个月开始对来自加拿大的大量商品征收 50% 的关税。此举势必重新点燃与美国最大贸易伙伴之一的贸易摩擦。自去年加拿大对特朗普的第一轮关税采取报复性反击以来，一直处于白宫的打击范畴内，预计加拿大将成为新一轮关税附加费打击的目标之一。虽然最高法院今年早些时候废除了特朗普广泛使用关税的权力，但行政当局正通过引用更隐蔽的法律条款寻找替代方案。

在另一项事务中，特朗普宣布新版空军一号（Air Force One）专机将于今年秋季暂时退役，进行白宫所称的“升级与改进”。尽管具体细节未公开，《纽约时报》此前报道称美国特勤局对这架由卡塔尔捐赠的客机提出了安全担忧，多名官员透露该机缺乏旧版空军一号所具备的反导弹防御能力。急于让豪华专机投入使用的特朗普对安全漏洞报道感到愤怒，司法部正试图追查向媒体提供信息的匿名来源，不仅向记者发出传票，还调取了记者母亲及配偶等亲属的通讯记录。司法部发言人对此表示，所有传票均完全符合联邦法律。

福利制度重构：SNAP食品券政策调整与官僚困境

自国会通过重大法案（One Big Beautiful Bill）数年来，失去联邦补充营养协助计划（SNAP，俗称食品券）的美国人数量呈上升趋势。降幅最为剧烈的是亚利桑那州，当地领取 SNAP 的人数几乎减少了一半，意味着近 50 万人失去了联邦食物资助。相关报道追踪了包括残障人士、大学生、无家可归家庭以及因省吃俭用让给孙辈而体重骤降的患癌老人在内的受影响群体。

长期关注贫困问题的记者分析指出，改变始于法案引入的工作要求，但最显著的影响来自于对发放失误的州政府设立的高额惩罚机制。面对巨额罚款威胁，亚利桑那州在全州范围裁减行政人员的同时，大幅增加了申请材料审核要求，直接导致系统陷入混乱。合法合规的申请者因繁文缛节无法获得资助，电话打不通、文件丢失、通知滞后，食品券办事处门前排起长队，甚至有母亲因排队耗时过长不得不让孩子退学。尽管亚利桑那州共和党人将人数下降归因于遏制了系统内的大规模诈骗（Massive Fraud），但全国范围内 10% 的下降趋势预示着其他各州在应对联邦惩罚机制时可能面临相似困境。

英文原文

In the years since Congress passed the one big beautiful bill, a growing number of Americans are no longer receiving federal food stamp benefits, commonly known as SNAP. The state with the highest drop-off is Arizona, where the number of people on SNAP has dropped by roughly half, meaning almost 500,000 people there no longer get federal food assistance.

"I've talked to a 47-year-old man who's disabled. Um I talked to a 21-year-old college student. I've talked to a family that was homeless and living in their car. I talked to a grandmother with breast cancer and she's lost a lot of weight because she's skipping meals to feed her grandkids."

My colleague Jason DeParle has covered poverty for more than three decades. He says that things began to change as soon as President Trump's signature legislation was signed into law. It introduced some work requirements for people getting benefits, which likely accounts for some of the drop-off. But Jason says the most significant change was the huge new penalties states can now face if they make mistakes when giving out food stamps. Because of that threat, Arizona started requiring way more paperwork from people seeking benefits at exactly the same moment there were statewide staffing cuts. The result was chaos with many people who qualify for benefits simply unable to get them due to the new red tape.

"All of a sudden, people just couldn't get through on the telephones. The state was losing their documents. People were getting notices in April saying, 'If you didn't meet a March deadline, you were going to get cut off.' There were lines around the block at the food stamp office. People waited hours just to get in. Several mothers told me that they kept their kids out of school because they knew the waits at the SNAP office were going to be so long that they wouldn't have time to leave in the middle and go pick up their kids from school."

Republicans in Arizona have cheered the drop-off in SNAP recipients, saying it's a sign that there has been, quote, "massive fraud in the system." And some have said the new paperwork was a necessary response to the fact that mistaken overpayments to SNAP recipients had been rising in recent years. Overall, Jason says that while Arizona's huge drop-off in SNAP enrollment is something of an outlier, nationally it's only dropped by about 10%, it could be a preview of what's to come as other states try to figure out how to avoid the threat of new federal penalties.

环境与生态危机：加拿大野火扑救失控与跨国烟雾漫延

近期全美范围内因野火烟雾导致的严重空气污染虽有所缓解，但随着太平洋西北地区新的火情以及加拿大烟雾向南扩散，空气质量改善可能只是暂时的。目前加拿大境内仍有 900 多起野火持续燃烧，其中半数因雷击引发的火灾发生在极其偏远的无路区域，仅能通过飞机输送力量。

消防专家指出，空中空中洒水仅能为地面救援争取时间，但面对幅员辽阔的荒野火情，即使投入大幅增加的资金也无法阻止火势蔓延。

英文原文

Across the US, there's been some relief in the last day or two from wildfire smoke that turned the air unhealthy. But that improvement could be fleeting with fires burning in the Pacific Northwest and another shot of smoke forecast to spread south today from Canada. More than 900 wildfires are still burning there and many Canadian fires are impossible to fight. Half of the country's blazes, which are often caused by lightning strikes, burn in areas that are incredibly remote. They may be dotted with small indigenous communities or completely unpopulated, accessible only by plane and with no roads for firefighters. Aircraft can drop water from above, but a fire management expert in Canada told the times that's just intended to buy time until you can get boots on the ground. He added, quote, "To be blunt, even if we spend a lot more money, I don't think we could stop these fires."

科技与产业洞察：生成式 AI 垃圾图书（AI Slop）现象调查

《纽约时报》科技记者卡什米尔·希尔（Kashmir Hill）在参加一次会议时，意外发现亚马逊平台上竟然有一本关于她个人的传记《Kashmir Hill 传》（The Biography of Kashmir Hill）。这本书封面简陋，售价 26.99 美元，内容夹杂着从网络抓取的公开信息与大量虚构内容（例如声称阅读过她的童年日记）。这正属于典型的 AI 垃圾内容（AI Slop: 利用大语言模型批量生成的低质量出版物）。

调查发现，类似图书的出版商利用 ChatGPT 等工具将网络信息等同于传统采访和文献搜集，在短时间内进行规模化生产。例如一名退休出版者在一年内向亚马逊提交了超过 400 本图书，月销量达数百本，每本获利约 7 美元。虽然读者反馈极差，满是关于重复、错误和质量低劣的一星差评，但绝大多数消费者在购买前并未意识到这些内容由 AI 生成。亚马逊平台通常允许此类 AI 生成图书的存在，甚至部分作品曾登上畅销榜，仅在引发大量投诉或内容极度违规时才会下架处理。

英文原文

And finally,

"Recently I was a speaker at a conference and one of the other speakers went to go try to buy my book on Amazon and ended up finding a book about me and sent me a text asking me why I hadn't told them I had a biography and I clicked the link. I had no idea I had a biography and saw this very plain-looking book with this brown cover called The Biography of Kashmir Hill. And in my mind immediately I thought, 'This is an AI slop biography.'"

Kashmir Hill covers tech at The Times, meaning she had seen these kinds of books for sale before, biographies of public figures churned out by AI. She just never expected to be the subject or victim of one herself. Obviously, she got a copy, $26.99, and it had some generic facts about her that you could find on the internet. But the writer, using my air quotes here, also made up a lot of stuff, at one point claiming to have read her childhood diary. Kashmir tried to reach out to the person selling the book to understand why they were doing this, but she never got a response.

"So I went looking for someone else who uses AI to write books and I found a man named Bill Johns who is 70, he's retired, he lives in Maryland, and he has published more than 400 books to Amazon over just a year. And he told me, 'Hey, everything that's ever been thought or said is on the internet. So ChatGPT can get it in the same way that an author could go out and interview people or find documents.' He kind of felt like this was an equivalent process. I asked if I could see his Amazon sales reports and he is selling a few hundred books per month. He makes about $7 per book. So, for him, this experiment has been a success. I went to look at what people thought of the books and the reviews were not great. He had a lot of one-star reviews, people saying it was the worst book they'd ever purchased. It was repetitious, it was full of mistakes. So, it seems like customers don't love the books and I don't think they realize when they're buying them that they've been written with AI."

Cashmere says that Amazon is generally okay with AI-generated books. Some have even become best-sellers on the site, though the company will take them down if they're really, really bad and generate a lot of complaints. Cashmere's own unauthorized biography has since disappeared from Amazon. In the meantime, Bill Johns offered to whip up a new one for her. She declined.
