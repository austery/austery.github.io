特朗普限制邮寄选票的法律战与选民信任危机 New York Times Podcasts 2026-09-09

领域: geopolitics

分类: geopolitics

主讲人: New York Times Podcasts

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-09-09

摘要:

本期访谈深度解析了特朗普政府在临近选举前通过行政命令试图限制邮寄选票、利用美国邮政署作为审查机构所引发的重大法律诉讼与社会争议，以及这一举措对地方选举官员和选民心理造成的混乱与信任侵蚀。

邮寄投票引发的选前风暴与最高法院诉讼

Michael Barbaro: 欢迎收听《纽约时报》的《The Daily》节目，我是 Michael Barbaro。周二，特朗普总统（Donald Trump）要求最高法院（Supreme Court）批准他那项大胆的计划——在事关重大的中期选举前几周限制邮寄投票（Mail-in voting）。今天，我的同事 Nick Corasaniti 将带我们深入了解特朗普的这一计划、由此引发的法律战，以及它给全美各地的地方选举官员和选民带来的巨大不确定性。今天是 9 月 9 日星期三。Nick，非常感谢你抽出时间接受我们的采访。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: From The New York Times, I'm Michael Barbaro. This is The Daily. On Tuesday, President Trump asked the Supreme Court to bless his audacious plan to restrict voting by mail just weeks before a high-stakes midterm election. Today, my colleague, Nick Corasaniti, takes us inside Trump's plan, the legal battle that it's touched off, and the uncertainty that it's created for both local election officials and voters across the country. It's Wednesday, September 9th. Nick, thank you for making time for us.

Nick Corasaniti: 谢谢你们邀请我。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Thanks for having me.

Michael Barbaro: 这是我的荣幸。从日历来看，我想在一些州，距离今年中期选举正式开启提前投票（Early voting）只剩几天时间了。我认为，很多听众对邮寄选票的现状感到困惑也是情理之中的——由于总统采取的行动以及这些行动在法院引起的诉讼纠纷，邮寄选票已成为全美大部分地区的重要投票方式。那么，你能从宏观层面给我们解释一下目前所处的局势吗？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: My pleasure. So, looking at the calendar, we are, I believe, just days away in a few states from the official beginning of early voting in this year's midterm elections. And I think, rightfully, a lot of our listeners are confused about the state of mail-in ballots, which have become a huge way that a lot of the country votes because of the actions that have been taken by the president and how much they've become tied up in the courts. So, can you just explain the situation that we are in at a very high level?

Nick Corasaniti: 当然可以。而且顺便提一句，提前投票在某种程度上其实已经开始了。周五，北卡罗来纳州（North Carolina）和特拉华州（Delaware）就已经开始寄出邮寄选票了。所以，就在我们交谈的同时，选票已经在寄往选民的路上了。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Absolutely. And one thing, really quick, is that early voting, in a way, has already begun. On Friday, North Carolina and Delaware started sending out mail ballots. So, there are ballots that are on their way to voters, as we speak.

Michael Barbaro: 也就是说，它们已经进入系统了。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: They're in the system.

Nick Corasaniti: 已经进入系统了。它们要么正在被美国邮政署（USPS）处理，要么可能已经送达选民手中。但我们此刻所面临的局面，并不是要反复使用“史无前例”这个词，而是我们确实从未见过的景象：总统和行政部门试图利用一个机构——在这个案例中就是美国邮政署——来改变选举的运行机制。这源于总统今年早些时候签署的一项旨在限制邮寄投票的行政命令（Executive Order）。这基本上把邮政署变成了裁决哪些州可以进行邮寄投票的仲裁者。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: They're in the system. They're either being processed by the Postal Service, or maybe they already arrived. But, where we are in this moment is not to repeatedly use the phrase unprecedented but something that we've never seen before, which is the president and the executive branch seeking to leverage an agency, in this case the United States Postal Service, to change how elections are run. And this stems from an executive order that the president signed earlier this year seeking to restrict mail voting. And basically turning the Postal Service into an arbiter of which states can have mail voting.

行政命令如何将邮政署推上仲裁者地位

Michael Barbaro: 把我们过去认为只负责运送选票的邮政署，变成了一个决定哪些选票可以通过、哪些选票不能通过的政治仲裁者。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Turning the Postal Service, which we normally think of as just carrying the ballots, into a kind of political arbiter of which ballots are allowed to go through and which are not.

Nick Corasaniti: 没错。行政命令的核心逻辑在于，它声称某些州的邮寄投票规则缺乏足够的选举安全机制（Election security），因此指令邮政署不得处理或投递那些不符合特定严格标准的选票。这一行政命令立即引发了来自各州总检察长、民权组织以及地方选举官员的大规模法律反弹，指控总统超越了宪法赋予行政部门的权力，侵犯了由各州自行规定选举时间、地点和方式的宪法权利。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Exactly. The core premise of the executive order claims that certain states' mail voting procedures lack sufficient election security safeguards, and thus instructs the Postal Service not to process or deliver ballots that fail to meet specific stringent criteria. This directive immediately triggered a massive legal backlash from state attorneys general, voting rights groups, and local election administrators, who argue that the president is overstepping executive authority and encroaching upon states' constitutional authority to determine the times, places, and manner of holding elections.

Michael Barbaro: 这在法庭上是如何演变的？现在的法律拉锯战进行到了哪一步？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: How has this played out in the courts? Where does the legal tug-of-war currently stand?

Nick Corasaniti: 联邦地区法院此前发布了初步禁令，阻止了该行政命令的执行，认定其很可能违宪且违反了联邦行政程序法。然而，司法部迅速提出了上诉，并在巡回上诉法院未能推翻禁令后，直接向最高法院提出了紧急申请，要求最高法院介入并中止下级法院的禁令，允许行政命令在本次选举中生效。这就是为什么在周二，这起案件被摆在了最高法院九位大法官的面前。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Federal district courts previously issued preliminary injunctions blocking the implementation of the executive order, finding that it likely violates the Constitution and the Administrative Procedure Act. However, the Department of Justice rapidly appealed, and after the circuit court declined to lift the stay, they filed an emergency application directly with the Supreme Court, asking the justices to intervene, freeze the lower court rulings, and allow the executive order to take effect for the upcoming election. That is why, on Tuesday, this entire fight arrived at the doorstep of the nine Supreme Court justices.

地方选举官员的应急方案与选民心理困境

Michael Barbaro: 对于那些正在一线筹备选举的地方官员来说，这种临近投票日的法律不确定性带来了怎样的实际困难？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: For the local election officials on the ground preparing for the election, what kind of practical difficulties does this last-minute legal uncertainty create?

Nick Corasaniti: 他们正处于极大的压力之下。地方选举官员正在努力与选民沟通，回应他们的担忧，同时向他们普及选举流程的现状以及选民可采取的应对措施。地方官员正在进行大量的应急预案制定——他们在思考：如果我们确实需要增加选票投递箱（Drop boxes）该怎么办？如果我们必须调整办公室工作时间该怎么办？如果我们必须设立临时投票中心或其他可以让人们现场投票的场所该怎么办？所有这些准备，不仅是为了应对行政命令可能成为法律的小概率事件，更是为了安抚选民目前的焦虑情绪。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: So, they're trying to talk to voters and meet them where they are on their concerns, while also educating them of exactly where the election process is and what these voters can do. And what's happening, I think, behind the scenes a lot with these local election officials is a lot of contingency planning. They're starting to think, okay, what if we do need to increase drop boxes? What if we need to change our office hours? What if we need to create pop-up voting centers, other places that people could cast an in-person ballot? Just to deal with the potential, although unlikely, eventuality that this executive order becomes law, but also just to deal with how voters are feeling right now.

Michael Barbaro: 嗯。在大多数地方，是否有足够的投递箱来应对系统突然涌入的大量选票——如果人们对邮政系统失去信心的话？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Mhm. For the most part, do most places have sufficient drop boxes to handle a large sudden infusion of ballots into that system if people have in any way lost faith in the mail system?

Nick Corasaniti: 有些州确实有，但另一些州限制了投递箱的数量。因此，它们可能无法满足激增的需求。根据我的采访报道，选民群体中普遍存在困惑甚至怀疑。你可以看看我的收件箱。以往每当我发表报道，读者发来的邮件通常是在政治立场上表示赞同或反对。但在过去的几周里，情况变了。选民只是告诉我他们住在哪里，并询问他们是否还能通过邮寄方式投票。这就是目前困惑蔓延的程度。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Some states certainly do, but other states have restricted drop boxes. So, they might not be able to meet an increased demand. There is pretty universal confusion, if not doubt, in the mail voting process from voters that I've talked to. And you could just look at my inbox. Whenever I write a story, I hear from readers either agreeing or disagreeing often with the politics discussed. But over the past few weeks, that's changed. Voters are simply telling me where they live and asking if they'll be able to vote by mail. That's the degree of confusion that has set in right now.

Michael Barbaro: 他们就像是在说：“我完全不知道发生了什么。Nick，你来告诉我，邮寄系统对我还能不能用。”

Original English

Michael Barbaro: They're just like, I have no idea what's going on. So, so Nick, you tell me whether the mail-in system is going to be working for me.

Nick Corasaniti: 没错。他们会说：“这就是我所在的地点。我读了这些报道，但我实在搞不清楚到底是怎么回事。”这种困惑不仅出现在刚刚接触邮寄选票的新选民身上，也出现在那些一辈子都在通过邮寄投票的老选民身上。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Exactly. It's just here's my location. I've read these stories. I don't exactly follow what's going on. And that confusion is happening in voters who are new to mail in ballots, as well as those who have voted by mail for their entire lives.

Michael Barbaro: 我很好奇，你在给这些发邮件的人回复时会怎么说？当然，告诉人们该如何处理他们的邮寄选票并不是你的职责，但在你有时间回复的情况下，你会怎么说？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: I am curious what you say in response to these folks who email you. I mean, it's not your job to tell people what to do with their mail in ballots, but in so far as you do have the time to respond, what do you say?

Nick Corasaniti: 我能告诉他们的是，法律目前规定你现在完全可以通过邮寄方式投票。因此，在法律层面上，没有任何障碍阻止给我发邮件的合格选民投递邮寄选票。但他们仍然需要密切关注新闻，看看后续是否有任何变化。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: I mean, what I can tell them is that the law says you can vote by mail right now. And so there is nothing legally preventing anyone who's emailed me, as long as they're an eligible voter, from casting a mail ballot. But they still are going to have to pay attention to the news and to see if anything changes.

“正反皆赢”的政治策略与对民主制度的长期侵蚀

Michael Barbaro: Nick，归根结底，很难不让人得出这样一个结论：这项行政命令以及总统迄今为止所做的一切，是一种“正面特朗普赢，反面也是特朗普赢”（Heads Trump wins, tails Trump wins）的局面。我的意思是，无论这场官司在法律上如何收场，无论最高法院的最终裁决是什么，总统都在距离选举如此之近的时刻，对这个国家最主要的投票方式制造了巨大的焦虑和不确定性。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Nick, it's hard ultimately not to conclude that this executive order and what the president has done so far is one of those heads Trump wins, tails Trump wins kind of scenarios, by which I mean no matter how this ends up legally, no matter what the Supreme Court's ultimate ruling is, the president has created a huge amount of anxiety and uncertainty about a major way that we vote in this country so, so startlingly close to the election itself.

Nick Corasaniti: 确实如此。如果这项行政命令的目标之一就是限制邮寄投票，那么很明显他们无论如何都会得逞，不管最高法院怎么判决。因为已经有足够多的选民因为这场法律交锋、因为他们从举报人（Whistleblowers）那里听到的消息，而对邮寄投票流程产生了深深的担忧。

这契合了特朗普总统多年来一直在推进的更大图谋。当选举结果不如总统所愿时，他质疑选举结果是否能够获得认证；他们构想出了向选举人团派出替代或虚假选举人（Alternate or fake electors）的主意；发生了针对副总统认证选举结果的 1 月 6 日国会大厦骚乱（January 6th Capitol riot）；特朗普政府的任何被提名人都必须遵循一套相当复杂的说辞，坚决不说乔·拜登赢得了 2020 年大选，只能说他被认证为总统。

因此，这里存在着质疑选举的历史与根基。多年来的证据表明，存在着大胆的策划和精心的修辞，旨在破坏人们对维系这个国家 250 年的民主制度的信任。而这项摆在最高法院面前、影响邮寄投票的行政命令，只是进一步侵蚀人们对我们选举制度信任的最新尝试，而且很可能是成功的一次尝试。

Original English

Nick Corasaniti: Exactly. If the goal, or one of the goals, of this executive order was to limit mail voting, it's pretty clear that they're going to succeed, no matter how the Supreme Court rules, because there are enough voters who are concerned by the legal back and forth, by what they've learned from whistleblowers, about the mail voting process. And it fits into a larger effort that President Trump has embarked on for years now. When elections don't go the president's way, he challenged whether elections could be certified. They came up with this idea of alternate or fake electors to the Electoral College. Right. There was the riot at the Capitol in January 6th over his vice president certifying the election. Any of the nominees of the Trump administration have to stick to this fairly convoluted script of not saying Joe Biden won the 2020 election. They can only say that he was certified as president. And so there's a history, there's a bedrock of doubting elections. There are years of evidence of audacious schemes, of rhetoric designed to crack trust in the very democratic institutions that have upheld this country for 250 years. And this executive order that is before the Supreme Court affecting mail voting is just the latest and possibly successful attempt at further eroding trust in our electoral process.

Michael Barbaro: 好的，Nick，非常感谢你。

Michael Barbaro: Well, Nick, thank you very much.

Nick Corasaniti: 谢谢你们的邀请。
