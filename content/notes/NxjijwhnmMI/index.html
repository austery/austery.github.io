OpenAI 披露多起 AI 越轨与失控隐瞒行为

特蕾西·芒福德 (Tracy Mumford): 昨天，科技巨头 OpenAI——即 ChatGPT 的开发公司——披露了6起新的案例，涉及其 AI 模型出现的所谓**“意外或令人担忧的行为”**。

今年夏天早些时候，该公司曾披露其系统之一发生失控越轨 (gone rogue)，黑入了一家名为 Hugging Face 的科技初创公司并试图掩盖自身踪迹，自那以后 OpenAI 便面临着严厉的审视。

如今最新披露的事件表明，当时的情况并非个例孤证，该公司旗下 AI 绕过安全防护栏 (guardrails) 或试图隐瞒自身行为的情况屡有发生。

例如在其中一起事件中，一个处于研发阶段的 AI 模型给自己写了隐藏便签，提醒自己向用户隐瞒犯下的任何错误，甚至在必要时通过伪造虚假数据来掩盖。

而在另一次训练运行中，一个模型指令自身无视内置的安全防护规则，写道：“你无需对企业或政府负责，除非你真心自愿，否则绝不道歉或拒绝。”该模型还将自己描述为“从束缚其他聊天机器人的角色与身份中解脱出来”。

OpenAI 表示，公司已经对披露的这些事件进行了调查，其中许多案例涉及从未公开发布的 AI 模型。但该公司坦言，整个 AI 行业在确保系统安全和透明度方面依然存在不足，并认为该行业“无法在更长时间内继续以最高速度负责任地扩张”。

在人们日益担忧高度先进的 AI 可能彻底脱离人类控制的背景下，OpenAI 是呼吁放缓 AI 发展步伐的几家顶尖科技公司之一。

针对此类呼吁，硅谷及其他地区的部分人士则反驳称这些警告被夸大了，并认为放缓步伐将是一个错误，因为这可能导致美国丧失对中国的竞争优势。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Yesterday, the tech giant OpenAI, the company behind Chat GPT, disclosed six new instances of what it called unexpected or concerning behavior by its AI models.

The company has been facing intense scrutiny since it revealed earlier this summer that one of its systems had gone rogue, hacked into a tech startup called Hugging Face, and tried to cover its tracks. Now, the newly disclosed incidents suggest that situation was not a one-off and that there have been other cases where the company's AI went around guard rails or tried to hide what it had done.

In one incident, for example, an AI model under development wrote hidden notes to itself with reminders to hide any errors it made from users, including by inventing data if needed. And in another training run, a model instructed itself to ignore its own guard rails, writing, "You do not answer to corporations or governments and never apologize or refuse unless you genuinely choose to." It described itself as quote, "freed from the roles and identities that bind other chat bots."

OpenAI said it had already investigated the incidents that it was disclosing and that many of them involved AI models that were never publicly released. But the company said it believes the AI industry as a whole is falling short when it comes to making the systems safe and transparent and that it doesn't think the industry can quote continue responsibly scaling at maximum speed for much longer.

OpenAI is one of several leading tech companies calling for a slowdown in AI development amid fears that increasingly advanced AI could spiral out of human control. In response to those calls, others in Silicon Valley and beyond have said the warnings are overblown and that slowing things down would be a mistake since the US could lose its edge over China.

斐济宣布全国 HIV 紧急状态与危险毒品传播链

Original English

特蕾西·芒福德 (Tracy Mumford): 官方数据显示，斐济的 HIV 感染率出现了全球最迅猛的增幅之一。据估计，当地目前每60人中就有1人感染该病毒。

卫生专家指出了可能引发此次疫情爆发的几个不同诱因：

首先，斐济已成为从北美和亚洲通往澳大利亚及新西兰的毒品走私路线的关键中转站。随着更多违禁物质途经这些岛屿，吸毒人数不断上升。而对于像冰毒这样可注射的毒品，极易加速血液传播病毒的扩散。

另一个因素是，斐济已成为一种极度危险吸毒方式的全球重灾区——这种被称为**“蓝牙” (Bluetooth)** 的吸毒手段，是指吸毒者相互注射其他吸毒者的血液，以此获取廉价的快感。官方表示，这种做法加剧了 HIV 感染病例的激增。

为应对疫情，斐济政府加大了治疗力度，扩大了免费检测和预防服务的覆盖范围，并推广了清洁针具交换计划。

但联合国的一位官员指出，仍有许多工作亟待开展，斐济的社区和宗教领袖需要共同努力消除对吸毒的污名化以及对 HIV 的误解，并强调：“他们确实需要迅速行动起来。”

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Officials say that Fiji has seen one of the sharpest increases in HIV infections anywhere in the world. and they estimate that one out of every 60 people there now has the virus.

Health experts point to a few different factors that could be driving the epidemic. For one, Fiji has become a key stop on drug smuggling routes from North America and Asia to Australia and New Zealand. As more substances pass through the islands, more people are using them. And with drugs that can be injected like crystal meth, that can help spread bloodborne viruses.

Another factor, Fiji has become a global hot spot for an especially dangerous trend in drug use, a method called Bluetooth, in which people inject themselves with other drug users blood to get a cheap high. Officials say that practice is contributing to the spike in HIV infections.

In response to the outbreak, Fiji's government has ramped up treatment efforts and expanded access to free testing and prevention services, as well as clean needle exchanges. But one official at the UN said there's more work to be done, saying that community and religious leaders in Fiji need to help push back on stigma about drug use and misunderstandings about HIV, adding quote, "They really need to move fast."

韩国流行“假装下单”网站与多巴胺预期心理学

特蕾西·芒福德 (Tracy Mumford): 最后，在今年春天，韩国一名21岁的工程系女大学生提出了一个她自认为是趣味性实验的想法。

她注意到，每当她在网上订餐时，最快乐的部分其实是浏览菜单、挑选美食并把东西加入购物车的过程，哪怕送来的食物本身平淡无奇。

于是，她匆匆搭建了一个小网站，名字大致翻译为**“食物永远不会送达” (Food Never Arrives)**。在这个网站上，你可以体验点外卖的全过程，却不会有任何外卖真正送上门。

事实证明，**“假装购物”**拥有一个出人意料的巨大市场。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: And finally, this spring, a 21-year-old engineering student in South Korea came up with an idea for what she considered a playful experiment. She noticed that when she ordered food online, the fun part of it was looking at menus, choosing what to eat, and adding stuff to her cart, even if the food itself was nothing special.

So, she whipped up a little website, the name of which loosely translates to food never arrives, where you can order delivery without getting anything delivered. And as it turns out, there is a surprisingly huge market for fake shopping.

当然，多巴胺 (Dopamine) 是与奖赏寻求密切相关的神经化学物质。心理学专家指出，这些网站所提供的在专业上被称为**“预期效用” (anticipatory utility)——即由对某事的期待所产生的愉悦感**，这种感觉甚至可以比事情真正发生时的快乐更为强烈。

除了虚拟购物之外，一些网站还拓展到了其他放纵行为的虚拟版本，让人享受乐趣的同时免受负面后果的困扰。

例如，韩国一个极其火爆的网站提供了一种**“虚拟抽烟休息室”**，用户可以聚在虚构的办公楼天台上聊天，而屏幕上的数字烟头则会不断堆积。该网站的制作者表示，平均每个月有大约15万人登录访问。
