奇里卡瓦确立为美国第64个国家公园

美国迎来了一个新的国家公园。特朗普总统本周签署的一项法案使之正式确立，将最高级别的联邦保护赋予了原先的奇里卡瓦国家纪念区（Chiricahua National Monument）。

我的意思是，这个地方看起来像是属于《阿凡达》电影或《侏罗纪公园》之类的地方。

该公园位于亚利桑那州东南部，距图森（Tucson）约2小时车程。

哇，我是说，看看这个岩柱（hoodoo）。经历了几千年之后，这东西怎么还能保持那样的平衡？

它占地超过12,000英亩，位于奇里卡瓦山脉，拥有由古代火山喷发形成的、被称为岩柱的高耸岩石尖顶迷宫。

山脉从周围沙漠中巍然拔起的方式，使该公园成为了被称为“天空之岛”生态系统的所在地，这是一种在该国独一无二的景观类型。它具有极高的生物多样性，据称拥有世界上最好的一些观鸟点。这片土地作为奇里卡瓦阿帕奇人（Chiricahua Apache）的故土，也具有丰富的文化意义。

奇里卡瓦是自2020年西弗吉尼亚州的新河峡谷（New River Gorge）凭借其陡峭的峡谷绝壁和白水急流获得该称号以来的第一个新国家公园。目前全美共有64个国家公园。

America has a new national park. A bill signed by President Trump this week made it official, giving the highest level of federal protection to what had been Chiricahua National Monument.

I mean, this place looks like it belongs in an Avatar movie or a Jurassic Park or something.

The park is in southeast Arizona, about a 2-hour drive from Tucson.

Wow, I mean, look at this hoodoo. How is this thing even still balanced like that after all these thousands of years?

It's spread over 12,000 acres in the Chiricahua Mountains and features a maze of towering rock spires known as hoodoos, which were shaped by ancient volcanic eruptions.

The way the mountains rise dramatically out of the surrounding desert makes the park home to what's known as a sky island ecosystem, which is a type of landscape unlike any other in the country. It's incredibly biodiverse with apparently some of the world's best bird-watching. The land also has rich cultural significance as the homeland of the Chiricahua Apache.

Chiricahua is the first new national park since 2020, when the distinction went to the New River Gorge in West Virginia with its soaring canyon walls and whitewater rapids. There are now 64 national parks across the country.
