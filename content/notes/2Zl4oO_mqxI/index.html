青年就业市场的“煤矿金丝雀”：AI 冲击与结构性失业

在 2026 年初的安大略省，青年失业率（Youth Unemployment Rate: 15 岁至 24 岁人群中未就业比例）已突破 15%，这一数字揭示了宏观经济的脆弱性。青年群体被视为经济的“煤矿金丝雀”：当经济下行时，他们往往是最后被录用、最先被裁撤的对象。当前的困境由技术变革、移民政策及复杂的宏观动力共同交织而成。虽然 人工智能（Artificial Intelligence: 模拟人类智能的计算系统）在亚利桑那州某社区学院的毕业典礼上因读错姓名引发了小范围的尴尬，但这仅仅是 AI 复杂性的冰山一角。在职场中，AI 不仅改变了招聘流程，更对初级知识工作岗位产生了深远影响，使得缺乏经验的年轻人更难跨出门槛。

英文原文

New graduates at a community college in Arizona recently had a rocky commencement. The wrong names were called or no names at all. So what happened? Well, Glendale president Tiffany Hernandez explained. We're using a new AI system as our reader. So that is a lesson learned for us. So AI messed up their graduation, just like AI could complicate their lives after graduation, at least according to some of the headlines. And it's true that the youth unemployment rate is high in Ontario, more than 15% in the first quarter of 2026.

But is AI really to blame? Turns out the answer may be far more complicated, but no less of a problem. We look at why it's so hard for young people to get jobs right now. Ilona Dougherty is the managing director of the Youth and Innovation Project at the University of Waterloo, and Dan Kelly is the CEO of the Canadian Federation of Independent Business. What's causing this cohort unemployment to look the way it does? It's very complicated. But the reality is young people are the canary in the coal mine. When the economy is tough, young people are the last to get hired, the first to get let go. But again, we're in a very complex moment with technology, with immigration. So there's a lot of different dynamics going on.

经验悖论：从体力劳动规避到“3-5年经验”的硬门槛

当前的劳动力市场呈现出显著的经验悖论（Experience Paradox: 职位要求经验，但年轻人因无法入职而无法获得经验）。对于 15 至 18 岁的青少年，他们面临着服务业和零售业的激烈竞争，且许多职位已不再通过传统的线下方式发布。而对于大学毕业生，招聘启事中普遍要求的“3 至 5 年相关经验”成了难以逾越的鸿沟。此外，青年群体的择业偏好也发生了结构性变化：根据最新调查，50% 的年轻人不考虑重体力劳动（Heavy Physical Labour），40% 拒绝户外工作，而三分之一的人明确表示不接受最低工资（Minimum Wage: 目前安大略省为 17.60 加元，10 月将升至 17.95 加元）。这种对薪酬和环境的高要求，与中小企业主在面临销售下滑压力下，对“即战力”人才的急切需求形成了尖锐矛盾。

英文原文

What are the challenges that a teenager faces versus a university or college graduate? So teenagers, it's usually the kinds of jobs where it's their first job. They're looking to get experience and they don't have any experience. There's a lot of competition for those kinds of service jobs, retail jobs. As you get a little bit older, it's the 3 to 5 years experience that's on every job posting that they can't get or they don't know how to get. There's an expectation that they'll come into the job market with a bow tied, perfectly ready.

50% of young people will not consider a job that has heavy physical labour. 40% of young people will not consider a job that has outdoor work. One third of young people will say that they will not consider a job that pays the minimum wage. The minimum wage these days, remember, is between 15 and $18 an hour. In Ontario, it's $17.60. It'll go up in October to $17.95. If I'm paying 17 bucks an hour for that person, I kind of need them to be able to start running here and serve my customers.

社会化的断裂：直升机父母与职场软技能的缺失

专家指出，青年就业难的另一深层原因在于社会化技能（Socialization Skills: 如主动倾听、创造力、人际互动等）的退化。在 AI 时代，这些属于人类特有的软技能比以往任何时候都更重要，但这些技能无法在教室中习得，必须通过实践磨炼。目前的趋势显示，直升机父母（Helicopter Parenting: 过度保护、干预子女生活的教育方式）在很大程度上削弱了年轻人的独立性和风险承担能力。甚至出现了父母代替子女参加面试，或在子女面临职场纪律处分时出面干预的现象。这种过度保护导致年轻人在进入职场时缺乏必要的韧性，无法应对真实世界的挑战。

英文原文

Businesses aren't willing to invest in young employees and train them. There's a lot of skills, creativity, active listening, all these human skills that are becoming more and more important in the age of AI. You have to learn that through experience. 60% of small employers say that they are struggling with young people, that they have to have attentiveness to the job, to not be sitting on their phone while they're supposed to be serving a customer.

We have a tendency, the helicopter parenting, is certainly a thing where we kind of do too much for our young people and then don't allow them to be themselves, to develop skills through experience. We kind of overprotect them. One of the trends was mom or dad coming and talking for junior in the interview to get the job. And if there's a problem in the workplace and there's some disciplinary action, the parent comes to talk to the manager. We got to stop doing that.

霍尔木兹海峡冲突：能源市场的需求破坏机制

转入宏观经济的另一侧，2026 年 2 月爆发的伊朗战争彻底改变了全球石油贸易格局。霍尔木兹海峡（Strait of Hormuz: 全球石油贸易的关键咽喉）的吞吐量从战前的每天 150 艘骤降至目前的 2 至 9 艘。尽管面临如此巨大的供应冲击，油价尚未突破 200 美元大关，这主要得益于 2022 年后建立的全球石油高库存缓冲及战略储备。专家提出了需求破坏（Demand Destruction: 由于价格飙升或供应中断导致消费永久性减少）的三种形态：

  1. 价格弹性破坏: 消费者因油价过高转而选择骑行或公共交通。
  2. 收入弹性破坏: 高油价拖累全球经济，导致企业破产、失业，进而减少通勤需求。
  3. 政府强制破坏: 类似于疫情期间的封锁政策，通过配给制强制降低消耗。目前，市场主要依靠战略储备和政府干预来维持平衡。
英文原文

About a quarter of the world's maritime oil trade used to pass through the Strait of Hormuz. That all changed in February with the start of the Iran war. Now we're dealing with price hikes and an uncertain future. Rory Johnston is an oil market researcher. Pre-war levels were like 150 ships a day. We're still looking between 2 and 9 ships a day transiting the strait.

Why aren't we seeing oil prices higher than what we are seeing right now? At the beginning of 2026, we had a very oversupplied oil market. That allowed us to build up inventories that have effectively acted as a buffer. $200 or so a barrel would be required to destroy the level of demand required to balance the market without the Strait of Hormuz. Demand destruction is three types: Price elastic (I choose a substitute), Income elastic (my job goes bankrupt and I don't drive), and Government mandated (like Covid lockdowns, rationing the available supply).

加拿大的管道战略：寻找地缘政治的“战略期权”

在对美贸易关系趋紧的背景下，加拿大正被迫重新审视其能源主权。长期以来，加拿大 90% 以上的石油出口流向美国，形成了高度的买方垄断（Monopsony: 市场上只有一个买家的权力结构），使美国精炼厂在价格谈判中占据绝对优势。为了建立战略期权性（Strategic Optionality: 拥有多个选择以应对不确定性的能力），加拿大政府正讨论包括西北海岸石油管道（Northwest Coast Oil Pipeline）在内的四个大型项目。这些项目虽然耗资可能超过 300 亿加元且面临环保挑战，但其核心价值在于直通太平洋盆地，通过深水港口接纳超大型原油运输船（VLCC: Very Large Crude Carrier，载重 200 万桶以上），从而绕过美国、直接服务于印度等亚太增长市场。

英文原文

What projects should Canada prioritize? Southbound into the states has always been cheaper, which is why 90 plus percent of our oil has always gone there. The challenge is that further entrenches us into the monopsony or the overwhelming dependence on the US market. That's where we go to this Northwest Coast oil pipeline. It provides a strategic optionality to access the Pacific basin directly. If it terminates in a deepwater port, it allows us to have those big VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier) tankers that could service the India trade route. We were in a trade war with United States last year, and they were threatening to put tariffs on our oil. That threat was most consequential because we didn't have anywhere else to put the oil.
