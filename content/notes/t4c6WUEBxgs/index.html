安大略省在线赌博合法化及其带来的社会影响与监管挑战

关键字 gambling public-policy mental-health regulation

摘要

安大略省政府通过修订《刑法典》并建立 iGaming Ontario 框架，将原本受限的单场体育博彩和在线赌场游戏开放给私营市场。虽然此举带来了显著的税收增长和经济机会，但研究显示，在线赌博的普及与年轻男性群体中日益增长的赌博成瘾、心理健康问题及急诊就诊人数增加密切相关。目前，政府正面临在促进自由市场与限制有害广告之间寻求平衡的挑战。

安大略省在线赌博的合法化历程

在线赌博是如何变得如此唾手可得的？要回答这个问题，我们必须回到几年前，专门看看体育博彩。几十年来，合法体育博彩在加拿大一直受到限制；而在安大略省，如果你想合法进行体育博彩，你必须亲自到场。

英文原文 How did online gambling become so available? Well, to answer that question, we have to go back a few years and look at sports betting specifically. For decades legal, sports betting was restricted in Canada, and here in Ontario, if you wanted to bet on sports legally, you had to go in person.

MAN: 在 2021 年之前，实际上并没有办法通过手机或电脑，以合法的方式在安大略省政府那里下注——在 2021 年之前，安大略省政府是垄断性的体育博彩运营商。而且你不能对单场比赛的结果下注。在加拿大，对单场比赛下注是非法的。普通消费者会走进便利店或安大略省彩票与博彩公司（OLG，Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation）的持牌机构，他们必须下注过关串关体育博彩。

英文原文 **MAN**: Prior to 2021, there was not really a way to bet on your phone or on your computer, in a legal way with the Ontario government, which was the monopoly sports betting operator prior to 2021. And you couldn't bet on the outcome of a single game. It was illegal to bet on a single game in Canada. The average consumer would walk into a convenience store or an OLG - Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation - licensed entity, and they would have to bet on a parlay sports bet.

LUCAS: 过关串关投注（Parlay bets）是一种要求下注者同时对多场比赛结果下注的投注方式。之所以允许过关串关投注而禁止单场投注，是出于对体育诚信的担忧。当时的想法是，强迫人们下注多场比赛可以防止假球，因为操纵三场比赛比操纵一场比赛更难。随后在 2021 年，规则改变了。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: Parlay bets are a type of wager that require a person to bet on the outcome of several games at once. (Dinging) The reason parlay bets were allowed, but single-game bets were outlawed, was because of concerns around sports integrity. The idea was that being forced to bet on more than one game prevented match-fixing, because it's harder to fix three games rather than one. Then, in 2021, the rules changed.

今天，我提出了一项立法，旨在将加拿大单项体育赛事博彩非刑事化。

英文原文 Today, I introduced legislation to decriminalize single sport betting in Canada.

LUCAS: 加拿大政府修订了《刑法典》（Criminal Code），使各省首次能够提供合法的单场投注。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: The Canadian government amended the Criminal Code so provinces could offer legal single-game betting for the first time.

这项立法的目标很简单，就是将一种普遍存在的做法从暗处带到明处。

英文原文 The goal of the legislation is simple. It is to bring a common practice out of the shadows and into the open.

LUCAS: 但这还不是全部。虽然安大略省早在 2015 年就推出了一个名为 Play OLG 的政府运营网站，用于在线赌场和彩票游戏，但 2021 年对《刑法典》的修订现在将允许该省也将体育博彩搬到网上。因此，在单场投注合法化后的几天内，安大略省就推出了一个名为 Proline+ 的政府运营体育博彩平台。然后，在 2022 年，该省又向前迈出了一步。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: But that's not all. While Ontario already had a government-run website for online casino and lottery games called Play OLG, that launched in 2015, the 2021 Amendment to the Criminal Code would now allow the province to also bring sports betting online. So, within days of single-game betting becoming legal, Ontario launched a government-run sports betting platform called Proline+. Then, in 2022, the province took things one step further.

安大略省发生的事情是创建了这个 iGaming Ontario 框架。它是安大略省酒精和博彩委员会（Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario）的分支机构，它创造了一种不再是政府垄断的局面。它也不是寡头垄断。它是一种自由市场局面，在这种局面下，安大略省的监管机构——即 iGaming Ontario——会接受来自各家第三方营利性公司的申请，这些公司随后将获得合法化或牌照，可以在线上（而不必亲自到场）运营体育博彩和/或赌场博彩。

英文原文 What happened in Ontario was the creation of this iGaming Ontario framework. It's an offshoot of the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario, and it created a circumstance in which it wasn't a government monopoly anymore. It wasn't an oligopoly either. It was a free market circumstance in which the Ontario regulator, so iGaming Ontario, would accept applications from various third-party for-profit corporations who would be then legalized or licensed to operate a sports book and-or a casino book virtually, not necessarily in person.

这是全国首个面向私营公司的在线博彩市场，也是 19 岁及以上的安大略省居民首次能够合法、便捷且轻松地接触到在线体育博彩。

英文原文 This was the first online gaming market for private companies in the country, and for the first time online sports betting became legally, readily, and easily accessible for Ontarians 19 and older.

开放私营市场背后的经济考量与市场爆发

那么，为什么联邦政府和安大略省政府要做出这些改变呢？答案是：钱。以税收和其他收入的形式。支持单场投注合法化的联邦国会议员（MPs）认为，加拿大人在灰色和黑市上花费了估计 140 亿加元（$14 billion dollars），这些钱一直在逃避税收。想象一下，如果他们能分得这 140 亿美元（$14 billion）中的一部分，他们能做些什么。安大略省政府也提出了类似的论点。他们知道安大略省人喜欢赌博，他们希望创造一个机会来利用这一点，并确保这些税收能够回到安大略省的公共金库中。

英文原文 So, why did the federal and Ontario government make these changes? The answer? Money. In the form of taxes and other revenue. Federal MPs who supported legalizing single-game betting argued that Canadians were spending an estimated $14 billion dollars on grey and black markets, money that was dodging taxation. Imagine now what they could do with their share of $14 billion. The Ontario government made a similar argument. They knew that Ontarians love to gamble and they wanted to create an opportunity to capitalize on that, and ensure that that tax revenue was coming back into the Ontario public coffer.

就赌博活动而言，安大略省是北美排名前五的司法管辖区。所以，如果你想到前五名，你谈论的是像新泽西州、纽约州、科罗拉多州这样的地方，而安大略省就在同一个行列中。安大略省一经向私营公司敞开大门，市场就爆发了。

英文原文 Ontario is a top-five jurisdiction in North America when it comes to gambling activities. And so, if you think about the top five, you're talking about places like New Jersey, New York, Colorado, and Ontario is in that same conversation. As soon as Ontario opened the doors to private companies, the market exploded.

MICHAEL: 最终发生的情况是，安大略省政府通过其新方案创造了很低的准入门槛：牌照申请费每年为 10 万美元（$100,000），而税率只有 20%。20% 的税率，你知道，算是处于中等水平。因此，许多行业巨头——比如 FanDuel、DraftKings 和 BetMGM 这些在全球运营的大公司——非常迅速地进入了安大略省市场。

英文原文 **MICHAEL**: What ends up happening is the Ontario government creates, with its new scheme, a low barrier of entry, licensing is $100,000 application fee per year, and the tax rate is only 20%. And so, 20%, you know, kind of in the middle. And so, you had a lot of big fish like the FanDuels and the DraftKings and the BetMGMs, the big corporations that operate globally, enter the Ontario market very rapidly.

截至 2026 年 9 月，安大略省拥有超过 80 个持牌赌博网站或应用程序，其中超过 30 个用于体育博彩。在 iGaming 的第一个财年，安大略省居民在这些新推出的网站和应用程序上投注了 355 亿美元（$35.5 billion）。仅仅几年后，在 24/25 财年，这一数字翻了一倍多，安大略省居民的投注额达到了 827 亿美元（$82.7 billion）。所有这些投注额并不是凭空增长的。首先，那些私营公司必须吸引安大略省居民的注意力。因此，无论是在社交媒体、播客、地铁还是电视上，感觉体育博彩广告一夜之间开始随处可见。

英文原文 As of September, 2026, Ontario had more than 80 licensed gambling websites or apps, and over 30 of those were for sports betting. In iGaming's first fiscal year, Ontarians wagered $35.5 billion on these newly available websites and apps. Just a few years later, in fiscal 24/25, that number more than doubled with Ontarians wagering $82.7 billion. All this wagering didn't increase in a vacuum. First, those private companies had to capture Ontarians' attention. So, whether it was on social media, podcasts, the subway, or TV, it felt like ads for sports betting started popping up everywhere overnight.

MICHAEL: 安大略省的终端消费者之所以觉得广告很多，其中一个原因是——从无形或主观上讲——与我们以前完全没有广告的旧体制相比，现在的确很多。另一方面，对广告的监管确实非常有限。我们没有严格的广告监管，因为没有人真正愿意去管这件事。安大略省政府不想限制其自由市场，加拿大广播电视和电信委员会（CRTC）不想限制媒体运营商，而媒体运营商也不想放弃广告收入。于是，不同的利益相关方都在说：“这不是我们的工作，这是别人的工作，该由别人来解决这个问题。”

英文原文 **MICHAEL**: One of the reasons why the end consumer here in Ontario feels like it's a lot, is because, like, intangibly are subjectively, is because it is a lot when you compare it to the previous system where we had zero. (Crickets chirping) And the other part of this is there are really limited regulations on the advertisements. We don't have stringent advertising regulations, because no one really wants to handle that. The Ontario government doesn't want to constrain its free market, the CRTC doesn't want to constrain the media operators, and the media operators don't want to not take advertising revenue. And so, you've got different stakeholders saying, "It's not our job, it's someone else's job to solve this."

广告轰炸、年轻男性群体与赌博危害

LUCAS: 一项对 2023 年在安大略省电视上播出的七场体育赛事转播的分析发现，与赌博相关的信息——包括部分博彩公司的标志或品牌宣传——占到了总转播时长的五分之一以上。而体育博彩对一个特定人群构成了“完美风暴”……那就是年轻男性。年轻男性是体育的核心受众，他们也是加拿大体育博彩者中规模最大的人群。一项由加拿大卫生研究院（Canadian institutes of Health Research）和阿尔伯塔赌博研究所（Alberta Gambling Research Institute）资助的研究发现，60% 的年轻男性表示体育博彩广告正在产生预期效果。年轻男性一直是一个赌博参与率较高的人群，并且经历过更高比例的赌博危害。所以，我认为这些政策变化在某种程度上加剧了本已存在的潜在风险。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: An analysis of seven sports broadcasts that aired on TV in Ontario in 2023 found that gambling related messages, including logos or branding of some of the betting companies, made up just over a fifth of the total coverage time and sports betting presents a perfect storm for one specific demographic... young men. Young men are a core sports audience, and they also represent the largest demographic of sports betters in Canada. A study funded by the Canadian institutes of Health Research and Alberta Gambling Research Institute found that 60% of young men said that sports betting advertisements are having the intended effect. Young men are a demographic that has always had higher rates of gambling participation, and have experienced higher rates of gambling harms. So, I think the policy changes have kind of intensified an underlying risk.

LUCAS: 2026 年一项针对安大略省 24 小时心理健康与成瘾求助热线的研究，考察了 2012 年至 2025 年期间与赌博相关的求助联系次数。该研究估计，与未发生私有化的情景相比，到 2025 年，与赌博相关的求助热线联系次数几乎翻了三倍。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: A 2026 study of Ontario's, 24-hour mental health and addictions helpline looked at the number of gambling-related contacts between 2012 and 2025. A study estimated that gambling-related helpline contacts nearly tripled by 2025, compared to a scenario where privatization hadn't happened.

RYAN: 我们还做了另一项研究，考察急诊科（emergency department）就诊记录，其中就诊原因部分或全部归因于赌博。

英文原文 **RYAN**: We also did another study looking at emergency department visits where the reason for the visit was either partially or wholly attributable to gambling.

LUCAS: 他们估计，在私营在线赌博公司获准进入该省后，到 2025 年 6 月，与赌博相关的急诊室（ER）就诊人数与该省未做出改变的情况相比几乎翻了一倍。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: They estimated that ER visits related to gambling nearly doubled by June 2025 after private online gambling companies were allowed into the province, compared to if the province hadn't made the change.

RYAN: 这些人代表了处于极度痛苦中的人群。他们的赌博已经到了极其严重的地步，或者与心理健康挑战或药物使用交织在一起，以至于他们最终进了急诊室。我确实担心这也许只是冰山一角。可能有一大批人正在经历各种伤害，而这些伤害并未在这份特定数据中体现出来。

英文原文 **RYAN**: These represent people in very acute distress. Their gambling has gotten to a point where it's so severe or has become wrapped up with a mental health challenge or substance use that they are ending up in the emergency department. I do worry that this is perhaps just the tip of the iceberg. There may be a large group of people experiencing a range of harms that are not represented in this particular data.

任何形式赌博的风险都在于它可能演变成成瘾，而在这种情况下，用户只需动动手指，就能在手机或电脑上直接访问体育博彩应用程序和网站。当你可以在手机上或电脑上每天 24 小时随时在线访问它时，你根本无法摆脱它。当你产生那种渴望时，你更有可能付诸行动，因为这太容易做到了。而赌博成瘾会对人们的财务生活、社交生活以及心理健康产生深远且毁灭性的后果。赌博会带来一系列后果，大多是由经济损失间接引起的，但也会出现很多心理健康问题、抑郁症以及人际关系问题。当他们变得越来越绝望时，他们可能会把自杀视为唯一的出路。

英文原文 The risk of gambling of any kind is that it can turn into an addiction, and in this case, users have direct access to sports betting apps and websites right in the palm of their hands. When you have it online available on your phone or in your-- on your computer 24 hours a day, it's impossible to get away from that. When you get those cravings, you're more likely to act upon it, because it's easier to do. And gambling addictions can have far-reaching and devastating consequences on people's financial lives, their social lives, and their mental health. There are a number of consequences that follow from gambling, mostly indirectly caused by the financial losses, but there's a lot of mental health problems, depression, relationship problems. As they become more and more desperate, they may see suicide as the only way out.

税收对比与广告监管的博弈

既然有这些危害的证据，为什么还要允许人们接触在线赌博呢？让我们回到前面谈到过的事情：安大略省政府当初向私营公司敞开大门的原因。

英文原文 With evidence of this harm, why allow access to online gambling at all? Let's go back to something we talked about earlier. The Ontario government's reason for opening the door for private companies in the first place.

在线赌博将创造巨大的经济发展机遇。它现在受到了监管——它一直在安大略省发生着，而现在我们将对它进行监管，从而为每个人创造一个更安全的环境，而且它本身就是一个巨大的经济发展驱动力。

英文原文 There will be a huge economic development opportunity created from the online gambling. It's now regulated, it's been happening here in Ontario, and now we'll have it regulated so it's a safer environment for everybody, and it's just a huge economic development driver.

LUCAS: iGaming Ontario 从持牌在线赌博应用程序和网站的总博彩收入中抽取 20%。在 2024/2025 财年，私营公司的总博彩收入达到了 29 亿美元（$2.9 billion dollars）。安大略省的分成约为 5.741 亿美元（$574.1 million），其中一部分流向联邦政府、原住民（First Nations）以及用于运营支出。这略低于该省当年总收入的 0.1%，但与其他地方相比，20% 的税率算是低的。例如，纽约州对移动体育博彩征收 51% 的税。英国则将其在线赌博税率从 25% 提高到了 40%。值得注意的是，赌博已经发生了从线下到线上的结构性转变，与此同时，赌博相关危害的证据也变得越来越清晰。英国也对广告进行了打击。

英文原文 **LUCAS**: iGaming Ontario takes 20% of the gross gaming revenues that come from licensed online gambling apps and websites. In the 2024/2025 fiscal year, private companies saw $2.9 billion dollars in gross gaming revenue. Ontario's cut came to about $574.1 million, some of which goes to the feds, First Nations, and towards operating expenses. That's a little less than 0.1% of the province's total revenue for that year, but that 20% in taxes is low compared to other places. New York state, for example, charges a 51% tax on mobile sports wagering. The UK raised its tax rate on online gambling from 25% to 40%. Notably, there's been a structural shift from in person to online gambling, and at the same time, evidence of gambling related harms has become even more clear. The UK has also cracked down on ads.

MICHAEL: 在很长一段时间里，体育博彩和赌博公司可以赞助足球队，并把它们的标志印在球衣上；这种情况在过去一年里已经消失了，但在很长一段时间里，那是常态。在加拿大，广告正是研究人员和政治家最担心的领域。我希望看到对广告的内容以及播出时间施加更多限制。减少青少年在比赛期间看电视时的广告，这样他们就不会像现在这样，把体育运动与赌博如此强烈地联系在一起。

英文原文 **MICHAEL**: For a long period of time, sports betting and gambling companies were able to sponsor soccer teams and have their logos on their jerseys, and that has since gone away over the last year, but for a long period of time, that was the norm. Here in Canada ads are the area researchers and politicians are most concerned about. I'd like to see more limits placed on what advertisements are conducted and when they are shown. Reduction of advertisements when youth are watching TV during games so that they don't come to associate sports with gambling as strongly as is currently being done.

安大略省已经做出了一些改变。2024 年，政府禁止在在线赌博商业广告中使用运动员，但批评人士表示，这还远远不够……

英文原文 Ontario has made some changes. In 2024 the government banned the use of athletes in commercials for online gambling, but critics said, this didn't go far enough...

我今天很荣幸能在这里就第 107 号法案（Bill 107）——《2026 年制止有害赌博广告法》（The Stop Harmful Gambling Advertising Act 2026）发言。

英文原文 I'm honoured to be here today to speak to Bill 107. The Stop Harmful Gambling Advertising Act 2026.

……提出一项法案，旨在全面禁止该省在线赌博的广告和宣传。结束正在助长该省赌博应用程序成瘾的铺天盖地的在线赌博广告。

英文原文 ...proposing a bill that would place an outright ban on advertising and promotion of online gambling in the province. Bringing an end to the flood of online gambling advertisements that are supercharging gambling app addiction in this province.
