开场：亏损数字与类比

OpenAI 在 2026 年第一季度每产生 1 美元营收，就亏损 1.22 美元。公司预计全年亏损 140 亿美元，大约是 2025 年的三倍糟。从 2023 年到 2028 年底，OpenAI 预计将亏损 440 亿美元。到 2029 年的累计亏损预计为 1150 亿美元，之后公司预计才会盈利。作为参照，曼哈顿计划在今天的美元计价下大约花费 30 美元（原文如此）。阿波罗计划把人送上月球并带回来，13 年花费 2880 亿美元。OpenAI 到 2029 年的预计烧钱规模与把宇航员送上真正的月球在同一个量级，只不过这一次，没人完全确定月亮是不是真的在那儿。老实说，如果尼尔·阿姆斯特朗对 NASA 说："嘿，伙计们，我在月球表面每走一步，就要多花我们 1.22 美元。"他们大概会直接把他留在俄亥俄老家。

英文原文 Open AI lost $1.22 for every dollar of revenue it generated in the first quarter of 2026. The company projects a $14 billion loss for the full year, which is roughly three times worse than 2025. Over the period from 2023 through to the end of 2028, Open AI expects to lose $44 billion. Cumulative losses through 2029 are projected at $115 billion before the company expects to become profitable. For context, the Manhattan Project cost roughly $30 in today's dollars. The Apollo program, sending human beings to the moon and bringing them back here, cost $288 billion over 13 years. Open AI's projected burn through 2029 is in the same neighborhood as sending astronauts to the actual moon, except in this case, nobody is entirely sure whether the moon is really there. And honestly, if Neil Armstrong had told NASA, "Hey guys, every step I take on a lunar surface is going to cost us an extra $1.22."

They probably would have just left him back in Ohio.

我的立场：既不支持也不反对 AI

在深入细节之前，我想先回应一件事，因为很多人一直问我："你是支持 AI 还是反对 AI？"每次我的回答都完全一样：我两者都不是，我也不必是。这样提问就像问你是支持火还是反对火。火可以给你的家取暖，也可以把它烧掉。技术只是技术，重要的是它如何部署、谁受益，以及有没有人在划火柴之前费心想过后果。

AI，包括大语言模型，在人类的某些领域有巨大的应用价值——研究、医疗、无障碍、某些创意和分析工作流——而在其他领域则没那么多。说你支持 AI 或反对 AI，坦率地说是一个不完整的想法，它跳过了每一个真正重要的问题：哪种部署、为了谁、代价是什么、有什么保障措施、谁受益？这些才是值得问的问题。这个频道就是为了提出这些问题，去看数据，去面对现实本来的样子，而不是某个随机 CEO 在网上或公开场合说的话，好让他们从投资者那里吸引更多的钱。炒作是希望的一种形式，而希望是非常美好的东西，我绝不会说任何反对希望的话，但希望不是战略；没有数据、没有以实际发生的事情为根基的希望，那不过是一场带着融资演示文稿的白日梦，不是吗？这个频道就是为了从那场白日梦中醒来，看看什么才是真实的，有趣的是，这恰恰是 Sam Altman 曾经说 OpenAI 会做的事。"像对待成年人一样对待成年人"是他的原话，对吧？我们稍后会回到这一点。

英文原文 Before I get into all of this detail, I want to address something because a lot of people keep asking me, "Are you pro AI or are you anti-AI?" And every time my answer is exactly the same. I'm neither. I don't have to be. Framing it that way is like asking whether you're pro or anti-fire. Fire heats your home, fire burns it down. The technology is just the technology. What matters is how it's deployed, who benefits, and whether anyone bothered to think through the consequences before striking the match.

AI, including large language models, has tremendous applications in some areas of humanity, research, medicine, accessibility, certain creative and analytical workflows, and not so much in other areas. To say that you're pro AI or anti-AI is frankly an incomplete thought. It skips over every question that actually matters. Which deployment, for whom, at what cost, with what safeguards, and who benefits? Those are the questions worth asking. This channel exists to ask them, to look at the data, to engage with what reality actually is, not what some random CEO says on the internet or in a public forum so that they can attract more money from investors. Hype is a form of hope, and hope is a very beautiful thing. I would never say anything against hope, but hope is not strategy, and hope without data, without grounding it in what is actually happening, well, it's just a daydream with a pitch deck, isn't it? This channel exists to wake up from that daydream and look at what's actually real, which funny enough is exactly what Sam Altman once said OpenAI would do. Treat adults like adults was his phrase, right? We're going to come back to that.

OpenAI 的实际数字与成本

那么，来看实际数字。Sam Altman 预计营收到 2029 年将达到 1000 亿美元，盈利随后不久就会到来。增长是真实的：年化营收 250 亿美元，每周活跃用户 8 亿，是地球上最容易被认出的 AI 品牌。但成本同样是真实的。算力——也就是为以 ChatGPT 这种规模运行模型而建设的原始基础设施——是亏损比率的主要推手，而且它不会迅速或轻易地压缩下来。

每一个用户的服务都要花钱，用户越多，亏的钱越多，因为每用户收入还没有赶上每用户成本。

英文原文 So, the actual numbers. Sam Altman expects revenue to reach a hundred billion dollars by 2029 and profitability to follow shortly after. The growth is real. 25 billion dollars in annualized revenue, 800 million weekly active users, the most recognizable AI brand on Earth, but the costs are also real. Compute, which is the raw infrastructure built for running models at the scale of chat GPT demands, is the primary driver of the loss ratio and it does not compress quickly or easily.

Every single user costs money to serve and the more users you have, the more money you lose because revenue per user has not yet caught up with cost per user.

Altman 本人最近在一次企业场景的演讲中承认，公司们开始抱怨定价，而这在以前从未出现过。他几乎是顺带一提，然后就继续往下讲了，对于可能是他整个推介中最关乎生死的一句话，这种处理方式相当惊人。当一家试图以万亿美元估值 IPO 的公司的 CEO 随口提到客户第一次抱怨成本，然后并没有真正回应，没有给出计划，没有解释经济性如何改善，这不是一个小细节。这是以一句闲话的方式抛出的万亿美元问题。

英文原文 Altman himself recently acknowledged during an enterprise presentation that companies are starting to complain about pricing and that this never came up before. He said it almost in passing and then kind of moved on, which is a striking way to handle what might be the most existential sentence in his entire pitch. When the CEO of the company trying to IPO at a trillion dollars casually mentions that customers are complaining about cost for the first time and then doesn't really address it, doesn't offer a plan, doesn't explain how the economics improve, that is not a minor detail. That is the one trillion dollar question delivered as an aside.

IPO：庆典还是下一轮融资

现在 OpenAI 正准备上市。高盛和摩根士丹利在负责路演，这在金融上相当于雇了两个非常昂贵的房产中介，去卖一栋严格来说正在着火的房子。申报最早可能在今年秋天提交，挂牌描述大概会写："迷人的高能科技资产，开放式布局，现金流熊熊燃烧，结构性烈火带来宏大的自然采光，爱 GPU 者必看。"

英文原文 And now open AI is preparing to go public. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are running the roadshow, which is the financial equivalent of hiring two very expensive estate agents to sell a house that is technically on fire. The filing could come as early as this autumn and the listing description will probably say something like charming high energy tech asset with open plan burning cash flow, grand natural light from the structural inferno, must love GPUs.

不过，通常 IPO 是一件美好的事。你去证券交易所，敲钟，开香槟，人们鼓掌，每个人都高兴。它代表一个巨大的里程碑：一家公司成长了、稳定了，并邀请公众参与分享它的成功。公平地说，2026 年亏损公司上市并不罕见。自 1980 年代以来，亏损公司上市的比例已从大约 20% 上升到约 80%。Uber 上市时不盈利，Lyft 不盈利，Peloton 不盈利。其中一些成功了，一些现在成了很贵的衣架。Amazon 众所周知亏了好几年钱，然后成为历史上最有价值的公司之一。但"我们在投资于增长，盈利会随着规模到来"，和"我们每赚 1 美元就亏 1.22 美元，我们预计在盈利之前有 1150 亿美元的累计亏损，而来自软银、沙特资金和微软的私人资本已基本投入完毕"，这两者之间是有区别的。

英文原文 And look, normally an IPO is a beautiful thing. You go to the stock exchange, you ring the bell, you pop the champagne, people applaud, everyone's happy. It represents a huge milestone. A company that has grown, stabilized, and is inviting the public to participate in its success. And to be fair, unprofitable IPOs are not exactly unusual in 2026. Since the 1980s, the proportion of loss-making companies going public has risen from roughly 20% to about 80%. Uber was unprofitable at IPO, Lyft was unprofitable, Peloton was unprofitable. Some of those worked out, some of those are now very expensive coat hangers. Amazon famously lost money for years before becoming one of the most valuable companies in history, but there is a difference between we are investing in growth and profitability will come as we scale, and we'll lose $1.22 for every dollar we make. We project $115

billion in cumulative losses before we turn a profit, and the private capital from SoftBank, the Saudi money, and Microsoft has been largely committed.

你在这场路演中会反复听到的 Amazon 对比，有启发性，但并不完整。Amazon 早期的亏损是一种刻意的策略，把利润率再投入到基础设施、物流和市场份额中。那些亏损是一种选择，Amazon 本可以更早盈利，但选择不这样做。OpenAI 的亏损是结构性的。Amazon 选择亏钱，OpenAI 亏钱的方式则像我们其他人丢袜子一样——非情愿的，并且没有明确的挽回计划。只不过当我丢了一只袜子，并不需要一座专门的核电站来为它买单。算力成本——电力、GPU、为服务 8 亿周用户所需的数据中心——对他们来说不是一种选择，而是产品得以存在的成本。每次有人使用 ChatGPT，OpenAI 都要花钱。而且与 Amazon 的物流网络不同，那里的单位经济性随规模改善，并不能保证 AI 推理成本会以弥合这一差距所需的速度压缩。

英文原文 The Amazon comparison, which you will hear repeatedly during this road show, is instructive but incomplete. Amazon's early losses were a deliberate strategy, reinvesting profit margins into infrastructure, logistics, and market share. The losses were a choice. Amazon could have been profitable earlier and chose not to be. OpenAI's losses are structural. Amazon chose to lose money, OpenAI loses money the way the rest of us lose socks, involuntarily and with no clear plan for recovery. Except when I lose a sock, it doesn't require its own dedicated nuclear power plant to fund it. Compute costs, the electricity, the GPUs, the data centers required to serve 800 million weekly users are not a choice for them. They are the cost of the product existing. Every time somebody uses ChatGPT, it costs OpenAI money. And unlike Amazon's logistics network, where unit economics improved with scale, there is no guarantee that AI inference costs compress at the rate needed to close this gap.

OpenAI 的 IPO 看起来并不是一场庆典，而是下一轮融资。私人资金已经花掉了，公众资金是下一个资本来源。我并不是在愤世嫉俗，这只是这种规模的创业公司在耗尽私人选项时的运作方式。但这意味着公众投资者被邀请去填补 OpenAI 现状与它所说三到四年后将达到的状态之间的缺口。那是对一条时间线的押注。如果时间线滑坡——这个行业的时间线有记录在案的滑坡倾向——公众股东就要承担成本，当你把主权财富基金和软银都用完时，风险转移正是这样运作的。相比之下，Anthropic 预测把现金消耗降到 2026 年营收的大约 1 trillion（原文如此），到 2027 年降到 9%。OpenAI 预计其烧钱率到 2027 年都将保持在 57%。

英文原文 The OpenAI IPO doesn't seem to be a celebration. It is the next funding round. The private money is spent. The public money is the next source of capital. I'm not being cynical. It is just how startups at this scale work when they exhaust private options. But it means that public investors are being invited to fund the gap between where OpenAI is and where it says it will be in 3 to 4 years. That is a bet on a timeline. And if the timeline slips, as timelines in this industry have a documented tendency to do, public shareholders bear the cost, which is exactly how risk transfer works when you run out of sovereign wealth funds and SoftBank. Anthropic, by comparison, forecasts dropping its cash burn to roughly 1 trillion of revenue in 2026 and down to 9% by 2027. OpenAI expects its burn rate to remain at 57% through 2027.

这两家最有价值的 AI 创业公司走在非常不同的轨迹上，而市场却被要求把它们定价得好像都会抵达同一个终点。

所以，三个与 AI 相关的重大 IPO 即将到来：OpenAI、SpaceX 和 Anthropic。如果你希望我对这三者以及它们的意义做一期专门的拆解，请在下方评论告诉我。

在这三者中，OpenAI 是最受瞩目的案例，但成本危机远远超出一家公司。我认为这是整个故事里对理解这个行业的真实处境与它自称的处境之间的差距最重要的部分。

英文原文 The two most valuable AI startups are on very different trajectories, and the market is being asked to price them both as though they will arrive at the same destination.

So three major AI-adjacent IPOs are on the horizon: OpenAI, SpaceX, and Anthropic. If you'd like me to do a dedicated breakdown of all three and what they mean, let me know in the comments below.

OpenAI, out of these three, is the highest-profile case, but the cost crisis extends far beyond one company. And this is the part of the story I think matters most for understanding where the industry actually is versus where it says it is.

企业的成本危机：Uber 等

Uber 在 4 月中旬、仅仅 4 个月就用完了它整个 2026 年的 AI 预算。该公司向大约 5000 名工程师推出了 AI 编码工具，建立了一个按 AI 总使用量给团队排名的排行榜，并鼓励员工尽可能多地使用 AI。烧钱最多的团队排名第一。想象一下公司的 Slack 频道："祝贺后端团队在周二下午之前花掉了相当于一个小国 GDP 的钱，这是你们的星巴克礼品卡。"CTO Praveen Neppalli Naga 做了一场 2 小时的内部演示，花掉了价值 1200 美元的 token，而这场演示讲的是 AI 将如何降低成本。所以，演示降低成本的成本超过了不用 AI 做这件事的成本，这种讽刺自己就能写出来，然后还向你收取 1200 美元的特权费。COO Andrew Macdonald 后来把这些数字形容为"脑袋炸裂的时刻"。他的公开评估非常直白："很难在 AI 使用量增加与向客户交付有意义的新功能之间画出一条线。"排行榜此后被倒转了。Uber 现在把每位工程师限制在每月 1500 美元，曾经奖励最大使用量的指标，现在奖励最小使用量，这基本上相当于企业里给某人颁发吃蛋糕最多奖，然后立刻让他节食。这是工程版的"停，什么都别碰，谁都别看那个 AI，你要是敢想一个提示词，你就被开除"。当你为激励采用而建的东西变成你为限制采用而建的东西，这是值得关注的信号。

英文原文 Uber blew through its entire 2026 AI budget by mid-April, just 4 months. The company had rolled out AI coding tools to roughly 5,000 engineers, created an entire leaderboard ranking teams by total AI usage, and encouraged employees to use AI as much as possible. The team that burned the most money was ranked number one. Just imagine the company's Slack channel. Congratulations to the back-end team for spending a small nation's GDP by Tuesday afternoon.

Here's your Starbucks gift card. The CTO, Praveen Neppalli Naga, ran a 2-hour internal demo that cost $1,200 in tokens, a demo about how AI would actually reduce costs. So, the cost of demonstrating cost reduction exceeded the cost of doing it without AI, which is the kind of irony that writes itself and then charges you $1,200 for the privilege. The COO, Andrew Macdonald, later described the numbers as a head-exploding moment. His public assessment was very blunt. It's very hard to draw a line between increased AI usage and the delivery of meaningful new features to customers. The leaderboard has since been inverted. Uber now caps each engineer at $1,500 per month, and the metric that once rewarded maximum usage now rewards minimum usage, which is basically the corporate equivalent of giving somebody an award for eating the most cake and then immediately putting them on a harsh diet. It's the engineering equivalent of stop, don't touch anything. Nobody look at the AI. If you even think about a prompt, you're fired. When the thing you built to incentivize adoption becomes the thing you built to restrain it, that is a signal worth paying attention to.

Uber 的 CEO 指出，公司大约 10% 的代码现在是 AI 生成的，公司因此正在放缓招聘节奏。就业这一面与成本这一面并不是分开的，它们完全是同一个故事。Walmart 建了一个内部 AI 编码代理，据报道算了算它花了多少钱，然后缩减了这个项目。Microsoft 取消了大部分直接的 cloud code 许可——顺便说一句，这家公司对 OpenAI 有数十亿美元的投资——却让自己的工程师停止使用它所资助的 AI 工具之一。GitHub Copilot 从固定订阅改为按 token 计费，一些客户的成本增加了一百倍。不是百分之一百，是一百倍，这是"这比我预想的多"和"我需要坐下来"之间的区别，是查看晚餐账单和意识到自己不小心买下了整家餐厅之间的区别。

英文原文 Uber CEO has noted that roughly 10% of the company's code is now AI-generated, and the company is moderating its hiring pace as a result. The jobs angle is not separate from the cost angle. They are exactly the same story. Walmart built an internal AI coding agent, reportedly ran the numbers on what it was costing, and scaled the program back. Microsoft canceled most of its direct cloud code licenses, and this is a company with a multi-billion dollar investment in OpenAI, by the way, telling its own engineers to stop using one of the AI tools it's funding. GitHub Copilot moved to token-based billing rather than a flat subscription, and some customers saw their costs increase a hundredfold. Not a hundred percent, a hundred times, which, in case you were wondering, is the difference between that's more than I expected and I need to sit down. It's the difference between checking your dinner bill and realizing you accidentally bought the restaurant.

Gartner 预测，2026 年 AI 代理软件支出将达到近 2070 亿美元，比 2025 年增长 139%。这个行业花的钱越来越多，对自己得到什么回报却越来越不确定。

AI 领导层对这些成本抱怨的回应很能说明问题。Altman 在 3 月说："我们看到这样一个未来：智能是一种公用事业，就像电或水，人们按表向我们购买。"Nvidia 的 CEO Jensen Huang 则建议，AI token 预算应成为工程师薪酬包的一部分，本质上是提议公司在喂养 AI 上的花费与支付人类的花费一样多。当"太贵了"的解决方案是"把它当成公用事业账单，而且还要开始用你的薪水来付"，经济性就不处于一个健康的位置。

英文原文 Gartner forecasts AI agent software spending will reach nearly 207 billion dollars in 2026, up 139% from 2025. The industry is spending more and more money with less and less certainty about what it's getting back.
