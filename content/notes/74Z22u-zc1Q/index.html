摘要:

本期节目深入探讨了肯塔基州共和党众议员托马斯·马西在初选中面临的严峻挑战。作为一名多次违背特朗普意愿的特立独行者，马西因推动爱泼斯坦文件披露等行为引发特朗普不满。通过对马西与其竞争对手加尔文的竞选活动对比，揭示了当前共和党内“绝对忠诚”与“个人原则”之间的激烈冲突，以及特朗普对党内纪律的强力掌控。

共和党的“复仇”竞赛

Michael Barbaro: 欢迎收听《每日新闻》（The Daily），我是 Michael Barbaro。在肯塔基州，尽管面临着创纪录的低支持率，特朗普总统正要求共和党初选选民拒绝一位他们曾七次选出的共和党国会议员。

Massie is a complete and total disaster as a congressman and frankly as a human being. a fiercely proud conservative who has broken with Trump on just a handful of votes. We got to get rid of this loser. This guy is bad. He's disloyal to the Republican party. He's disloyal to the people of Kentucky and most importantly, he is disloyal to the United States of America and he's got to be voted out of office as soon as possible.

Michael Barbaro: 相反，特朗普希望肯塔基州的共和党人选出他亲手挑选的挑战者艾德·加尔文（Ed Galvin），这已成为美国历史上最昂贵的众议院初选。

Michael Barbaro: Instead, Trump wants Kentucky Republicans to elect his handpicked challenger, Ed Galron, in what has become the most expensive House primary in American history.

Donald Trump: 另一方面，艾德·加尔文得到了我的完全支持。他是一个真正的美国英雄，一个伟大的爱国者。

特立独行的“异类”马西

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Anytime. We have established quite clearly on the show that President Trump has decided to use these midterm primary elections to try to take out his rivals within the Republican party and impose as much discipline on the party as humanly possible. And so far, he's largely succeeded in that goal. And there's one last revenge race that remains. And in your capacity as a chronicler of the Republican party and the fights within it, you decided that that race was the most important or at least the most interesting, which is why I'm talking to you from the place where that race is about to occur, Kentucky. Robert Draper: 很大程度上是因为托马斯·马西，这位肯塔基州第四国会选区的七任现任议员，比特定的所有其他共和党人加起来都更加公然违抗。马西一再违抗特朗普的意愿，而且似乎乐在其中，根本不在乎特朗普想要他做什么。

Repeatedly, Massie has defied Trump's wishes and has seemed cheerfully uncaring about what Trump wants him to do.

Michael Barbaro: 对吧？他在推行“走自己的路”这一品牌时，没有任何细微差别或保留。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Right? There's nothing nuance or hedging about his brand of I'm doing my own thing here.

Robert Draper: 一点也没有。结果，特朗普支持了一名对手来反对他，并鼓励共和党大捐助者支持那名对手。

Original English

Robert Draper: Not in the least. No. And as a result of this, Trump has endorsed an opponent against him and is encouraging big Republican donors to back that opponent.

Michael Barbaro: 再多告诉我们一些关于那个对手的情况。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: And tell us more about that opponent.

Robert Draper: 当然。特朗普亲手挑选来对抗马西的人是艾德·加尔文，他是一名奶农、海豹突击队成员，也是特朗普的坚定支持者，他承诺将站在特朗普一边，帮助推动其议程。马西对此并不陌生，他以前也经历过。2018年，特朗普就想搞垮马西，但没有结果。但这一次，情况要紧迫得多。马西以前经常以 81% 的选票轻松赢得初选，而这一次，民调显示两人平分秋色。

Original English

Robert Draper: Sure. the man that Trump has handpicked to oppose Thomas Massie is Ed Galrine, a dairy farmer, Navy Seal, and Trump Stalwart who has pledged to stand alongside Trump and help get his agenda passed. But this time, things were a great deal closer where Massie has comfortably won his primaries sometimes with 81% of the vote. This time around, the polls suggest that the two candidates are dead even.

爱泼斯坦文件：马西的“致命一击”

Michael Barbaro: Robert，请提醒我们，马西议员是如何变得在特朗普面前如此公然违抗的，以及为什么总统觉得他如此难以忍受？

Original English

Michael Barbaro: Well, Robert, remind us how Representative Massie came to be so defiant when it comes to Trump and why the president finds him to be so intolerable.

Robert Draper: 马西一直保持着这种个人主义者的形象，他是一个公认的怪人，一种财政绝对主义者。他坚持个人权利，主张取消所有对外援助，包括对以色列也不例外——而几乎所有其他共和党人都会给以色列留个口子。

Original English

Robert Draper: I think that it's in keeping with the brand that Massie has held up from the outset of his political career as this individualist, a confirmed eccentric, a kind of fiscal absolutist, a person who is for individual rights that he would hold the line on things like no foreign aid and that includes no carveout exemption for Israel,

Michael Barbaro: 所以马西被视为共和党内某些元素的心头好，因为他是个纯粹主义者。

Original English

Original English

Robert Draper: He's opposed Trump's intervention in both Venezuela and Iran, saying they betray the idea of America first. Michael Barbaro: 没错。马西成为了释放爱泼斯坦文件的领军人物。在议长迈克·约翰逊（Mike Johnson）对特朗普百依百顺时，马西却坚持不懈，与民主党人 Ro Khanna 以及几位女性共和党人组成联盟。马西的决心如此之大，以至于特朗普最终不得不察言观色，勉强支持《爱泼斯坦文件透明度法案》。特朗普被迫宣布失败，这显然是他不会忘记也不会原谅马西的一刻。

Original English

Michael Barbaro: That's right. 选民的纠结：特朗普还是马西？

Original English

Robert Draper: Their party leader has said, "I want you to vote against Massie. I want you to vote for Ed Galrine." And now the question is how important is that dictate from a president they so admire. So about a week before the primary Caitlyn O'Keefe and I went to a Massie event in Boone County. It was an extremely well attended event in the courthouse. Standing room only.

Thomas Massie: 如果你在掩盖恋童癖，如果你试图在没有搜查令的情况下监视美国人，如果你在让这个国家破产，那么我不会投赞成票。有些时候我不得不挺身而出，这就是为什么我现在的竞选是全国共和党初选中最昂贵的，也是肯塔基州众议院竞选历史上最昂贵的。

Original English

Robert Draper: 许多观众都对马西非常崇拜，赞扬他的原则和独立性。但也有一些人比较怀疑。有一位名叫 Elizabeth Smith 的女性，她说自己这次很纠结。她过去支持马西，但真的是特朗普的狂热追随者。她问马西：“既然特朗普要我投给别人，请给我一个投票给你的理由。”
