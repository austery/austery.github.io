虚无主义暴力极端主义：定义、成因及应对策略 TVO Today 2026-03-12

领域: society-thinking

分类: politics-society

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-03-12

摘要:

本访谈深入探讨了“虚无主义暴力极端主义”（NVE）这一新兴现象。与传统极端主义不同，NVE缺乏明确的意识形态或目标，其根源在于网络社群和对暴力的迷恋。专家指出，理解其运作模式、区分纯粹的暴力崇拜与意识形态驱动的极端主义是关键挑战。访谈还讨论了社会两极化如何加剧极端主义的污名化，以及当前去激进化、干预和重新融入社会的策略及其局限性，强调了结构性改变和持续资金支持的重要性。

虚无主义暴力极端主义的定义与困惑

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “什么是虚无主义、暴力极端主义，我们又该如何应对？制作人 Eric Bombicino 专访了女王大学副教授 Amarnath Amarasingam，这是他们关于极端主义对话的第二部分。好的，我想谈谈，我认为这是一种新的极端主义形式。我想我们刚才讨论的所有关于理解极端主义的内容，在这里可能都会被推翻。虚无主义、暴力极端主义。那是什么，又为何如此令你感到困惑？”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "What is nihilistic, violent extremism and what can we do to combat it? Producer Eric Bombicino gets insight from Amarnath Amarasingam, an associate professor at Queen's University, in part two of their conversation on extremism.

Okay, so I did want to talk about, I guess it's a new form of extremism. And I think everything we just talked about in terms of understanding extremism might just get punted off the table here. Nihilistic, violent extremism. What is that and why is it, I guess, so confounding for you?"

Amarnath Amarasingam: “我的意思是，问题就在于‘虚无主义’这个词，对吧？从哲学上讲，虚无主义是一种无意义感、无根感、无目的感，而很多极端主义团体恰恰是它的反面，对吧？他们想要建立一个‘白人至上国家’（white ethnostate）或‘哈里发国’（caliphate）之类的。他们在构建某样东西，他们在构建一个‘乌托邦愿景’（utopian vision）。而许多这些人，他们来自……你知道，来自网上的核心社群，他们来自‘真实犯罪社群’（true crime community）。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "I mean, the problem is in the nihilism word, right? Where nihilism, at least philosophically, is a sense of meaninglessness, rootlessness, purposelessness, and a lot of extremist groups are the complete opposite of that, right? They, they want a white ethnostate or they want a caliphate or whatever it is. They're, they're building something. They're building a utopian vision. Whereas a lot of these guys and they come out of, you know, the core communities online, they come out of the true crime community online."

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “那是什么样的社群？”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "So what are those?"

Amarnath Amarasingam: “就是那种在线论坛。是的。人们从中获得很多刺激，我甚至不想称之为社区，但他们消费了很多……他们所消费的是‘血腥内容’（gore content）。比如人们被汽车撞倒，狗被杀死，诸如此类的内容，而他们就坐在那里消费这些内容。‘真实犯罪社群’也是一样，就像……你知道，崇拜‘连环杀手’（serial killer worship）之类的事情，对吧？他们还崇拜‘校园枪手’（school shooter worship），比如像哥伦拜恩（Columbine）枪击案的凶手，人们对他们的那种依恋。所以，从这些社群中就出现了这种被称为‘虚无主义暴力极端主义’（nihilistic violent extremism）的新现象，他们会‘欺凌年幼儿童’（bullying young children）让他们在镜头前伤害自己。所有这些事情都在发生。所以人们不知道该如何解释它，因为它听起来像暴力极端主义，但却没有目的。对吧？所以这就是麻烦所在，人们在纠结的是：这仅仅是为了暴力而暴力吗？英国政府称之为‘暴力迷恋’（violence fixation）。在加拿大，我们称之为‘非虚无主义暴力极端主义’（non nihilistic violent extremism）。所以这只是一种奇怪的痴迷、依恋和对暴力的固着。但这不像‘右翼的种族战争’（race war in the far right），他们认为暴力和混乱中会诞生出美好而荣耀的东西。对他们而言，暴力本身就是目的。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "So it's like there are online boards. Yeah. Where people are getting a lot of their excitement from, I don't even want to say community, but a lot, a lot of what, what they're consuming is gore content. So people getting hit by cars and dogs getting killed and that sort of content, and they're sitting there consuming this content. And the true crime community is the same as like, you know, the serial killer worship and that sort of thing, right? They come out of school shooter worship also like people that kind of attachment people have to the Columbine shooters from way back. So out of these communities is this new phenomenon called nihilistic violent extremism, where they are bullying young children into kind of harming themselves on camera. All of that stuff is happening. And so people don't know how to kind of explain it because it sounds like violent extremism, but there is no purpose to it. Right. And so that's where the trouble is, where that's where people are kind of struggling with is, is this just violence for violence sake? The UK government calls it violence fixation. In Canada, we call it non nihilistic violent extremism. So it's just this kind of weird obsession and attachment and fixation to violence. But it's not like the race war in the far right. We're out of that violence and chaos. Something beautiful and glorious arises for them. It's the violence is the purpose."

“小丑化”的极端主义与目的性缺失

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “这是否像是极端主义的‘小丑化’（Joker-ization）？我的意思是，有些人就是想看到世界燃烧。”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "Is this like the Joker-ization of extremism? Like I'm trying, I'm like, yeah, some people just want to see the world burn."

Amarnath Amarasingam: “‘有些人想看到世界燃烧’（Some people want to see the world burn）是极端主义研究者、政策制定者和执法部门不知道如何处理的事情。好吧。因为一方面，我们习惯于找到一份‘宣言’（manifesto）、一面‘旗帜’（flag）或一本书之类的东西，你读《我的奋斗》（Mein Kampf），找到指向‘意识形态’（ideology）和‘信仰体系’（belief systems）的线索，而这些人只是想摧毁一切。这毫无意义。是的，但他们具有难以想象的暴力性，特别是针对年轻人，非常非常小的年轻人。我们见过最小的案例只有九岁、十岁、十一岁。所以，如何界定这一点很困难，我们现在全国的许多‘预防项目’（prevention programs）都在看到这些案例在他们的案件量中有所增加。是的，有些情况只是他们需要社会工作者、咨询师和治疗师来沟通。但那里没有任何……没有任何意识形态的东西。’”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "So some people want to see the world burn is something that extremism researchers and policymakers and law enforcement don't know what to do with. Okay. Right. Because on the one hand, we're used to finding a manifesto or a flag or a book or something, you know, you read Mein Kampf, something to like point us towards ideology and belief systems, whereas we just want to destroy everything. Doesn't make any sense. Yeah, but they're unimaginably violent, especially towards young people, very young people. We've seen cases as young as nine and ten and 11. And so where to place that is difficult in a lot of our prevention programs now across the country are seeing an up tick in these cases as part of their caseload. And yeah, some of it is just they just need social workers and counsellors and therapy and talk things through. But there is no there's no ideological anything there."

从过渡期到纯粹破坏：动机的演变

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “这最终会导向某种‘信仰体系’，某种你可以称之为‘意识形态’的东西吗？我们现在处于一个‘过渡期’（transition phase）。”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "Could it lead eventually to some sort of belief system, something you'd call an ideology? We're in like a transition phase."

Amarnath Amarasingam: “它可能是一个过渡期。有些人认为，他们所做的，他们所做的是，你可能会在他们的硬盘上找到一段‘ISIS视频’（ISIS video），你可能会找到一段‘新纳粹视频’（neo-Nazi video）。人们会问：‘为什么，两套相互竞争的意识形态怎么会存在于同一个人的电脑里？’原因在于，他们真正关心的只有视频中的‘暴力’（violence），对吧？他们喜欢暴力部分。他们并不真正关心意识形态部分。所以，这可能是一个向其他东西过渡的阶段，但我们不知道它会是什么样子。但与此同时，这是他们留下的‘破坏痕迹’（trail of destruction）。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "It could be a transition phase. A few people have kind of argued that what they do, what they do is they'll you might find an ISIS video on their hard drive. You might find a neo-Nazi video on their hard drive. And people are like, how come, how can two competing ideologies be a part of the same person's computer? The reason for that is because the only thing they care about in those two videos is the violence, right? They like the violence part of it. They don't really care about the ideological part of it. So it could be a transition to something else, but we don't know what that's going to look like. But in the meantime, it's the trail of destruction that they're leaving behind."

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "So folks are angry at the world. Yeah. They need to get back at it. They need to do something, but nothing means anything. There's no hope. Then I guess all you have is revenge. Does seem like revenge can be its own thing to motivate."

Amarnath Amarasingam: “是的。我甚至不知道这是否是复仇。对他们而言，这仅仅是……他们只是为了‘刺激’（lulls）而做。他们只是为了‘好玩’（kicks）而做，对吧？他们只是因为‘能做到’（they can）而做。而网络环境也允许这种行为。他们只是为了‘博取关注’（clout）而做，对吧？他们只是因为他们是这个怪异、邪恶群体的一员，并且大家都在互相挖苦，看谁能做得更过分，对吧？就像……就像他们在玩《使命召唤》（Call of Duty），只不过是真人版。是的。对吧？然后，年轻人们……联系到你之前关于‘自由主义’（liberalism）的问题，这是否也是‘意义缺失’（meaninglessness）的最终结果？现代社会未能为这些年轻人提供有意义、有目标的东西，导致他们现在以一种非常怪异的方式对这种‘无根感’（rootlessness）做出反应。是的，也许吧。对吧？所以，这可能是……所有这一切的最终结果是：‘我们无事可活’（we have nothing to live for）。这一切都很疯狂。这一切都是混乱，毫无意义……你知道，没有天堂，没有地狱，我们只是……我们只是漂浮在太空中。所以，为什么不尽可能地造成最大的破坏呢？”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "Yeah. I don't even know if this is revenge. Like, for them. For them, it's just. They're just doing it for the lulls. They're just doing it for the kicks, right? They're just doing it because they can. And the online landscape kind of allows for some of this behaviour too. And they're just doing it for, for clout, right? They're just doing it because they're part of this weird, nefarious group and everyone's like digging each other up and seeing who can one up each other, right? It's like a it's like they're all playing Call of Duty except with real people. Yeah. Right. And, and young people tying it back to your liberalism question, like, is this the kind of end point meaninglessness also, right? Is this is this kind of the, the, the failure of modernity to provide these young people with something meaningful and purposeful that they're now reacting very bizarrely to this sense of norm, like rootlessness. Yeah, maybe. Right. And so that might be the end of it. The end result of all of this also is like, we have nothing to live for. This is all crazy anyway. This is all chaos and nothing meaning, you know, there's no heaven and there's no hell and it's all we're just all this and that, right? We're just all floating through space. And so why not cause as utmost, utmost destruction as humanly possible."

“主角情结”与极端主义的吸引力

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “我不知道这是否与虚无主义暴力极端主义有关，但你确实提到了‘主角’（main character）这个概念，我想深入探讨一下。现在是否有越来越多的愤怒的年轻男性，他们在生活中感到被边缘化，他们想成为‘英雄之旅’（hero's journey）中的‘主角’，而英雄之旅最终会带来‘宣泄’（catharsis）和‘救赎’（salvation）？”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "So I don't know if this is related to nihilistic, violent extremism, but you did mention this idea of being a main character, and I wanted to sort of pick up on that. Are there more and more angry young men now that want to be they feel sidelined in their lives. They want to be the main character on a hero's journey. And hero's journey is a thing, and it ends in catharsis and salvation."

Amarnath Amarasingam: “我不知道这是否是虚无主义极端主义的一部分，但我会说，许多其他的意识形态群体，如‘极右翼’（far right）、‘圣战主义者’（jihadist），甚至是‘阴谋论’（conspiracy theories），它们尤其擅长将你塑造成一个‘宇宙战士主角’（cosmic warrior main character）。对吧？比如说，阴谋论经常这样做，好像你现在被赋予了任务，因为你的‘眼睛被打开了’（your eyes have been opened），你已经获得了关于‘世事真相’（what's really going on in the world）的真理。而其他人都在沉睡。你现在有责任唤醒所有人，让他们认识到‘真实现实’（true reality），并引导他们走上一条‘解放人类’（liberating humanity）的道路。所以，许多阴谋论的‘黄金时代’（golden age）不是‘哈里发国’或‘白人至上国家’，而是当每个人都‘觉醒’（wakes up）的时候。对吧？每个人都意识到，一直有一个‘阴影精英’（shadowy elite）在控制着一切。他们从未拥有那么多‘自主权’（agency）。一切似乎都被剥夺或被他人控制。而现在我们‘明白了’（hip to it）。对吧？所以，是的，这确实创造了一种‘深远的目的感’（sense of very deep purpose），去帮助人类前进。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "Yeah, I don't know if that is part of nihilistic extremism, but I would say a lot of the other ideological groups, far right jihadist, even conspiracy theories in particular, they're very good at turning you into a cosmic warrior main character. Right. And so, I mean, conspiracy theories do this a lot where it's like you are now tasked because you've been your eyes have been opened and you've been granted the truth about what's really going on in the world. Everyone else is asleep. You now have a responsibility to wake up everybody to the true reality and get them on a path towards like, liberating humanity. And so the golden age for a lot of these conspiracy theories isn't like a caliphate or white ethnostate, but it's when everyone kind of wakes up, right? Everyone realizes, realizes that, that there's always been this shadowy elite controlling everything. They've never had that much agency. Everything's always been taken away from them or controlled for them. And now we're kind of hip to it. Yeah. Right. And so, yeah, it does create the sense of very deep purpose to kind of help humanity along."

意识形态在极端主义中的作用

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “你提到‘哈里发国’是‘伊斯兰极端分子’（Islamist extremists）的最终目标，‘白人至上国家’是‘右翼极端分子’（right wing extremists）的目标。我记得很久以前，在‘议程’（the agenda）节目中，我们曾访谈过一位‘新纳粹分子’（Neo-Nazi）和一位‘伊斯兰极端分子’，他们谈论了加入的原因，而且原因惊人地相似。那么，‘意识形态’（ideology）在这里是否仍然像以前那么重要，还是说它几乎是次要的？”

Original English

Producer Eric Bombicino: "So you mentioned the caliphate as sort of the end goal for Islamist extremists, the ethnostate for right wing extremists. I remember quite a long time ago on the agenda we had on a X Neo-Nazi and an X Islamist extremist, and they talked about why, why they joined. And it was it was identical. So does the ideology matter as much here or is that almost secondary?"

Amarnath Amarasingam: “‘意识形态’是重要的。所以，当你谈论‘我因为身份认同’（identity）或‘群体认同’（group identity）或‘社区’（community）而加入时，意识形态就介入了，它会说：‘是哪个社区？’对吧？因为如果仅仅是任何社区，我们就可以去参加扑克联赛，对吧？或是‘人类家园’（Habitat for Humanity）。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "The ideology matters for. So when you say like I joined for identity or I joined for group identity or community, the ideology comes in and says, which community. Right. Because if it was just any community, then we can go join a poker league, right?

Habitat for humanity."

Amarnath Amarasingam: “但不行，‘社区’是经过‘隔离’（quarantined）的，它定义了谁是‘内群体’（in-group），谁是‘外群体’（outgroup）。意识形态对于告诉你‘这是什么’（what that is）非常重要。对吧？所以你不是一个普通的‘白人’（white person），而是‘一个骄傲的、被种族化的、‘白人至上民族主义者’（proud, racialized, white nationalist）；或者你不是一个普通的‘穆斯林’（Muslim），而是一个相信非常保守的‘伊斯兰教’（Islam）形式的人，‘逊尼派穆斯林’（Sunni Muslim），你知道。所以它告诉你‘层级’（tiers）是什么，以及你‘属于这里’（you belong here）。这就是意识形态的作用。所以，是的，他们都在追求‘目的’（purpose）、‘归属感’（belonging）和‘意义’（meaning），但意识形态使这些意义和社群变得非常不同。所以，这就是意识形态的作用。它负责‘解释’（explaining），对吧？因为‘社区’可以指任何社区，对吧？但这并不令人满足。对吧？所以你不是一个‘游泳俱乐部’（swim meet）的成员，你是一个‘团体’（group）的成员，对吧？那才真正有意义。”

Original English

Amarnath Amarasingam: "Or habitat for humanity. But no, the community is quarantined in terms of who is the in-group and who is the outgroup. And ideology is important for telling you what that is. Right? Right. So you're a not just a white person, but a proud, racialized, white nationalist, or not just a Muslim, but someone who believes in a very conservative form of Islam, Sunni Muslim, you know. So it tells you what the what the tiers are, and you belong here. That's what ideology does. And so, yes, they're both going after purpose and belonging and meaning, but they ideology makes it very different kinds of meaning and very different communities. So that's what the ideology does. It does the explaining, right? Because community could mean any community, right. But that's not satisfying. Right? Right. And so you're not part of a swim meet, you're part of a, you're part of a group, right? That actually means something."

极化社会中的极端主义标签化

制片人 Eric Bombicino: “我记得你之前提到，有些人可能会认为‘乔·罗根’（Joe Rogan）是‘新纳粹’（Neo-Nazi），我觉得这很奇怪。在关于极端主义的整个讨论中，我们谈到了出现更多极端主义的可能性，我想反其道而行之。随着我们变得越来越‘两极分化’（polarized），你是否看到人们更容易因为政治分歧而给对方贴上‘极端分子’、‘激进分子’的标签？因为我经常听到‘极端分子’这个词被滥用，而这似乎仅仅是人们在进行一场普通的‘政治辩论’（political debate）。”

Original English
