调查结果令人震惊。埃拉收集并分析了数十名快艇驾驶员的数据。数据显示，超过 70% 至 80% 的快艇驾驶员长期遭受严重的脑震荡后综合征症状：严重偏头痛、恶心眩晕、认知迟钝、视力模糊以及严重的焦虑和抑郁。而在非快艇驾驶员的对照组中，这些症状的发生率极低。

更关键的是，埃拉发现这些症状与他们在快艇上的累计驾驶小时数呈现出高度的正相关。开船时间越长、在恶劣海况中颠簸越多的人，大脑受损的症状就越严重。

Original English

Dave Phillips: Using her father's connections in the tight-knit surfman community, she designed a detailed epidemiological survey. She surveyed active-duty and retired surfmen, and set up a control group of Coast Guard members who worked desk jobs or on larger cutters.

The results were staggering. Ella gathered data from dozens of boat operators. The data showed that over 70% to 80% of these surfmen suffered from classic post-concussion syndrome symptoms: chronic migraines, vertigo, cognitive fog, vision problems, and depression. In contrast, the control group showed negligible rates of these issues.

Crucially, Ella showed a direct correlation between the number of hours spent driving small rescue boats in rough seas and the severity of neurological symptoms.

Zolan Kanno-Youngs: A high school student produced clear epidemiological evidence of occupational brain injury. How did the Coast Guard leadership react?

最初，官方反应迟缓，甚至试图淡化这一发现。但随着这份报告在整个警卫队内部疯狂传阅，越来越多的现役和退役士兵站出来发声，表示自己就是调查中所描述的受害者。与此同时，独立生物力学研究机构通过在船体和头盔上加装传感器，证实了快艇在波浪撞击时施加在人体大脑上的巨大 G 力。

Original English

Dave Phillips: Ella won top honors at the science fair, but she and her father didn't stop there. They sent the study directly to the leadership of the Coast Guard.

Initially, leadership was slow to respond and tried to downplay it. But the study began circulating virally among boat crews. More and more veterans stepped forward saying, "This is exactly what happened to me." Independent biomechanical studies using sensors on boats and helmets confirmed the massive repetitive G-forces slammed into these operators' heads.

The evidence was undeniable. Coast Guard leadership could no longer ignore the reality.

佐兰·卡诺-扬斯: 那么海岸警卫队现在采取了哪些改进措施？这对于未来军事人员的健康防护意味着什么？

Original English

Zolan Kanno-Youngs: What changes has the Coast Guard implemented now, and what does this mean for the future of military health?

戴夫·菲利普斯: 面对调查和来自美国国会的质询压力，海岸警卫队不得不启动了全面的系统性改革：

  1. 出海时间严格限制：大幅削减快艇驾驶员在恶劣海况下的单次操作时间和年度累计暴露时长。
  2. 装备升级与减震改造：为快艇全面加装军用级高规格减震浮动座椅，并研发具备冲击波和震动吸收功能的新型特种头盔。
  3. 健康基线筛查与监测：将脑震荡和微创伤认知测试纳入常规体检，一旦发现早期脑功能受损迹象立即停止出海任务并进行医疗干预。
  4. 工伤抚恤与医疗认可：退伍军人事务部（VA）开始将快艇反复剧烈颠簸造成的神经退行性病变和脑损伤正式认定为服役相关工伤。

这一转变具有极其深远的意义。过去军方对脑损伤的定义极其狭隘——要么是头盔直接挨了物理撞击，要么是遭遇了路边炸弹爆炸。如今，从火炮射击产生的微冲击波，到快艇在海浪上反复颠簸造成的累积性微创伤，整个军界终于开始正视这类非爆炸性、重复性隐性脑创伤。

正如珍娜·邦纳所说的，虽然这些改变无法挽回布雷特的生命，但至少能拯救未来成百上千名年轻快艇驾驶员的健康与家庭。

Original English

Dave Phillips: Facing scrutiny from Congress and public reporting, the Coast Guard has begun instituting sweeping reforms:

  1. Operating Limits: Strictly capping the hours boat drivers can spend in heavy surf and limiting cumulative lifetime exposure.
  2. Shock-Mitigation Technology: Retrofitting boats with state-of-the-art shock-absorbing suspension seats and developing specialized impact-dampening helmets.
  3. Cognitive Baseline Monitoring: Mandating regular neurological baseline testing to catch early signs of cognitive decline before permanent damage occurs.
  4. VA Benefits and Recognition: The Department of Veterans Affairs is beginning to recognize chronic wave-impact brain trauma as a service-connected disability.

This marks a profound shift in military medicine. For decades, brain injuries were only recognized from direct blunt trauma or massive bomb explosions. Today, from artillery blast overpressure to the repetitive pounding of ocean waves, the military is finally recognizing the danger of cumulative subconcussive head trauma.

As Jenna Bonner told me, while these changes come too late to save Brett, they may protect the minds and lives of hundreds of future surfmen.
