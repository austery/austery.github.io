序言：重塑创意的非正式碰撞

在过去5年里，我听到很多CEO抱怨说，在混合和虚拟工作环境中，我们失去了创意碰撞。我确实认为大多数人都在怀念创意碰撞，但真正赋予其力量的并不是自发性。创意碰撞奏效的原因在于，当你与平时接触不到的人交流时，会产生新想法，因为他们为你带来了新鲜视角；而且在非正式交流中，你不会受限于死板的议程。因此，我们完全可以通过结构化来安排更多的非结构化互动。我所见过关于这一点最好的数据表明，如果你让销售人员每周只进行一项简单的30分钟活动，4个月后他们的个人业绩飙升了24%。每对人每周投入30分钟，就带来了巨大的回报。

英文原文

I've heard a lot of CEOs complain in the last 5 years we've lost our creative collisions in the hybrid and virtual world of work. And I do think most people are missing creative collisions, but it's not spontaneity that actually drives the power of those. The reason that creative collisions work was you end up having new ideas when you interact with people you don't normally have access to because they bring you fresh perspectives. And when you have informal interaction because you're not just stuck to a wrote agenda. And so there's no reason why we can't structure more unstructured interaction and probably the best data I've seen on this show that if you take sales people and you just have them do a simple 30-minute activity every week, 4 months later their individual revenue has spiked by 24%. That is a tremendous return on a 30-minute weekly investment per pair.

另一种则是任务凝聚力，也就是我们共同认同一个目标。我们不一定非要在团队内部亲密无间，但我们紧密依附于这个群体。如果查看数据，无论在工作团队还是体育队伍中，任务凝聚力对绩效的影响都远远大于社交凝聚力。因此，我认为领导者的首要任务不是让大家享受彼此的陪伴，而是让大家认同：“看，我们认同共同的方向，并拥有一套凝聚我们的价值观。”

英文原文

>>: Well, it's not affection or even agreement, it's alignment that matters the most. And I think the evidence on this is is so clear. It's about, you know, singing Kumbaya and wanting to hang out together after work and do happy hours. And then there's task cohesion, which is we say we share a purpose. And we're not necessarily bonded within a group, we are bonded to the group. And so I think that a leader's first job is not to get people to to enjoy each other's company, it's to get people to say, "Look, we're bought in to a common direction and we have a set of values that unite us."

入职培训的误区：提前入职与展现独特优势

英文原文

>>: Now, another interesting idea in the book is that people can form truly meaningful connections relatively quickly. And I'm wondering, you know, so what does that tell us about, you know, onboarding, team formation, you know, bringing strangers together inside organizations? Are we Are we thinking the right way about these things?

>>: 我认为并不正确。我们在处理入职和融入时犯了两个系统性错误。首先是我们等得太久了。

另一个错误是，一旦新员工正式入职，正如我的同事丹·凯布尔（Dan Cable）主导研究所发现的那样，我们往往致力于“驯化”员工。我们会说：“听着，我们希望你吸收我们的价值观，内化我们的规范。我们要给你灌迷魂汤，你的任务就是把它喝下去。”这种做法在帮助人们做出有意义贡献方面的效果，远不如不强求他们顺从、而是邀请他们脱颖而出。
