刻意筛选模型与主观划分数据

而且众所周知，这些能力指数度量很容易让所有的分数随着时间的推移向上漂移。一些批评者甚至表示，这些能力指数，引用如下：“没有良好的 y 轴”，引用结束。除了解释随着时间推移的增长却使用了一个随时间向上漂移的指数之外，他们还在哪些模型算入或不算入度量中进行了极其严重的挑拣（cherry picked）。

英文原文 And these capability index measurements are notoriously bad at allowing all of the scores to drift upwards over time. Some critics have even said that these capability indexes have, quote, "No good y-axis," unquote. And then, on top of the fact that they're using an index that drifts up over time to measure an increase over time, they cherry picked a hell out of which models do or don't count towards their measurements.

这是他们论文中的图表。对于 Opus 4.7、4.8、5 和 5.5，哦，还有 Mythos 5.1，那些白色圆圈里的每一个……是的，他们认定那些都不算。为什么？因为它们“不是前沿模型”（not frontier）。所以 Mythos 5 算作前沿，但 5.1 就不算。还要注意，在他们确实算作前沿的 7 个内部模型中，有 4 个要么是“早期”（early）要么是“准早期”（pre-early）模型。他们不算 2024 年或 2025 年模型的早期或准早期内部版本，只算 2026 年新模型里的。这是为什么呢？这么说吧，这改变了线条的走势。我们稍后会回到这一点。

英文原文 So here's the graph in their paper. Each of those white circles for opus 4.7, 4.8, 5, and 5.5, oh, and Mythos 5.1... Yeah, they decided those don't count. Why? Because they're "not frontier." So mythos 5 counts as frontier, but not 5.1. Also note that four of the seven internal models that they DO count towards the frontier are either "early" or "pre-early" models. They don't count early or pre-early internal versions of models from back in 2024 or 2025, only from the new ones in 2026. Now why is that? Well, let's just say it changes the shape of the line. We'll get back to that a second.

如果这还不够作弊的话，他们还武断地将数据集分成两组：右边一组，左边一组。每一组都被赋予了一条自己的趋势线，然后他们画上一个黄色圆圈，试图让这看起来像是有什么依据似的。武断地选定某一点并声称“右边的一切都与左边的一切不同”，根据你划分位置的不同，你会得到很多不同的答案。

英文原文 And if that wasn't enough cheating, they just arbitrarily divide the data set into two different groups. One group on the right, and one group on the left. Each are given their own trade line, and then they draw a yellow circle in to make it look like there was some reason for it.
