在AI超现实世界中起舞：人类想象力与机器协作的艺术探索

艺术与技术的交织：从超现实到数字机器

Szauderland（绍德兰）是一个游走在艺术与技术边缘的虚拟国度。创作者在过去的17年里持续构建这个世界，让虚构的角色通过服饰、舞蹈和动作进行交流，宛如一场永不停歇、充满欢笑与华丽背景的歌剧。创作者最初险些成为一名艺术史学家，文艺复兴的有机质感与超现实主义的奇幻美学对其产生了深远影响，尤其是超现实主义，成为了他灵魂共鸣的归宿。随着计算机与编程技术的兴起，他开始探索机器人的世界，并尝试了许多创作。他曾设计了一台绘图机器人以记录疫情带来的抽象印记，还制作了一台甚至骗过了达芬奇专家的增强现实（Augmented Reality: 一种将虚拟信息与真实世界融合的技术）达芬奇深伪机器。直到三年前的某个清晨，一张社交媒体上的AI生成图像彻底震撼了他，其繁复的视觉复杂度开启了他与人工智能共同创作的旅程。

英文原文

I want to invite you to a foreign land. [Chapter one: Tango] A place where imaginary characters communicate to dress, to dance and movement. They are living in an ever-changing opera, with costumes, with backdrops and fun and laughter. I have been developing this idea [for] the last 17 years on the border of art and technology. It's a dance revue, a place called Szauderland. This is where I live. [Chapter two: Manipulative games] At the beginning, I almost became an art historian. That was the renaissance and the surrealism that made the greatest impression on me. I love the organic quality of the renaissance, and I love my wife and my daughter, but I feel most connected to surrealism. Then came the machines and the programming. I explored the beautiful world of the robots. I tried many things. I created once a drawing robot to record the abstract imprint of the pandemic and an augmented reality deepfake Leonardo da Vinci machine, which even fooled a Leonardo expert. [Chapter three: AI] Until one morning, three years ago, I found an image online on the social media that froze me. I couldn't even figure out how it was made, but I was so captivated by its complexity.

炼金术式的AI创作：数据烹饪与视觉调味

在建立这种艺术直觉后，创作者将人工智能（Artificial Intelligence: 模拟人类智能的计算机系统）的协同创作比作一场精妙的烹饪过程。刚开始使用AI时，他如同圣诞节拆礼物的孩子般充满好奇与期待。每一次创作都始于脑海中酝酿的灵感，例如构建一座充满现代隐喻的巴别塔（Babel: 源自圣经故事，隐喻人类语言混乱与沟通隔阂的宏大建筑）。随后，他开始搜集各种视觉模式与摄影素材，将所有原材料倒入AI这口“大锅”中进行“煮沸”熔炼。在这个算法熔炼的过程中，往往需要进行细致的“调味”：有时是某种风格过于浓烈，有时是色彩不够饱和。最后则是创作者最钟爱的“摆盘”阶段，即通过后期制作调整细节，打上独一无二的个人美学印记，实现人机协同的视觉升华。

英文原文

[Chapter four: Babel] When I started to work with AI, I tried all sorts of things. Like a child unwrapping presents at Christmas and awaiting for the results. For me, working with AI is like cooking. There is always an idea stirring in my head. Right now you see the modern Babel. Then I start to collect patterns, capture photos. And when I have everything ready, I throw all the ingredients into the pot. And when it's boiling, which is the AI process itself, it often needs some seasoning because there is too much of one style or too little of one color. Then comes the post-production. That's my favorite part. The plating. When I can clear all the details and I can give my personal signature.

虚拟原住民的舞步：投射自我与疗愈心智

通过这种独特的协同创作模式，创作者在跨越国界的迁徙旅途中，利用AI构建起一个让自己感到安适的超现实世界，并在这个世界中创造了属于他的虚拟原住民（Virtual Earthlings: 在虚拟世界中生存的数字化人格角色）。这些角色在某种程度上是创作者高度自我投射的产物：他们有的努力维持着微妙的平衡，有的躲在暗处静静观察，有的穿着奇装异服招摇过市，还有的在自由起舞。让这些虚拟原住民跳舞是创作者的一种自我补偿，因为现实中的他虽然热爱舞蹈却极其笨拙，这种缺憾让他感到沮丧。因此，他将自己笨拙的舞步赋予了角色，让这种数字化舞蹈变成了一场治愈内心缺憾的心理疗愈。在服饰上，他将日常时尚视为一种束缚性的制服，而在Szauderland中，他打破了这种框架，为角色设计了极具想象力的奢华高定服装，实现了精神上的彻底释放。

英文原文

[Chapter five: Unknown familiar] So my work is always starting with an idea. Right now, you see my recent moving from one country to another country. Then I start to use artificial intelligence to create a kind of surreal world where I can feel comfortable. Then I start to populate it with my virtual Earthlings, which is obviously a very, very selfish thing because these characters are a bit like me. The one is trying to keep the balance. The one is hiding somewhere, but trying to observe closely. The one can wear any kind of dress on the street. And the one can dance freely. [Chapter six: New Year’s swing] I make my virtual Earthlings dance because I love to dance. Dancing is good, although I never knew how to do that. And that frustrates me terribly. (Laughter) I even passed my slightly clumsy movements onto my character, you see. It will help one of us. Like in a therapy session. [Chapter seven: Art of Hiding] I found fashion always fascinating. I see as a kind of costume, a uniform, what we put on every day. But in my land, in Szauderland, I created the freedom from that exactly. These characters there are wearing truly extraordinary clothes. Like a kind of luxury collection. Would you wear it too, I see it in your eyes. We can talk about it later.

机器与灵魂的边界：作为工具的AI与人类想象力

尽管AI辅助构建了这个令人惊叹的新世界，但创作者清醒地认识到，Szauderland真正的设计师并非技术本身。生成式人工智能（Generative AI: 能够生成文本、图像或其他媒体的AI模型）本质上只是一种极其精美的工具，它自身缺乏真正的幽默感、美感和喜悦。Szauderland的秘密始终根植于人类的灵感，以及那些挥之不去的虚拟角色。创作者需要将这些角色从自己的内心“编舞”释放出来，让内在的剧场在外部世界登台演出。这场人机协作的艺术秀证明了一点：唯有借助人类独特的想象力，如此富有生命力和情感温度的艺术世界才有可能诞生。

英文原文

[Chapter eight: Doppelganger] My exploration with AI allowed me to build a new world. But this wasn't really AI [who] designed this place. I found it as a beautiful tool, but I feel sorry for its lack of humor, of beauty and joy. That's roughly the secret of Szauderland. An idea, a place filled with characters. They won't let me go. I need to choreograph them out of myself. Then the show begins, my inner theater turns outward. But I'm happy to say that such a world can only come from a human person's imagination. Thank you very much. (Applause)
