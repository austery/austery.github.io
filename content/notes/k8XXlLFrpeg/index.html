开场与人工智能泡沫是否已破裂

尽管中东发生了战争，尽管他们加征了关税，尽管特朗普总统及其政策、反移民立场遭到了种种批评，所有这些因素都摆在那里。然而，世界上人们似乎唯一愿意把钱投进去的国家，仍然是美国。

英文原文 Despite having the war in the Middle East, despite the tariffs that they have done, despite all the criticism heaped on President Trump and his policies, the anti-immigration stance, all of this has been thrown in. And yet the only country in the world where people want to put money into seems to be America.

大家好，我是挪威主权财富基金首席执行官 Nicolai Tangen，今天我非常有幸请到了投资者、《金融时报》专栏作家兼 Rockefeller International 主席 Ruchir Sharma。一年前我们交谈时，Ruchir 预测人工智能泡沫将在 2026 年结束，我当时答应过我们会再回来检验一下。很少有人比他更擅长挑战大众共识，我非常期待这次对话。Ruchir，热烈欢迎。

英文原文 So what are some of the more cracks you're seeing now?

英文原文 Yeah. So I think the big thing is rising rates, that financing is becoming much more difficult, right? So rates have started to tick up. What is going to happen next?

英文原文 I think that it's, I don't see rates coming down much, right? So I think that rates have gone up. Absent of that, I think that the path is the fact that in the next few months, the financing becomes more and more difficult and some of the big projections you have about how much CapEx is gonna be spent are gonna start coming down. I think that's the way I see it.

美国赤字、利率与市场波动

现在，大型科技公司过去是用自己的钱来支付人工智能开支，而现在它们开始举债。为什么这让你感到担忧？

英文原文 Now, Big Tech used to pay AI out of its own pocket, and now they raise debt. Why does it worry you?

正如我所说，现在你需要外部融资了。当你用自己的资产负债表来支付时，这没什么大不了的，因为你有自己的现金流，你有能力这么做。现在所有这些公司都在变成净负债状态。但这次热潮有一个很大的不同，我认为这是我们过去从未见过的，那就是如果你回顾过去的热潮，政府并没有和这些公司在人工智能领域争夺资本。即使你回看 2000 年或其他例子，事实上在 2000 年，政府当时还处于财政盈余状态。

英文原文 As I said, now you need external financing. When you were paying it out of your own balance sheet, it was not such a big deal because you had your own cash flow, you were able to do that. Now all these companies are turning into becoming net debt. But there's one big difference in this boom, which I think is something which we have not seen in the past, which is that if you looked at the past booms, the government was not competing for capital AI today with these companies. That even if you look back at 2000 or the other instances, in fact, in 2000, the government was running a surplus.

但你会说它们真的在竞争吗？我的意思是，与美国国债市场相比，科技公司的规模仍然相对有限。

英文原文 But would you say they're really competing? I mean, the tech companies are still relatively limited compared to the treasury market.

是的，但我想说这就是区别所在，你说得对，这些公司现在的借款规模——当然，即便如此，今年人工智能生态系统的借款总额也已经达到了 6000 亿美元。现在你可能会说，这不如美国政府正在运行的 2 万亿以上的赤字那么大。但所有这些因素加在一起，就会对利率产生上行压力。我认为这就是我一直在强调的观点，这是这一次最大的不同。

I think that is the point that I've been making, that that's the big difference this time.

英文原文 And the, you know, the other point that we had spoken about with the US government's finances, which is that they really keep deteriorating, we are back to now running a budget deficit of six and a half percent of GDP, you know, so this is not something which we've seen in the past. So we can look at the past for some clues about what may happen, but the big difference this time is that the government's running a deficit of six and a half percent of GDP. That is taking away a lot of capital away from the other markets.

英文原文 No, I think that if you look at the, and people have deconstructed this, that we have gone from interest rates of being sub 1% five years ago or so, to now interest rates of 5%, right? So it's already beginning to get priced to the stock market.

而对于整个利率市场来说，那种拥有极低利率、可以为一切提供资金的日子已经结束了。这就是我所定义的“范式转变”（regime shift），我们曾经拥有的极度宽松货币的时代已经结束了。所以为这次热潮、这次人工智能热潮融资将会变得困难得多。你说得对，现在并没有出现那种警报，觉得赤字变得太大、政府无法再融资。但长端利率也在上升。而它们上升的部分原因，正是因为出现了你在过去从未有过的、更加持久的赤字。

英文原文 The days, and for the general interest rate market, the days when you had very low interest rates which could fund everything are over. And a part of the reason why they're going up is because of the fact that you have much more persistent deficits that you did not have in the past.

全球资金流向美国与散户狂热

英文原文 No, I mean, the fact of the matter is that you are still less than the index or the average person globally, because they're, as I said, over the past year, 80% of the flows have gone into, equity flows have gone into one country, which is America. 英文原文 So you have this incredible disconnect that you have 28% of the global economy is America.
