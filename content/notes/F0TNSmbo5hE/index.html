非确定性与链路追踪的作用

那么，从更宽广的视角来看，我们来讨论一下问题到底是什么。

英文原文So, to take a broader view, let's discuss what the problem is.

问题在于智能体是非确定性的。在传统软件中，你可以阅读代码，并能带着某种信心说出那段代码究竟会执行什么。而面对智能体，你毫无头绪。智能体是非确定性的。

英文原文The problem is that agents are non-deterministic. In traditional software, you can read code and say with some confidence what exactly that code will do. With agents, you have no idea. Agents are non-deterministic.

智能体执行数个步骤，做出决策，即便给予它们完全相同的输入，它们也不会产生相同的结果。而且即便它们产生了相同的结果，每次达成该结果所遵循的路径也不一定相同。这意味着你的代码不再是关于智能体正在做什么的唯一真相来源。

英文原文Agents take several steps, make decisions, and even if they are given exactly the same input, they will not produce the same result. And even if they produce the same result, they won't necessarily follow the same path to it every time. This means that your code is no longer the sole source of truth about what the agent is doing.

相反，真相来源是链路追踪，特别是在规模化之下的链路追踪。你需要掌握大量数据来理解智能体在平均意义下正在做什么，而不仅仅是单次运行期间的表现。因为当你构建智能体时，你本质上是在盲目工作。

英文原文Instead, the source of truth is traces, and especially traces at scale. You need to have a lot of data to understand what the agent is doing on average, not just during a single run. Because when you create agents, you are essentially working blindly.

这是你在下载代码仓库时得到的东西：一堆代码和提交信息。这在过去足以理解将要发生什么，但现在不行了。

英文原文Here's what you get when you download the repository: a bunch of code and commit messages. This used to be enough to understand what was going to happen, but now it's not.

取而代之的是，你得到的是链路追踪。链路追踪——对那些错过入门课的人来说——简而言之，就像 AI 应用的日志文件。你的 AI 应用的每一次操作、每一次 LLM 调用、每一次工具调用、每一个智能体步骤——这一切都成为了链路追踪的一部分。

英文原文Instead, you get traces. Traces—for those who missed the introductory lesson—are, in short, like log files for AI applications. Every action of your AI application, every LLM call, every tool call, every agent step—it all becomes part of the trace.

链路追踪可以嵌套，包含其他链路追踪，而这正是 Arise 所处理的对象。它们被发送到我们的服务器，我们为你们提供所观察内容的便捷可视化呈现。

英文原文Traces can be nested, contain other traces, and that's what Arise works with. They are sent to our servers, and we provide you with a convenient visualization of what you see.

在实际操作中，它看起来像这样。这里有一些当前的实时链路追踪。我来打开具体的链路追踪，呃，来自我们今天将要处理的内容，也就是 Wonder Toys 智能体。嗯。

从人工瓶颈到评估与自动化

链路追踪的问题在于，人成为了跟踪循环中的“瓶颈”。

英文原文The problem with tracing is that people become a “bottleneck” in the tracking cycle.

当你部署一个应用时，你会得到一个链路追踪。事实上，每当智能体执行任何操作时，你都会得到一堆链路追踪。当你在生产环境中运行它并拥有每秒数千次请求时，那就意味着数以百万计的链路追踪。

英文原文When you deploy an application, you get a trace. In fact, you get a bunch of traces every time the agent performs any action. When you run this in production and have thousands of requests per second, that means millions and millions of traces.

你可以选择性地检查它们。你可以去阅读你的链路追踪。在开发期间，你能准确看到各个组件正在做什么。在生产环境中，你就会成为“瓶颈”，无法通过一条一条去阅读来获得所有链路追踪的全貌。

英文原文You can selectively check them. You can read your traces. You can see exactly what individual elements are doing during development. In production, you become a "bottleneck" and you won't be able to get an overview of all traces by reading them one by one.

所以解决方案，传统的解决方案——我所说的传统指的是 2025 年的解决方案——就是评估。

英文原文So the solution, the traditional solution, and by traditional I mean the 2025 solution, was evaluations.

评估要么是代码，或者更好的是，由另一个语言模型来分析智能体的表现，审查所有的链路追踪，并根据你的定义判定它们是好是坏。关键在于评分来自语言模型，这意味着它们不仅提供 1 或 0 的评分，还提供解释。它们仿佛在说：“你已经定义了什么是好。因为这样或那样的原因，它是糟糕的。”而这正是整个改进循环的基础。

英文原文An evaluation is either code or, better yet, another language model that analyzes the agent's performance, examines all the traces, and determines whether they were good or bad according to your definition. The thing is that ratings come from language models, which means they provide not just a one or zero rating, but also an explanation. They seem to be saying: “You have defined what is good. "It was bad for this or that reason." And this is the basis of the entire cycle of improvement.

评估可以为你提供机器可读的，或者更好的是，人类可读的解释——如今连机器也能阅读这些解释——说明：“这就是你的评估失败的原因。那是你可以做得更好的地方。”

英文原文Assessments can give you machine-readable, or better yet, human-readable explanations that even machines can read these days, that say, "Here's why your assessment failed. "That's what you could have done better."

而且你可以把这些反馈给编程智能体，随后它将自动改进你的软件。一旦你正确定义了“好”的含义，你无需亲自干预就能闭合软件开发循环。你创造了这座山峰，而你的智能体可以自己去征服它。

英文原文And you can feed that back to the coding agent, which will then automatically improve your software. You can close the software development cycle without your own intervention once you correctly define what “good” means. You create this hill, and your agent can conquer it on their own.

所以，这就把我们带到了 2025 年的评估循环。你从完全没有可见性走向使用链路追踪，再到将链路追踪用于评估，最后能够根据你的链路追踪分析手动进行改进。

英文原文So, that brings us to the 2025 evaluation cycle. You go from having no visibility at all to using traces, then traces in evaluation, and finally being able to manually make improvements based on your trace analysis.

但 2026 年的问题在于，人类在这个循环中再次成为了瓶颈，因为评估现在面对的是极其庞大的规模。你的智能体以极快的速度生成代码。你的应用在大规模下运行。你拥有的评估数量超出了你的处理能力。

英文原文But the problem in 2026 is that humans are once again the bottleneck in this cycle, as evaluation is now dealing with enormous scale. Your agents generate code incredibly fast. Your applications operate at scale. You have more assessments than you can handle.

虽然评估过去能让你将成千上万条链路追踪压缩为分数，但现在其数量如此之多，以至于你作为个人已经无法独自跟踪这一切了。

英文原文And while assessments used to allow you to compress thousands of traces into scores, there are now so many that you as a person can no longer keep track of it all on your own.

这就把我们带到了观测与评估演进的下一个阶段：当我们在以智能体的速度创造解决方案时，我们必须同样迅速地改进它们。我们需要找到一种在规模化下闭环的方法，就像智能体所做的那样。

英文原文This brings us to the next stage in the evolution of observation and evaluation: as we create solutions at the speed of agents, we must improve them just as quickly. We need to find a way to close the loop at scale, just like agents do.

这是正在监测领域发生的一场转变，并且所有人都在同时感受到它。观测正在从人类通过仪表盘进行分析，转变为由智能体进行链路追踪分析。智能体是一种将我们对其他行动的理解进行规模化扩展的方式

英文原文This is a shift that is happening in the surveillance industry, and it is being felt by everyone at the same time. Observation is shifting from dashboard analysis by humans to trace analysis by agents. Agents are a way to scale our understanding of the actions of other

以一种切合当今工作规模和速度的方式来评估智能体。

英文原文agents in a way that is practical for today's scale and speed of work.

因此，当智能体去分析你那些智能体的行为时，你就能在 trace 中检测到信号。比方说，你原本可能会有 10,000 个评估结果，每一个都显示有错误，并且每一个都有单独的解释需要你去阅读；现在不同了，你可以让一个智能体去集中分析这些失败用例，识别出你错误中的信号和模式，然后告诉你：“好的，今天你一共有 10,000 个成功的请求和 1,000 个失败的请求。在这 1,000 个失败请求中，有 100 个是相同的错误。这就是我们要去修复的问题。”当你将从 trace 和评估中获取信号的理解过程自动化时，你能做到的就是这些。

英文原文So, when agents analyze your agents' actions, you can detect signals in your traces. You can, instead of having, say, 10,000 evaluation results, each one saying there was an error, and each one having a separate explanation that you would have to read, have an agent analyze those failures en masse, identify signals and patterns in your errors, and say, "Okay, today you had 10,000 successful requests and 1,000 failed ones." Among this thousand, there are a hundred with the same error. That's what we're going to fix. Here's what you can do when you automate the understanding of signals obtained from traces and estimates.

在 Arize，我们将这个功能做成了一款名为 Signal 的产品，我会在最后谈到它。但这并不是广告。这是在展示向这种全新抽象层级跃迁所能实现的能力。当你从逐条阅读 trace 跃迁到查看评估指标——这是一个新的抽象层级，而从我的评估和 trace 中读取信号，则是更高的层级。因为可观测性从单纯的观察迈向了行动这一大步。

英文原文At Arize, we built this into a product called Signal, which I'll talk about at the end. But this is not advertising. This is a demonstration of what can be done by moving to this new level of abstraction. When you go from reading traces one by one to reading estimates—that's a new level of abstraction, and this is an even higher level: reading signals from my estimates and traces. Because observability takes a big step from simple observation to action.

我们正在从“告诉你发生了什么”的软件，转向“根据你对质量的定义自动优化系统来改进流程”的软件。信号变成了纠正措施。它们变成了自动修复，从而让你进入一个全新的循环。这就是 2026 年的循环：从毫无可观测性，到 trace、评估、信号、自动修正，然后再循环往复。你正在创造一个软件自我纠正、自我完善、不断精进的新世界。这就是我们的理念、愿景以及我们前进的方向。

英文原文We are moving from software that tells you what is happening to one that improves processes by automatically optimizing the system based on your definition of quality. Signals become corrections. They become automatic fixes, which puts you on a new cycle. This is the 2026 cycle where you go from no observability to traces, estimates, signals, automatic corrections and back again. You are creating a new world where software corrects and improves itself, rising to the top. So, this is our concept, vision, and direction we are heading.
