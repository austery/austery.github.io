特别经济区（SEZ）的红利与风险：安大略省的实验与全球教训

关键字 special-economic-zones economic-policy investment-incentives market-segmentation regulatory-standards

摘要

本文探讨了特别经济区（SEZ）的概念、运作机制及其在全球范围内的应用。重点分析了安大略省通过《第五号法案》引入SEZ的背景，以及首席经济学家 Pedro Antunes 对此举可能导致市场分割、环境标准下降和劳动力标准降低的担忧。通过对比中国、澳大利亚和墨西哥的案例，揭示了SEZ在吸引投资与维护整体经济竞争力之间的权衡。

Pedro Antunes: Well, I think traditionally, especially economic zone is meant to kind of spur is a zone marked I guess, by a government and is intended to spur new investment. And typically how they would do that would be to offer up a better tax incentives. They may offer, you know, a reduced import duties if you're operating with it within that zone, they may reduce regulatory requirements. There's all sorts of or, you know, perhaps allow for cheaper labour. As we've seen in some cases, most of these are typically set up in developing economies when they're just trying to spur that investment and kind of adjust the rules a little bit to spur the investment in in a localised area.

地理限制与行业覆盖

主持人: 请说明一下，这些区域是真的地理区域吗？还是可以对整个行业实施的一揽子政策？

英文原文

Host: Help us understand are these zones really are they geographic zones, or could they be something that you could put on a blanket, say, on an industry in itself?

Pedro Antunes: 嗯，我想你可以将其更广泛地应用于某个行业，但通常来说，它们会被指定为一个特殊的、特定的区域。你可以以中国为例，当他们从本质上的农业农村经济转型时，他们正在向现在的状态——即世界制造中心过渡。他们本质上是想把人口迁往城市，也就是迁往特定的区域。因此，他们允许这些区域在能源获取、住房获取，或者本质上在税收和其他政策方面拥有特殊的规则和优势。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, I guess you could sort of apply them more broadly to an industry, but generally speaking, they have been assigned a special, a specific area. You can think about China, for example, for, you know, as they were transitioning from essentially an agricultural rural economy, they were transitioning to what they have become, which is the manufacturing hub of the world. They were essentially trying to move people to essentially to cities to, to densify a a sorry, I suppose, special areas. And so they were allowing for these zones to have essentially advantages in terms of be it access to energy, be it access to housing, or be it essentially special rules around tax and other policies.

安大略省的实验与争议

主持人: 现在，随着去年夏天《第五号法案》（即《通过释放经济潜力保护安大略省法案》）的通过，省政府因为使用特别经济区而受到了很多批评。这是为什么呢？

英文原文

Host: Now, with the passing of Bill five, Protect Ontario by Unleashing our Economy Act last summer, the provincial government has received a lot of flak for using special economic zones. Why would that be?

Pedro Antunes: 嗯，我的意思是，从个人层面上看，我认为无法在全省范围内提供一个公平的竞争环境并以此吸引投资，这让人感到有些失望。安大略省这里的省政府目前似乎专注于资源开发。当然，我们也听说了关于机场的消息。但我认为，其本质是为了促进和加速投资，特别是在环境审查程序和许可方面，或许还会放宽其中的一些规则。不仅仅是审查过程本身，还可能通过降低某些标准或规则来促进投资。所以我们现在还不太确定具体情况，但我认为总体的意图是加速资源项目的开发。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, I mean, I think it's to personally, on a personal level, I think it's a bit of a disappointment that we cannot kind of level the playing field across the board and entice investment in that way. So the provincial government here is looking to try and essentially really the zones in Ontario are really focused in, I think, on resource development. And of course, we've heard about the airport as well. But I think it's essentially to facilitate and expedite investments really around the environmental review process, perhaps around permitting, perhaps around lessening some of those rules as well. Not just not just the the review process itself, but maybe facilitating investment by lowering some of the standards or rules. So we're not quite sure here what's going on specifically, but I think the general intent is to really expedite resource projects in general.

全球视野下的经验与教训

主持人: 自从省长开始随《第五号法案》宣布这个概念以来，特别经济区似乎已经进入了每个人的词汇库。但当然，他并不是发明了这个概念。你提到了中国作为例子，但在世界其他地方，我们还能在哪里看到特别经济区？

英文原文

Host: Now, special economic zones seem to have come into everyone's lexicon since the Premier started announcing it with Bill five. But of course, he did not make up this concept. You had mentioned China as one example, but where else in the world can we see special economic zones?

Pedro Antunes: 事实上，在为今天的谈话做准备时，我也对它们的普及程度感到惊讶。它们在世界许多不同的国家都得到了广泛应用。但同样，回到发达经济体，这确实带来了另一个问题，即市场分割、缺乏统一的竞争环境，以及不允许投资在效率更高的地方发生。我认为在资源项目本身的情况中，遗憾的是，我们试图在这里挑选一个想要开发的区域，也许那里有建设矿山的经济依据。我们想为了那个区域稍微弯曲一下规则，以促进那里的投资。当我审视我们在内部贸易壁垒方面的表现时，如果你回到联邦政府试图推动的“一个加拿大”法案（旨在加速项目），那实际上是试图统一我们的市场。而我认为这（特别经济区）遗憾的是，也许它在加速某些资源项目上是有效的，但在更大的蓝图中，它将使我们的经济更加分割。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, in fact, I was surprised just in preparing here for our talk today as to how widespread they are. I mean, they are pretty much, you know, used across the world in many different countries. But again, when we come back to developed economies, this really segments, you know, it's another problem in terms of segmenting the market, not having a uniform playing field, not allowing for investment to occur where it's more efficient. And I think in the case of the resource projects themselves, unfortunately, what we're trying to do here is, is pick an area where we want to develop and where perhaps there is an economic case to be made for a mine. And we want to kind of bend the rules a little bit for that, for that area to kind of promote that investment. When when I try to look at how we're doing in terms of our internal trade barriers, you know, when we go back to the one Canada, the one Canadian economy bill that the federal government is trying to push to to expedite projects as well, it really is to try and unify our market. And I think this is, unfortunately something that maybe it will be effective in expediting some resource projects. But in the bigger picture, it is going to segment our economy even more.

加拿大其他省份的现状

主持人: 让我们从省级层面来了解一下，是否有其他省份正在使用这种策略，或者讨论过使用它？

英文原文

Host: Well, let's understand from a provincial standpoint, are there other provinces that are using it or have discussed using the strategy?

Pedro Antunes: 嗯，我的意思是，目前安大略省的《C-5号法案》是唯一一个实际定义了特别经济区的法案。我们在其他任何地方都没有看到。阿尔伯塔省政府一直在讨论这样做，但我们还没有看到任何实际落实的举措。我再次以澳大利亚这样的经济体为例，它在吸引投资方面要成功得多。它的经济与我们平行，在许多方面非常相似，在过去几十年里生产力表现要好得多，而且他们没有任何特别经济区。所以我认为我们真的应该对实施这些政策保持警惕。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, there I mean, Bill C-5 is is the only one right now in Ontario that actually has defined a special economic zones. We haven't seen any that anywhere else. Alberta has been the Alberta government has been discussing doing so as well, but we haven't seen anything implemented. And again, I go back to an economy like Australia, which is much has been much more successful in attracting investment. It's an economy that is parallel to ours, very similar in many respects, has had a much better productivity performance over the last few decades, and they have no special economic zone. So I think we should really be wary about implementing these these these policies.

负面影响与“竞相逐低”

主持人: 话虽如此，批评者也指出，理论上这些区域听起来像是工业化的积极举措，但实际上执行起来可能非常困难。在全球范围内，已经出现了一些什么样的负面影响？如果我们只看非洲大陆，就能看到几个例子。你能带我们了解一些特别经济区方面的失败案例吗？

英文原文

Host: Well, with that being said, you know, critics have said in theory these zones sound like a positive move for industrialisation, but in reality can be quite difficult to actually implement. What are some of the negative implications that have occurred globally? I think if we just look at the continent of Africa, we can see a couple of examples there. Can you walk us through some some I guess some fails when it comes to these zones?

Pedro Antunes: 嗯，我认为这归根结底还是回到了市场分割的问题上。这涉及到在环境审查和环境规则上的**“逐底竞争”**（race to the bottom）。当然，这可能会导致并最终产生对环境具有潜在灾难性的后果。但更广泛地说，我们也看到了市场分割，也就是在本质上制造分裂而非统一经济，并以此来加强经济。我们见过分割经济的情况，正如我们在这个国家所经历的。创建一个统一的市场、让企业能够在整个经济体中开展业务，从而变得规模更大、生产力更高、能够投资更多，这些都面临困难。我认为，这些都是加拿大过去几十年一直面临的挑战，而我不认为这些（特别经济区）一定是解决方案。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, I think it goes down. It goes back to this segmenting the market. It goes back to a race to the bottom on environmental review, on environmental rules. And of course, it can lead to and end up with, you know, things that are potentially disastrous on the environment. Just more broadly, though, we've also seen segmenting the market, you know, essentially creating rather than unifying an economy and strengthening and strengthening it in that way. We've seen a situations where we are segmenting an economy and just creating more, you know, as we are, you know, we live here in this country. Difficulty around creating that one market, around having firms able to conduct business across the entire economy and thus becoming bigger, more productive, able to invest more. These are all of the challenges that Canada has been facing over the last number of decades, I would say. And I don't think these are necessarily a solution.

劳动力标准与墨西哥案例

主持人: 另一个我们还没真正谈到的因素是劳动力。当我们谈论发展中国家时，要在加拿大实现其中一些目标可能更难，我想这是因为与其他国家相比，这里的劳动力实践有所不同。

英文原文

Host: Another factor that we haven't really talked about is labour. When we're talking about, you know, developing nations a little bit harder to accomplish some of these things here in Canada, I imagine, with just labour practices in comparison to other countries.

Pedro Antunes: 嗯，就是这样。我的意思是，你不想开始降低劳动力标准。我刚才更多是在谈论环境标准，也就是规则。你不想放宽这些标准，但你也不想放宽劳动力标准。我认为我们已经看到了一些例子。比如在墨西哥，他们有 maquiladora（保税工厂）的例子，其本质上专注于制造业，以服务于他们边境北面庞大的美国经济。因此，那里设立了一些特别经济区，有人会说取得了一定的成功，因为他们看到那里的制造业有所增加。但与此同时，它也阻碍了那种——我该怎么说——富裕化，即生产力的提高，而生产力的提高本可以带来更高的工资。而且，现在美国也在回击那些本质上拥有廉价劳动力的经济体的贸易。所以这再次创造了一种“逐底竞争”的感觉，这无论是在税收方面，还是在这些劳动力标准或环境标准方面，都不一定是好事。

英文原文

Pedro Antunes: Well, that's it. I mean, you don't want to start to reduce labour standards. I've been talking more about environmental standards, you know, are rules. You don't want to slacken those, but you don't want to slacken labour standards either. And I think we've seen some examples of that. I mean, just you know in Mexico they have the maquiladora example, which is essentially focused on manufacturing to service the very large US economy just north of their border. And so, you know, there are special economic zones that have been set up, some would say with some success because they have seen manufacturing increase there. But at the same time, it's prevented that kind of, how should I say, enrichment, that increase in productivity that would allow for stronger wages as well. And, you know, now the US is pushing back against trade with economies that have essentially cheaper labour. So again, this creates this kind of sense of race to the bottom, which is not necessarily a good thing, both in terms of tax or in terms of these kind of labour standards or environmental standards.

英文原文
