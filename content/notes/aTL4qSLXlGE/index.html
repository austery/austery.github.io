音乐的人性史：跨越百万年的律动、演化与大脑共振 Big Think 2026-05-08

领域: society-thinking

分类: culture-history

主讲人: Big Think

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-05-08

摘要:

迈克尔·斯皮策教授通过对音乐百万年历史的回溯，揭示了音乐如何深深植根于人类的生物性、文化演进与社会结构中。从双足行走产生的原始节律，到东西方记谱法带来的审美分歧，再到数字时代的参与式回归，音乐不仅是大脑重塑的工具，更是人类身份识别与情感连接的核心载体。

音乐起源的生物底色：双足行走与大脑的余响

音乐的起源并非始于作曲家，而是深深植根于人类的生理演化之中。音乐（Music: 艺术化的声音表达）的历史比智人至少早一百万年。迈克尔·斯皮策教授指出，人类音乐的核心基石之一是双足行走（Bipedalism: 依靠两足直立行走的能力）。大约440万年前，地猿阿法种开始直立行走，每一步的节奏都在人类的大脑中打下了节律的烙印。这种行走带来的节奏感，促使大脑在肌肉劳作与声音之间建立了深刻的联系。

随着人类脑容量的三倍增长，我们获得了控制手指的精细能力和复杂发声的生理基础。当人类的喉头（Larynx: 呼吸道和消化道的交汇器官）下降，舌骨（Hyoid bone: 支撑舌头的骨骼）演化，我们能够发出的声音种类呈指数级增长。当声音的产生超过了单纯的“预警”功能（如灵长类动物的简单呼叫），人类开始为了声音而玩味声音，这种摆脱了功能性约束的“声音游戏”，便是音乐诞生的契机。

Original English Source Music is at least a million years older than Sapiens. And ultimately the musical human is a musical animal. One of these elements was bipedalism that what marks the first hominins apart from apes and our common ancestor was getting up on our feet. 4.4 million years ago, an oralopythesine called Adi stood on her legs and walked. And ever since then, the rhythm of walking has stamped human music. The first steps put us on the path to forging links between the brain and muscular exertion and sound. And the hominins learn to hear footsteps as a pattern. Our cranial volume triples in size. We become a lot smarter and with our increased brain size comes a capacity to control our fingers... our larynx descends through our vocal tracts. our hyoid bone which supports our tongue evolves so we can articulate what we sing... our capacity to make sounds exceeded their function. When you can produce a thousand kinds of sounds, there's an excess of sounds. And this is where music starts to become a possibility where you're playing with sound.

社会演进与时空容器：从洞穴仪式到音乐厅的专业化

社会结构的变迁决定了音乐的形态与功能。在狩猎采集（Hunter-gatherer: 以狩猎和采集为生的原始社会形态）时代，音乐是流动的、即兴的，且具有极高的便携性。最初的乐器如骨笛（Bone flute: 用秃鹫骨头制成，约有4万年历史）便是在洞穴中发现的。有趣的是，这些骨笛残片往往位于洞穴中共鸣最强的位置，这揭示了音乐与建筑声学（Architectural acoustics: 研究建筑中声音传播规律的学科）之间原始而自然的结合。

随着定居农业（Sedentary farming: 依靠耕种维持生活的社会形态）的出现，人类的心态转向了季节的轮回。音乐开始扎根，诞生了可以重复的“作品”和固定的仪式。当音乐从农田迁徙到城镇，社会阶级开始分化，音乐逐渐演变为权力的随从，服务于王子或教会。原本全民参与的歌舞活动被专业化，职业音乐家随之诞生。这种转变标志着音乐会（Concert: 供有闲阶级坐下来聆听的专业表演）的起源，即音乐从一种“生存活动”变为了一种“审美客体”。

Original English Source The epochs are hunter gatherer, sedentary or farming community and then the founding of cities and city states. Hunter gatherers tended to be nomadic... music has to be portable. When you invent farming and agriculture, you settle down and your whole mindset becomes fixed on the circle of the seasons, the circle of life. And music has roots. You invent the work, the repeatable work. Once music migrates from the farm to the town, music's function now also changes with the growth of social hierarchy. The job of music is to be a handmaidaden to serve the power. And musicians become professionalized and their job is to create music to be listened to for people with leisure. And this is the origin of what we call concerts. When we discovered the first bone flute, some of the shards were discovered in points of maximum resonance within the caves, suggesting a natural marriage between music and sound and acoustics and architecture.

记谱法的权力：西方音乐的全球化与审美异化

西方音乐在世界范围内的主导地位，很大程度上源于一千年前的一场技术革命：谱表记谱法（Staff notation: 在五条线上记录音符的方法）。1020年，意大利僧侣圭多（Guido d'Arezzo）发明了线谱，这最初是教会为了确保整个教区诵唱一致而设计的控制工具。记谱法如同捕捉蝴蝶的标本夹，将流动的声音冻结成精准的音符，这种精准虽然促进了复调音乐的发展，却也带来了一些副作用。

首先，记谱法导致了作曲家与表演者的权力剥离，表演者沦为机械复制作品的工具。其次，它剥离了声音的自然流动性，使其变得冷峻而机械。与之相对，印度等地的口头传承传统则通过师徒授受，保持了音乐的有机演化与即兴活力。此外，西方文明倾向于将音乐视为独立的“作品”或“对象”，而东方文化（如中国古代的古琴传统）则强调音乐与自然环境（风声、竹林）的浑然一体，认为音乐本就不应与自然音景割裂。

Original English Source A thousand years ago in 1020 an Italian monk called Guido invents staff notation and life was never again the same for western music. Staff notation was a tool of church control... you can control what people were doing across the entirety of the empire. By pinning notes down to a page, almost like capturing a butterfly, you're taking a note away from the voice. You're making it very precise. Notation freezes a note. it becomes rather cold and mechanical. It also freezes music as an object. You create a division between the composer of the object and those who merely mechanically reproduce it, the performer. A major difference between the west and the east is that the west conceptually distinguish notes from sounds. In China, there was a tradition of viewing the very delicate sounds of the chin as melding into the sounds of the wind and the bamboo.

情感共鸣与分形宇宙：大脑如何处理音乐

音乐的普适性在于它与宇宙自然及人类大脑深层结构的共鸣。音乐在本质上具有分形（Fractal: 具有自相似性的几何结构）特征，从波形到音节，再到乐句和整个作品，都体现出跨尺度的自相似性。这种结构反映了自然界声音（如风声、流水声）的底层规律。

在神经科学层面，音乐通过镜像神经元（Mirror neurons: 在观察他人动作时会发生共振的神经细胞）触发情感传染。当我们听到忧伤的旋律，即便没有经历丧亲之痛，大脑也会模拟这种情感。音乐的处理过程跨越了大脑的多个层级：最古老的脑干（Brain stem）响应声音冲击，边缘系统（Limbic system）和杏仁核（Amygdala）处理情感。此外，长期的音乐训练能重塑大脑，使其从右脑主导转向由左脑处理逻辑模式，这种“大脑重布线”所带来的纪律性、注意力和团队协作能力具有高度的迁移价值。

Original English Source Music is fractal in its nature. It's as fractal as a universe. Music is the art of repetition. The waveforms exhibit self similarity. And music has the same structure as natural noise. We love to imitate rhythm and that's due to the existence of mirror neurons in our brains. Emotions are contagious. When I hear a sad song, my mirror neurons are instinctively sympathizing. The deeper you dive into the human brain, the more universal one's propensity for music and for emotion goes. Brain stems flinch to reflexes in sound. The mamalian brain, the amygdala is where emotions happen. The astonishing fact is musical training rewires the brain. If we train a musician they become left-brain. They hear music through the same temporal lobe which processes language.

技术的共生：回归参与式音乐的未来

面对全球化的冲击，音乐并不会走向同质化，因为人类对独特性和身份识别的竞争驱动始终存在。斯皮策教授预言，未来的音乐将更加工具化（Prescriptive: 作为治疗或增强某种功能的手段），如同定制化的处方药。同时，数字技术正在打破“创作者”与“听众”的藩篱，使我们回归到数千年前那种全民参与（Participatory condition: 所有人都有份参与创作和享受的音乐状态）的常态。

无论是K-pop的互动模式，还是人工智能辅助创作（如Watson Beat），技术并非敌人，而是人类能力的延伸，正如最初的骨笛一样。未来的音乐可能不仅限于听觉，还将整合触觉、视觉甚至味觉，并扩展我们的感知频段。正如贝多芬无法想象今日的音乐盛况，我们也无法预知未来等待人类的是何种感官盛宴。

Original English Source I don't think music will become homogenized because every artist wants to be distinctive. Music has always been an extraordinary tool to express human identity. I would forecast two main developments. one is that music will become ever more instrumentalized or to have a function. prescribed exactly the right kind of sound to treat a condition. My other prediction would be an ever greater integration with technology. We're regaining the participatory condition of music which was the norm thousands of years ago. The original bone flute was a piece of technology. It serves to extend human capacity. In the future music may not be just about sound. It may involve tastes and colors and our bodies. We can't even begin to imagine the possibilities awaiting us in the future.
