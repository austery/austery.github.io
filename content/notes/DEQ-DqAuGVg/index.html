国际法院裁定缅甸犯下种族灭绝罪

昨天，国际法院（ICJ）作出一项里程碑式的判决，裁定缅甸对罗兴亚穆斯林犯下了种族灭绝罪。在 2017 年针对该少数族裔的残酷军事行动中，仅几周时间内就有至少 10,000 人丧生。村庄被夷为平地，数十万人被迫流亡。自那时起，暴力事件一直持续。昨天的判决是首次有国家被裁定犯有种族灭绝罪；在此之前，只有个人受到过该判决。

英文原文 [music] In a landmark decision yesterday, the International Court of Justice ruled that the country of Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya Muslims. Villages were burned to the ground and hundreds of thousands were forced to flee. The violence has only continued since. The ruling yesterday was the first time a country has been found culpable of genocide. Until now, only individuals had faced that verdict.

谷歌收购破产精神航空员工数据用于训练AI

[music] 当精神航空（Spirit Airlines）在今年春季破产时，该公司的资产被挂牌出售。这包括其标志性的黄色飞机，但也包括其数据，其中涵盖员工记录。如今，这些记录已被谷歌以 1000 万美元收购。像谷歌这样的公司希望训练其人工智能模型，使其听起来更像人类，尤其是像在职场环境中工作的人类。因此，从一家破产公司挖掘或用海量真实人类数据来训练大语言模型（LLM）相当具有吸引力。

英文原文 [music] When Spirit Airlines went bankrupt this spring, the company's assets went up for sale. Its iconic yellow planes, but also its data, including employee records. And now those records have been bought by Google for $10 million. So companies like Google want to train their artificial intelligence models to sound more like humans and specifically humans operating in a workspace. So the idea of mining or training the LLM on troves of actual human data from a failed company is pretty appealing.

我的同事 Rebecca Davis O'Brien 一直在跟踪这起交易。与她交谈的专家表示，这可能是首次有破产大公司的数字资产被这样剥离出售，用于训练像 Gemini 或 ChatGPT 这样的大语言模型。而这可能只是一场数据淘金热的开端。但数据涉及的相关人员正在进行抵制。Rebecca 表示，代表前精神航空空乘人员的工会在法庭上提出异议，辩称员工的个人信息可能会遭到泄露。在破产法官批准该交易之前，谷歌随后同意对其获取的数据施加某些限制，例如不包含医疗记录或银行记录。我认为撇开破产法的细微差别不谈，在人工智能时代，这是劳动法和劳工问题的一个新前沿，人们完全有理由担心自己的职业数据可能会被出让给最高出价者去训练 AI 产品。[music]

英文原文 My colleague Rebecca Davis O'Brien has been covering the sale. Experts she spoke with say this may be the first time the digital assets of a large bankrupt company have been peeled off like this to train large language models like Gemini or Chat GPT. And it could be just the start of a data gold rush. But the people whose data is in the records are pushing back. Rebecca says a union representing former Spirit flight attendants objected in court, arguing that employees personal information could be exposed. Google then agreed to some limitations on what data it's getting, like no medical records or bank records before a bankruptcy judge approved the sale. I think setting aside the nuances of bankruptcy law, this is a new frontier for labor law and labor issues in the AI era when people are concerned that rightfully so that their professional data might be signed away to the highest bidder to train AI products.
