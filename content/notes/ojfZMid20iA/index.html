黄金的隐性成本：亚马逊雨林中的汞污染与健康危机 TED 10/31/2025

领域: society-thinking

分类: general

主讲人: TED

来源: 查看

发布时间: 10/31/2025

摘要:

Claudia Vega揭示了手工黄金开采对亚马逊雨林造成的严重汞污染及其对环境和人类健康的深远影响。全球约20%的黄金来自这种开采方式，导致大规模毁林，并释放大量剧毒汞。文章探讨了汞的神经毒性、对原住民的危害，以及如何通过科学研究和信息传播来推动更负责任的采矿方式和消费选择，强调森林的价值远超黄金。

序言：森林的价值与黄金的代价

从小我就热爱大自然，我一直喜欢置身森林的感觉。

View/Hide Original English

Since I was little, I always loved nature. I always loved the feeling of being in a forest.

对我而言，森林比黄金更有价值。

View/Hide Original English

For me, the forest is more valuable than gold.

我叫Claudia Vega，在秘鲁亚马逊（Peruvian Amazon: 南美洲秘鲁境内的亚马逊雨林区域）工作。

View/Hide Original English

My name is Claudia Vega and I work in the Peruvian Amazon.

我正在研究马德雷德迪奥斯（Madre de Dios: 秘鲁东南部的一个省份，以其丰富的生物多样性和手工黄金开采而闻名）地区因手工黄金开采（artisanal gold mining: 使用简单工具和方法进行的小规模黄金开采）造成的汞污染问题。

View/Hide Original English

I'm studying mercury contamination due to artisanal gold mining in Madre de Dios.

我认为人们没有意识到，有时你购买的黄金可能正在毒害亚马逊雨林或其他热带地区。

View/Hide Original English

I think people are not aware that sometimes the gold that you buy maybe is poisoning the rainforest in the Amazon or other tropical areas.

手工黄金开采：汞污染的全球性根源

全球约有20%的黄金产自手工黄金开采，这个比例并不小。

View/Hide Original English

About 20 percent of the gold produced in the world come from artisanal gold mining. It's not that small.

当提到“手工”时，这并非因为它天然，而是因为他们不使用昂贵的设备。

View/Hide Original English

And when you say artisanal, it's not because it's natural. It's called artisanal because they don't use fancy equipment.

他们采用一种非常原始的方式，自加利福尼亚淘金热以来，人们就一直在使用这种方法，当时他们就使用汞（mercury: 一种有毒的重金属元素）。

View/Hide Original English

They have a very, like, rustic way. People have been doing that since the gold rush in California. They were using mercury.

所以，自那时起，这项技术并没有太大进步。

View/Hide Original English

So the technique hasn't advanced that much since then.

使用汞是因为它是一种简便的黄金提取方法。

View/Hide Original English

Mercury is used because it's an easy way to extract the gold.

矿工们的操作是，黄金通常以沙粒状存在于河流的沉积物中。

View/Hide Original English

So what the miners do is, the gold is like, in the sandy form, and it's usually in the sediment of the rivers.

他们加入汞，汞会与黄金结合，形成汞齐（amalgam: 黄金和汞的混合物）。

View/Hide Original English

They put mercury, the mercury binds with gold, then they have the amalgam, which is a mix of gold and mercury.

然后他们燃烧汞齐，汞就会蒸发到空气中，留下黄金。

View/Hide Original English

And then they burn the amalgam. And then the mercury just evaporates to the air. And they got the gold.

在与黄金混合的过程中，一些汞会进入河流。

View/Hide Original English

When they're doing the mixing with gold, some of the mercury goes into the river.

汞很容易蒸发，无需加热就能进入空气，并能随风传播很远的距离。

View/Hide Original English

It's easy to evaporate, you don't have to heat it, it goes to the air, and it can be transported like, long distance.

这主要发生在热带地区，是地球上最大的人为汞污染来源。

View/Hide Original English

This happens mainly in the tropics and is the biggest anthropogenic source of mercury on the planet.

每年约有1400吨汞因手工黄金开采而被释放到环境中。

View/Hide Original English

Around 1,400 tons of mercury annually is released into the environment because of artisanal gold mining.

这是一个国际性的问题，因为它不仅危害使用汞的地区。

View/Hide Original English

It's an international concern. And it's not because it harms just the place where it's used.

例如，甚至在北极的白鲸体内也发现了非常高水平的汞。

View/Hide Original English

For example, there are even very high levels of mercury in the whales, in the beluga whales, in the Arctic.

环境破坏与健康危害

在马德雷德迪奥斯，约有13万公顷的森林因手工黄金开采而被砍伐。

View/Hide Original English

In Madre de Dios, about 130,000 hectares has been deforested because of artisanal gold mining.

这是一个大规模的作业，你可以看到大片雨林被砍伐殆尽。

View/Hide Original English

It is a big operation, you can see huge areas of rainforest just deforested.

它看起来像一个布满采矿池的沙漠，有许多人工湖。

View/Hide Original English

It looked like a desert with mining ponds. There are artificial lakes.

在许多沙漠般的区域中，没有树木，也没有动物。

View/Hide Original English

And in the middle of a lot of desert, there's no trees, there's no animals.

由于没有植被，它看起来有点像月球表面。

View/Hide Original English

It looks like the moon, kind of, because there's no vegetation.

汞是一种我们无法销毁的元素，它是一种强效的神经毒素（neurotoxin: 能够损害神经系统并影响其功能的毒素）。

View/Hide Original English

Mercury is an element we cannot destroy. It is a potent neurotoxin.

它会影响神经系统，还会引起头痛、记忆力问题和失眠。

View/Hide Original English

It can affect the neurosystem, it also can give you headaches, can give you problems with memory. It can give you insomnia.

它可能在怀孕期间伤害儿童，导致婴儿出现学习障碍。

View/Hide Original English

It can harm children during pregnancy, you could have a baby who will have learning disabilities.

根据暴露程度，甚至可能导致畸形。

View/Hide Original English

Depending on how much is exposed, it can even have malformation.

汞被称为“沉默的毒素”，因为它需要一段时间才能显现出影响。

View/Hide Original English

Mercury is called the silent toxin because it takes a while to show effects.

日本的水俣湾（Minamata Bay: 日本的一个海湾，因20世纪中叶的严重汞中毒事件而闻名）是汞中毒更具代表性的案例。

View/Hide Original English

Minamata Bay in Japan is a more iconic case of mercury poisoning.

1932年，一家公司向海湾排放汞。

View/Hide Original English

In 1932 there was a company that was releasing mercury into the bay.

大约25年后，人们才开始出现中毒症状。

View/Hide Original English

It took about 25 years to show the effects in people.

大约1000人死亡，至今已有约1万人被确认为受影响者。

View/Hide Original English

About 1,000 people died, and until now they have certified like 10,000 people that were affected.

所以人们并不知道他们正在中毒，也没有感觉到。

View/Hide Original English

So people didn't know they were being intoxicated and they didn't feel it.

但最终，他们出现了症状并因此死亡。

View/Hide Original English

But at the end, they had the symptoms and people died.

世界卫生组织（WHO: World Health Organization，联合国下属的国际公共卫生机构）表示，汞是十大对公众健康构成重大威胁的化学品之一。

View/Hide Original English

WHO says that mercury is one of the top ten chemicals that are a major concern for public health.

科学创新与社区赋能

亚马逊科学创新中心（Centro de Innovación Científica Amazónica: 致力于在亚马逊地区进行科学研究和创新的机构），我们在秘鲁亚马逊建立了第一个汞实验室。

View/Hide Original English

Centro de Innovación Científica Amazónica, which is Amazonian Center for Scientific Innovation, we implemented the first mercury lab in the Peruvian Amazon.

它就位于雨林深处，在丛林之中。

View/Hide Original English

And it's in the middle of the rainforest. It's in the middle of the jungle.

我们进行科学研究，也将科学知识以非科学家能理解的方式传播给大众，以便他们能获取信息并做出更好的决策。

View/Hide Original English

We produce science. [We] also communicate the science to people that are not scientists in a way that they can have information to make better decision.

我们与矿工合作，向他们解释汞的危害；也与原住民合作，向他们解释如何避免接触汞。

View/Hide Original English

We work with miners and also explain to them the dangers of mercury and with Indigenous people, also explain to them how they can avoid being exposed.

此外，我们还与政治家合作，以便他们能基于科学做出决策。

View/Hide Original English

And also we work with politicians so they can base their decisions on science.

鱼是亚马逊原住民主要的蛋白质来源。

View/Hide Original English

Fish is the main source of protein for Indigenous people in the Amazon.

他们通过食用受污染的鱼类，接触到非常高水平的汞。

View/Hide Original English

They are exposed to very high levels of mercury through consuming contaminated fish.

他们目前的汞暴露水平是报告中最高的之一。

View/Hide Original English

They have one of the highest levels reported right now.

所以我们与他们合作的一件事是，向他们解释他们必须选择更安全的鱼类。

View/Hide Original English

So one thing that we do with them, we explain to them that they have to choose safer fish.

有时这非常困难，因为你无法给他们一个完美的答案。

View/Hide Original English

Sometimes it's very difficult because you don't have the right answer for them.

我的意思是，他们住在自己的家园，然后陌生人过来告诉他们正在吃的东西有毒。

View/Hide Original English

I mean, they're in their home and then strange people come, telling them that what they are eating is poisoned.

但我认为信息就是力量，拥有正确的信息可以帮助你做出正确的决定。

View/Hide Original English

But I think information is power, and having the right information may make you do the right decision.

所以我们坚持这一点：提供信息并重复信息。

View/Hide Original English

So we insist on that: give the information and repeat the information.

一些矿工告诉我们：“我这样做已经30年了，所以我很好。”

View/Hide Original English

Some of the miners tell us, "I have been doing this for 30 years, so I'm good."

我问他们：“你睡得好吗？你经常头痛吗？你是否更容易忘记事情？”

View/Hide Original English

I asked them like, "Do you sleep? Do you have headaches? Do you forget things more often?"

而且汞有时还会增加患心血管疾病的几率。

View/Hide Original English

And also mercury sometimes can increase the chance of having cardiovascular disease.

所以也许有些人血压很高，却不知道原因，这可能就是因为汞。

View/Hide Original English

So maybe some of the people have high pressure and they don't know why. It could be because of mercury.

其中一些人正在转向更负责任的采矿方式。

View/Hide Original English

And some of them are changing for a more responsible way of mining.

另一些矿工可能知道危害，但他们不在乎，因为他们需要钱，而黄金就是钱。

View/Hide Original English

Some other miners, maybe they know, but they don't care because they need money. And gold is money.

消费者责任与地球健康

目前没有激励措施来生产无汞黄金。

View/Hide Original English

There are not incentives to produce mercury-free gold.

人们只是购买黄金，他们不在乎，也没有良好的可追溯系统。

View/Hide Original English

People just buy gold, they don't care, there's no good traceability system.

所以我认为作为消费者或黄金贸易的中间商，我们必须关心黄金的来源。

View/Hide Original English

So I think as a consumer or as a mediator of gold trade, we have to care about where the gold is coming from.

有时我们使用一些东西，却甚至不知道它们对世界产生了多大的影响。

View/Hide Original English

Sometimes we use things and we don't even know how much impact they have in the world.

归根结底，我们只有一个健康、一个地球，万物皆有联系。

View/Hide Original English

At the end, we have one health, one planet, and everything is connected.

当你影响环境时，你也会影响动物的健康，进而影响人类的健康。

View/Hide Original English

When you are affecting the environment, you can affect the animals' health and then human health, too.

所以对我来说，我的梦想是人们能看到森林的价值。

View/Hide Original English

So for me, my dream is like, people can see the value of the forest.

它实际上正在为我们做贡献，它生产空气，生产水。

View/Hide Original English

It's actually doing something for us. It's producing air, it's producing water.

这比黄金更有价值。

View/Hide Original English

That's more valuable than gold.

我知道黄金有价值，但你不能吃它，不能呼吸它，也不能喝它。

View/Hide Original English

I know gold is valuable, but you cannot eat it, you cannot breathe it, and you cannot drink it.
