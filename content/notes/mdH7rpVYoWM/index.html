加拿大安省学生教育负担：抗议、政策与未来 TVO Today 2026-03-06

领域: society-thinking

分类: politics-society

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

摘要:

加拿大安大略省学生联合会主席 Cyrielle Ngeleka 讨论了教育可负担性危机，批评了 OSAP 资助模式的改变、学费上涨以及政府资金投入不足，并呼吁建立真正由公共财政支持的可持续教育体系。

安省学生教育负担与政策争议

As a student that's almost going to graduate, I'm reconsidering whether graduate studies is the path that I want to take. And for many students who are about to enter the post-secondary education sector are wondering how they're even going to pay for education to begin with. Right.

It's still money moving in the wrong direction. And although I don't have necessarily exact numbers program per program, one can understand and expect that a 2% increase in the first year is going to be compounded over the years. At the end of the day, students never asked for an increase in tuition fees. And if the government truly cared about, you know, students and a sustainable education sector, they would not be increasing tuition fees at all.

Original English

Cyrielle Ngeleka: Right.
