本次TED演讲深入探讨了语言如何被用来制造“邪教式”思维，并呼吁听众提高“邪教素养”。演讲者通过泰勒·斯威夫特粉丝群体、Synanon邪教的案例，揭示了思想终止式陈词滥调、‘我们 vs. 他们’的标签以及加载语言这三大语言诡计。演讲强调了认知偏见在信息时代如何被放大，并提供了识别和防范的实用建议，鼓励人们在保持社群归属感的同时，独立思考，不被信息洪流裹挟。

揭示“邪教式”思维：从粉丝文化到语言策略

各位观众，请坐好。因为我将与你们分享一个关于世界上最狂热的邪教之一的真实故事。这个故事发生在邪教的圣日。凌晨时分，信徒们抵达了现场，有些人跨越海洋，牺牲了毕生积蓄，才得以抵达。他们带来了手工串珠的祭品，上面刻着神圣的数字：22、13、89。但各位，这不是一个关于遥远基地里末日邪教的故事。不，那是一场泰勒·斯威夫特（Taylor Swift）的演唱会。我说出来了。护身符是友谊手链，圣经般的篇章被称为“时代”（eras），而那位魅力四射的领袖，是一位亿万富翁的流行乐女祭司，老实说，如果她真的想，她几乎可以统治自由世界。

现在，请不要误会我的意思。我是“Red”专辑的铁杆粉丝。我在这里不是要指责“Swifties”是邪教信徒。不，我绝不敢。但作为一位拥有语言学背景的作家和文化评论家，我在这里分享我们每个人是如何——无论好坏——都容易受到“邪教式”思维的影响。我们日常的词汇就是我们忠诚的证据。我在这里分享需要注意什么，需要倾听什么，这样，在我们经历这些不可避免的“邪教式”时代时，我们就能保持既着迷又赋权。

我对邪教的迷恋源于个人。那是因为我的父亲。在我十几岁的时候，他被迫加入了Synanon，这是一个70年代加州的团体，有着统一的背带裤和一种名为“The Game”的创伤性真相揭露仪式。但我的父亲逃了出来，成了一名神经科学家，并抚养了一个爱管闲事的孩子，这个孩子痴迷于理解如何在日常生活中识别“邪教式”的影响。随着我长大，我注意到我父亲在Synanon描述的那些语言策略，几乎随处可见。比如，在我高中的戏剧社，在健康产业，以及在我的社交媒体动态中。我就是这样开始研究“邪教光谱”的，影响的程度各不相同，其中没有任何一个是以LSD和长袍开始的，而是悄悄地，通过语言。

Acolytes arrived at dawn, some having crossed oceans and sacrificed life savings in order to get there. They came bearing hand-beaded offerings inscribed with sacred numbers: 22, 13, 89. But this, my friends, is not the story of an apocalyptic sect on a faraway compound. No, this was a Taylor Swift concert. (Laughter) I said it. The talismans are friendship bracelets, the biblical books are known as eras, and the charismatic leader is a billionaire pop priestess who, let's be honest, could probably rule the free world if she really wanted to. Now, don't get me wrong. I'm a deep-dyed "Red" album girlie. I'm not here to call out Swifties as cult followers. No, I wouldn't dare. But I'm an author and a cultural commentator with a background in linguistics. And I’m here to share how we’re all susceptible to cult-ish thinking, for better and for worse. And our everyday vocabularies are evidence of our devotion. I'm here to share what to pay attention to, what to listen for, so that, as we move through these inevitably culty times, we can stay both enchanted and empowered. Now, my fascination with cults is personal. That's because of my dad. As a teenager, he was forced to join Synanon, a '70s California compound with matching overalls and a traumatizing truth-telling ritual called The Game. But my dad escaped, became a neuroscientist, and brought up a nosy kid who became obsessed with understanding how to identify cultish influence in everyday life. As I got older, I couldn't help but notice that the same language tactics that my dad described in Synanon could be found kind of everywhere. Like, in my high school theater program and in the wellness industry and on my social media feed. That's how I came to study the cultish spectrum, degrees of influence, none of which start out with LSD and robes, but instead, sneakily, with words.

语言诡计的运作机制：陈词滥调、标签化与认知捷径

我想指出三种在日常生活中需要留意的“邪教式”语言策略。第一种叫做思想终止式陈词滥调（thought-terminating cliche）。它由精神病学家罗伯特·杰伊·利夫顿（Robert Jay Lifton）在1961年提出，这类陈词滥调是简洁有力、易于记忆和重复的表达方式，旨在扼杀独立思考和质疑。假设你是一个团体的成员，想要反驳某条规则，你可能会被一句“相信过程”（trust the process）或“一切都是神的计划”（it's all in God's plan）来让你闭嘴。在Synanon，他们常用“就好像”（act as if）这句话，意思就是假装相信，直到你真的相信为止。如今，在阴谋论类群体中，“去做你的研究”（do your research）这句话基本就等于“别再问我关于我的研究了”。

接下来，我想谈谈“我们”与“他们”的标签。在Synanon，叛逃者被称为“splitees”。如今，你有你的“sheeple”（愚民）、“NPC”（无思考能力的群体）、“industry plants”（行业内鬼/托）。当一个标签让所有那些人看起来全然邪恶而我们高人一等时，这就是一个危险信号。

第三，我想提一下加载语言（loaded language）。“企业协同愿景家”（Corporate synergistic visionaries）。“健康五维意识”（Wellness 5D consciousness）。起初，像这样的情绪化流行语会让你感觉像是获得了启蒙。但有一天你醒来，会发现你已经完全放弃了自己说话和思考的能力。

这种语言之所以有效，是因为它直接利用了我们的认知偏见（cognitive biases）。这些根深蒂固的决策捷径，是在早期人类大脑中形成的，帮助我们处理周围世界的信息以求生存。但如今，像确认偏见（confirmation bias）、沉没成本谬误（sunk cost fallacy）和光环效应（halo effect）这样的心智魔法技巧，导致我们只相信我们已经认同的信息，在不靠谱的选择上加倍投入，并将从未谋面的凡人崇拜为全知的神明。我们曾经有用的认知偏见与信息时代的这种冲突，就是我称之为“魔法式过度思考”（magical overthinking）的现象。

Original English

I want to point out three cultish language tactics to listen for in everyday life. The first is called the thought-terminating cliche. Coined in 1961 by the psychiatrist Robert Jay Lifton, thought-terminating cliches are zingy stack expressions that are easy to memorize, easy to repeat and aimed at shutting down independent thinking and questioning. So let's say you're a member of a group, and there's a rule that you want to push back against. You might get hit with a phrase like, "trust the process," or "it's all in God's plan" to shut you down. In Synanon, the phrase "act as if" effectively meant pretend that you believe until you do. Today, in conspiracy theory-type groups, the phrase “do your research” basically means “stop asking me about mine.” (Laughter) Next, I want to talk about "us" versus "them" labels. In Synanon, defectors were called "splitees." Today, you’ve got your “sheeple,” your “NPCs,” your “industry plants.” When a label makes all of those people seem unilaterally evil and us superior, that's a red flag. And thirdly, I want to mention loaded language. Corporate synergistic visionaries. Wellness 5D consciousness. At first, emotionally charged buzzwords like this feel like enlightenment. Then one day you wake up and you realize you've completely surrendered your ability to talk and think for yourself. This language works because it plugs straight into our cognitive biases. These deeply ingrained decision-making shortcuts that developed in earlier human brains to help us process information from the world around us enough to survive it. But today, mental magic tricks like confirmation bias, the sunk cost fallacy, and the halo effect cause us to believe only the information we already agree with, double down on sketchy choices and worship mortal human beings we've never even met as all-knowing deities. This clash between our once useful cognitive biases and the information age is this phenomenon that I've been calling magical overthinking.

信息时代的审慎：成为“邪教素养”达人

这是一个问题，因为研究表明社交媒体损害了我们的心理健康和注意力，同时又让“邪教式”领导者极易接触到。当你拥有评论区时，谁还需要基地呢？现在，我这么说并不是要吓到任何人。我只是想指出“敬畏”与“灌输”之间的区别。我想留给大家几点小建议来帮助我们做到这一点。

首先，当你发现自己身处一个环境，即使是数字空间，当你感到情绪高度激动，并且使用了大量让你觉得自己属于某个部落（tribe）的流行语，但你却无法用简单的英语准确定义你在说什么，或者为什么这么说，那就意味着你应该退一步，咨询其他信息来源。

接下来，要关注退出成本（exit costs）。健康的人际关系或团体可能会让离开感觉有些尴尬，但绝不会是末日般的或毁灭性的。

最后，我们可以利用“邪教语言”来做好事。振奋人心的口号、押韵的咒语，也可以用来让真实的信息变得朗朗上口。我不是来剥夺任何人手中的友谊手链。我们比以往任何时候都更需要社群。所以我认为，生活在这个有史以来最“邪教化”的时代，目标不是要变得“邪教免疫”（cult-proof），而是要成为“邪教素养达人”（cult-literate）。听明白了吗？

Original English

And it's a problem because studies show that social media has damaged our mental health and our attention spans, all the while making cultish leaders mega accessible. Who needs compounds when you have comment sections? Now, I don't say this to freak anyone out. I'm just here to point out the difference between awe and indoctrination. Next, pay attention to exit costs. Healthy groups might make leaving feel awkward, but never apocalyptic or earth-shattering. And finally, we can use cult language for good. Rousing chants, rhyming mantras, they can be used to make true information catchy, too. I'm not here to take away anyone's friendship bracelets. We need community more now than ever.
