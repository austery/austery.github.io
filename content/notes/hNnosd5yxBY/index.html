摘要:

本期简报报道了美军在伊朗海岸扣押货轮引发的军事紧张局势，以及特朗普政府启动1600亿美元关税退款的程序。此外，还探讨了美国务院官员在欧洲推动右翼议程、研究生联邦贷款上限调整对学生的影响，以及加州发生的“熊”式保险诈骗等奇葩案件。

美军在伊朗海岸扣押货轮

Tracy Mumford: 欢迎收听《纽约时报》简报。我是 Tracy Mumford。今天是 4 月 20 日星期一。以下是我们要报道的内容。

[广播录音]: 动力船舶，动力船舶。请撤离你们的机舱。

Original English

[Recording]: Motor vessel. Motor vessel. Vacate your engine room.

Tracy Mumford: 昨天在 伊朗 海岸附近，美国海军 对一艘试图绕过美国封锁的船只进行了打击并将其扣押，这似乎是美军首次此类攻击。被扣押的是一艘伊朗货轮。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Off the coast of Iran yesterday, the US Navy carried out what appeared to be its first attack on a ship trying to get around the American blockade, striking and seizing an Iranian cargo vessel.

Tracy Mumford: 五角大楼 发布了事件视频，并发表声明称，一艘美国驱逐舰向该货轮的机舱开火，在船只无视停船要求并继续驶向伊朗港口后，致其失去动力。随后，海军陆战队 从直升机索降并控制了船只。

今天凌晨，伊朗军方警告称将对此次被其称为 “武装海盗行径” 的袭击进行报复。根据伊朗媒体的报道，这次打击标志着伊朗周边关键水道混乱周末的顶峰。

特朗普总统 和伊朗此前都表示 霍尔木兹海峡 (Strait of Hormuz) 在周五是开放的。但在周六，伊朗外交部长表示该国将再次对海峡实施 “严格控制”，随后伊朗对两艘试图通过的船只开火。目前，美国和伊朗均指责对方违反了脆弱的、将于周二到期的两周停火协议。

随着时间紧迫，特朗普表示美国谈判代表正前往 巴基斯坦 进行新一轮谈判。但伊朗今天表示没有计划参加，指责美国对通过外交途径解决问题缺乏诚意。

关税退款程序启动

Tracy Mumford: 今天，特朗普政府正采取初步措施，退还其征收的超过 1600 亿美元关税。特朗普总统去年实施了这些关税，声称这是提振美国制造业和平衡全球贸易失衡所必需的。但 最高法院 随后裁定部分关税违宪。

现在，政府正在推出一个系统供企业提交退款申请。但只有正式支付关税的实体（如 联邦快递 (FedEx) 或 好市多 (Costco)）才能收回资金。这意味着受关税影响的更广泛群体——包括数百万支付了更高产品价格的美国人——无法申请直接补偿。

即使退款申报系统已经运行，仍存在大量不确定性。因为涉及的公司数量庞大，需要处理数百亿资金，而且特朗普政府尚未明确是否会再次通过法律手段阻止退款发放。时报采访了一位女性户外服装公司的创始人，她希望能从联邦政府获得约 25 万美元的退款。但她补充道：“我一点也不乐观这些钱能及时到账。”

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Today, the Trump administration is taking its first steps toward returning more than $ 160 billion it collected in tariffs. President Trump put the duties in place last year, claiming they were necessary to boost American manufacturing and even out global trade imbalances. But the Supreme Court later struck some of the tariffs down. Now the administration is debuting a system for businesses to submit refund requests. But only the entities that officially paid the tariffs will be able to recover money. Think FedEx or Costco. That means the broader group of people affected by the tariffs, including millions of Americans who paid higher prices for products are not able to apply for direct relief. And even as the refund filing system gets up and running, there's still plenty of uncertainty around it given how many companies could file, how many billions need to be processed, and the fact that the Trump administration hasn't said whether or not it will try to go back to court to fight giving the refunds at all. The Times talked with the founder of a women's outdoor clothing company who said she was hoping to get back around $250,000 in refunds from the federal government. But she added, quote, I wouldn't say I'm at all optimistic that they are going to come in a timely manner.

特朗普在欧洲的“信使”

Tracy Mumford: 最后关于特朗普政府的更新。在过去一年半里，美国彻底改变了与欧洲的关系。《纽约时报》一直在调查一名大学毕业仅五年的 国务院 雇员是如何成为这一变化的核心人物的。特别是，他在这些国家推动特朗普总统的 文化战争。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: And one last update on the Trump administration. Over the past year and a half, the US has drastically upended its relationship with Europe. And the Times has been looking at how one State Department employee just five years out of college has been at the center of that. In particular, driving President Trump's culture war in those countries.

Michael Shear: Sam Samson 绝不是典型的高级联邦雇员。他现年 27 岁，曾是一名保守派活动家，在极短的时间内晋升为国务院非常重要的高级顾问，实际上是特朗普总统在整个欧洲的信使。

Original English

Michael Shear: Sam Samson is not at all your typical highlevel federal employee. He's 27 years old, was a conservative activist, and in just a very short time has now risen to become a very important senior adviser at the State Department, and really a messenger for President Trump across Europe.

Tracy Mumford: 时报记者 Michael Shear 是调查团队的一员，他们访问了二十多位美国和欧洲的外交官、议员及前官员，以追踪 Samson 的足迹。

Samson 去年三月在英国与 Nigel Farage 进行了秘密会面，讨论堕胎和审查制度。五月，他在法国试图辩称极右翼领袖 Marine Le Pen（曾因挪用公款被定罪）受到了不公正的迫害。他还会见了德国右翼 AFD 党成员，德国情报部门已将该党列为疑似极端组织。

Samson 的部分行程是会见各国的极右翼团体，基本上是在问：“我们能提供什么帮助，来影响你们多年来一直追求的政策，比如言论自由、移民、反觉醒 (Anti-woke)、反多样性政策？现在基本上是 Samson 穿梭于欧洲说，美国政府想在这方面帮助你们。”

欧洲各国政府反应强烈，主要是因为他们一直在努力抑制这些极右翼政党在本国的影响力。因此，一名美国高级官员周游各国承诺支持这些团体，更重要的是承诺美国政府的支持，这确实让许多欧洲在任政治家感到恐惧。

针对时报的提问，Sam Samson 拒绝接受采访。国务院发言人在一份声明中表示，政府正在与欧洲进行所谓的 “艰难对话”，横跨大西洋的关系将因此变得 “更加强大”。

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Times correspondent Michael Shear is part of a team who talked to more than two dozen American and European diplomats, lawmakers, and former officials to trace Samson's efforts. Samson had a secret meeting with Nigel Farage in Britain last March to discuss abortion and censorship. He was in France in May trying to argue that Marine Le Pen, a far-right leader convicted of embezzlement, had been unjustly persecuted. And he met with members of Germany's right-wing AFD party, which German intelligence has designated as a suspected extremist organization. Part of Samson's travels have been meeting with far-right groups in each of these countries to essentially say, "What can we do to help you affect policies like free speech, migration, anti-woke, anti-diversity policies that these far-right groups have been pursuing for years. But now essentially Samson is the one traveling around Europe saying the United States government wants to help you with that."

The European governments have reacted with alarm mainly because they have themselves been fighting against some of these far-right parties to tamp down their influence throughout their countries. And so to have a senior US official traveling around the country pledging his support, but more importantly pledging the support of the United States to these groups has really been something that strikes fear into the hearts of many of the politicians that are in office right now across Europe. In response to questions from the Times, Sam Samson declined to be interviewed. In a statement, a State Department spokesman said that the administration is having what it called hard conversations with Europe and that the transatlantic relationship will be quote stronger because of it.

研究生贷款上限变动

Tracy Mumford: 在全美各地，现在正是学生决定是否接受研究生录取通知书以及是否负担得起的时节。二十年来，他们将首次无法通过 联邦学生贷款 自动覆盖全部学费。

从今年开始，政府对大多数研究生课程的此类贷款设定了上限，每年略高于 2 万美元。这远低于许多课程的实际成本。例如，想要成为助理医师或麻醉护士的顶尖课程每年收费约 4.5 万美元，这意味着无法自掏腰包的学生必须申请数万美元的 私人贷款。

这引发了一些民主党议员的担忧，他们指出私人学生贷款市场一直存在 掠夺性行为。学生贷款专家表示，对于一些学生来说，获得私人贷款可能是不可能的，因为这通常需要 共同签名人。一位学生借款人倡导组织的分析师告诉时报：“最终结果是，研究生教育将基本上只属于最富有家庭的学生。”

Original English

Tracy Mumford: Across the US, it's the time of year when students are deciding whether they want to say yes to grad school acceptance offers and whether or not they can afford to go. And for the first time in two decades, they won't be able to automatically cover the full price tag with federal student loans. Starting this year, the government has capped those loans for most graduate programs at just over $20,000 a year. And that's far less than what many of the programs actually cost. For example, top programs for students wanting to become physicians assistants or nurse anesthetists charge about $45,000 a year, meaning that students who can't pay out of pocket will have to sign up for tens of thousands in private loans. That's raised concerns among some Democratic lawmakers who have flagged that there's persistently been predatory behavior in the private student loan market. And student loan experts say getting a private loan at all could be impossible for some students since it often requires having a co-signer. One analyst at an advocacy group for student borrowers told the Times, quote, "The end effect is to essentially make it so that graduate school is only accessible to students from the wealthiest families."
