关键字 relational-realism perception-box sensory-processing cognitive-humility

摘要

心理学专家 Lisa Feldman Barrett 提出，现实既非纯粹客观也非纯粹主观，而是依赖视角的“关系实在论”。感知是大脑利用过往经验与当前感官信号共同构建的产物。面对分歧与冲突，我们无法诉诸单一的客观权威，而必须承认他人经验的等价真实性，通过保持好奇与谦逊来拓展对世界的感知边界。

超越客观与主观：关系实在论的提出

自人类掌握语言交流以来，关于现实本质的争论就从未停歇。传统观念倾向于认为，外部世界存在着一个独立于人类意识的客观现实，人们只是以不同程度的精确度对其进行感知。例如，在心理咨询室的经典场景中，若来访者表示自己感到极度愤怒，而咨询师却断言其本质上是悲伤，传统思维往往会追问究竟谁对谁错，或是判定双方皆偏离了客观事实。然而，神经心理学的最前沿探索表明，现实既非纯粹的客观存在，亦非封闭的纯主观幻象，而是一种关系实在（Relational Realism: 认为现实并非独立实体，而是外部物理信号与生物神经系统互动关系的具象涌现）。

现实是依赖于视角（Perspective-dependent）而存在的。在上述情境中，来访者体验到愤怒，咨询师观察并体验到悲伤，两者在交互中共同构成了当下的情境现实。作为美国东北大学心理学杰出教授，Lisa Feldman Barrett 指出，千百年来哲学家们关于“现实是客观存在还是大脑虚构”的二元对立辩论，从根本上就设错了问题前提。

英文原文

Since humans have been able to talk to each other, they've been debating the nature of reality. So traditionally, people believe that there is an objective reality out there, separate from us, and that we perceive reality with more or less accuracy. So if I'm in the therapist's office, and I'm telling the therapist, well, I am really, really mad. And the therapist looks at me and says, no, actually, you're really sad. Who's right? Well, there are some people who would say one of us is right or we're both wrong because there's some objective reality. But reality is not purely objective and it's not purely subjective. It is relational. There is one reality that is perspective dependent. I experience anger. My therapist is experiencing me as sad. And together, that's the reality of the situation. My name is Lisa Feldman Barrett. I am a university distinguished professor of psychology at Northeastern University in Boston, and I'm here to talk to you today about the nature of reality. You know, a small topic, the nature of reality. Is reality objective, out there, separate from you? Or is reality all in your head? Philosophers have been debating this question for millennia. And I think they're probably asking the wrong question.

感知黑盒：记忆经验与当下感官的协同建构

在确立了关系实在论的视角后，大脑生成具体感知的神经生理机制便清晰显现。人类所经历的每一次体验与采取的每一步行动，本质上都是记忆经验与当下感官输入的复合交融。外界环境中的物理扰动刺激视网膜、耳蜗毛细胞以及皮肤表层的机械感受器，形成特定的信号模式。脑科学的核心证据揭示：大脑无时无刻不在利用过去的经验，对来自外部世界的感官信号进行主动预测（Predictive Coding）与加工匹配。

这种运作机制催生了感知黑盒（Perception Box: 指由个体过往全部记忆、经验与概念系统所构成的认知过滤框架）的概念。感知黑盒刻画了颅内先验表征与外界物理世界之间的映射关联。在关系实在论的框架下，感知黑盒提供了调配与构建“当下关系现实”的核心原料。哲学中著名的森林落木寓言——“若树木在无人旷野中倒下，它究竟是否发出了声响？”——在此得到了精确解答。倒下的树木仅引起空气的机械振动；若要将其定义为“树木倒塌的轰鸣”，必须同时具备两个条件：

  • 存在匹配的生物感受器来探测并接收空气振动物理波；
  • 该生物体的神经系统具备将特定物理模式赋予语义意义的认知模型。

因此，如果缺乏能够检测并解码信号的有机体，树木倒下就仅仅是振动，并没有产生听觉意义上的“声音”。

英文原文

In reality, everything you do, and every experience that you ever have, is a combination of the remembered past and the sensory present. That is the signals that are hitting your retina in each eye, the cochlea in each ear, the receptors in your skin. The idea is that reality is made up of stable patterns of signals. Our best available evidence for how the brain is using past experience to anticipate sensory signals from the world, it suggests that reality may not be out there in the world, separate from you, and it's definitely not only in your head. It's actually a combination of the two. Which means that reality exists, but it exists in relation to you. Perception box is another way of talking about the remembered past. It's a concept that captures the idea that there is a relationship between what is inside your head and what is outside in the world. What relational realism implies for perception box is that your box is the ingredients, the signals that you bring to creating the relational present. So there's an old parable that people often appeal to when they're talking about the nature of reality. And it it goes something like this: If a tree falls in the forest and nobody is there to hear it, does it make a sound? A falling tree will cause vibrations in the air. So a relational realist would say there needs to be a set of receptors that detect those vibrations. And in order for it to be a sound of a tree falling, an organism has to know that that's what those sounds mean. So a relational realist would say, if there's no organism there to detect the signals, then no, the tree does not make a sound. There are other signals that are required to translate those vibrations into a sound.

跨物种感知鸿沟与人际冲突的化解范式

这种由感官受体与神经模型所决定的关系现实，不仅存在于微观生理层面，更广泛主导着跨物种及人类社会的复杂交互。非人生物往往配备了人类所不具备的感受表面，其所栖息的现实与人类截然不同。典型例如海洋中的低频振动波噪声，人类由于感知受限无法察觉，且这些物理波在人类认知中毫无意义；但对座头鲸等海洋哺乳动物而言，其专门演化的感知表层与大脑回路将这些振动纳入了其生存的核心现实之中。人类对其他物种感知视角的无知，直接造成了对其交流体系的严重干扰。

这一逻辑平移至人际交互与社会争端中，构成了化解冲突的根本法则。在日常争辩中，人们习惯性地诉诸所谓“客观现实”的至高权威，试图单方面论证自身立场的合法性。然而，一旦认同现实具有关系性特征，这种预设便不复成立。化解人际冲突的核心前提，是必须接纳一个根本原则：他人的主观经验对于现实的构建，具有与自身同等的权威与合法性。

承认对方体验的真实性绝不意味着否定自身感知的有效性，而是确认个体体验仅仅构成了整体现实的一个切面。现实包含共同分享的公约交集，也包含彼此完全无法直接通达的异质领域。如果个体长期浸泡在同质化的社交圈层中，与反应模式高度一致的人为伍，其认知系统就会不断强化既有的感知黑盒；唯有怀揣好奇心与认知谦逊（Epistemic Humility: 承认自身视角存在局限性并主动接纳多元维度的思维品质），主动探索全新信号与意义系统，才能有效破除认知壁垒，最大程度地拓宽触达现实完整面貌的可能空间。

英文原文

Animals have sensory surfaces that we don't have. Reality as it pertains to them, in relation to them, is different than it is for us. So an example would be, there's a lot of vibration-based noise in the ocean that we don't detect and is not really relevant to us. But it turns out that those signals are detected by whales. And those signals are because of the whale's sensory surfaces and the whale's brain are part of reality from their perspective. And we interfere with their ability to communicate with each other by our ignorance of their perspective. And it's not that hard to go from that kind of an idea to our interactions with each other. Often in an argument, you want to appeal to the authority of objective reality to say that you're right. And if reality is relational, you can't do that. In order to resolve the conflict, it means you have to admit the idea that somebody else's experience has equivalent authority to your own. The idea that somebody else's experience is part of reality as much as yours doesn't invalidate your experience. It means that your experience is a part of reality, but it's not the only part. There are parts of reality that we share, and there are parts of reality that each of us has access to that the other doesn't. If you spend time around people who are just like you, who have the same reactions as you, or the same experiences as you, you'll be reinforcing the remembered past that's easy for you to conjure. But if you are curious, if you're foraging for new sets of signals, new meanings, you are very likely to expand the aspects of reality that you have access to. The goal there is to be curious and be humble. As a person, I think it's incumbent upon us -- we really ought to experience as many aspects of reality as we can. Otherwise, we're ignorant, and ignorance is not helpful. It has nothing to do with objectivity. It has everything to do with being able to experience as much of reality as possible.
