全美多系统故障与漏洞修补潮

我知道过去这一周对许多美国人来说是一段令人不安的时期。显而易见，我们在过去一周看到了一系列技术故障对人们的生活造成了影响。所以我想首先从……

国务卿马尔科·卢比奥（Marco Rubio）正试图回应美国人对连环发生并冲击日常系统的技术故障与瘫痪的担忧。身兼特朗普总统国家安全顾问的卢比奥发表讲话之前，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）和微软刚出现故障，随后主要银行、苹果公司甚至美国国家安全局（NSA）也接连遭遇了问题。

英文原文 >> I know the last week has been a troubling time for many Americans. Obviously, we've seen a series of technical failures over the last week impact people's lives. So I want to begin by

Secretary of State Marco Rubio is trying to address Americans' concerns about the cascade of tech glitches and outages that have been hitting everyday systems. Rubio, who is also President Trump's national security advisor, spoke after troubles at the FAA and Microsoft were followed quickly by issues at major banks, Apple, and even the NSA.

我首先想明确的一点是，我们没有任何证据。我们没有任何迹象、没有任何证据，也完全没有任何理由相信这些事件之间存在任何相互关联。

卢比奥表示，截至目前，这些中断事件没有任何迹象指向网络攻击。相反，他提出，为了防止系统被新的AI能力黑入而急于给系统打补丁，可能才是导致故障的原因。

Rubio said so far nothing about the disruptions pointed to cyber attacks. Instead, he suggested that the rush to patch systems before they're hacked by new AI capabilities may be what's causing the failures.

所以有时候，当你因为发现了一个漏洞而进去修复系统时，会在系统的运行方式中造成短期的中断。

许多网络安全专家对这些故障也一直围绕着类似的解释，而且紧急修补的热潮近期也不会停止。昨天，一家网络安全初创公司披露，它在Java中发现了一个严重的软件漏洞；Java是被政府机构、医院、航空业以及90%的世界500强企业所使用的编程语言。该初创公司利用AI发现的这一漏洞，可能使黑客远程获取敏感内部系统的访问权限。

针对这一Java问题的一个补丁现已发布，美国政府正在敦促各机构立即处理该漏洞。

Yesterday, a cybersecurity startup revealed that it had uncovered a critical software bug in Java, the programming language that's used by government agencies, hospitals, the aviation industry, and 90% of Fortune 500 companies. The bug, which the startup used AI to identify, could enable a hacker to gain remote access to sensitive internal systems.

A patch for the Java issue has since been released, and the US government is urging organizations to immediately address the vulnerability.

政府调查报告：AI聊天机器人潜在安全风险

[音乐] 接下来是另一项关键的科技进展。今天上午发布的一项政府调查显示，AI聊天机器人如何能被用于策划造成大规模伤亡的袭击。

在测试过程中，研究人员成功绕过了四款聊天机器人的安全限制，促使它们生成可用于策划暴力袭击或针对该国经济机构袭击的信息。

这份来自美国政府问责局（GAO）的报告涉及了ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini和Grok。

其中两款模型生成了可用于制造或引爆简易爆炸装置（IED）的结果。在另一项测试中，两款模型生成了有助于瞄准电网的信息。有时，它们甚至提供了实施袭击的日期和时间，以及实现最大破坏的指南。

英文原文 [music] Now another critical tech development. This morning, a government investigation released [music] showing how AI chatbots could be used to plan mass casualty attacks.

Over the course of testing, researchers were able to bypass safety restrictions in four chatbots, prodding them to generate information that could be used in planning either violent attacks or attacks on the country's economic institutions.

The report from the Government Accountability Office involved ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.

Two of the models generated results that could be used to build or detonate an IED. And in another test, two models generated information that would help target the electrical grid. At some points, they supplied dates and times of when to carry out an attack and guides to maximize damage.

报告并未具体说明哪些模型通过或未通过特定测试，因为他们不希望其实验被复现。

这些聊天机器人背后的公司——Anthropic、谷歌、OpenAI和SpaceX AI——均对该报告作出了回应，称他们已在更新的模型中作出了改进以防止滥用。

委托开展该政府报告的新罕布什尔州民主党籍参议员玛吉·哈桑（Maggie Hassan）表示，她计划在明年初举行听证会，审查AI带来的安全风险，旨在制定法律防线。

英文原文 The report didn't specify which models passed or failed the specific tests because they didn't want their experiments to be recreated.

The companies behind the chatbots, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and SpaceX AI, all responded to the report saying they had made improvements to prevent misuse in their more recent models.

The senator who commissioned the government report, Maggie Hassan, a Democrat from New Hampshire, said she plans to hold hearings early next year examining the security risks posed by AI with the goal of developing legal guardrails.

民主党赢得阿拉斯加州参议院席位

[音乐] 昨晚，中期选举最后一项未决的参议院竞选有了结果，民主党人在阿拉斯加州斩获胜利。据美联社报道，玛丽·佩尔托拉（Mary Peltola）在这个保守州将该席位翻蓝。

这场竞选受到密切关注，部分原因是围绕谁最终登上选票的争议。据《纽约时报》报道，民主党人招募了一名名字与佩尔托拉的共和党对手几乎完全相同的候选人，试图以此分流选票。在美国，选票堆叠有着悠久的历史，这并不违法，但引发了共和党人的强烈不满。最终，佩尔托拉似乎以大约13,000票的优势获胜。民主党招募的搅局候选人获得了不到5,000票。

她的获胜使民主党在参议院的多数席位达到56席，该党在众议院的优势本周也上升至257席，目前仍有几场竞选尚未公布结果。

英文原文 [music] Last night, the final outstanding Senate race of the midterms was called with Democrats notching a victory in Alaska. According to the Associated Press, Mary Peltola flipped the seat in the conservative state blue.

The race had been closely watched in part because of a controversy surrounding who ended up on the ballot. The Times reported that Democrats recruited a candidate with almost the exact same name as Peltola's Republican opponent in an effort to try and peel off votes. There's a long history of ballot stacking in the US, which is not illegal, but it drew outrage from Republicans. In the end, Peltola appears to have won by roughly 13,000 votes. The spoiler candidate the Democrats recruited got just under 5,000.

Her win brings Democrats' majority in the Senate to 56, and the party's margin in the House also ticked up this week to 257 with a few races still to be called.

美国失去麻疹消除认证地位

对公共卫生而言是一次令人震惊的倒退：美国已正式失去了其麻疹消除状态。该状态由泛美卫生组织（PAHO）判定，该机构昨天宣布了这一令人担忧的里程碑。

其官方指标是：高度传染性的病毒在一国境内持续传播超过一年。

在始于2025年1月的最近一轮美国疫情期间，已有10人死亡，数百人住院，其中许多是儿童。

英文原文 In a stunning setback for public health, the US has officially lost its measles elimination status. That status is decided by the Pan American Health Organization, which announced the ominous milestone yesterday.

Its official metric is when the highly contagious virus has been spreading in a country for more than a year uninterrupted.

Over the course of the recent US outbreaks, which started in January 2025, 10 people have died and hundreds of people have been hospitalized, many of them children.

一些批评人士指出，失去麻疹消除状态是对小罗伯特·F·肯尼迪（Robert F. Kennedy Jr.）及其卫生政策的全民公决，因为麻疹疫情最严重的两年恰好与他担任卫生部长的任期相吻合。

我的同事阿普尔瓦·曼达维利（Apoorva Mandavilli）负责报道公共卫生领域。她说肯尼迪有时会嘲讽麻疹威胁、推广未经证实的治疗方法，并削弱公众对儿童疫苗的信心。

英文原文 >> Some critics say that the loss of the measles elimination status is a referendum on Robert F. Kennedy Jr. and his health policies because the two worst years of measles outbreaks have coincided precisely with his tenure as health secretary.

My colleague Apoorva Mandavilli reports on public health. She says Kennedy has at times mocked the threat of measles, promoted unproven treatments, and undermined confidence in childhood vaccines.

肯尼迪先生指出其他国家也失去了麻疹消除状态，这一点他说得没错。但我交流过的专家指出，疾控中心（CDC）现在采取的措施，远没有达到你通常在出现此类疫情时所期望的那种力度。在这种情况下，你会期望看到CDC主任频繁且大声疾呼接种疫苗的重要性，你会期望看到面向社区的宣传以提高疫苗接种率，但我们看到的这些非常少。要说有什么动向的话，那就是CDC一直与一些州就如何定义麻疹死亡病例发生争执，而且尚未完全确认各州报告的所有死亡病例。

阿普尔瓦表示，据公共卫生专家称，美国如果要控制住麻疹，就必须回归基本。这意味着要让疫苗接种率回升，让麻疹检测更快捷、更方便，并探索如何改进该疾病的治疗。麻疹在很长一段时间里都非常罕见，以至于许多医生都没有这方面的经验，因此医院和医疗机构目前正在加紧培训员工。

英文原文 >> Mr. Kennedy has pointed out that other countries have also lost their measles elimination status, and he is right about that. But the experts I talk to point out that the CDC is not taking anywhere near the kind of measures you would normally expect it to when there are outbreaks like this. In a situation like this, you would expect to see the CDC director talking often and loudly about the importance of vaccination, and you would expect to see outreaches to communities to increase vaccinations, but we've seen very little of that. If anything, the CDC has been engaging in fights with some states over how to define a measles death and has not yet fully acknowledged all of the deaths that states have reported.

Apoorva says that according to public health experts, the US has to go back to basics if it wants to get measles under control. That means getting vaccination rates back up, making it quicker and easier to test for measles, and figuring out how to improve treatment of the disease. Measles has been so rare for so long that many doctors have no experience with it, so hospitals and medical organizations are now scrambling to train their staff.
