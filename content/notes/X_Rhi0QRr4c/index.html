全美多领域遭遇技术故障与漏洞修补潮

国务卿马可·卢比奥（Marco Rubio）正试图缓解美国人对接连冲击日常系统的技术故障和停机的担忧。卢比奥同时担任特朗普总统的国家安全顾问，在联邦航空局（FAA）和微软出现问题，并紧接着波及主要银行、苹果甚至国家安全局（NSA）之后发表了讲话。

英文原文 >> Secretary of State Marco Rubio is trying to address Americans' concerns about the cascade of tech glitches and outages that have been hitting everyday systems. Rubio, who is also President Trump's national security advisor, spoke after troubles at the FAA and Microsoft were followed quickly by issues at major banks, Apple, and even the NSA.

我首先想明确的一点是，我们没有任何证据。我们没有任何迹象、没有任何证据，也完全没有理由相信这些事件之间存在任何关联。

英文原文 >> The first thing I want to make very clear is that we have zero evidence whatsoever. We have no indication, no evidence, and no reason to believe at all that any of these incidents are related to each other.

卢比奥表示，截至目前，没有任何迹象表明这些中断与网络攻击有关。相反，他指出，赶在系统被新型AI功能攻破之前仓促修补系统，可能正是导致这些故障的原因。

英文原文 >> Rubio said so far nothing about the disruptions pointed to cyber attacks. Instead, he suggested that the rush to patch systems before they're hacked by new AI capabilities may be what's causing the failures.

所以有时候，当你因为发现了一个漏洞而去修复系统时，就会在系统运行方式中造成短期的混乱。

英文原文 >> So sometimes when you are going in to fix your system because you've identified a vulnerability, it creates a short-term disruption in the way your system works.

许多网络安全专家也围绕类似的解释来分析这些故障，而修补系统的热潮短期内不会停止。昨天，一家网络安全初创公司披露，他们发现了一个关键的Java软件漏洞；Java是政府机构、医院、航空业以及90%的财富500强公司所使用的编程语言。该初创公司利用AI发现的这个漏洞，可能使黑客能够远程访问敏感的内部系统。针对该Java问题的补丁现已发布，美国政府正督促各机构立即处理这一漏洞。

英文原文 >> Many cyber experts have been circling around similar explanations for the outages, and the rush to patch things up is not going to stop anytime soon. Yesterday, a cybersecurity startup revealed that it had uncovered a critical software bug in Java, the programming language that's used by government agencies, hospitals, the aviation industry, and 90% of Fortune 500 companies. The bug, which the startup used AI to identify, could enable a hacker to gain remote access to sensitive internal systems. A patch for the Java issue has since been released, and the US government is urging organizations to immediately address the vulnerability.

政府报告揭露AI聊天机器人的安全风险

[音乐] 接下来是另一项关键的技术动态。今天上午，一项公布的政府调查 [音乐] 显示了AI聊天机器人可能如何被用于策划造成大规模伤亡的袭击。在测试过程中，研究人员成功绕过了四款聊天机器人的安全限制，促使它们生成可用于策划暴力袭击或针对该国经济机构袭击的信息。

英文原文 [music] Now another critical tech development. This morning, a government investigation released [music] showing how AI chatbots could be used to plan mass casualty attacks. Over the course of testing, researchers were able to bypass safety restrictions in four chatbots, prodding them to generate information that could be used in planning either violent attacks or attacks on the country's economic institutions.

这份来自政府问责署（GAO）的报告涉及ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini和Grok。其中两款模型生成的结果可用于制造或引爆简易爆炸装置（IED）。在另一项测试中，两款模型生成了有助于将电网作为攻击目标的信息。在某些测试环节中，它们还提供了实施袭击的日期与时间，以及最大限度增加破坏程度的指引。报告并未指明哪些模型通过或未通过具体测试，因为他们不希望其实验被复现。

英文原文 The report from the Government Accountability Office involved ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Two of the models generated results that could be used to build or detonate an IED. And in another test, two models generated information that would help target the electrical grid. At some points, they supplied dates and times of when to carry out an attack and guides to maximize damage. The report didn't specify which models passed or failed the specific tests because they didn't want their experiments to be recreated.

聊天机器人背后的公司——Anthropic、Google、OpenAI和SpaceX AI——均对报告作出回应，表示已在更新的模型中进行改进以防止滥用。委托开展该政府报告的新罕布什尔州民主党籍参议员玛吉·哈桑（Maggie Hassan）表示，她计划在明年初举行听证会，审查AI带来的安全风险，旨在制定法律层面的防范护栏。

英文原文 The companies behind the chatbots, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and SpaceX AI, all responded to the report saying they had made improvements to prevent misuse in their more recent models. The senator who commissioned the government report, Maggie Hassan, a Democrat from New Hampshire, said she plans to hold hearings early next year examining the security risks posed by AI with the goal of developing legal guardrails.

民主党赢得阿拉斯加州中期选举参议院席位

[音乐] 昨晚，中期选举中最后一场悬而未决的参议院竞选尘埃落定，民主党在阿拉斯加州取得胜利。据美联社报道，玛丽·佩尔托拉（Mary Peltola）使这个保守州的席位翻蓝。这场竞选一直备受瞩目，部分原因是围绕谁最终出现在选票上的争议。《纽约时报》报道称，民主党招募了一名名字与佩尔托拉的共和党对手几乎完全相同的候选人，试图以此分流选票。选票分流（ballot stacking）在美国有很长的历史，这并不违法，但也引来了共和党人的愤怒。最终，佩尔托拉似乎以大约13,000票的优势获胜。而民主党招募的那位搅局候选人获得了不到5,000票。她的获胜使民主党在参议院的多数优势扩大到56席，而本周该党在众议院的优势也微升至257席，目前仍有几场竞选结果尚未宣布。

英文原文 [music] Last night, the final outstanding Senate race of the midterms was called with Democrats notching a victory in Alaska. According to the Associated Press, Mary Peltola flipped the seat in the conservative state blue. The race had been closely watched in part because of a controversy surrounding who ended up on the ballot. The Times reported that Democrats recruited a candidate with almost the exact same name as Peltola's Republican opponent in an effort to try and peel off votes. There's a long history of ballot stacking in the US, which is not illegal, but it drew outrage from Republicans. In the end, Peltola appears to have won by roughly 13,000 votes. The spoiler candidate the Democrats recruited got just under 5,000. Her win brings Democrats' majority in the Senate to 56, and the party's margin in the House also ticked up this week to 257 with a few races still to be called.

美国失去麻疹消除状态

公共卫生领域遭遇重大挫折，美国已正式失去其麻疹消除状态。该状态由泛美卫生组织（PAHO）评定，该组织昨天宣布了这一令人担忧的节点。其官方判定标准是该高传染性病毒在一个国家持续传播超过一年且未被中断。在自2025年1月开始的美国近期疫情中，已有10人死亡，数百人住院，其中许多是儿童。

英文原文 In a stunning setback for public health, the US has officially lost its measles elimination status. That status is decided by the Pan American Health Organization, which announced the ominous milestone yesterday. Its official metric is when the highly contagious virus has been spreading in a country for more than a year uninterrupted. Over the course of the recent US outbreaks, which started in January 2025, 10 people have died and hundreds of people have been hospitalized, many of them children.

一些批评人士表示，失去麻疹消除状态是对罗伯特·F·肯尼迪二世（Robert F. Kennedy Jr.）及其卫生政策的一次公投，因为麻疹疫情最严重的两年恰好与他担任卫生部长的任期完全重合。

英文原文 >> Some critics say that the loss of the measles elimination status is a referendum on Robert F. Kennedy Jr. and his health policies because the two worst years of measles outbreaks have coincided precisely with his tenure as health secretary.

我的同事阿普瓦·曼达维利（Apoorva Mandavilli）负责报道公共卫生领域。她说，肯尼迪有时会嘲讽麻疹的威胁，推广未经验证的治疗方法，并削弱公众对儿童疫苗的信心。

英文原文 >> My colleague Apoorva Mandavilli reports on public health. She says Kennedy has at times mocked the threat of measles, promoted unproven treatments, and undermined confidence in childhood vaccines.

肯尼迪先生指出其他国家也失去了麻疹消除状态，在这点上他说得没错。但我交流过的专家指出，疾控中心（CDC）采取的措施，远没有达到人们在面临此类疫情时通常所期望的程度。在这样的情况下，你本会预期看到CDC主任频繁且大声疾呼接种疫苗的重要性，并且会预期看到向社区开展宣传以提高接种率，但我们极少看到这些行动。如果说有什么动作的话，那就是CDC一直在与某些州就如何定义麻疹死亡病例发生争执，并且尚未完全承认各州报告的所有死亡病例。

英文原文 >> Mr. Kennedy has pointed out that other countries have also lost their measles elimination status, and he is right about that. But the experts I talk to point out that the CDC is not taking anywhere near the kind of measures you would normally expect it to when there are outbreaks like this. In a situation like this, you would expect to see the CDC director talking often and loudly about the importance of vaccination, and you would expect to see outreaches to communities to increase vaccinations, but we've seen very little of that. If anything, the CDC has been engaging in fights with some states over how to define a measles death and has not yet fully acknowledged all of the deaths that states have reported.

阿普瓦表示，据公共卫生专家称，如果美国想要控制住麻疹，就必须回归基本面。这意味着要恢复疫苗接种率，使麻疹检测更加快捷简便，并摸索如何改善对该疾病的治疗。麻疹在很长一段时间内都非常罕见，以至于许多医生没有诊治经验，因此医院和医疗机构目前正在紧急培训其工作人员。

英文原文 >> Apoorva says that according to public health experts, the US has to go back to basics if it wants to get measles under control. That means getting vaccination rates back up, making it quicker and easier to test for measles, and figuring out how to improve treatment of the disease. Measles has been so rare for so long that many doctors have no experience with it, so hospitals and medical organizations are now scrambling to train their staff.
