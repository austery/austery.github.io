专访前特朗普官员：为伊朗战争与‘灵活现实主义’辩护 The Ezra Klein Show 2026-03-10

领域: geopolitics

分类: geopolitics

主讲人: The Ezra Klein Show

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-03-10

摘要:

本期节目邀请了特朗普首任期的副国家安全顾问 Nadia Schadlow。她阐释了特朗普第二任期外交政策背后的‘灵活现实主义’逻辑，讨论了美国对伊朗发动大规模军事行动的动因、对国际机构有效性的质疑，以及在‘美国优先’框架下，如何通过主动承担风险来重塑全球威慑力。

特朗普的“不战”承诺与伊朗战火

Ezra Klein: 这不是许多美国人以为他们投票选出的那个总统。就在不久前，美国军队在伊朗开始了大规模战斗行动。这并不是唐纳德·特朗普和他身边的人声称他们会带来的那种总统职位。他曾说过：“我将是那个让你们远离战争的人。我将让你们远离战争。我们不会在中东爆发战争。我们没有战争。我没有发动战争。他们说他会发动战争，但我不会，我要停止战争。”

对我来说，过去几天最令人惊讶的是，看到至少一个许多人认为特朗普及其政府拥有的红线——即不在伊朗部署地面部队——甚至这条红线也不再成立了。特朗普接受《纽约邮报》采访时说：“关于地面部队，我没有‘畏难情绪’（yips）。就像每位总统都会说‘不会有地面部队’一样，我不这么说。我说可能不需要他们。可能不需要。”

我显然反对这场战争。我认为这没有咨询美国公众，没有咨询国会，显然也没有咨询联合国。我不认为他们为可能引发的后果做好了准备。但我不想仅从我的角度来理解这件事，而是想从一个更认同特朗普外交政策、曾思考过他的学说和方法意味着什么、甚至在第一任期内帮助制定了该政策的人的角度来理解。

Nadia Schadlow 是哈德逊研究所（Hudson Institute）的高级研究员。她在特朗普第一任期内担任副国家安全顾问，主导起草并发布了**《2017年美国国家安全战略》**。因此，我想看看她如何理解特朗普第二任期的外交政策、他现在承担的风险、可以从中提取的哲学，以及这对美国人民和世界意味着什么。Nadia，欢迎来到节目。

什么是“灵活现实主义”？

Ezra Klein: 在2018年，你将唐纳德·特朗普描述为一名保守现实主义者（conservative realist）。你那是什意思？

Original English

Ezra Klein: In 2018, you described Donald Trump as a conservative realist. What did you mean by that.

Nadia Schadlow: 嗯，现实主义本质上意味着你按照世界的本来面目来看待它，而不是你希望它成为的样子。我认为唐纳德·特朗普是以一种特定的方式看世界的：这是一个充满竞争的世界，在这个世界里权力至关重要，民族国家（nation states）至关重要，利益至关重要。这就是我说的现实主义部分。

至于保守部分，我想在当前的**《2025年国家安全战略》中——我们稍后肯定会谈到——他们实际上使用了灵活现实主义**（flexible realism）这个词。所以我现在可能会说，这个词可能更准确。你不想在任何地方做任何事；你会承担风险，但本质上你不想……你想确保美国的利益得到保护，美国的实力得到保存，而不是不必要地消耗。

Original English

Nadia Schadlow: Well, realism essentially means that you look at the world the way it is, not as you wish it to be. And I think Donald Trump is someone who sees the world in a particular way. It’s a world that’s competitive. It’s a world in which power matters. It’s a world in which this, nation states matter, interests matter. So that’s what I meant by the realism part and the conservative part.

I guess, that’s something today in the current national security strategy of 2025, which I’m sure we’ll get into, actually, they use the term flexible realism. So I might actually say that might be more accurate today. You don’t want to do everything everywhere. You will take risks, but essentially you don’t want to. You want to make sure that American interests are American power is preserved and not expended unnecessarily.

Ezra Klein: 在你的构想中，这与人们更熟悉的共和党倾向流派有什么不同？比如新保守主义（neoconservatives），即小布什时代；以及旧保守主义（paleoconservatives）或孤立主义，这通常是人们界定特朗普的方式。

Original English

Ezra Klein: So how did that in your conception, differ from maybe the Republican leaning schools of thought people are more familiar with which is neoconservatives, right. The George W Bush era and then paleoconservatives or isolationists, which is often how people have at least framed Donald Trump.

Nadia Schadlow: 我认为自由国际主义（liberal internationalism），有些人也称之为“新保派”，本质上有一种更强的干预主义哲学，认为美国可以而且往往应该尝试按自己的形象重塑世界。这是经典的定义，认为那是好事，而且它对所有国家都能采纳民主（甚至是像我们这样的民主）可能持更乐观的态度。我们想在全世界推广人权和自由价值观，并愿意为此在世界各地进行干预。

而共和党中更倾向于孤立主义的一翼（坦率说，这不只是共和党，在进步左翼中也能看到），有一种“撤退”的感觉，认为我们在国内有很多问题。有些人称之为“收缩派”（retrenchers）。我认为左翼有时采取这种观点，理由是美国是问题的根源，通过走向海外，我们制造了抗体，我们不应该在那些地方瞎搅和。右翼也有一些这种观点，但在右翼，收缩派或孤立主义的观点是我们要专注于国内问题，世界其他地方发生的事情并不真正重要，我们需要首先在国内巩固实力并发展经济。我认为（虽然可能是错误的）这两者被看作是互斥的，而实际上它们是相互关联的。

Original English

Nadia Schadlow: Yeah I mean, I think this article that we’re talking about was in 2018. So it was about a year. It was about a year after I left, a few months after I left the administration. I think at that point. And it liberal internationalism, some people use the term neocons essentially is a more has a more interventionist philosophy, a sense that America can go in and should often try to reshape the world in its image. This is the classic definition that that’s a good thing, and it’s probably more optimistic about a sense that all countries can adopt democracy and maybe democracy that looks like us. We want to promote human rights around the world. We want to promote liberal values around the world. And we are open to interventions around the world to do that.

The more isolationist wing of the Republican Party, usually it’s not. Frankly, it’s not just the Republican Party. I think you also see that in the progressive left to a sense of let’s pull back. We have problems at home. But some people call that group retrenchers. I think the left sometimes adopts that view with the rationale that America is a cause of problems. And by going abroad, by being abroad, we create antibodies and we shouldn’t be Messing around in those places. There’s a little bit of that on the right too, but on the right, I think the retrench or isolationist view is that we have to focus on our problems at home. What happens in the rest of the world really doesn’t matter, and we need to consolidate our power here at home first and grow our economy. And that the two, I think wrongly, are mutually exclusive, right. I think they’re interlinked.

特朗普眼中的“美国衰落”与利益重塑

Original English

Nadia Schadlow: 没错，假设是不同的。比如当特朗普在2017年就职时，他专注于三个广泛的主题：第一，美国正在衰落，且已经衰落了一段时间，他要去修复并助力复兴；第二，作为复兴的一部分，他要对机构进行重大的秩序重组（reordering）。

对于特朗普总统的第一任期（以及现在的延续），他认为衰落源于几个领域：全球化的影响和美国的去工业化带来的衰落；损害美国利益且持续已久的贸易失衡带来的衰落；对美国价值观和身份缺乏自豪感带来的衰落；以及其他国家利用我们的安全保障带来的衰落。这些都是原因的例子。

在复兴方面，特朗普总统认为复兴的起点是解决这些贸易失衡，确保盟友和伙伴在安全负担中承担更多份额，重新激发对美国的自豪感，并将其放在首位。然后在第三点秩序重组上，他非常乐于不从现有的机构开始。他来自一个会问“我们为什么需要这些机构？它们做了什么？它们为我们做了什么？总体上它们取得了什么成果？”的世界。我认为我们今天在很多方面仍能看到这一点，正在伊朗发生的事情也是如此。

Original English

Nadia Schadlow: Right, so I think that you’re right. The assumptions are different. So for instance, when Trump came into office in 2017, he was focused, I think, on three kind of broad themes one, this idea that America was in decline and had been in decline for some time. And he was going to fix that. He was going to help to renew it. And as part of that, he was going to do some significant reordering of institutions.

So for President Trump first term, and I think continuing, he saw the roots of that decline in several areas, decline due to the effects of globalization and the deindustrialization of the United States, declined due to trade imbalances that harm the United States, and had been harming the United States for quite some time. Decline due to a lack of pride in what America stood for and what America was, decline due to other countries taking advantage of our security guarantees. I’m giving you examples of the reasons now.

On renewal. The way that President Trump saw beginning that renewal was addressing those trade imbalances, making sure that allies and partners did more for their share of security burdens, reinvigorating a sense of pride in America, putting that first and foremost, I mean, there are a list of policy issues. And then third, on reordering, he was very comfortable in not beginning with the existing set of institutions. He came from a world in which said, well, why do we need these institutions? What have they done? What are they doing for us and what have they done overall. What outcomes have they achieved? I still think we’re seeing this today. In many ways. We’re seeing it as playing out in what’s happening in Iran. But I’ll leave it there.

从“不打仗”到轰炸伊朗：逻辑断裂了吗？

Ezra Klein: 特朗普曾非常严厉地批评政权更迭及其引发的战争。他说：“我们认为美国军队的工作不是在世界各地发动无止境的政权更迭战争。”他谈论过这些战争的愚蠢和浪费。他还标榜自己是长时间以来首位没有发动任何新战争的总统。

但在第二任期，我们在八周内已经推翻了两个国家元首。我们抓获了尼古拉斯·马杜罗，现在正在轰炸伊朗，杀死了伊朗最高领袖。这些事情如何在你看来逻辑自洽？

Ezra Klein: Well, let me see how this fits into it. 国家利益与威慑力的重塑

Ezra Klein: 你提到了《2025年国家安全战略》，该文件称外交政策的目的是保护核心国家利益。这是该战略的唯一重点。那么对你来说，在伊朗问题上，美国的核心国家利益是什么？

Original English

Ezra Klein: So you mentioned the National Security Strategy in 2025. And so that document says the purpose of foreign policy is a protection of core national interests. That is the sole focus of this strategy. So to you, what are America’s core national interests when it comes to Iran?

Nadia Schadlow: 我认为核心利益是相对不变的：保护美国本土、促进美国繁荣、通过实力维持和平。差异在于你如何达成这些利益。

显然，对于特朗普总统来说，保护本土的关键部分是关闭边境、建立导弹防御系统。而在通过实力维持和平方面，既要有强大的军事力量，也要有威慑。我认为特朗普总统现在的行动（或者至少其效果）将严重加强美国的威慑态势。没有人会再认为当我们说要采取行动时我们不会行动。他认为自己的使命是恢复那些未被尊重的红线。他不想在多年后被批评为像奥巴马那样，在叙利亚对本国人民使用化学武器时没有坚守红线。

Original English

That was key to him build a missile defense system. And also in terms of preserving peace through strength, strong military, but also deterrence and part of deterrence. No one is going to think that we’re not going to act when we say we’re going to act. I think he saw it as his mission to restore red lines that had not been respected. He didn’t want to be seen years later as criticized in the way that Obama was criticized for not backing the red line when Syria used chemical weapons against its own people.
