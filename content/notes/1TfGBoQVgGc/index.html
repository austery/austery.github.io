摘要:

对话《金融时报》Alphaville 主编 Robin Wigglesworth，深入剖析美国国债市场的运行逻辑、近期异常波动根源。探讨巨额美债赤字、特朗普政府与贝森特的干预举措、美联储角色、外国买家动向及未来主权债务危机风险。

美债市场的重要性与当前异动

Ezra Klein: 美国国债市场是全世界最重要的金融市场，无可匹敌，没有任何市场能与之相提并论。我们大多数人并不直接参与其中，不会在早晨起床后去买国债。但美国国债却决定了一切——从股市的表现，到房贷利率、汽车贷款和信用卡利率。在金融领域，几乎没有什么东西是它触及不到的。

而最近，美国国债市场看起来有点异常。美国政府的借贷成本正在上升。这可能是因为我们的债务最近突破了 40 万亿美元大关。现在我们每年为这些债务支付的利息，已经超过了整个国防预算。但与此同时，唐纳德·特朗普的言行也变得越来越难以预测。

Original English

Ezra Klein: The US Treasury market is the most important financial market in the world. Bar none. Nothing is even close. Most of us don't participate in it directly. We don't go in the morning and buy treasury bonds. But treasury bonds define everything from how the stock market ends up performing to the cost of a mortgage, a car loan, a credit card. There is almost nothing financial they do not touch.

And the US Treasury market, it's been looking a little weird lately. The cost of borrowing for the US government is going up. Probably because our debt recently passed $40 trillion. We now spend more on interest on that debt yearly than we spend on the entire defense budget. But also, Donald Trump has been more and more erratic.

Donald Trump: 历史上从来没有哪个国家像我们现在这样，有如此巨额的资金流入。

Original English

Donald Trump: There's never been in history the kind of money coming into a country as we have right now.

Ezra Klein: 他的财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）一直在对市场采取更加激进的干预举措。

Original English

Ezra Klein: His Treasury Secretary, Scott Bessent, has been making some more aggressive moves into the market.

Scott Bessent: 你可以把它想象成拉满的弹弓。我们积蓄了巨大的势能，这些势能即将转化为动能。

Original English

Scott Bessent: You think of it as pulling back the slingshot here. We have a lot of potential energy that will turn into kinetic energy.

Robin Wigglesworth is the editor of the Financial Times blog, Alphaville. A quick time stamp here because a lot is happening in the bond markets lately. We spoke on Monday, August 24th. Robin Wigglesworth, welcome to the show.

Robin Wigglesworth: 谢谢邀请我。

Original English

特朗普与贝森特对债市的罕见干预

Ezra Klein: 我想从上周五唐纳德·特朗普被问及财政部长斯科特·贝森特近期对债券市场干预的片段开始。

Original English

Ezra Klein: So, I wanted to begin with this clip of Donald Trump being asked last Friday about Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's recent interventions in the bond market.

Reporter: 你是否指示了贝森特部长干预债券市场？

Donald Trump: 完全没有。他是一个非常有能力的人。是他自己想这么做的，而且他非常擅长这个。他很有手腕，对债券和利率有着非常出色的直觉。于是他就去做了。

Reporter: 收益率在短暂下降后又回升了……

Donald Trump: 它们会降下来的。它们一定会降下来。

Original English

Reporter: Did you direct Secretary Bessent to intervene in the bond market?

Donald Trump: Not at all. No. He's a very capable man. He wanted to do it. He's very good at it. He has a good touch. Very good natural touch for the bonds and interest. And he did that. Yeah.

Reporter: The yields have come back up though...

Donald Trump: They'll come down. They will come down.

Ezra Klein: 听到美国总统和财政部长公开谈论要压低长期债券收益率，甚至承认他们试图干预市场以达到这一目的，这有多不寻常？

Original English

Ezra Klein: How unusual is it to hear an American president and an American Treasury Secretary talk about wanting to get long-term bond yields down, and even acknowledge that they are trying to intervene in the market to do it?

Robin Wigglesworth: 这极其不寻常。通常情况下，美国财政部长会把货币政策和利率留给美联储去处理。历任财长往往信奉“强势美元政策”，但实际上他们大多尽量避免直接对日常市场走势发表评论，更不用说主动试图压低收益率了。

传统的财政部职责在于以对纳税人最有利、成本最低且风险可控的方式，为美国政府的巨额借款提供融资支持。这通常意味着保持可预测性和透明度（Regular and Predictable）。而贝森特所做的，恰恰打破了这种长期惯例，采取了非常激进且直接的市场操作手段。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: It is exceptionally unusual. Typically, the US Treasury Secretary leaves monetary policy and interest rates to the Federal Reserve. Treasuries over the years have had a "strong dollar policy," but essentially they try to avoid commenting on day-to-day market moves, let alone actively trying to suppress yields.

The traditional Treasury role is to fund the vast borrowing needs of the US government in a way that is least disruptive to the market and most cost-effective for taxpayers over the long term. That usually means being "regular and predictable." What Bessent has done breaks with that long-standing dogma in favor of aggressive, tactical interventions.

债券市场的基本机制：价格与收益率

Ezra Klein: 在我们深入探讨贝森特具体做了什么之前，我想先为听众梳理一下债券市场的底层运作逻辑。为什么债券收益率（Bond Yields）如此关键？它和债券价格之间是什么关系？

Original English

Ezra Klein: Before we get into the specifics of what Bessent is doing, I want to establish the basic mechanics for listeners. Why are bond yields so crucial, and what is their relationship with bond prices?

Robin Wigglesworth: 债券从本质上讲就是借据（IOU）。政府或公司发行债券以借入资金，承诺在未来偿还本金，并在持有期间定期支付利息（即票面利息，Coupon）。

债券价格和收益率呈反向变动关系。当市场对债券的需求增加、价格上涨时，买家相对于所付出的本金所获得的固定利息回报率就下降了，也就是收益率下降；反之，当大家都在抛售债券、价格下跌时，收益率就会上升。

对于美国政府来说，10年期或30年期国债收益率的上升，意味着其新增借款或到期债务置换的利息成本更高。同时，因为国债被视为全球无风险基准资产，它的收益率直接构成了房贷、车贷和企业债等所有信贷产品的定价基石。国债收益率飙升，整个经济体的借贷成本就会被全面推高。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: A bond is essentially an IOU. A government or corporation issues a bond to borrow money, promising to pay back the principal at maturity and make regular interest payments, known as coupons, along the way.

Bond prices and bond yields move in opposite directions. When demand for bonds is high and prices rise, the yield—the return an investor gets relative to the price paid—goes down. Conversely, when investors sell bonds and prices drop, yields go up.

For the US government, higher yields on 10-year or 30-year Treasuries mean higher borrowing costs when issuing new debt or rolling over existing debt. And because US Treasuries are the risk-free benchmark for the global financial system, their yields dictate the interest rates for mortgages, auto loans, and corporate debt. When Treasury yields spike, borrowing costs across the entire economy rise.

40 万亿债务大山与财政恶化

Ezra Klein: 让我们谈谈美国当前的财政赤字和总债务规模。40 万亿美元是一个令人难以置信的数字，而且我们每年的利息支出已经超过了军费开支。市场为什么现在开始对此感到焦虑？

Original English

Ezra Klein: Let's talk about the current fiscal deficit and the debt load. $40 trillion is a staggering number, and annual interest expense now exceeds the defense budget. Why is the market starting to panic about this now?

Robin Wigglesworth: 长期以来，投资者都默认美国拥有无尽的举债空间，因为美元是全球储备货币，美债被视为终极安全资产。但如今的数学逻辑正在变得极为严峻。

当基准利率从过去的接近于零上升到现在的 4% 至 5% 水平时，滚动置换过去低息发行的旧债变得极其昂贵。美国每年的财政赤字仍在占 GDP 的 6% 至 7% 左右徘徊，这在和平且经济处于扩张周期的时期是前所未有的。

市场开始担忧供需失衡：财政部需要发行天文数字的新债，但传统的买家群体——无论是美联储（正在进行量化紧缩 QT）、外国央行，还是国内商业银行——其购买胃口都在相对收缩。供给过剩而需求不足，必然导致收益率面临持续上行压力。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: For a very long time, investors operated under the assumption that the US had an almost infinite borrowing capacity because the dollar is the world's reserve currency and Treasuries are the ultimate safe asset. But the pure arithmetic is becoming brutal.

When benchmark interest rates rose from near zero to 4% or 5%, rolling over legacy low-yielding debt became astronomically expensive. The US is running budget deficits of 6% to 7% of GDP during a peacetime economic expansion, which is historically unprecedented.

The market is waking up to a fundamental supply-demand imbalance: Treasury must issue gargantuan amounts of new paper, but traditional buyers—the Fed doing quantitative tightening, foreign central banks, domestic commercial banks—are not keeping pace. Massive supply facing softer demand puts persistent upward pressure on yields.

贝森特的非常规操作：缩短久期与回购策略

Ezra Klein: 那么斯科特·贝森特究竟采取了什么手段来试图扭转这一局面？他的“拉满弹弓”到底指什么？

Original English

Ezra Klein: What exactly is Scott Bessent doing to combat this? What does he mean by "pulling back the slingshot"?

Robin Wigglesworth: 贝森特主要采取了两套组合拳：

第一，大幅转向短期国债（T-Bills）发行，减少长期国债（10年期、30年期）的发售规模。通过减少长端债券的供给，试图人为压低长端收益率。

第二，利用财政部的现金账户（TGA，Treasury General Account）和国债回购计划（Buyback Program），主动在公开市场上买回流动性较差的旧券，向市场注入流动性。

这种做法在金融界引发了巨大争议。将债务期限结构大幅短期化，本质上是把政府的融资成本与短期利率紧紧绑在一起。如果短期利率不迅速下降，政府将面临极其频繁的展期风险和高昂的利息支出。这是一种极其激进的豪赌。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: Bessent has essentially used a two-pronged strategy:

First, heavily skewing issuance toward Treasury bills (short-term debt) while cutting back on longer-term notes and bonds (10-year, 30-year). By artificially starving the market of long-term supply, he hopes to push long-term yields down.

Second, utilizing the Treasury General Account (TGA) cash buffer and expanding Treasury buyback operations to purchase off-the-run bonds and inject liquidity directly into the market.

This has caused huge debate. Shortening the average maturity of the national debt ties the government's borrowing costs directly to prevailing short-term interest rates. If the Fed doesn't aggressively slash rates, the rollover risk and ongoing interest bill become immense. It is a very risky tactical gamble.

债券义警的回归与主权债务风险

Ezra Klein: 市场上经常提到一个词叫“债券义警”（Bond Vigilantes）。这些投资者是如何通过抛售债券来惩罚不负责任的财政政策的？

Original English

Ezra Klein: We often hear the term "Bond Vigilantes." How do these market actors discipline governments with unsustainable fiscal policies?

Robin Wigglesworth: “债券义警”是经济学家埃德·亚德尼（Ed Yardeni）在 1980 年代创造的名词。它指的是当债券投资者认为政府的财政赤字失控、通胀风险加剧或货币政策过于宽松时，通过集体抛售国债、推高收益率，从而迫使政府削减开支或收紧政策。

在过去十几年低通胀和央行量化宽松（QE）的时代，债券义警几乎销声匿迹。但随着全球通胀抬头和各国债务规模膨胀，义警们正在重新夺回定价权。如果美国政府继续推行减税并扩大开支，而试图单方面通过金融财技压制收益率，市场最终会通过更剧烈的抛售来进行反击。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: The term "Bond Vigilantes" was coined by Ed Yardeni in the 1980s. It describes investors who sell government bonds en masse when they believe fiscal or monetary policy is inflationary or reckless, driving borrowing costs up and forcing politicians to rein in spending.

During the era of low inflation and central bank quantitative easing, the bond vigilantes were largely dormant. But with persistent inflation and swollen debt piles, they are back. If the administration pursues further tax cuts and unfunded spending while trying to use financial engineering to mask the cost, the market will eventually revolt by demanding even higher risk premia.

终局思考与书籍推荐

Ezra Klein: 如果长端收益率居高不下，甚至进一步失控，这最终会把我们引向何方？

Original English

Ezra Klein: If long-term yields stay elevated or spiral higher, where does that ultimately lead us?

Robin Wigglesworth: 这将迫使华盛顿面临非常痛苦的选择：要么实施严厉的财政紧缩（大幅削减开支或增税），要么走向金融抑制（Financial Repression）——即强制国内金融机构持有低息国债，或者迫使美联储重新下场实施收益率曲线控制（Yield Curve Control, YCC）并货币化赤字，但这将以长期高通胀和美元信誉受损为惨重代价。

历史上没有哪个帝国能够长期无视债务复利的数学规律。债券市场并非天生冷酷，它只是一个庞大的算术反馈机器。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: It will force Washington into painful choices: either genuine fiscal austerity (spending cuts and tax hikes), or overt financial repression—forcing domestic financial institutions to absorb government debt at below-market rates, or forcing the Federal Reserve to implement Yield Curve Control (YCC) to monetize the deficit. The latter carries immense risks of entrenched inflation and debasing the dollar.

History shows no empire can perpetually outrun the arithmetic of compound interest. The bond market isn't malicious; it's simply a massive, unyielding calculation machine.

Ezra Klein: 按照我们节目的传统，最后请向我们的听众推荐三本你最喜欢的书籍。

Original English

Ezra Klein: As is our tradition on the show, could you recommend three books to our listeners?

Robin Wigglesworth: 第一本是利雅卡特·艾哈迈德（Liaquat Ahamed）的《金融之王》（Lords of Finance）。这是一部关于大萧条时期四位主要央行行长的杰作，极具叙事张力与历史教训。

第二本是爱德华·钱斯勒（Edward Chancellor）的《时间的代价》（The Price of Time: The Real Story of Interest）。它深入探讨了利率的历史演变及其被压制后对现代资本主义造成的扭曲。

第三本是迈克尔·刘易斯（Michael Lewis）的《说谎者的扑克牌》（Liar's Poker）或者塞巴斯蒂安·马拉比（Sebastian Mallaby）的《风险投资之王》（The Power Law）。如果非要选一本把复杂金融故事讲得最引人入胜的读物，迈克尔·刘易斯的作品始终是顶级范本。

Original English

Robin Wigglesworth: First, Lords of Finance by Liaquat Ahamed. A masterpiece detailing the four central bankers who broke the world economy during the Great Depression.

Second, The Price of Time: The Real Story of Interest by Edward Chancellor, which brilliantly explains the history of interest rates and the perils of ultra-low rates.

Third, Michael Lewis's classic Liar's Poker, or Sebastian Mallaby's The Power Law. Lewis tells complex financial history with extraordinary verve and narrative power.

Original English
