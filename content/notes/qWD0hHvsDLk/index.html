摘要:

钢琴家 ELEW 分享了他从古典与爵士钢琴跨界到 DJ 领域的探索历程。他将大脑结构与功能比作混音器，将人生选择比作播放的曲目，提出了“人生俱乐部”的哲学。通过融入 DJ 的同理心、敏锐度与精心准备，他不仅战胜了恐慌症与抑郁，还开创了独特的“钢琴唱盘主义”艺术风格，启迪每个人如何成为自己心态的掌控者。

我们都是人生俱乐部的 DJ：钢琴家 ELEW 的生命律动哲学

在我们所生存的这个被称为“现实俱乐部”的生命舞台上，我们每个人在本质上都是自己生活的唱片骑师（DJs）。我们的意识与感知塑造了自我，而我们在日常生活中做出的每一个选择，就是我们在人生混音台前切入的一轨轨音乐。尽管每天我们都会带着某种精心设计的规划或温柔的构想开启新的一天，但现实俱乐部总会以意想不到的方式——例如航班延误或突发的混乱变故——将一切化为废墟。即便如此，我们依然必须像神话中的阿特拉斯一样，默默承受着荒谬而沉重的负荷，让那些代表着我们生命抉择的唱片源源不断地旋转。因为我们别无选择，不能停止喂养宠物，也不能停止推进价值百万美元的商业交易。我们只能不断前行，并时刻铭记自己是掌控节奏的 DJ。

这种将人生具象为 DJ 混音的哲学，源于我个人的艺术挣扎与蜕变。作为家族中第四代钢琴家，我自幼便坐在钢琴前接受训练，随后在母亲的引导下接触并爱上了爵士乐。然而，我很快发现自己与传统爵士乐产业的陈规格格不入。这种深层的认知失调最终引发了严重的恐慌症（Panic Attacks: 一种突发且强烈的焦虑与恐惧反应）。为了自我救赎，我将这种负面情绪转化为创造力，开创了属于我自己的宣泄式摇滚爵士风格。这也为我后续融入 DJ 艺术的探索奠定了基础。

My philosophy is that we are all DJs. No matter whether you’re a schoolteacher or a neuroscientist or a VC, a genius or even a piano player, we're all DJs. I think that the club that we exist in is called “Club Reality,” and the choices that we make are the tracks that we drop, and the perceptual entity that we identify with that is us. We are the DJs. Now every day, we approach with some sort of plan, some sort of gentle idea that we're going to execute. But then of course, “Club Reality” comes along and oftentimes, like with the flights, like with all other kinds of things, sets everything on fire. No matter what happens, no matter how improbable or absurd the load, we have to bear it like the mythical Atlas. We have to keep those tracks spinning our life choices. We have to keep everything going on -- and it’s not like we have much of a choice in the matter. I mean, you can't stop feeding your dog or you can't stop closing that million-dollar deal. We just have to keep pushing forward and remember that we are the DJs. So I want to share with you how I came to this kind of concept. I want to share with you my journey into the world of DJing, and how it affected my piano playing from a technical perspective, as well as my thoughts, my life. When I was about two years old, my great-grandmother put me at the piano and said, "OK, it's time for you to become a fourth-generation piano player in our family." And so there I am. Eventually, my mother introduced me to jazz music, and I fell in love with it, but I was always a little bit out of sync with the jazz industry. This led to panic attacks, which led to me creating my own cathartic rock-jazz style.

钢琴唱盘主义与大脑混音：重构心流的实操方法

这种重构始于纽约著名的夜总会 1 Oak 和 Finale。在那里，我亲眼目睹了嘻哈与电子舞曲（EDM）DJs 的艺术与科学。嘻哈 DJ 们如同拥有多只手臂的炫技巨匠在指尖摩擦黑胶，而 EDM DJs 则如释放太古能量的乐神般用音乐浸润整个空间。在震撼之余，我意识到这些现代 DJ 实际上是弗朗茨·李斯特（Franz Liszt: 19世纪浪漫主义钢琴大师、炫技派代表）与法茨·瓦勒（Fats Waller: 美国传奇爵士钢琴家、作曲家）在现代科技下的逻辑延伸。我下定决心要掌握这项技能，天真地以为凭借深厚的钢琴背景可以轻松驾驭，然而现实却给了我沉重一击。

在经历了一段相当令人尴尬的学习曲线后，我逐渐领悟到 DJ 艺术的真谛——它关乎极致的同理心（Empathy）、对环境的敏感度（Sensitivity）以及极其严谨的准备工作（Rigorous Preparation）。它极大地锻炼了我的常识，例如：绝不能在静谧的中午咖啡馆播放激昂的 Avicii 电子舞曲，也绝不能在夜总会瓶装酒服务的黄金巅峰时段切入一首小众的非主打冷门单曲。从神经科学的视角来看，我们的大脑天生就是一台精密运行的混音器。大脑的左半球与右半球分别处理不同性质的信息，而连接两者的胼胝体（Corpus Callosum: 连接大脑左右半球的神经纤维束）则扮演着同步器的角色，这证明我们在生理构造上就是天生的 DJ。

基于这一认知，我将 DJ 混音的逻辑融入钢琴演奏，开创了钢琴唱盘主义（Piano Turntablism: 将 DJ 搓盘和混音手法用钢琴键盘呈现的独特技法），即同时用双手演奏两首完全不同的曲目。这种独特的音乐融合引起了著名歌手 Sting 的关注，并让我获得了为其演唱会开场表演的机会。更重要的是，这套哲学帮助我彻底驯服了曾经困扰我的恐慌症与抑郁症。我的生活信条由此重塑：我的心灵是俱乐部，我是掌控音轨的 DJ，而我的每一个选择都决定了这里的氛围。因此，我必须时刻让这片心灵空间的律动保持美妙与轻松。无论命运向我们抛出怎样的难题，通过将自己视作操控人生唱盘的 DJ，我们都能找到最合拍的生命节奏。

At “1 Oak,” there would be these hip-hop DJ virtuosos. And I mean, it seemed like they had like, multiple arms. But then the EDM DJs over at "Finale," these were like mythical, eldritch-energy gods. They’d be immersing the room in these amazing sounds. I was like, is there a mammoth in the room? Either way, I was so fascinated with this stuff that I had to become a DJ. And I began to think that these guys were logical extensions of Franz Liszt and Fats Waller. Or -- I had to become a DJ. I said, “I’ve got to learn how to do this stuff。” I thought it would be easy, given my piano background, and of course I was dead wrong. Reality had much different plans. After a pretty embarrassing learning curve, I began to feel a little more comfortable with DJing, and I began to notice that it's so much about empathy and sensitivity and rigorous preparation. Ultimately, it enhanced my common sense. I learned stuff like, don’t play a huge Avicii EDM rager at a lunchtime cafe, and don’t play [an] obscure, amazing B-side in the middle of bottle service at the peak hours of a nightclub. I actually studied the neuroscience of it all, and I noticed that even our brain is set up like a mixer. There's the right hemisphere and the left hemisphere, and the corpus callosum, that strip between the two of them, [that] keeps them in sync. So we're DJs organically. I even came up with an idea I call “piano turntablism,” this kind of thing here: Playing two different tunes. Sting heard that -- that’s how I got the gig. I opened for him. Pretty funny. So ultimately, this all has helped me get in control of those panic attacks that I touched on, the depressions, all that stuff. My mantra is: my mind is the club, I am the DJ [and] my choices affect the atmosphere -- so let’s keep that atmosphere groovy. I hope that you find this idea useful, applicable, because no matter what life throws at us, I believe we can always be enhanced by thinking of it as if we were a DJ, because in the end, we are all DJs. Having said that, I'm going to play a quick tune for you. This is an original I call "The Inspirer." I find you all so very inspirational and so very groovy. Please enjoy.
