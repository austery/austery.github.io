伊朗政权继任与生存资源的战略博弈

伊朗局势已进入冲突爆发后的第二周。伊朗最高神职人员任命了 Mushaba Ham（56岁）继任国家最高领袖（Supreme Leader: 伊朗拥有最终决策权的最高政治与宗教职位），他是上周在美以空袭中丧生的 Ayatollah Ali Ham 之子。尽管 1979 年伊斯兰革命曾承诺终结权力的世袭制，且其父生前曾向顾问表示不希望儿子继任，但这一任命表明伊朗高层在危机时刻选择了“闭关锁国”式的政权延续。与此同时，针对海水淡化厂（Desalination Plant: 极其脆弱但关键的平民生存命脉设施）的袭击已在伊朗和巴林蔓延。中东专家警告，打击这些设施正将军事对抗直接转变为对平民生存的直接威胁。

受冲突影响，全球原油价格在四年来首次突破每桶 100 美元。霍尔木兹海峡（Strait of Hormuz: 全球约五分之一石油供应必经的战略水道）的航运受阻导致油价和天然气价格飙升。特朗普总统对此表示，军事行动的优先级高于对价格的担忧，并坚信在战争结束后价格会迅速回落。

英文原文 The war with Iran has now entered its second week. The country has named a new supreme leader who both Israel and the US have denounced. Attacks have continued in Iran and across the Gulf, hitting civilian infrastructure that's raised concerns about a potential water crisis. And the price of oil has surged above $100 a barrel for the first time in nearly four years. To start, Iran's top clerics have named Mushtah Ham to lead the country. He's the 56-year-old son of Ayatollah Ali Ham, who was killed in the US-Israeli strikes last Saturday. He was groomed for the position and has strong military and political connections. Though at one point, his father had indicated to his close advisers that he didn't want his son to succeed him. According to three Iranian officials familiar with the selection process, the Islamic Revolution in 1979 was about toppling a monarchy and promising to end the hereditary transfer of power. His selection, despite that, suggests that those high up in the Iranian government have closed ranks at a time of crisis and are looking for continuity for the regime. President Trump warned this weekend that the next Supreme Leader "is not going to last long without the approval of the United States."

Second, over the weekend, the US and Israel hit hundreds of targets in Iran. Israel went after several Iranian fuel depots, and water desalination plants came under attack in Iran and Bahrain. One expert on the Middle East said the plants, which turn seawater into drinking water, are essential lifelines for tens of millions of people. And quote, "Striking them risks turning a military confrontation into a direct threat to civilian survival." The ongoing conflict has driven up the price of fuel fast. last night, oil prices surged past $100 a barrel. The Secretary of Energy, Chris Wright, said the US is working on securing the Strait of Hormuz, a vital shipping route for oil and gas that's been choked by the conflict.

执法策略微调与“特赦产业”的交易内幕

在美国国内，移民执法策略出现了微妙调整。内部数据显示，在经历了数月高强度的激进搜捕后，特朗普政府近期略微缩减了逮捕规模，ICE（Immigration and Customs Enforcement: 负责边境与移民事务的联邦执法机构）在洛杉矶和芝加哥等地的活动有所减少。这可能与此前全美范围内爆发的抗议冲突，以及包括两名美国公民被误杀在内的负面舆论压力有关。然而，当前的拘留人数仍是拜登政府最后一年的四倍，且被捕人员中超过 40% 并没有任何犯罪记录。

与此同时，一项针对白宫权力的深度调查揭露了围绕特赦（Clemency: 总统减轻刑罚或撤销定罪的特权）形成的巨大利益链条。游说公司在 2025 年收到的相关业务款项较前一年增长了八倍。典型案例包括养老院大亨 Joseph Schwarz，他在因税务欺诈被判刑三年前，支付了近百万美元给拥有“白宫门路”的说客。尽管他仅入狱三周，便获得了总统的全面特赦。这种新兴的特赦产业不再强调传统的“悔罪”或“改过自新”，而是彻底演变为一种基于人脉触达的商业交易。

英文原文 In the US, internal government data indicates that after months of high-profile aggressive immigration raids, the Trump administration has slightly scaled back arrests, at least for now. In Los Angeles, a group that monitors immigration agents says there's been a noticeable decline in ICE and Border Patrol sightings. In Chicago, officers are no longer stationed at Home Depot parking lots to question and arrest day laborers. The change comes after chaotic clashes between agents and protesters, including the killing of two US citizens in Minneapolis by federal agents. Polls show that a majority of Americans believe ISIS tactics went too far. Despite the dip in arrests, the number of people detained last month was still quadruple what it was in President Biden's last year in office.

A new investigation from the Times has found that there's a lucrative industry emerging around trying to get pardons from President Trump. In 2025 alone, lobbying firms disclosed receiving about eight times more money from clients trying to get clemency than the year before under Biden. One example is Joseph Schwarz. He owned a nursing home empire and pleaded guilty to tax fraud. Before Schwarz reported to jail, he paid almost a million dollars to two men who talked about their ability to gain access to the president. He was freed by a full and unconditional pardon after just three weeks behind bars. This pardon industry really prioritizes the ability to find someone who can get to the president and make the case for the pardon and not remorse, atonement, and the unlikelihood of recidivism.

通胀传导：从停摆的安检线到萎缩的快餐消费

政府部分停摆的后果正在基建领域显现。由于国土安全部预算受限，TSA（Transportation Security Administration: 运输安全管理局）工作人员在仅能拿到三成薪水的情况下被迫维持工作，这导致新奥尔良和休斯顿等地的机场安检排队时间长达数小时。民主党坚持要求在恢复拨款前必须限制移民执法的权力，这种僵局正严重干扰春假期间的民众出行。

在消费端，高昂的价格正在终结“沙拉碗”时代。以 Chipotle 和 Sweetgreen 为代表的快餐品牌，其价格在过去四年中分别上涨了 25% 和 20%。中产阶级消费者对动辄超过 50 美元的两人午餐（Slop Bowl: 社交媒体对这类混合了蛋白质、谷物和蔬菜的半泥状健康餐的戏称）感到厌倦。Sweetgreen 的股价在过去一年暴跌 76%，而麦当劳和汉堡王等传统快餐巨头正通过价值菜单（Value Menu: 针对价格敏感消费者的廉价套餐组合）积极抢夺流失的市场份额。

英文原文 In New Orleans this weekend, the security lines at the city's airports stretched for hours. The airports are struggling with staffing amid the ongoing partial government shutdown. Funding has lapsed for the Department of Homeland Security, which includes TSA. Agents are still required to work, but they only got about 30% of their pay. Congress is still at odds with Democrats refusing to fund DHS without limits on immigration enforcement. The TSA app has not always been updated; lines in Houston stretched to baggage claim while the app showed a 0-15 minute wait.

And finally, Americans on the go used to be all about the bowl. People were piling up the ingredients onto what social media deemed the "slop bowl." But customers are getting tired of them and the high price tags. Restaurant prices have been rising at a sharper rate than groceries. Over the last four years, the prices at Chipotle went up nearly 25%. Sweetgreen stock plunged 76% in the last year. At the same time, their fast food cousins, McDonald's, Burger King, Taco Bell have been pushing their value menu deals, trying to scoop up newly price conscious customers.
