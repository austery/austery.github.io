摘要:

芬兰总统亚历山大·斯图布在播客中深度探讨世界格局巨变。他指出西方霸权时代已终结，全球南方将决定未来秩序走向。面对大国博弈与AI科技颠覆，斯图布提出“基于价值的现实主义”，并就北约转型、俄乌冲突、芬兰治理与领导力哲学分享独到见解。

世界新秩序的前奏

亚历山大·斯图布: 民主国家目前还没能很好地应对社交媒体和现代科技带来的冲击，甚至在这场博弈中逐渐落入下风。在很多方面，我们看到科技巨头的力量已经超越了主权国家。因此你完全可以提出这样一个问题：Anthropic 或 Meta (Facebook)，是不是已经比挪威或芬兰还要强大？

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Democracies have not been able to cope with social media and with modern technology yet and they're losing the struggle. We also see big tech companies in many ways being more powerful than states. So you can ask the question, you know, is Anthropic or is Facebook more powerful than Norway and Finland?

尼古拉·坦根: 大家好，我是挪威主权财富基金的首席执行官尼古拉·坦根。今天我有幸邀请到芬兰总统亚历山大·斯图布。芬兰拥有550万人口，与俄罗斯有着长达1340公里的边界，连续九年被评为全球最幸福的国家。芬兰的影响力远超其体量，斯图布总统也是如此。当欧洲需要与白宫建立沟通渠道时，亚历山大通常就是那个接到电话的人。今年早些时候，亚历山大出版了一本新书，探讨旧世界秩序如何瓦解，以及接下来由谁来主导未来。我对此非常期待。亚历山大，热烈欢迎你。

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Hi everyone, I'm Nicolai Tangen, the CEO of the Norwegian Sovereign Wealth Fund. And today I'm in really good company with Alexander Stubb, the president of Finland. Finland has 5 and a half million people, a 1,340 km border with Russia, one of the happiest countries in the world nine years in a row. It punches far above its weight and so does President Stubb. When Europe needs a way through to the White House, Alexander is often the one who gets the call. Earlier this year, Alexander wrote a book about how the old world fell apart and who decides what comes next. I've really been looking forward to this. Alexander, be welcome.

亚历山大·斯图布: 很高兴能与你交流，尼古拉。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Oh, nice to be with you, Nikolai.

世界格局的历史性转折

尼古拉·坦根: 简而言之，你书中最重要的核心信息是什么？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: In short, what's the most important message in your book?

亚历山大·斯图布: 最重要的核心信息是：世界秩序正在发生剧烈变革，就像它在1918年、1945年和1989年所经历的那样。而决定未来格局走向的将是全球南方，关键在于选择建立在多边规范、机构秩序与合作之上的体系，还是陷入多极、交易型、充满混乱与冲突的世界。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: The most important message is that the world order is changing much like it changed in 1918, 1945 and 1989. And the ones that are going to decide what it looks like is going to be the global south and the choices between multilateral norms institutional order and cooperation versus multipolar transactional chaos and conflict.

尼古拉·坦根: 为什么是由全球南方来决定未来？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Why is the south going to decide the future?

亚历山大·斯图布: 因为从人口、经济和科技的角度来看，他们基本具备决定未来的体量和规模。西方独霸的时代已经结束了。但我也不认为东方会成为绝对的主导。因此，我们将迎来大量全新的参与者。从我作为一个小国的代表的切身利益出发，我希望看到一个以联合国为基石的多边体系。但这意味着联合国必须进行改革，让全球南方在其中拥有真正的话语权与能动性。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Because they basically have the size and scale to do that in terms of population, economy and technology. So the end of western dominance is over. But I don't think the east is going to dominate either. So, we're going to start getting a whole bunch of new players. And my vested interest coming from a small country is that it would be a multilateral system based on the UN. But that means that the UN has to be reformed so that the south has agency in it.

告别傲慢与外交反思

尼古拉·坦根: 非洲领导人常说，我们欧洲人去那里往往只是指手画脚，而中国人则是带着真金白银的资本前去。中国在南方国家的影响力是否让你感到担忧？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: The African leaders say that we in Europe we just come and wag the finger whilst the Chinese come with the capital. Is that a worry for you, that Chinese influence in the south?

亚历山大·斯图布: 确实，中国在长期战略博弈上的表现显然比我们欧洲人出色得多。有句老话说得很生动：一位西方领导人访问某个非洲国家，降落在由中国人建造的机场，驱车行驶在由中国人修建的高速公路上，随后在一家由中国人盖起的酒店里召开新闻发布会，警告当地人“你们不应该与中国合作”。我认为这恰恰反映了某些时候的心态。因此我的看法是，我们需要一种更有尊严、更加谦逊的外交政策。我们需要少一些高高在上的说教，多一些务实具体的合作。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Yeah, I mean definitely the Chinese have played the long strategic game much better than we Europeans. You know the good old saying goes that a western leader came to an African country, landed at an airport which was built by the Chinese, drove down a highway which was built by the Chinese and then gave a press conference in a hotel built by the Chinese saying that you should not be cooperating with the Chinese. And I think that's sometimes the mentality. So my take is that we need a more dignified foreign policy. We need to be more humble. We need to wag the finger a little bit less and do a little bit more concrete cooperation.

尼古拉·坦根: 你提到西方犯的错误并非出于恶意，只是我们习惯了完全透过自己的视角看世界。那么我们究竟错在哪里？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: You say that the West mistakes wasn't bad intent. It's just that we just saw the world through our own eyes. So what did we get wrong?

亚历山大·斯图布: 我觉得这很正常，我们都是自身身份的囚徒。你和我都是斯堪的纳维亚人、北欧人，也是欧洲人。我们高度西方中心主义，并且出生于或至少职业生涯成长于后冷战秩序之中。这必然会偏斜我们看待世界的方式。而我们现在必须认识到，要想在这个新世界中生存，就必须做出改变。

我们错在骨子里那种与生俱来的傲慢，以为“历史已经终结”，西方将永远主导一切，所有人都必须跟随我们。但事实早已不再如此。看看极其繁荣的海湾国家，看看如今全球人口最多的印度，看看非洲的尼日利亚、南非、埃及等关键大国，再看看巴西和阿根廷，你猛然发现，西方已不再是世界的唯一主导。当然，更复杂的一个因素在于美国新一届政府，他们在多边主义上的理念与我们西方多数传统观念截然不同。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Well, I think it's quite normal. I mean, we're all prisoners of our own identity. I mean, you and I are Scandinavians or Nordics. We're Europeans. We're very western oriented and we've been born or at least had our professional life in sort of a post-cold war order. So that sort of skews what we think about the world and what we now have to understand that in order for you to be able to survive in this new world, you need to change. What we got wrong was this sort of innate arrogance that history ended and the west would kind of dominate and everyone would follow. And that's not the case anymore. You look at the Gulf States which are extremely prosperous. You look at India which is now the most populated country in the world. You look at some of the big players in Africa like Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt. You look at Brazil and Argentina and suddenly you realize that it's not the west that dominates anymore. And of course an additional spice to this comes with the new US administration which has quite a different approach to multilateralism than many of us in the west in general.

第四种权力：算力、数据与AI

尼古拉·坦根: 你提到除了传统力量之外还有第四种权力。你和马克·卡尼 (Mark Carney) 今年夏天撰文指出，我们迎来了由算力、数据与人工智能构建的技术阵营。这一局面将如何演变？你认为它是否已经成为四大力量中最强有力的一极？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: And some additional spice from the fourth power, and you and Mark Carney wrote this summer that we now have this incoming technology block built on compute, data, and AI. And so how is this going to pan out? Is that now the most powerful of the four, you think?

亚历山大·斯图布: 严格来说，它并不完全是一个独立的第四阵营。如果让我重新给书起个名字，我可能会称之为**“权力的矩形”**。原本我划定的是由美国领导的“全球西方”，但现在美国非常自觉、甚至相当自豪地不再必然愿意充当该集团的领头羊，因此我或许会把我们称为“全球北方”。

我和马克毫无疑问都坚信，技术尤其是人工智能正在彻底改变一切：它改变经济运作与工作模式；改变政治生态与信息传播机制；改变战争形态与交战方式；它甚至还在重塑科学本身以及我们作为人类的定义。但核心在于获取技术资源的机会。我的观点是，我们应当将此视为一种双赢合作，而非纯粹零和的交易。因为如果想在AI时代占据制高点，你需要两样东西：第一是算力，即海量数据及其处理能力；第二是能源，更具体地说是充足的电力。我认为在东西方以及南北半球之间必须保持一种平衡，这绝非仅仅是美中两强争霸的游戏，现实远比这错综复杂。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Yeah, it's not really a fourth block as such. I mean, if I would rename my book, it would be the rectangle of power because I had the global west led by the US, but I think now out of choice and very proudly the US would not want necessarily to lead that block anymore. So I would call us perhaps the global north. But Mark and I think obviously that technology, especially AI, is going to change everything. It changes the way in which we work in the economy. It changes politics and the way in which we communicate. It changes war and the way in which we conduct it. And it changes science and the way in which we are as human beings. But of course this is about access. So my argument is that we should see this more as a win-win not as a transaction because AI of course if you want to dominate you need two things: you need compute so kind of data and capacity to handle it, and secondly you need energy or more specifically electricity. And I think we need to have a balance between the east, west, north, and south on this and think that this is not a game with you know only US versus China, it's much more complicated than that.

尼古拉·坦根: 民主制度目前是如何适应这些颠覆性新技术的？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: How are democracies adopting to the new technology?

亚历山大·斯图布: 表现非常糟糕。原因在于，现代民主制度在17或18世纪由约翰·洛克、孟德斯鸠、卢梭和霍布斯等人奠定时，其设计初衷就是缓慢而审慎的——需要经过幕后博弈、妥协权衡，有充分的时间来做出反应。你看美国宪法基本上就是在密室妥协中诞生的。

但今天你不再拥有那种从容应对的奢侈了，你必须时刻对所有事情做出即时反应。回到我的新书，我最初起草的暂定书名其实是《数字民主还是数字独裁》。我认为民主体制至今未能很好地适应社交媒体与现代科技，并且在这场博弈中节节败退。我们也看到大型科技企业在诸多维度上拥有了超越主权国家的力量。所以你会提出那个问题：Anthropic 或 Meta 是否比挪威和芬兰加起来更有权势？这是个非常严肃且合理的问题，而政治学目前尚无法给出标准答案。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Badly. And the reason is that, you know, democracy as such was originally created say in the 1700s by the likes of John Locke and Montesquieu and Rousseau and Hobbes and others to be very slow and cumbersome, you know, sort of back deals and you had a lot of time to respond to things. And you know the US constitution was basically created in secret as a compromise. And you don't have that luxury anymore; you have to react to everything all the time. And coming back to the book, the original version actually of the working title was "Digital Democracy or Digital Dictatorship". And I think democracies have not been able to cope with social media and with modern technology yet and they're losing the struggle. We also see big tech companies in many ways being more powerful than states. So you can ask the question, you know, is Anthropic or is Facebook more powerful than Norway and Finland? And I think it's a legitimate question and political science can't necessarily give you the answer.

尼古拉·坦根: 我们在金融市场上也能清晰看到这一点，其中某些企业的市值甚至超过了许多主权国家。你认为AI会成为拉平阶层的均衡器，还是会加剧不平等与不公？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Well, we see it in financial markets. The market capitalization of some of these companies are bigger than countries. Do you think AI will be an equalizer or do you think it will amplify injustice?

亚历山大·斯图布: 两种可能都存在，这完全取决于我们在未来几年做出的抉择。如果观察生物科技与数字技术的融合，它极可能演变成极其严重的去稳定化因素，进而加剧不平等。你可以想象一下，未来富人阶层不仅能够长寿，还能通过科技在智力和身体素质上全面超越普通大众，这几乎就像在智人内部演化出了两个截然不同的物种。

但从积极的一面来看，AI也能带来跨越式的赋能。例如在非洲部分地区，教育和医疗资源曾经极度匮乏，但如今通过AI大模型，孩子们可以随时获得相当于世界顶级导师的教学辅导，偏远村庄也能获得顶尖水平的AI辅助医疗诊断。关键在于我们如何规制并引导这一过程。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: It can go both ways and that depends on the choices that we make in the upcoming years. I mean if you look at the mix between biology and technology then it can really be a destabilizing factor which will increase injustices. I mean, imagine that, you know, rich people will live longer and will become much more clever than other people, so that you basically have two different types of human beings, homo sapiens. But the positive thing about it is that AI can be a great equalizer. You know, you take education or health care in some parts of Africa where you don't have access to teachers or doctors, and suddenly through AI and large language models you can get a world-class teacher or medical diagnosis. So it depends on how we regulate and steer it.

尼古拉·坦根: 比尔·盖茨一直积极倡导建立应对AI的国内与国际监管框架。对此你怎么看？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Well, another person, Bill Gates, he is advocating creating a domestic and international framework for dealing with AI. What are your thoughts about that?

亚历山大·斯图布: 我非常赞同。但问题在于，我们目前设立的大多数制度架构都是为了管理主权国家而设计的，而不是为了应对无国界的跨国技术网络。科技巨头往往凌驾于国家监管之上，甚至在制定自己的全球规则。此外，这场技术革命与以往大不相同。第一次工业革命由蒸汽机驱动，第二次由电力与内燃机推动，第三次是信息技术时代，如今我们迎来了第四次工业革命。每一次重大技术飞跃都会引发地缘政治力量的剧烈重组，催生出新的地缘赢家与输家。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Oh, I definitely agree with that. But the problem is that most of the institutional architecture that we have created is for states, not for technology or tech platforms. Tech companies operate across borders and create their own rules. And this sort of technological revolution is quite different from previous ones. The first industrial revolution was steam, the second electricity, the third ICT, and now we are in the fourth. Each technological shift reshuffles geopolitical power, creating new winners and losers.

尼古拉·坦根: 考虑到现在AI已经被深度嵌入到武器系统、汽车、电池、前沿科研以及日常一切领域，你真的认为促成美中两国在此领域展开合作是可行的吗？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Given how we now embed AI into weapons and cars and batteries and research and into everything pretty much, do you really think it's feasible to get the US and China to cooperate on this?

亚历山大·斯图布: 出于生存的必要性，我认为是可行的。某种程度上，当前各方在关键矿产、算力储备、芯片制造能力以及顶尖企业生态上形成了一定的战略平衡，这其实是一件好事。双方都清楚意识到，如果AI完全脱离控制，无论是失控的自主武器还是颠覆社会契约的未知风险，对谁都没有好处。冷战时期美苏即使处于严重对抗中，最终依然达成了核军控条约。面对AI，美中同样需要建立这种底线机制，以确保事态不会滑向万劫不复的失控深渊。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: I think out of necessity, yes. So that's why it's kind of good that there's a certain balance of power here with critical minerals and capacity to compute and the power of the chips and then the companies. Both sides realize that if this goes completely wild, whether it's autonomous weapons or destabilizing societies, nobody wins. During the Cold War, the US and the Soviet Union were able to agree on nuclear non-proliferation because the alternative was mutual destruction. With AI, you need a similar guardrail to make sure that things don't run astray.

基于价值的现实主义

尼古拉·坦根: 转向你对世界新秩序给出的解决方案，你将其命名为**“基于价值的现实主义” (Values-Based Realism)**。这究竟是什么意思？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Moving on to the new order then, you call your answer values-based realism. What does that mean exactly?

亚历山大·斯图布: 它包含两层含义：

第一，坚定坚守自身的核心价值。对于芬兰、挪威、北欧乃至西方欧洲而言，这些价值观是民主、人权、自由、法治以及基于规则的国际体系。在这些根本原则上，我们绝不妥协放弃。

第二，必须具备彻底的现实主义视野。你必须承认，世界上的绝大多数国家并不认同我们的体系，他们也并不愿亦步亦趋地模仿我们。如果你始终采取“非黑即白”的说教态度，你将无法在当今世界达成任何实质性合作。现实主义意味着你必须与体制完全不同的国家打交道，无论是在气候变化、贸易还是安全问题上。为了取得实际成果，你必须懂得求同存异与妥协。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: Well, it means two things. One is that you stay true to your values. So in your case, in my case, Finland, Norway, the Nordics, West and Europe, it's probably about democracy, freedom, rule of law, human rights, and a rules-based system. You don't abandon those. But the second part is realism. You have to realize that the vast majority of the world does not share our system and doesn't want to copy it. And if you approach them with a binary, lecturing mindset, you will get nowhere. Realism means you engage with countries that have different political systems, whether it's on climate, trade, or peace, because in order to get results, you need compromises.

尼古拉·坦根: 如果针对每一个具体问题都去建立因事而异的临时联盟，在实践中该如何运转？这难道不会极其混乱吗？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: If you have all these ad hoc coalitions with a different set of countries for every problem, how is that going to work in practice?

亚历山大·斯图布: 确实会极其混乱，而且我们现在已经看到这种趋势在显现。我们目前正处于三大未来路径的分水岭：

第一种路径是滑向多极交易型的混乱无序，区域冲突频发，没有普遍遵循的规则，强权即公理；

第二种路径是通过深度改革联合国等核心多边机制，建立一个包容全球南方的新型多边治理框架；

第三种路径则是二者之间的妥协产物：一个碎片化的世界，充斥着针对特定议题组建的微型多边主义联盟。尽管并不完美，但相比彻底的丛林法则，这种灵活务实的联盟至少能维系关键领域的全球协作。

Original English

Alexander Stubb: It's going to be extremely messy and we're starting to see that already. You kind of have three options if I may. First one is that we sort of continue with this almost chaotic transactional world where we see an increase in conflicts like Russia's war of aggression in Ukraine or what's happening in the Middle East, with no global rules. The second option is that we revitalize and reform the multilateral institutions, primarily the UN, and give the Global South real agency. The third option is somewhere in between: fragmented coalitions and regional transactional deals. It is messy, but pragmatic engagement is better than complete breakdown.

芬兰的幸福秘诀与双首长制

尼古拉·坦根: 亚历山大，让我们把焦点转向芬兰。芬兰为什么能成为如此幸福的国度？

Original English

Nicolai Tangen: Alex, let's zoom in a bit on Finland. Why is it such a happy place?
