乔几乎将他的整个成年生活和职业生涯都奉献给了探索混乱亲密关系的电影创作。你可能看过他的电影《酒友》（Drinking Buddies）或是 Netflix 剧集《随性所欲》（Easy）。这些作品都在探讨爱情究竟能有多复杂：有人嘴上说着一件事，心里想的却是完全相反的；你某天醒来，突然对自己伴侣的感觉变了，却说不清为什么；有人保守着秘密，一切随之失控——就是这种一团糟的复杂现实。

而在与妻子相伴二十多年后分开，乔自己的生活也陷入了这种混乱之中。分居后不久，乔开始与一位年轻女性交往。这听起来当然非常俗套，但关键在于，乔自己完全清楚这很俗套。在我们的整场谈话中，让我十分惊讶的是，他对于自己犯下的错误以及从中汲取的教训展现出了极高的坦诚度。

今天，我和乔一起探讨了这一切是如何启发他创作新片《永不落幕的太阳》（The Sun Never Sets）的。我们聊到了他的离婚、他的中年危机，以及当爱情摆在面前时，人会在多大程度上出乎自己的意料。

英文原文

Anna Martin: From the New York Times, I'm Anna Martin. This is Modern Love, and today I'm talking to director Joe Swanberg.

Joe has spent pretty much his entire adult life and his career making movies about messy relationships. You might know his movie Drinking Buddies or the Netflix series Easy. And these are all about how complicated love can be. Someone says one thing, they actually mean the total opposite. You wake up one day and you feel differently about your partner. You don't know why. Someone's keeping secrets and everything spirals out of control. It's that kind of mess.

Joe's life got messy after he and his wife separated after more than 20 years together. And pretty soon after, Joe started seeing a younger woman. That is, of course, quite cliche, but the thing is, Joe knows it's cliche. And throughout our conversation, I was really surprised by how open he was about the mistakes he made and everything you learn from them. So, today Joe and I discuss how all of this inspired his new movie, The Sun Never Sets. We get into his divorce, his midlife crisis, and how much you can surprise yourself when there's love on the line.

安娜·马丁: 乔·斯万博格，欢迎来到《现代爱情》。

探索亲密关系的道德灰色地带

安娜·马丁: 完全不用紧张，我们只是随便聊聊（笑）。乔，你的电影总是在探讨浪漫关系的灰色地带，那些没有明确好人坏人的复杂处境。为什么你总是被这种叙事吸引？

英文原文

Anna Martin: No nerves whatsoever. We're just chatting. Joe, you make movies about the gray areas of romantic relationships. Why are you so drawn to stories that don't have a clear good guy or bad guy?

乔·斯万博格: 是的，我想从小时候起，我就一直觉得电影有一种强大的力量，能让我感到不那么孤独。因为在生活中，我们常常会产生一些让自己感到羞耻的想法，或者发现自己做出了一些并不光彩的行为……

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Yeah, I think that from a young age I always felt that movies had the power to make me feel less alone. Because in real life, you often have thoughts that you're ashamed of, or you find yourself behaving in a way that is not flattering...

安娜·马丁: 噢，我可从来没干过那种事，乔。其实经常有（笑）。

英文原文

Anna Martin: Oh, I've never done that, Joe. Actually, [laughter]

乔·斯万博格: （笑）从来没有过对吧？但我一直觉得，如果某件事在现实中让我感到复杂、困难或者羞耻，那么很可能成千上万、甚至数百万人也有过同样的感受。

所以，我一直被把这些东西搬上银幕所吸引，去展示人们把事情搞砸的一面。这既能减轻我自己的羞耻感，也能让我作为一个观众，在看别人把生活过得一团糟时，心想：“好吧，看来不是只有我一个人是混蛋。”

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: [laughter] Never, right? But I always just thought that if something feels complex or difficult or shameful to me in real life, chances are thousands or millions of other people are feeling that way, too. And so, I've just always been drawn to putting that stuff on screen, showing people mess up, in a way that alleviates my own shame, but also as an audience member allows me to watch other people be a mess and think, OK, well, it's not just me who's an asshole.

安娜·马丁: 是的。

英文原文

Anna Martin: Yeah.

乔·斯万博格: 你懂这种感觉吧。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: You know.

英文原文

Anna Martin: Totally. I think we go to the movies to see ourselves, even the parts of ourselves that we don't love.

乔·斯万博格: 没错。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Exactly.

二十年婚姻的终结与反思

安娜·马丁: 那么，我们来聊聊你真实生活中的“混乱”吧。如果我没算错的话，你二十出头就结婚了，对吗？

英文原文

Anna Martin: So let's get into your real-life mess. If I have this right, you got married in your early 20s, correct?

乔·斯万博格: 我 25 岁结的婚。我们是在大学认识的，大约 19 岁就在一起了。我们在 2020 年正式分居，当时我已经 39 岁，马上就要 40 岁了。所以，我们在一起度过了非常漫长的时光。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: I was 25 when we got married. We met in college, so we were about 19. And we separated in 2020 when I was 39, turning 40. So, it was a very long run.

安娜·马丁: 超过 20 年的时光。你们俩都在电影行业工作，对吧？

英文原文

Anna Martin: More than 20 years. And you both worked in the film industry, right?

乔·斯万博格: 是的。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Yeah.

安娜·马丁: 你们一起拍电影，还一起养育了两个孩子。

英文原文

Anna Martin: You made movies together, and you have two kids together.

乔·斯万博格: 没错。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Yeah.

安娜·马丁: 既然你们的关系如此紧密交织，是什么导致了最终的分开？

英文原文

Anna Martin: So given how connected your lives were, what led to the separation?

乔·斯万博格: 我想，归根结底是我们都随着年龄的增长发生了改变。我们认识的时候才 19 岁，在那个年纪，你根本不知道自己 25 岁、30 岁或 40 岁时会变成什么样的人。

我很感激我们在一起走过了那么多年，并且依然深爱着彼此。但不可否认的是，我们渐渐成长为了不同的人，对生活有了不同的追求。我们共同抚养孩子，合作拍片，但作为伴侣的核心连接却逐渐磨损了。当意识到这一点时，我们知道是时候做出改变了。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: I think it really just came down to growing and changing. We met when we were 19, and at 19 you have no idea who you're going to be at 25 or 30 or 40. I'm grateful that we had such a long run and that there was so much love there. But we just grew into different people who wanted different things out of life. We were great co-parents and creative collaborators, but the romantic partnership had really shifted. And once that became clear, we knew it was time.

分居后的约会与“教科书式”中年危机

安娜·马丁: 经历了一段持续了整整二十年的长久亲密关系后，重新回到单身世界是什么感觉？

英文原文

Anna Martin: What was it like entering the dating world after being in a relationship for literally your entire adult life?

乔·斯万博格: 坦率地说，那既令人兴奋，又极度令人恐慌。因为我以前从来没有真正意义上作为一个成年人去“约会”过。我所有的成年经验都是在一段固定关系中度过的。突然之间，我拥有了自由，但同时也充满了迷茫和不安。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: It was honestly thrilling and completely terrifying. Because I had never dated as an adult. All of my adult reference points were within a committed relationship. So suddenly I had this freedom, but I was also filled with confusion and insecurity.

安娜·马丁: 然后你就遇到了那位年轻女性。你能谈谈那段经历吗？

英文原文

Anna Martin: And then you met a younger woman. Can you talk about that?

乔·斯万博格: 是的。分居后不久，我开始和一位比我小相当多的女性交往。我知道，这听起来完全就是中年危机的标准模板——40岁的离婚男人开始和20多岁的年轻姑娘约会，简直俗套得不能再俗套了。

但我当时完全深陷其中。在那段关系里，有一种强烈的被崇拜感和重新找回活力的感觉。但很快，年龄、生活阅历以及人生阶段的巨大差距就暴露了出来。我意识到自己并不是在建立一段真正成熟健康的伴侣关系，而是在某种程度上通过她来逃避我面对离婚、衰老和自我认同危机时的痛苦。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Yeah. Pretty soon after the separation, I started dating someone significantly younger than me. And look, I know how that sounds. It is the textbook midlife crisis cliché: guy turns 40, gets divorced, starts dating a woman in her 20s. It couldn't be more on the nose. But I was completely in it. There was a feeling of validation and feeling alive again. But very quickly, the reality of the age gap and the difference in life experience set in. I realized I wasn't really building a mature partnership; I was using this dynamic, in some ways, to avoid dealing with the grief of my divorce, aging, and who I was on my own.

安娜·马丁: 这种自省非常深刻。当你深处其中的时候，你能意识到自己在扮演这个“中年危机男”的角色吗？还是说这纯粹是事后的觉悟？

英文原文

Anna Martin: That's really insightful. Were you aware in the moment that you were playing out this midlife crisis archetype, or is that strictly hindsight?

乔·斯万博格: 一半一半吧。理智上，我知道我在做什么，我甚至会在心里自嘲：“天呐，乔，你正在把所有的老套情节都演一遍。”但情感上，那种多巴胺的冲动和对重获新生的渴望彻底压倒了理智。你明知道前面可能是个坑，但你还是闭着眼睛跳了下去。

直到这段关系结束，随之而来的痛苦和混乱才真正逼迫我去正视：我为什么会做出这些选择？我到底在恐惧什么？

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: It was a bit of both. Intellectually, I knew what I was doing. I would literally say to myself, "God, Joe, you are hitting every single cliché." But emotionally, the rush of dopamine and the desire to feel rejuvenated just overrode logic. You know you might be driving towards a wall, but you hit the gas anyway. It wasn't until that relationship ended and the dust settled that I had to confront why I made those choices and what I was actually running from.

新片创作：《永不落幕的太阳》与自我剖析

安娜·马丁: 而这种经历直接促成了你的新电影《永不落幕的太阳》（The Sun Never Sets）。能告诉我们这部电影讲述了什么吗？

英文原文

Anna Martin: And that directly led to your new film, The Sun Never Sets. Tell us about the movie.

乔·斯万博格: 这部电影讲述了一个刚经历长期婚姻破裂的中年男人，试图在现代约会世界中重新找寻自我，却陷入了一场充满激情但又注定充满矛盾的跨年龄恋情。

对我来说，拍摄这部电影是一种“驱魔”的过程。我不想在电影里把自己粉饰成受害者或是情圣。我想展示的是那种笨拙、自私、渴望爱却又不知道如何正确去爱的真实状态。通过角色去直面自己的虚荣和脆弱。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: The movie is about a middle-aged guy coming out of a long marriage who tries to navigate modern dating and falls into an intense, complicated relationship with a much younger woman. For me, making it was almost an act of exorcism. I didn't want to make myself look like a victim or a hero. I wanted to show the clumsiness, the selfishness, the genuine desire for connection mixed with total confusion. It was about confronting my own vanity and vulnerability on screen.

安娜·马丁: 拍这样一部高度取材于自己真实私密经历的电影，会不会感到害怕？尤其是在公众和你的前妻、孩子们面前？

英文原文

Anna Martin: Was it terrifying to make something so personal and vulnerable, knowing your ex-wife, your kids, and the public would see it?

乔·斯万博格: 当然非常害怕。但作为创作者，我一直相信，最具有个人特质的感受往往最具有普适性。我和前妻保持了非常诚恳和尊重的沟通，我也尽最大努力确保这不是一部指责任何人的电影，而是一部关于人类弱点和成长的电影。

我希望观众看完后能感受到：即使你在感情里把一切都搞砸了，即使你做出了让自己后悔的蠢事，你依然有机会去学习、去承担责任，并成为一个更成熟的人。

英文原文

Joe Swanberg: Terrifying, absolutely. But as a filmmaker, I've always believed that the most specific personal truths are often the most universal. I had open, respectful conversations with my ex-wife, and I tried very hard to make sure the film wasn't about assigning blame, but about exploring human frailty. My hope is that anyone watching it feels that even if you mess up completely, even if you do foolish things in romance, there is still room for accountability, grace, and growth.
