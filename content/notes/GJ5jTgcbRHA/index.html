Claude.ai 项目功能入门：提升协作与效率的智能工作空间 Anthropic 2025-12-02

领域: tech-engineering

分类: technology

主讲人: Anthropic

来源: 查看

摘要:

本文详细介绍了 Claude.ai 的“项目”功能，这是一个为用户提供自包含工作环境的强大工具。通过项目，用户可以创建独立的聊天历史、定制知识库和个性化设置，从而获得更精准、更符合团队目标的AI响应。文章深入探讨了项目知识库、定制指令、协作共享和权限管理等核心特性，并提供了新产品开发、课程设计、个人财务规划和家居装修等多种应用场景示例，旨在帮助用户利用Claude.ai项目优化信息组织、激发创意并简化工作流程。

Claude.ai 项目功能简介

什么是项目？它们如何改善与 Claude 的协作体验？通过项目功能，Claude 用户可以创建自包含的环境，这些环境拥有独立的聊天历史、特定的知识库和定制化的设置。我们将概述项目的功能、用途、创建方法以及一些高级特性。

What are projects, and how do they improve working with Claude? With projects, Claude users can create self-contained environments with their own chat histories, specific knowledge bases, and customized settings. We are going to provide an overview of projects, what they are used for, how to create them, and some advanced features.

接下来，让我们探讨项目的一些关键特性。

Let's talk about a few of the key features of projects.

项目核心特性：知识库、指令与协作

项目知识库通过允许您在每个项目中上传相关文档来增强 Claude 的理解能力。每次您在项目内部与 Claude 聊天时，这些知识都会与您的提示词一起被自动考虑。这种上下文信息使 Claude 能够提供更具洞察力、更符合项目需求的响应，从而与您团队的目标、术语和背景保持一致。

View/Hide Original English

Project knowledge enhances Claude's understanding by allowing you to upload relevant documents within each project. Every time you chat with Claude inside of a project, this knowledge is automatically considered alongside your prompt. This contextual information enables Claude to deliver more informed, project-specific responses that align with your team's goals, terminology, and background.

项目能够处理更多的内容而不会遇到限制。包含大量信息的项目会自动使用检索增强生成（Retrieval Augmented Generation, 简称 RAG：一种结合了信息检索和文本生成的人工智能技术，旨在通过从外部知识库中检索相关信息来增强大型语言模型的生成能力）进行处理，使 Claude 能够利用更多的上下文来帮助您。

View/Hide Original English

Projects can handle much more content without running into limits. Projects with lots of information are automatically processed using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), enabling Claude to use more context to help you.

在设置项目时，您可以为每个项目定义项目指令，以进一步定制 Claude 的响应。与上下文一样，这些指令在您每次与 Claude 聊天时都会被考虑。这是一个很好的空间，可以指导 Claude 的响应方式。例如，您可以要求 Claude 使用更正式的语气，或者从特定行业的目标视角回答问题。

View/Hide Original English

When setting up a project, you can define project instructions for each project to further tailor Claude's responses. Like context, these are considered every time you chat with Claude. It's a good space to guide how Claude responds. For example, you can ask Claude to use a more formal tone or answer questions from a specific perspective, targeting a specific industry.

如果您使用的是 Claude for work 计划，您可以与组织中的其他成员共享项目，从而实现强大的协作和知识共享能力。例如，一个品牌团队可以创建一个包含品牌声音和语调指南的项目，帮助公司中的任何人像营销人员一样写作。

View/Hide Original English

If you're on a Claude for work plan, you can share projects with other members of your organization, enabling powerful collaboration and knowledge-sharing capabilities. For example, a brand team can create a project with voice and tone guidelines that helps anyone at their company write like a marketer.

创建您的第一个项目

好的，让我们通过三个简单的步骤来创建您的第一个项目。首先，点击“新建项目”并为其命名。接下来，描述您的项目以及您希望实现的目标，例如：“我希望制定品牌指南，为我的花店业务撰写一系列案例研究博客。”然后，点击“创建项目”。在项目标题的右侧，您会看到几个菜单项。您可以为该项目加星标以便更快访问，编辑项目详情，以及归档或删除项目。

View/Hide Original English

Okay, let's get started by creating your first project in three simple steps. First, click 'New Project' and give it a name. Next, describe your project and what you are hoping to achieve, for example: 'I would like to make brand guidelines to write a series of case study blogs for my flower business.' Then, click 'Create Project.' To the right of the project title, you'll see a few menu items. You can star this project for quicker access, edit project details, and archive or delete the project. Provide Claude with relevant instructions and information for the chats within the project. This will work alongside user preferences and the selected style. Choose visibility for your project: you can select 'Private' for personal use or 'Public' for team collaboration.

为项目中的聊天提供相关的指令和信息。这将与用户偏好和所选样式协同工作。选择项目的可见性：您可以选择“私人”用于个人使用，或选择“公开”用于团队协作。

View/Hide Original English

Provide Claude with relevant instructions and information for the chats within the project. This will work alongside user preferences and the selected style. Choose visibility for your project: you can select 'Private' for personal use or 'Public' for team collaboration.

设置项目指令与知识库

从这里开始，为项目中的聊天提供项目指令和信息。这将与用户偏好和所选样式协同工作。点击“指令”以定义 Claude 应如何响应。在此空间内，您可以指定语气、专业水平、响应风格和预期结果，或者简单地提供更多关于您希望通过此项目实现目标的上下文。例如，在提供品牌指南时，解释思考过程并强调语气和品牌应用。完成指令提供后，点击“保存指令”。这将应用于项目中的每次聊天。

View/Hide Original English

From here, provide project instructions and information for the chats within the project. This will work alongside user preferences and the selected style. Click on 'Instructions' to define how Claude should respond. Within this space, you can specify tone, expertise level, response style, and desired outcomes, or simply give more context on what you're trying to achieve with this project. For instance, when providing the brand guidelines, explain the thought process and highlight tone and brand application. Once done providing instructions, click 'Save Instructions.' This will apply to every chat in the project.

您会在项目主页的右侧找到“文件”菜单。您上传到此空间的任何内容都将用于此项目中的所有聊天。点击加号按钮将内容添加到项目中。您可以上传 PDF、文档、CSV、文本文件，或连接到 Google Drive（谷歌云端硬盘：谷歌提供的一项云存储服务）。现在，Claude 可以在任何对话中即时引用这些内容。Claude 将处理这些信息并将其用作您在项目内聊天的上下文。例如，如果您正在为特定客户创建新的品牌指南，您可以上传他们现有的品牌材料，Claude 将在其响应中引用该文件。请注意，除非信息被添加到项目知识库中，否则项目内不同聊天之间的上下文不会共享。

View/Hide Original English

You'll find the 'Files' menu on the right side of your project's main page. Anything you upload to this space will be used across all of your chats within this project. Click on the plus button to add content to the project. You can upload PDFs, documents, CSVs, text files, or connect to Google Drive. Now Claude can instantly reference this content in any conversation. Claude will process this information and use it as context in your chats within the project. For example, if you are working on creating new brand guidelines for a specific client, you can upload their existing branded materials, and Claude will reference that file in its response. Note that context is not shared across disparate chats within a project unless the information is added into the project knowledge base.

有时，您的项目知识可能会接近上下文窗口的限制。Claude 将自动启用 RAG 模式（Retrieval Augmented Generation mode：检索增强生成模式）来扩展项目的容量。

View/Hide Original English

Sometimes your project knowledge will approach the context window limit. Claude will automatically enable RAG mode (Retrieval Augmented Generation mode) to expand your project's capacity.

协作与权限管理

一旦您的项目设置完成，您就可以在项目内与 Claude 展开聊天。您可以通过观看我们的其他视频，了解更多关于与 Claude 聊天的最佳实践。您可以将聊天设置为公开，以便队友可以查看并在此基础上继续您的工作。有三种权限级别控制着谁可以在您的项目中做什么。“可查看”成员可以查看项目内容、访问知识和聊天，但不能进行更改。可以将其视为具有讨论权限的只读访问。“可编辑”成员拥有完整的协作权限。他们可以修改指令、更新知识、管理成员，并积极为项目做出贡献。项目创建者控制一切；此外，他们决定谁可以看到项目。他们可以与特定的人共享，或者使私人项目对整个组织可见。正确的权限可以确保您的项目安全，同时实现顺畅的协作。因此，请选择最适合您团队的选项。以下是 Claude 的一些协作功能。

View/Hide Original English

Once your project is set up, you can initiate chats with Claude within the project. You can learn more about best practices for chatting with Claude by reviewing one of our other videos. You can make chats public so teammates can view and build upon your work. There are three permission levels that control who can do what in your projects. 'Can view' members can view project contents, access knowledge, and chat, but cannot make changes. Think read-only access with discussion rights. 'Can edit' members have full collaboration power. They can modify instructions, update knowledge, manage members, and actively contribute to the project. Project creators control everything; plus, they decide who sees the project. They can share with specific people or make private projects visible to the entire organization. The right permissions keep your projects secure while enabling smooth collaboration. So, pick the options that work best for your team. Here are some of Claude's collaboration features.

查看“与我共享”选项卡，可以轻松找到其他人与您共享的项目。当有人与您共享项目时，您将收到电子邮件通知，并且项目创建者可以根据需要修改权限或移除访问权限。多个成员可以在同一项目环境中贡献文档、创建聊天并协同工作，这使得项目成为团队协作的理想选择。有时您需要使用内容，但不想将其添加到项目的知识库中。没问题。只需在对话过程中上传，它就会与您的项目知识库分开。这是一种分享上下文或示例的完美方式，而不会使知识库变得混乱。

View/Hide Original English

Check out the 'Shared with me' tab to easily find projects that others have shared with you. You will receive email notifications when someone shares a project with you, and project creators can modify permissions or remove access as needed. Multiple members can contribute documents, create chats, and work together within the same project environment, making projects ideal for team collaboration. Sometimes you need to use content without adding it to your project's knowledge base. No problem. Simply upload it during the conversation, and it stays separate from your project knowledge. It's a perfect way to share context or examples without cluttering the knowledge base.

项目应用场景示例

以下是一些您可以创建的项目示例。您可以管理新产品的开发过程，从构思到发布，例如一款环保水瓶。这对于将所有产品相关信息集中在一个地方非常有用，可以利用 Claude 进行创意构思并跟踪产品设计的演变。Claude 可以通过生成内容创意、提供写作辅助和保持跨平台的一致性来简化内容创建过程。

Here are some examples of projects you can create. You can manage the development process of a new product from ideation to launch, such as an eco-friendly water bottle. This is useful for keeping all product-related information in one place, using Claude for creative ideation and tracking the evolution of the product design. Claude can streamline the content creation process by generating content ideas, providing writing assistance, and maintaining consistency across platforms.

您可以设计和开发教育课程，例如 Anthropic 的 AI 流利度课程。在项目中，您可以组织课程材料，使用 Claude 帮助解释复杂概念，并迭代改进课程内容。您可以创建一个项目来跟踪、分析和规划个人财务，以实现您的个人财务目标和预算。通过将所有财务信息安全地保存在一个地方，Claude 可以分析消费模式并协助进行财务计算和规划。

You can design and develop an educational course, such as Anthropic's AI Fluency coursework. In projects, you can organize course materials, use Claude to help explain complex concepts, and iteratively improve the course content. You can create a project to track, analyze, and plan personal finances for your personal financial goals and budget. By keeping all financial information in one secure place, Claude can analyze spending patterns and help with financial calculations and planning.

或者，您可以规划和管理家居装修，例如更新厨房空间。这允许您集中所有项目信息，使用 Claude 协助设计理念和预算计算，并跟踪所有决策和沟通。这些示例展示了 Claude 项目功能的灵活性。无论您是处理专业任务、个人目标还是协作工作，项目都可以帮助您组织信息、激发创意并简化工作流程。请记住，您可以自定义每个项目以适应您的特定需求和工作流程。有关项目入门的更多信息，请查阅我们的帮助中心文章。

Alternatively, you can plan and manage a home renovation, such as updating a kitchen space. This allows you to centralize all project information, use Claude to help with design ideas and budget calculations, and keep track of all decisions and communications. These examples demonstrate the flexibility of what can be done with projects in Claude. Whether you're working on professional tasks, personal goals, or collaborative efforts, projects can help you organize information, generate ideas, and streamline your work process.
