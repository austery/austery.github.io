In the footage, the woman's SUV is stopped in the street, partly blocking one lane. As cars approach, she waves at them to go around, but a truck flashing its lights stops and federal agents get out. She then shifts into drive and starts to move forward. The agent standing at the front near the driver's side headlight pulls out a gun and fires. He continues shooting as the vehicle moves past him. A Times analysis of three different camera angles shows she was driving away from the officer when he opened fire and it appears he was not in the path of the SUV. In the footage, the vehicle then accelerates and crashes into parked cars and a utility pole. The Minneapolis police chief said later the victim had been shot in the head and was pronounced dead at a hospital.
