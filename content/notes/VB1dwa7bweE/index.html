养宠正变成一种奢侈品？解读宠物通胀与应对之道 TVO Today 2026-09-03

领域: finance-wealth

分类: investment-assets

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-09-03

摘要:

随着通货膨胀蔓延至宠物领域，食品、药物和医疗等养宠成本大幅上涨，导致许多安大略省家庭无力承担甚至被迫弃养。节目探讨了导致宠物医疗成本激增的技术演进与经济动因，并介绍了社区紧急寄养、宠物食物银行、兽医沟通策略及 Farley Foundation 救助等应对方案。

宠物通胀来袭与弃养困境

女士: 我们把这种现象称为**“宠物通胀”（pet-flation）**。大家都能在杂货店里看到食品价格上涨，对吧？养宠物也是一模一样的道理。你不仅能看到宠物食品的价格在往上飙升，实际上宠物所需要的每一件必需品成本都在上涨。就像我们的衣物、杂货、买的苹果一样，所有东西的物料成本都在全面上涨。

Original English

WOMAN: We're calling it 'pet-flation'. We're seeing rising food costs in a grocery store, right? So, it's the same thing with pets. You're seeing the pet food-- the pet food prices go up, but you're seeing every single thing that a pet needs is going up in cost. Just like our clothing is going up, our groceries, our apples, all of it.

杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan): 试想一下，你必须在支付自己的生活账单和继续抚养宠物之间做出痛苦的选择。对于越来越多的**安大略省（Ontarians）**居民来说，这已经不再是一个假设性的问题。动物救援机构表示，他们看到越来越多人选择放弃抚养宠物并送去收容所，并不是因为主人不再爱它们，而是因为他们在经济上真的已经负担不起了。

食物成本变得更高，兽医看诊与医疗护理费用更高，药物价格也在上涨，与此同时，人们本身就已经在住房成本、食品杂货和儿童托育费用的重压之下艰难挣扎。那么，究竟是什么在推高这些物价？宠物主人又有什么切实可行的应对办法呢？我是杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan)。让我们先从许多宠物主人目前最关心的一个核心问题开始探讨：为什么在过去的几年里，照顾宠物的花费会出现如此剧烈的暴涨？

Original English

JEYAN: Imagine having to choose between paying your bills and keeping your pet. For more and more Ontarians, that isn't hypothetical. Animal rescues say they're seeing more people surrender pets not because they don't love them, but because they can't afford to keep them. Food costs more, vet care costs more, medication costs more, and at the same time, people are already struggling with the cost of housing, groceries, and child care. So, what's driving these rising prices? And is there anything pet owners can do? I'm Jeyan Jeganathan. Let's start with the question a lot of pet owners are asking right now. Why has the cost of caring for a pet increased so dramatically over the last few years?

社区支持与预防性关怀

女士: 现在对于很多普通家庭来说，获得价格合理的**兽医医疗护理服务（veterinary care）**已经变得遥不可及。这导致很多家庭一再推迟带动物去诊所看兽医的时间，无法及时完成基础检查和治疗。结果就是，很多家庭里的宠物在家里拖得病情越来越严重，当主人最终抱着它们来到我们收容所门前求助时，动物往往已经处于极其糟糕危急的健康状态，主人不得不痛苦地选择放弃抚养权并将其移交。

我们真切地看到了这种情况——我昨天刚和我们的一个救助中心沟通过，他们现在每周大约会接到 15 个关于无力抚养而申请移交弃养宠物的电话求助。这真的让人非常非常难过。为了从根本上解决这个问题，我们必须在社区层面提供可及的预防性护理（preventable care），让大家能够获得基础的兽医服务，同时还要设立宠物食物银行（pet food banks），通过与人类食物银行展开合作来提供急需的物资支持，并且在社区层面普及行为训练资源。

我们目前正在推行一个非常棒的合作项目，我们正在与 “送餐到家”（Meals on Wheels） 组织合作。这个项目的核心思路是关注到社区里所有的老年人群体：当这些老年人需要宠物粮或者人类食物援助时，我们在配送餐食的同时直接为他们送上宠物食品。这就成为了一个绝佳的服务切入点，我们的工作人员借此可以顺便关心询问：“嘿，您家的小猫最近剪指甲了吗？你们二位还需要什么其他生活帮扶吗？您上次带它去看兽医是什么时候？”通过这种方式，我们能把服务直接送到真正需要帮助的人群手中，并且是在基层社区层面切实落地。

Original English

WOMAN: Accessibility to affordable veterinary care is just not-- it's not in the reach of families right now and they're delaying those visits to the veterinarian to be able to see and to be able to treat their animals. So people are having animals at home that are getting sicker and sicker and then they're showing up at our doorstep when they're in a really rough state and they're having to surrender them, and we're seeing it-- I spoke to one of our centres yesterday. They're getting about 15 calls a week for cases of surrender. And it's-- it's-- it's just really sad, and to address it, we really just have to offer that preventable care at the community level, access to veterinary services, but also just, like, pet food banks, partnering with human food banks and being able to have that resource available, behaviour training accessible at the community level. We have this really neat program right now. We're partnering with Meals on Wheels where it's looking at, okay, all of those seniors out there that they need pet food or they need human food, let's just also give them pet food at the same time, and then it's that entry point to be able to go in and say, "Hey, has your has your cat had a nail trim? Do you guys need anything else? When's the last time you saw a vet?" So, it's just bringing those services to the population and those-- the people that really need them, um, and doing it at the community level.

住房危机下的紧急寄养与隐瞒

杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan): 在那些不得不做出求助或弃养决定的家庭中，你具体看到了他们处于怎样的人生困境？

Original English

JEYAN: What have you seen of people in the situations that they've had to be in where they've had to make those calls?

女士: 是的，我们其实提供紧急寄养服务（emergency boarding），但这在社区里并不总是被大家广泛知晓。比如就在上周，在**奥兰治维尔（Orangeville）**发生了一个案例：有一个家庭由于房租天文数字般的暴涨，他们再也承担不起原本住处的租金。他们养了两只狗和一只猫，于是他们找到我们，申请了紧急寄养服务，这样他们就可以在寻找下一个能容纳全家人和宠物一起居住的住所过渡期内，把宠物暂时安心寄存在我们这里。

所以，求助的情况形形色色，表现形式各异。有的家庭非常坦诚开放，他们会直接说：“嘿，你们能帮我们共同想个解决办法吗？我们真的非常渴望克服眼前的困难把宠物留住。”但正如你刚才提到的，也有一部分家庭，他们内心极度沮丧、心烦意乱，来到收容所时甚至不敢承认那是自己养的动物。他们会谎称这是自己刚在路上捡到的流浪动物。

对于救助机构而言，这种情况其实是最糟糕的。因为如果被登记为流浪动物，按照法定流程我们必须先执行一段强制性的**“流浪动物暂扣观察期”（stray hold）**；而如果不需要走流浪动物扣留流程，我们本可以直接为它们寻找领养家庭并迅速完成新家庭安置。因此，我们非常需要同大家进行真诚开诚布公的对话，深入了解宠物主人们所面临的实际生活处境。这样我们才能首先尽全力帮助他们和自己的毛茸茸伙伴继续相伴生活；其次，如果他们确实已经彻底无法继续照顾宠物，我们也能以最高效合适的方式帮助这些动物得到妥善安置。

Original English

WOMAN: Yeah, we offer emergency boarding, which isn't always readily known to the community. So, when there was this case, I think, last week in Orangeville where a family, their rent has gone up astronomically. They could no longer afford their place. They had two dogs and a cat. So, they came to us and they asked for emergency boarding so that they could keep their pets with us while they try to find that next step of their housing to have their animals together. So, it comes in all different types and shapes and forms where you have families that are really-- they're open and they're saying, "Hey, can you help us find a solution? We really want to figure this out," but then there's also families that, like you said, they're just-- they're really-- they're upset and they come and they don't even say it's their animal. They-- They'll say that they found this stray, which for us is the worst thing because if it's a stray animal, then we have to put a stray hold on it. And if it's not having a stray hold, then we can adopt it out into a family right away. So, really just having those conversations with people and trying to understand where they're at so we can really help them first of all stay with their furry friends, but second of all really just help those animals if they can't take care of them anymore.

宠物医疗成本上涨的技术与运营动因

杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan): 克里夫医生（Dr. Cliff），我们来聊聊宠物的医疗保健问题。这是一个很大的开销板块。目前的兽医医疗成本究竟处于怎样的水平？价格是在持续上涨吗？行业现状是怎样的？

Original English

JEYAN: Dr. Cliff, let's talk pet health care. That's a big one. What is the cost of veterinary care right now? Is it rising? What are we seeing?

克里夫医生 (Dr. Cliff): 是的，费用确实在不断上涨，其中很大一部分原因就包括刚才那位女士提到的诸多因素。就我自己的诊所来说，不仅我的诊所房租比过去大幅增加，我的实验室检测成本、医药采购成本——比如我必须进货采购的各种药剂耗材——都在涨价。

但与此同时，还有一个重要原因是兽医目前能够提供的医疗服务范围，以及宠物主人对宠物诊疗所期望和要求的服务水平，与哪怕仅仅五年前相比，技术水平都要先进得多。

Original English

DR. CLIFF: Yeah, it is, it is rising for a lot of the reasons that she mentioned. Not only are my, you know, my rents higher, significantly more than it used to be, my laboratory costs, my medical costs, like as far as medicines that I have to purchase, but also the services that veterinarians can now provide, and the services that pet owners are expecting and wanting for their pets is so much more technologically advanced than it was even 5 years ago.

杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan): 这具体指的是什么意思？

Original English

JEYAN: What do you mean by that?

克里夫医生 (Dr. Cliff): 这么说吧，现在每一家动物医院都必须配备 X 光机，而且现在你最好配备的是数字化 X 光机（digital X-ray machine），并且你必须能够非常非常迅速地完成阅片诊断。我经营的只是一家只有两位执业兽医的小型动物医院，但我配备了数字化 X 光机；我现在还购置了超声波设备，因为我们正在承接急症初筛与紧急诊疗护理，以尽量缓解大型急诊转诊医院的就诊负担；诊所内还配备了院内全套生化血液分析仪。现在所开展的外科手术种类在技术复杂度和精准度上也都有了极其显著的提升。

最后也是非常重要的一点，纵观整体宏观经济，所有东西都在涨价。因此，为了让我诊所的医疗团队保持积极稳定的工作状态，我必须支付给他们与其实际价值相匹配的薪酬——谢天谢地，我的员工们都非常优秀出色。所以我必须给他们提高工资待遇。我给他们涨薪的幅度可以说远远跑赢了通货膨胀率，因为这是他们应得的回报。但不可避免地，所有这些运营成本的增加，最终都直接推高了医疗账单的底线成本。

Original English

DR. CLIFF: Well, like, every single animal hospital has to have a X-ray machine, and now you better have a digital X-ray machine and you better be able to read it really, really quickly. I only have a two veterinarian hospital. I've got a digital X-ray machine. I now have an ultrasound, because we're doing urgent care to try and remove some of the burden on emergency hospitals, in-house blood machine. The types of surgeries that are being done are much more elevated in technique. And then last but not least, you know, the economy, everything's gone up in price. So, for me to be able to keep my team happy, I have to pay them, thank goodness, what they're worth. They're amazing. So, I have to pay them more, and they've-- I've been able to give them raises, you know, far beyond, let's say, inflation because they deserve it, and unfortunately all these things increase-- increase the bottom line.

降低医疗开销的实用建议与救助基金

杰扬·杰加纳森 (Jeyan Jeganathan): 那么对于广大的宠物主人，您有什么具体建议可以帮助他们降低兽医护理的开销吗？

Original English

JAYAN: What advice uh would you give to pet owners to reduce the costs of vet care?

克里夫医生 (Dr. Cliff): 你必须着眼于长期的规划管理：那就是坚持做好健康例行体检（wellness visits）与预防性医疗保健（preventative care），提供合格合理的饮食，保持规律的兽医复查。但如果你的宠物确实遇到了突发紧急医疗状况，你必须做好分诊与优先级评估。

这里最关键的一点是：请务必对你的兽医保持坦诚。从一开始看诊就直截了当地说明情况：“医生，我在医疗费用方面确实有一些经济顾虑。”并在就诊初期就明确要求提供费用预估报价单。请理解，作为兽医，我的本职工作是根据专业判断为你推荐我认为对你家宠物最好、最全面的诊疗方案，我不会预先根据你的经济支付能力去主观臆测；但如果你确实无法负担全部费用，那么我的职责就是和你共同商讨，看看我们是否可以在保证基本医疗安全的前提下合理精简流程、节约开支，这可能包括提供折扣或制定分期付款计划。

在刚开始养宠物时，尽量尽早购买宠物商业保险（pet insurance）。另外在安大略省，还有一个非常优秀的慈善组织叫做 Farley Foundation（法利基金会），它是由 OVMA（安大略省兽医协会） 设立的慈善基金，专门用于资助领取残疾人补贴及其他依赖政府救济金生活的低收入群体支付宠物的紧急救治费用。而且，作为兽医诊所，并不强制要求必须是 OVMA 的会员才能协助患者申请和使用这笔援助资金。

所以，遇到困难时大家一定要勇敢地大声说出来。我完全理解面对经济窘迫可能会让人觉得难为情或尴尬，但是我们这些兽医从业者非常希望能与宠物主人携手合作，共同探讨解决之道。我们的共同目标是让家庭不被迫骨肉分离，让毛孩子保持健康，并最大程度减轻 SPCA（防止虐待动物协会） 等收容机构的收容重压。我们都在同一条船上，唯有齐心协力才能共渡难关。

Original English

DR. CLIFF: You know, there's sort of the long-term thing you have to look at, which is sticking with the wellness visits and the preventative care, proper food, regular vet visits, but if you do have an emergency, you do have to triage it. And the big thing is be honest with your veterinarian. Be honest upfront and say, "Hey, I've got some concerns." Ask for a quote right at the beginning. Recognize that my job is to give you the best that I think is for your pet. I'm not going to make assumptions based on your financial ability, but if you can't afford it, it is then my job to work with you and see if we can reasonably cut corners, which may be discounts, payment plans. In the beginning, try and get pet insurance, and then in Ontario, there's a great organization called the Farley Foundation put on by the OVMA, which is basically a charity that helps cover emergency services for people on disability and other government support. So, and you do not have to be an OVMA member as a veterinarian clinic to access some of those funds. So, people just have to speak up. I understand it can be maybe embarrassing for them, but then also veterinarians, like myself, need to work with people and-- and see what we can do to keep those families together, keep those pets healthy, and minimize that-- that sort of strain on the SPCA, and we're all in this together. So, we just got to work together.
