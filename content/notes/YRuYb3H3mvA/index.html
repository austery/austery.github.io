俄罗斯战时经济为何比你想象的更强大？ Money & Macro 2025-10-17

领域: finance-wealth

分类: macro-economy

主讲人: Money & Macro

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2025-10-17

摘要:

本文分析了俄罗斯战时经济为何能够抵御西方对其崩溃的预测。文章阐释了战时经济的运作方式，俄罗斯如何通过激活闲置资源、实施“堡垒俄罗斯”战略，并在西方制裁下通过资本管制避免经济崩溃。通过对比历史案例，文章指出俄罗斯经济在资本外逃、关键进口受阻、过度债务和公众不满等方面的风险较低，并认为其战时经济仍处于早期阶段，远未达到崩溃点。

俄罗斯经济：出人意料的韧性

俄罗斯经济即将崩溃吗？

View/Hide Original English

Is Russia's economy about to collapse?

到目前为止，这些预测总是被证明是错误的。

So far, these predictions have always proved wrong.

But now, with oil prices down, a new Russia collapse narrative has emerged which essentially says that war has been a huge stimulus for the Russian economy.

然而，他们不可能永远这样做。

But they can't continue doing this forever.

现实是，他们很可能会耗尽资金。

The reality is, they're likely to run out of money.

View/Hide Original English

Therefore, as US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent recently said, we are in a race now between how long can Ukrainian military hold up versus how long can the Russian economy hold up?

所以，尽管早先的预测失误，许多优秀的西方经济学家现在再次认为俄罗斯的战时经济（War Economy: 国家经济体系为支持军事冲突而进行大规模调整和资源重新分配的状态）正走向崩溃。

View/Hide Original English

So despite getting it wrong earlier, lots of really good Western economists now again think Russia's war economy is heading for a collapse.

然而，你现在正在YouTube上听这位经济学家告诉你，如果我们审视历史上经济崩溃的四种主要方式，俄罗斯经济即将崩溃的迹象其实并不多。

And yet here you are listening to this economist on YouTube, no less, telling you that if we look at the four main ways that historic economies have collapsed, there really are not many signs that Russia's economy is about to collapse.

事实上，如果你将俄罗斯的战时经济与纳粹德国、苏联乃至越南战争时期的美国等历史案例进行比较，俄罗斯的战时经济看起来才刚刚开始。

View/Hide Original English

In fact, if you compare Russia's war economy to historical ones like Nazi Germany, the Soviet Union, and even Vietnam era USA, it actually looks like Russia's war economy is just getting started.

要理解其中原因，我们先快速回顾一下为什么俄罗斯的战时经济迄今为止让大多数分析师感到惊讶。

View/Hide Original English

To see why, let's do a quick recap of why Russia's war economy has surprised most analysts so far.

毕竟，自战争爆发以来，俄罗斯经济的表现实际上已经超过了英国和德国等主要欧洲经济体。

View/Hide Original English

After all, since the war, Russia's economy so far has actually outperformed major European economies like the UK and Germany.

那么，为什么大多数分析师都判断错了呢？

View/Hide Original English

So why did most analysts get it so wrong?

在我看来，最可能的原因是他们受过理解和平时期经济的训练，而不是战时经济。

View/Hide Original English

Well, in my view, the most likely reason is that they have been trained to understand peacetime economies, but not war economies.

要了解为什么俄罗斯迄今表现出色，是时候进行一次快速的战时经济学入门课程了，我们从一个简单的定义开始。

View/Hide Original English

To see why Russia has outperformed so far, it's time for a quick war. Economics 1 or 1 lesson, starting with a simple definition.

战时经济学101：基本概念

什么是战时经济？

View/Hide Original English

What is a war economy?

简而言之，战时经济就是为了支持军事努力而做一切必要的事情。

View/Hide Original English

In a nutshell, a war economy is about doing what is necessary to support the military effort.

从民用经济向战时经济的转变，因此是关于改变国家的重心，从“对人民最好”转向“对战争最好”。

View/Hide Original English

Transitioning from a civilian to a war economy is therefore about changing the nation's focus, from what's best for the people to what's best for the war.

当然，战争意味着破坏和死亡。

View/Hide Original English

Of course, war equals destruction and death.

从长远来看，这对经济不利。

View/Hide Original English

That is not good for the economy. In the long run.

然而，在短期内（这实际上可能持续好几年），战时经济的GDP大幅增长实际上是非常正常的。

View/Hide Original English

However, in the short run, which actually can be quite a few years, it's actually very normal for war economies to see their GDP increase by quite a lot.

例如，就在第二次世界大战之前，英国，特别是纳粹德国，都从快速的重新武装中获得了巨大的推动，而美国经济在认真投入战争后，才真正开始超越所有国家。

View/Hide Original English

For example, just before World War two, Britain and especially Nazi Germany got major boosts from rapid rearmament while the US economy really started outperforming them all when it got serious about the war as well.

更令人惊讶的是，在战时经济的第一阶段，普通民众甚至常常变得更富有。

View/Hide Original English

Even more strikingly, in the first phase of a war economy, regular civilians often even get richer.

这可以解释为什么在俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后，俄罗斯的平均生活满意度实际上达到了历史新高。

View/Hide Original English

This can explain why, after Russia invaded Ukraine, average life satisfaction in Russia actually rose to an all time high.

是的，没错，尽管俄罗斯遭受了前所未有的制裁，数十万俄罗斯人被杀或逃离国家，但其经济却在蓬勃发展，普通俄罗斯人的生活实际上变得更好了。

View/Hide Original English

Yes, that's right, as Russia was hit by unprecedented sanctions and hundreds of thousands of Russians were killed or fled the country. Its economy was booming and regular Russians were actually becoming better off.

平均而言，这种矛盾只有通过战时经济学入门课程的视角来看待，才能理解它最初是如何推动俄罗斯经济发展的。

View/Hide Original English

On average, this contradiction only makes sense if we look at it through the lens of war. Economics 101, and how it can explain why war boosted Russia's economy initially.

要完全理解这个故事，我认为通过动员本地资源用于战争，然后受到国际限制的视角来看待俄罗斯的战时经济至关重要。

View/Hide Original English

To fully understand this story, I think it's essential to look at Russia's war economy through the lens of mobilizing local resources for war, which is then constrained by international limitations.

激活闲置资源与货币理论

首先，重要的是要认识到，在战争之前，相当一部分俄罗斯工人没有工作，相当一部分俄罗斯工厂没有投入运营。

View/Hide Original English

To start, it's important to realize that before the war, a significant portion of Russian workers were not working and a significant share of Russian factories was not operational.

换句话说，俄罗斯经济在战时以低于其潜力的水平运行。

View/Hide Original English

In other words, Russia's economy was operating below its potential in times of war.

这是不可接受的。

View/Hide Original English

This is not acceptable.

因此，战时经济的第一阶段是激活未使用的资源。

View/Hide Original English

Therefore, the first phase of the war economy is about activating unused resources.

俄罗斯通过大幅增加国家支出、动用其财富并向经济体释放新资金来做到这一点。

View/Hide Original English

Russia did this by massively increasing state spending, by drawing down its wealth and essentially releasing new money into the economy.

向经济体注入新资金不会导致通货膨胀吗？

View/Hide Original English

New money into the economy. Won't that cause inflation?

不总是如此。

View/Hide Original English

Not always.

事实上，基本的货币理论告诉我们，如果新资金有助于创造新产品，那么这些新资金不一定会导致通货膨胀。

View/Hide Original English

In fact, basic monetary theory teaches us that if new money helps to create new products, this new money does not have to cause inflation.

将新资金用于雇佣失业工人，让他们在以前闲置的工厂工作，这意味着经济中现在有更多的资金，但俄罗斯在2022年的生产也增加了。

View/Hide Original English

New money spent on unemployed workers that are now working in factories that were previously idle means that there is now more money in the economy, but also increased production in Russia in 2022.

我们看到了这三件事：俄罗斯政府支出的巨额增长，失业率的大幅下降，以及工业产出的平均水平更高。

View/Hide Original English

We saw exactly these three things a massive increase in spending by the Russian government, a massive reduction in unemployment and a higher average level of industrial output.

这与1930年代英国、德国和美国等主要经济体为战争做准备时所看到的模式完全相同。

View/Hide Original English

This is exactly the same pattern that we saw when major economies like the UK, Germany and the US got ready for war in the 1930s.

但是等等，这不奇怪吗？

View/Hide Original English

But hold on a minute. Isn't this strange?

为什么国家只在战时才激活这些闲置资源？

View/Hide Original English

Why do countries only activate these unused resources in times of war?

和平时期不能这样做吗？

View/Hide Original English

Can't they do this in times of peace?

要回答这个问题，我们必须通过国际金融中最著名的理论之一——货币三难困境（Monetary Trilemma: 指一个国家不可能同时实现固定汇率、资本自由流动和独立的货币政策这三个目标，最多只能同时实现其中两个）的视角来看待俄罗斯的战前经济。

View/Hide Original English

To answer this question, we have to look at Russia's pre-war economy through the lens of one of the most famous theories in international finance the monetary trilemma.

货币三难困境与“堡垒俄罗斯”策略

在2000年代初期，俄罗斯仍在努力成为一个快速增长的现代资本主义经济体，因此力求拥有一个最优化的经济，并赋予其公民吸引外国投资和向国外投资的自由。

View/Hide Original English

In the early 2000, Russia was still trying to be a rapidly growing modern capitalist economy and therefore strive to have an optimally activated economy and give its citizens the liberty to attract foreign investments and to invest abroad.

货币三难困境告诉我们，因此俄罗斯不可能拥有稳定的货币；灵活的货币意味着俄罗斯卢布的价值主要由市场力量，即供求关系决定。

View/Hide Original English

The monetary trilemma teaches us that therefore Russia could not have a stable currency, a flexible currency meant that the value of the Russian ruble was primarily determined by market forces, by supply and demand.

为了说明这一点，经济学家通常使用国际收支框架（Balance of Payments Framework: 一种记录一个国家在特定时期内与世界其他国家之间所有经济交易的会计报表）。

View/Hide Original English

To see how this works. Economists often use the balance of payments framework.

它向我们展示了卢布需求可以通过两种方式增加。

View/Hide Original English

It shows us that demand for the ruble can increase in two ways.

第一种是通过增加出口。

View/Hide Original English

The first is by increasing exports.

俄罗斯出口商可能会要求以卢布支付，这意味着欧洲进口商必须先出售欧元购买卢布；或者他们可能要求以欧元支付。

View/Hide Original English

Russian exporters may ask to be paid in rubles, meaning that European importers have to sell euros to buy rubles first, or they may ask to be paid in euros.

在这种情况下，俄罗斯出口商很可能会很快将这些欧元兑换成卢布，因为他们的大部分成本当然是以卢布计价的。

View/Hide Original English

In that case, Russian exporters will likely sell these euros for rubles soon because most of their costs are, of course, in rubles.

结果是，如果俄罗斯出口增加，卢布相对于欧元的价值就会上升。

View/Hide Original English

As a consequence, the ruble becomes worth more compared to the euro. If there are more Russian exports.

同样，如果欧洲人更多地投资俄罗斯，他们将需要卢布来这样做，从而推高其价格。

View/Hide Original English

Similarly, if Europeans were to invest more in Russia, they will need rubles to do so, raising its price.

另一方面，如果俄罗斯人从欧洲进口或投资欧洲，他们很可能会出售一些卢布来这样做，从而压低卢布的价格。

View/Hide Original English

On the other hand, if Russians import from Europe or invest in Europe, they will likely sell some rubles to do this, driving the price of the ruble down.

这种情况在2013年发生了剧变，当时普京提拔了他的经济顾问埃尔维拉·纳比乌林娜（Elvira Nabiullina）担任中央银行行长。

View/Hide Original English

This situation changed drastically in 2013, when Putin promoted his economic adviser, Elvira Nabu Lena, as a central bank governor.

此前一年，美国刚刚通过制裁外国投资，使伊朗里亚尔的价值大幅下跌，这反过来又引发了对伊朗里亚尔的挤兑。

View/Hide Original English

When the year previously, the US had just decimated the value of Iran's riyal by sanctioning foreign investments, which in turn encouraged a run on the Iranian riyal.

在纳比乌林娜的领导下，俄罗斯采取了所谓的“堡垒俄罗斯”（Fortress Russia: 一种旨在通过积累外汇储备和限制资本流动来保护国家经济免受外部冲击的策略）战略，以不惜一切代价防止这种情景发生。

View/Hide Original English

Under neighborliness leadership, Russia adopted this so-called Fortress Russia strategy to prevent this scenario at all cost.

因此，她本质上力求使卢布更加稳定，将俄罗斯经济从货币三难困境的这里移到那里。

View/Hide Original English

Thus, she essentially strived to make the ruble more stable, moving Russia's economy from here to here. On the monetary trilemma,

卢布的价值现在不再完全由自由市场决定。

View/Hide Original English

the value of the Russian ruble was now no longer fully determined by the free market.

相反，俄罗斯中央银行（Bank of Russia）开始通过购买来自投资或出口的任何多余外币进行干预。

View/Hide Original English

Instead, neighborliness Bank of Russia started intervening by buying any excess foreign currencies from either investments or exports.

通过这样做，她积累了普京著名的“战争资金”，理论上这些资金可以在俄罗斯遭受西方制裁时用于捍卫卢布的价值。

View/Hide Original English

By doing so, she accumulated Putin's famous war chest, which could then, in theory, be used to defend the value of the ruble. If Russia was ever hit by Western sanctions,

然而，这种策略只有在俄罗斯持续地比支出带来更多的外汇时才能奏效。

View/Hide Original English

however, this strategy could only work if Russia consistently brought in more foreign currency than they spent.

然而，政府要大幅增加出口或流入的外国投资是极其困难的。

View/Hide Original English

It's extremely difficult for a government, however, to massively increase exports or incoming foreign investments.

因此，如果纳比乌林娜想要建立她的战争资金，并保持汇率相对稳定，同时让资金在俄罗斯自由流动，她只有一个真正的选择：减少俄罗斯人的进口量。

View/Hide Original English

So if Nabi Polina wanted to build her war chest and keep the exchange rates somewhat stable and keep money flowing freely in and out of Russia, she had only one real option. Reducing how much? Russians imports.

当然，在一个相对自由的经济体中，这说起来容易做起来难。

View/Hide Original English

This is easier said than done, of course, in a relatively free economy.

进口不受国家直接控制。

View/Hide Original English

Imports are not directly controlled by the state.

它们由俄罗斯公司和工人完成。

View/Hide Original English

They are done by Russian companies and workers.

然而，关键在于，如果他们有足够的钱，他们才能真正大量进口。

View/Hide Original English

However, crucially, they can only really import a lot if they have enough money to do so.

利用这一知识，纳比乌林娜和普京通过严格限制政府支出和保持相对较高的利率，使俄罗斯人比他们本应有的更贫穷一些，从而本质上减少了进口。

View/Hide Original English

Using this knowledge, Polina and Putin essentially reduced imports by making Russians a little bit poorer than they had to be by severely limiting government spending and keeping interest rates relatively high.

当然，事后看来，我们知道“堡垒俄罗斯”在现代世界中是一个巨大的失败。

View/Hide Original English

Of course, we know with the benefit of hindsight, that Fortress Russia was a massive failure in the modern world.

中央银行持有的外币不再是金库里的金条，而是其他国家发行的金融资产，比如美国国债或欧洲银行的存款。

View/Hide Original English

Foreign currencies held by a central bank are not bars of gold in a vault anymore, but rather financial assets issued by other countries like the US Treasury bonds or deposit in European banks.

换句话说，当俄罗斯说我们有3000亿美元储备时，其中大部分实际上是在俄罗斯境外、受西方管辖的账户中。

View/Hide Original English

In other words, when Russia said we have 300 billion in reserves, much of that was actually in accounts outside of Russia, under Western jurisdiction.

也许纳比乌林娜曾赌西方国家，特别是欧洲国家，没有胆量冻结他们的资产。

View/Hide Original English

Maybe Yelena had gambled that Western and especially European countries did not have the guts to freeze their assets.

她错了。

View/Hide Original English

She was wrong.

一旦俄罗斯的战争资金被冻结，资本开始逃离俄罗斯，卢布迅速崩溃。

View/Hide Original English

Once Russia's war chest was frozen, capital started fleeing Russia, quickly collapsing the ruble.

资本管制：俄罗斯经济的救命稻草

然而，正是在这一刻，纳比乌林娜展现了她作为经济学家的务实和技能。

View/Hide Original English

Yet it was at this moment that Nabih Olena showed her pragmatism and skill as an economist.

为了拯救卢布，她知道只有两个选择。

View/Hide Original English

To save the ruble, she knew there were only two options.

要么她可以通过让俄罗斯人更贫穷，让政府支出更少来减少进口。

View/Hide Original English

Either she could reduce imports by making Russians even poorer, by making the government spend even less.

然而，考虑到这场冲突需要越来越多的政府战争支出，她唯一的选择本质上是引入资本管制（Capital Controls: 政府对本国货币与外国货币之间兑换、转移和使用的限制），将俄罗斯经济从货币三难困境的这里移到那里。

View/Hide Original English

However, given that this conflict needed more and more government spending on the war, her only option left was essentially to introduce capital controls moving the Russian economy from here to here. On the trilemma.

现在，她直接控制资金流出俄罗斯的事实，使得俄罗斯政府能够在不导致卢布崩溃的情况下，充分激活俄罗斯经济。

View/Hide Original English

Now, the fact that she now directly controls money flowing out of Russia enabled the Russian government to fully activate the Russian economy without collapsing the ruble.

毕竟，尽管俄罗斯人现在拥有随心所欲进口的购买力，但他们根本不被允许这样做。

View/Hide Original English

After all, while Russians now had the purchasing power to import to their heart's content, they simply were not allowed to do so.

这一举动让许多经济分析师感到惊讶，因为他们不再习惯将资本管制视为一个可行的选择。

View/Hide Original English

This move surprised many economic analysis, because they were no longer used to thinking about capital controls as a viable option.

然而，这是理解为什么如果我们研究历史上的经济崩溃，俄罗斯经济不会崩溃的第一个关键步骤。

View/Hide Original English

It is, however, the first crucial step to see why Russia's economy will not collapse if we study historical economic collapses.

经济崩溃的四种方式与俄罗斯的低风险

我们基本上可以看到经济崩溃的四种方式。

View/Hide Original English

We can essentially see four ways economies collapse.

最常见的是突发的资本外逃（Capital Flight: 资金从一个国家迅速大量流出，通常是由于经济不稳定或政治不确定性）。

View/Hide Original English

The most frequent one is sudden capital flight.

这可能发生在金融泡沫（Financial Bubble: 资产价格在短时间内迅速上涨，远超其内在价值，最终破裂导致价格暴跌的现象）破裂时，就像1997年亚洲金融危机或2011年欧元区危机期间发生的那样。

View/Hide Original English

This may happen because a financial bubble pops, like what happens during the 1997 Asian financial crisis or 2011 eurozone crisis.

也可能因为一个国家长期进口过多而发生，比如2019年黎巴嫩危机或2022年斯里兰卡的货币崩溃。

View/Hide Original English

It may also happen because the country has been importing too much for too long. Like the Lebanese crisis of 2019 or Sri Lanka's 2022 currency collapse.

或者可能因为制裁而发生，例如2012年伊朗危机或2022年卢布崩溃。

View/Hide Original English
