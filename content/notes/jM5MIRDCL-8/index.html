重塑旅行的温度：如何在过度旅游时代找回人本与尊严 TED TED 2026-08-09

重塑旅行的温度：如何在过度旅游时代找回人本与尊严

关键字 overtourism smart-tourism crowd-management sustainable-travel

摘要

本演讲探讨了全球过度旅游（Overtourism）所引发的居民与游客之间的冲突危机。通过分析佛罗伦萨、哥本哈根等城市的实践，提出利用人工智能与数字化客流管理、深度本地真实体验以及“感恩市场”志愿机制，将冰冷的打卡式旅游转变为能够实现社区互利、促进跨文化连接的“喜悦之旅”。

完美假日下的阴影：被过度旅游吞噬的梦境

许多人在抵达心驰神往的梦想目的地时，往往会产生一种幻灭感：原本期待中充满异国情调和温情互动的街区，如今却变成了拥挤不堪的自拍排队现场。过度旅游（Overtourism：因游客数量超出目的地承载力而导致居民生活质量和游客体验双重下降的现象）正在摧毁全球最受欢迎的度假胜地。在罗马，狭窄石板路旁的咖啡馆人满为患，特莱维喷泉（Trevi Fountain）前被数以万计的游客围得水泄不通，连驻足买个开心果冰淇淋都成了奢望。在东京樱花季，人潮淹没了公园，本地居民由于拥挤甚至不再愿意带家人去樱花树下野餐。在巴塞罗那、葡萄牙和意大利，深受其害的居民纷纷发起反击，墙面上涂写着让游客“回家”的标语。生活成本攀升、交通拥堵、房租与物价飞涨正将旅行者与当地居民推向史无前例的冲突边缘。

全球旅游业雇佣了约3亿人，预计到2040年将达到15万亿美元的庞大体量，如此庞大的生计攸关的产业令我们无法对这场危机视而不见。旅行的本质是为了寻找喜悦（Joy：通过体验世界的多样性、不同的生活方式以及人与人之间的共享连接而产生的快乐）。为了将喜悦重新带回旅行中，我们必须改变现有的模式，利用技术与行为层面的调整，打破社交媒体盲目跟风与行业短期逐利所带来的双重困境。

英文原文

Last July I was in Rome. I walked from my hotel to the Trevi Fountain on the cobbled streets lined with beautiful cafes, bustling with residents and travelers, drinking their espressos and cheering and greeting each other. Small souvenir shops selling beautiful replicas of the Colosseum, the Sistine Chapel ceiling paintings. What an atmosphere. I soaked it all in, reached the Trevi Fountain, grabbed my favorite pistachio gelato and looked at the marvelous views. A perfect Roman holiday. Suddenly my alarm rang and I woke up from my dream. I was in Rome. I did walk from my hotel to the Trevi Fountain. But I felt rushed. The cafes were crowded. People had to even queue outside. The shopkeepers had no time to entertain visitors. I reached the Trevi Fountain and forgot to grab my favorite pistachio gelato or find a bench to sit. I couldn't get to the steps. Thousands and thousands of people all around. Have you felt the same way? When you reach your dream destination, you feel as if it has been overrun. You feel as if it has become a selfie queue. I had the same feeling. And this is not an isolated incident. I had the same exact experience in Tokyo when I went there during the cherry blossom season. Tourists crowd every park in the city to get a glimpse of the famous sakura. I asked a resident: “How do you feel seeing so many tourists all around?” He told me that he doesn't bring his family anymore for a picnic under the sakura. That response broke my heart. We are loving our favorite destinations to death. We are overrunning them. And residents are pushing back because it's impacting their livability. Their cost of living is increasing, congestion on the road, rental prices, food prices. They are pushing back. And they're pushing back like in Barcelona, with the graffiti asking tourists to go home. In Barcelona, in Italy, in Portugal, residents are pushing back. I have been researching the travel industry for the last 20 years and my two research subjects, the residents and the travelers, are on a collision course like never before. We have a travel crisis. The travel industry employs about 300 million people globally, and it’s going to be a $15 trillion industry by 2040. It’s too large, with too many livelihoods at stake for us to ignore this crisis. But let me pause and ask you a question. Why do we travel? We travel for joy. Joy by experiencing the diversity this world has to offer. Joy by experiencing different lives. Joy through a shared connection. My daughter, son and wife are here, and I want them to experience the same joy I experienced in my Roman dream. I want all of you to experience the same joy. We need to bring joy back in travel. But it's a complex problem. We have millions of tourists getting inspired by social media and planning their travel destinations, and planning their travel itineraries. We have the travel industry, which is looking for short-term profits, and we have cities who don't know how to manage the tourist inflow. There is a path forward, though. With the use of technology like AI and simple behavior changes, we could bring joy back in travel.

数字化分流：用技术为目的地“降温”

为了将喜悦重新带回旅行，旅游业的航空、酒店和地接旅行社需要将目标调整为“同时为居民和游客创造价值”，而数字化技术将是实现这一跨越的关键武器。首先在客流控制与分流管理方面，许多城市和景区正着手通过科技引导人们避开高峰期，探索那些被忽略的安静季节和隐藏路线。

以佛罗伦萨市开发的官方应用 FeelFlorence 为例，该平台利用传感器和监控摄像头收集的数据，实时分析各个景点的拥挤程度。系统在监测到某个区域流量过载时，会向游客发送避堵警报，并智能推荐个性化的替代游览路线。这种基于数据的智能动态分流，不仅极大地改善了游客的观光体验，也减轻了本地居民在日常生活中的出行压力。不仅是历史文化名城，像阿姆斯特丹，甚至麦加（Mecca）和瓦拉纳西（Varanasi）等宗教圣地，也正越来越多地部署人群管理技术，用智能排程与物联网监控手段来重塑古老朝圣地的秩序与安全。

英文原文

Now imagine if we were to make the entire travel industry's objective, the airlines, the hotel, the travel tour operators, to bring joy to both residents as well as the travelers. And they have everything at their disposal, from technology to people to skilling. They will rise to the occasion. They will do three things: one, help manage crowd, help discover authentic experiences and help drive a really, really respectful connection between the resident and the traveler. On your last trip, did you feel rushed? Did you feel like you were going through a checklist, moving from one place to another place? Did it feel like a chore or did you feel joy? We all are super busy and we all land to the same destinations during the same peak season times without realizing quieter seasons, quieter times, or hidden paths. What if technology could help us figure that out? FeelFlorence is an app by the city of Florence, which helps do exactly this for the travelers. It uses the data from sensors, from cameras, to understand crowded locations, and recommends personalized travel itineraries for tourists. It sends user alerts to avoid crowded locations. When this happens, it balances the load, and it is beneficial for both the tourists, as well as the residents. Cities are going down that path. Amsterdam. And even religious cities like Mecca or Varanasi are increasingly using technology to manage crowd.

重获本真体验：用人文连接替代景观打卡

在打卡式旅游的喧嚣之外，旅行中最令人难忘的往往不是壮丽的名胜古迹本身，而是人与人之间真挚的情感共鸣。那些在旅途中建立起的微小而具体的联系——比如在越南乘渔船出海时，渔民娓娓道来的本土生活故事；亦或是在伊斯坦布尔，咖啡馆老板耐心地讲授土耳其茶的历史、冲泡与品饮之道——能够赋予人们持久的内心愉悦。

这种对本真体验（Authentic Experience：能够融入当地生活方式并与社区居民产生深度情感关联的交互式活动）的渴望，为酒店与航空公司提供了服务创新的契机。当面对航班延误或日常运转中断时，航司与其只是按部就班地将旅客送进正对停机坪的普通酒店，不如组织一场土耳其茶艺工坊；同样，酒店也可以通过数字化手段，提供支持多国语言的多功能数字礼宾系统，智能推荐隐秘的本地宝藏景点并伴随游客同行。这种富有人情味的设计，将把因意外导致的旅行摩擦转变成充满惊喜的文化纽带，从而有效提升消费者的品牌忠诚度。

英文原文

What was your last unforgettable trip? Was it the famous landmark, the Instagrammable picture, or was it a shared smile with someone? Or was it a story that somebody told you, of course, in the backdrop of beautiful nature or a beautiful surrounding. I asked the same question to my friend. She said, "If I ignore my bachelorette party outside, my last unforgettable trip was in Vietnam. A fisherman took me on a boat ride, told me stories about Vietnam, told me stories about the life he has lived there. And I cannot forget that." What a beautiful connection. For me, it was my experience in Istanbul, in Turkey, where a cheerful cafe owner took interest in me and told me the history of Turkish tea, how to make it and how to drink it. Whenever I think about Turkey or Istanbul, my heart fills with joy. This is what we crave. This is what we seek. I'm sure some of you have been stranded at airports because of airline disruptions. The airline has two options: one to check you into a hotel overlooking the terminal, or maybe organize a Turkish tea-making workshop. Maybe some of us will like it, and if we like it, we will become a loyal customer of the airline. Same is the case with hotels. If hotels can use technology to give custom concierges, which speaks in your language, curates local suggestions, helps you discover hidden gems, and walks along with you, you will become a loyal customer of the hotel. Imagine the possibilities of uncovering authentic experiences that give us true joy.

构建感恩市场：让游客成为受欢迎的“喜悦之客”

很多目的地居民之所以对旅游业产生抵触心理，是因为他们只能承受负面影响，却无法直接从旅游经济中获益。对于希望贡献力量和时间的游客而言，以往也缺乏安全、便捷的参与途径。这种隔阂让居民的感觉就像小时候家里来了不带礼物的意外访客，只希望他们尽快离去。

为了打破这种局面，业内正在通过“感恩市场（Gratitude Marketplace：一种将游客的志愿服务时间或环保行为，与本地社区福利及景区权益进行价值兑换的互惠系统）”重建两者的连接。平台如 Golden Volunteer 通过严格的背景审查，为有志于在旅途中做志愿服务的游客与本地机构提供精准对接。哥本哈根推出的 CopenPay 机制则更进一步，游客通过骑行、参与社区园艺等绿色环保行为积累积分，就能直接兑换当地景点的门票或消费优惠。无论是去泰国大象救援中心提供支持，在印度参与海龟保护，还是像历史爱好者那样深度参与一座800年古庙的修复，将志愿服务融入行程，不仅能够使旅行充满成就感，更能让当地社区真正接纳并感谢旅行者的到来。在不断增长的旅游行业中，选择做一个消费资源的“游客”，还是做一个传递温度的“喜悦之客”，将决定我们所珍爱的那些目的地的未来。

英文原文

Many residents in the destinations we love don’t see the direct benefits of a traveler. They only see the negative side. I know we are contributing to the local economy through our money. But that may not be enough. And even if we want to contribute through our time, we don't know how to do it. Residents feel like how I used to feel as a child when unexpected guests arrived in my house, especially if they didn't carry any special gifts for me. I barely uttered a smile and I wished that they would leave as soon as possible. I'm sure many of you, if you could find a way to contribute through your time, you would. Again things are changing on this front. Golden Volunteer is an example of a platform which connects volunteers with organizations and projects looking for volunteers. It, in fact, goes to the extent of doing a volunteer background check. If we can unleash a “gratitude marketplace” at scale, we will create a deep connection between the traveler and the resident. Copenhagen is going on that path with CopenPay. You get points. If you do gardening, you get points. If you do cycling, you can use those points for redeeming at attractions. If you had the opportunity to contribute with your time, what would you do? Some of you are animal lovers. You may want to be part of the elephant rescue centers in Thailand, or want to be part of sea turtle rescue centers in India. If you're an environment or nature lover, you may want to be part of coral reef preservation in the Great Barrier or in Maldives. And if you are a history lover like me, you may be wanting to be a part of restoration of an 800-year-old temple like this. In fact, I will seek such an opportunity and bake it in my itinerary because I will love to be part of something like this. The possibilities are infinite. The travel industry is going to grow. It's one industry that is probably encouraging tolerance in this highly polarized world. It's for us to shape positively the destinations we love and the people who call it their home. I've talked about the tools at hand, and I've given you a few examples. I want to leave with one question. On your next trip, will you be a tourist or a joyful guest? It's time we bring joy back in travel. Thank you. (Applause)
