Original English

Will Jarvis: Outside a federal courthouse yesterday, lawyers for Sam Altman, the chief executive of OpenAI, celebrated a ruling that threw out a $150 billion lawsuit from Elon Musk. For now, Musk's failure to deal a major blow to Open AI solidifies the company's place near the pinnacle of the AI industry, and it clears the way for a potential initial public offering as soon as this year that could be one of the biggest in history.
