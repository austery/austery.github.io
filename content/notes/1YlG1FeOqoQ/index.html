建立“凌晨两点”的朋友圈：应对友谊衰退的革命

关键字 friendship loneliness social-connection mental-health

摘要

作者通过回顾在领养研讨会中被问及“凌晨两点能给谁打电话”的经历，探讨现代人面临的孤独危机。文章指出由于流动性、忙碌和文化忽视，成年人难以建立深厚友谊。作者提出将友谊视为一种“神圣的召唤”，并倡议通过建立互惠关系、优先考虑人际连接以及在彼此中共同成长来建立“友谊革命”。

凌晨两点,你能打给谁?

凌晨两点,一切都出了问题的时候,你能打电话给谁?不是下午两点,那时一切都好;是凌晨两点,那时你最脆弱,而对方最被打扰。

英文原文 Who could you call at 2am when everything has gone wrong? Not 2pm, when everything's fine, 2am, when you're at your most vulnerable and they're at their most inconvenienced.

2008年,我和妻子坐在一间沉闷的研讨室里。我们已经为组建家庭努力了八年,而这最终会成为一段十年的旅程。那天,我们和其他人聚在一起,了解领养的事。

英文原文 In 2008, my wife and I found ourselves sitting in a drab seminar room. We were eight years into what would become a 10-year journey trying to start a family. And on this day, we gathered with others to find out about adoption.

我们每个人眼中都满怀希望,以为领养的孩子会满足我们灵魂深处想要成为父母的渴望——当然,到那时为止,这个愿望还没有实现。这时,主持人神情严肃起来。她说:"没错,做领养父母会很棒,会有很多喜悦,但也可能很艰难。你们身边需要一个支持圈。"

英文原文 And seeing the hope in all of our eyes that an adopted child would fulfill the deepest longings of our soul to be parents, which, of course, until this point in time had not happened, our facilitator got serious. She said, "Yes, being an adoptive parent is going to be great. There's going to be great joy involved, but also it could be difficult. You're going to need a support group around you."

然后她问了一个问题,这个问题钻进我的心里,再也没有离开。她说:"一切都出了问题的时候,凌晨两点你能打给谁?"

英文原文 And then she asked a question that burrowed under my skin and never left. She said, "Who could you call at 2am when everything has gone wrong?"

我发现自己坐在那里,笔悬在练习册上方,因为我想不出该写下谁的名字。你呢?谁是你的凌晨两点朋友?

英文原文 And, you know, I found myself sitting there with my pen hovering over the workbook because I couldn't think of whose name to write down. What about you? Who's your 2am friend?

孤独与友谊

你看过那些新闻标题。称它为孤独危机也好,友谊衰退也好——总之出了很严重的问题。看看你的职场、你的社区、你的火车车厢,你看到的每四个人里,就有一个经常感到孤独。如果那就是你——在座的也会有一些人——不必担心,首先,你并不孤单。问题出在我们一直停留在那种状态里。

英文原文 You've seen the headlines. Call it a loneliness crisis or a friendship recession -- something is drastically wrong. Look around your workplace, your neighborhood, your train carriage. Every fourth person you see is regularly lonely. Now if that is you -- and there will be some of us here in the room -- do not worry, you're not alone for a start. The problem comes when we stay there.

数十年的研究如今证实了我们凭直觉就知道的事:孤独不只是伤害我们的情绪。它会增加焦虑和抑郁,耗尽我们的意义感和幸福感,削弱我们的心脏,甚至缩短我们的寿命。

英文原文 Decades of research now confirm what we intuitively know to be true -- that loneliness doesn’t just hurt us emotionally. It increases anxiety and depression. It drains us of meaning and happiness. It weakens our heart. It even shortens our lifespan.

但好消息是,友谊能逆转这一切。有亲密朋友的人更快乐、更健康,也更长寿。友谊是一种超能力,也是我们这个时代最被忽视的关系。数十年的研究告诉我们这一点。

英文原文 But here's the good news. Friendship reverses all of this. People with close friends are happier, healthier -- and they live longer. Friendship is a superpower. And the most overlooked relationship of our time. Decades of research tell us what we now know to be true.

成年人友谊的三大障碍

但问题是,那天我坐在研讨室里,并没有真正把自己看作一个需要朋友的人。我做了多年主持人,和陌生人交谈对我毫无困难。总的来说,我的社交自信相当高。我不是你想象中那种需要朋友的人。但那天坐在研讨室里,我面对着成年人在友谊中如今面临的三大障碍。

英文原文 But here's the thing. When I was sitting in that seminar room that day, I didn't really take on the picture of somebody needing friends. I've spent years as a presenter. I don’t have any trouble talking to strangers. In general, I’ve got fairly high social confidence. I wasn't your typical picture of somebody who needs friends. But as I sat in the seminar room that day, I was facing three of the biggest barriers that adults face in their friendships today.

第一个障碍:流动性。请举手,你们当中有多少人现在居住的城镇或城市,和出生地不同?如果那之后搬过两次家,请继续举着手。三次、四次、五次。我们还可以一直数下去。那就是我。我最近搬了城市,在别处有朋友,但我没有和他们保持联系。

英文原文 First barrier, mobility. Hands up, how many of you are living in a town or city different from where you were born? Keep your hands up if you've moved two times since then. Three times, four times, five times. We could keep on going on. That was me. I'd recently moved cities, I had friends in other places, but I wasn't keeping in touch with them.

第二个障碍:忙碌。老实告诉我,你们当中有多少人在人生中某些时候忙得顾不上和朋友保持联系?谢谢你们的坦诚。那也是我。但你们知道吗,要让一段关系从熟人变成普通朋友,需要50小时的有意义的接触,更别说成为好朋友或凌晨两点的朋友了。五十小时。简单地说,我没有投入这些时间。

英文原文 Second barrier, busyness. Be honest with me. How many of you have had times in your life where you've been too busy to keep in touch with your friends? Thank you for your honesty. That, too, was me. But did you know that it takes 50 hours of meaningful contact to move a relationship from an acquaintance to a casual friend, let alone a good friend or a 2am friend? Fifty hours. Simply put, I wasn't putting the hours in.

第三个是忽视。因为几十年来,我们的文化把友谊推到了阴影里,却把聚光灯打在它更漂亮的兄弟姐妹——爱情——身上。(笑声)如果有人不信,只要打开你的Spotify歌单。今天下午回到家,我挑战你,在你的歌单里找一首、仅仅一首关于友谊的歌。我说的可不是《玩具总动员》里那首可爱的歌。(笑声)如果你找到了那一首关于友谊的歌,你会发现它不过是情歌汪洋中的一滴水。

英文原文 And thirdly, there's neglect. Because for decades now, our culture has pushed friendship off into the shadows while we've shone the spotlight on its prettiest sibling: romance. (Laughter) If there's anybody who doesn't believe me, all you have to do is take out your Spotify playlists. When you get home this afternoon, I ask you, I challenge you to find one song in your playlist, one song about friendship. And I'm not just talking about that cutesy song from "Toy Story." (Laughter) If you find that one song about friendship, you will find that it is a mere drop in an ocean of love songs.

人人都知道情人节是哪一天。你们当中有多少人知道国际友谊日是哪一天?我们的学校有性教育,却很少有友谊教育。我们有书、应用、课程,还有整个机构来帮助我们约会、结婚和养育孩子。我感激所有这些资源,但直到最近,几乎没有什么东西能帮助成年人建立和维持深厚、高质量、美好、充满生命力的友谊。

英文原文 Everybody knows the date of Valentine's Day. How many of you know the date of International Friendship Day? Our schools have sex ed, but little friendship ed. We have books and apps and courses and whole organizations to help us date and marry and raise kids. And I'm grateful for all of those resources, but up until recently, we have had next to nothing to help adults form and maintain deep, good-quality, wonderful, life-giving friendships.

什么是朋友

正因为这种忽视,我们甚至不再知道朋友是什么。我的同事是我的朋友吗?我通过本地慈善机构帮助的人是朋友吗?那个比你配偶更了解你点单习惯的咖啡师是吗?(笑声)那个知道你所有家庭纠纷、甚至还知道几件你自己都没意识到的事的理发师是吗?

英文原文 And because of this neglect, we don't even know what a friend is anymore. Are my workmates my friends? Is the person I help through my local charity a friend? Is it your barista who knows your order better than your spouse does? (Laughter) Is it your hairdresser who knows all of your family dramas and even a few you didn't even realize you had?

喜剧演员们试图填补这个空白,给我们提供某种友谊的定义。Jim Hayes说:"朋友是帮你搬家的人,最好的朋友是帮你搬尸体的人。"(笑声)或者,借用Groucho Marx的话:"朋友是去监狱探望你的人,好朋友是把你保释出来的人,最好的朋友是在牢房里转向你说'刚才真好玩!'的人。"(笑声)

英文原文 Comedians have tried to step into the breach and kind of provide us with a definition of friendship. Jim Hayes said, “A friend is someone who helps you move house. A best friend is someone who helps you move a body." (Laughter) Or, to paraphrase Groucho Marx, “A friend is someone who visits you in jail, a good friend is someone who bails you out, and a best friend is someone who turns to you in the cell and says, ‘That was fun!’” (Laughter)

在我所领导的组织Friendship Lab,我们把朋友定义为:你可以与之交谈、可以依靠、可以共同成长、并且能一起享受的人。这四个要素都很重要。

英文原文 At Friendship Lab, the organization that I lead, we define a friend as someone you can talk to, depend on, grow with and enjoy. And all four elements are important.

可以交谈:谈小事也谈大事,从足球比分和假期计划,到我们最深的希望和恐惧。可以依靠:他们是我们在一切出错时可以打电话的凌晨两点朋友。共同成长:朋友帮助我们迎接人生的挑战,成为我们应当成为的人。还有享受:没有享受,就几乎没有友谊,无论只是因为我们一起欢笑,或者你壁球打得很棒,又或者像我一样,喜欢带字幕的阴郁欧洲电影。(笑声)你知道的,那种每个人最后都死了,但死得那么有意义的电影。(笑声)

英文原文 Talk to about the little things and the large. From football scores and holiday plans to our deepest hopes and fears. Depend on. They're the 2am friends we can call when everything has gone wrong. Grow with. Friends help us rise to meet life’s challenges, become the people we're supposed to be. And enjoy. There is little friendship without enjoyment, whether that's just because we laugh together or you play a great game of squash, or perhaps because you, like me, enjoy moody European films with subtitles. (Laughter) You know, the kind where everybody dies in the end, but they die so meaningfully. (Laughter)

追求这样的友谊,不只是平息我们的孤独。据牛津大学这方面的常驻专家Robin Dunbar说,友谊不只是一个因素。不,不,不。友谊是影响我们健康、福祉和幸福的那个因素。

英文原文 Pursuing this kind of friendship does more than just quell our loneliness. According to Oxford University's resident expert in this, Robin Dunbar, friendship is not just a factor. No, no, no. Friendship is the factor influencing our health, well-being and happiness.

友谊革命的三项承诺

我们需要改写这个故事。我们需要建立一个有凌晨两点朋友的世界。与其面对友谊衰退,我们需要一场友谊革命。想一起发起一场吗?

英文原文 We need to change the story on this one. We need to build a world of 2am friends. Instead of a friendship recession, we need a friendship revolution. Want to start one together?

革命需要宣言,宣言需要承诺。这里有三条。

英文原文 A revolution requires a manifesto, and a manifesto requires commitments. Here are three.

当我们把友谊视为一种使命时,革命就开始了。如果你我把做朋友看得和做父母、做诗人或做专业人士一样重要,会怎样?因为它本来就同样重要。做朋友是神圣的召唤,拥有朋友是神圣的托付。正如我们读书来成为更好的父母,练习来成为更好的诗人,上课来提升我们的职业,把友谊视为使命就意味着要掌握技能。朋友们,是时候重新发现,甚至也许是第一次发现,那些被遗忘的、从未言明的友谊规则了。

英文原文 The revolution will begin when we treat friendship as a vocation. What if you and I were to treat being a friend as just as important as being a parent, a poet, or a professional? Because it is. Being a friend is a sacred calling. Having a friend is a sacred trust. And just as we read books to become better parents and we practice to become better poets, and we take courses to progress our professions, treating friendship as a vocation means getting skilled. People, it is time that we rediscovered, maybe even discovered for the very first time, the forgotten, the unspoken rules of friendship.

当我们把朋友放在屏幕之前时,革命就开始了。一直把友谊推进阴影里的爱情之一,就是我们与设备之间的爱情。一小时又一小时,我们深情地凝视它的眼睛,(笑声)抚摸、抚摸、再抚摸。(笑声)

英文原文 The revolution will begin when we put friends before screens. One of the romances that continually puts friendship into the shadows is the romance we have with our devices. Hour upon hour we gaze lovingly into its eyes, (Laughter) caressing, caressing, caressing. (Laughter)

是的,我们都很忙,但当普通人每天独自看五个小时流媒体视频时——这是英国国家统计局(ONS)的数据,每天五个小时——问题就不在于时间,而在于优先级。给朋友发一条问候短信只需要10秒钟,一通叙旧电话大约10分钟,一次见面只占你一周中的一个小时。做到这些,你仍然有时间去看别人翻修你永远买不起的房子。(笑声)或者看人被投票淘汰出岛。(笑声)

英文原文 Yes, we're all busy, but when the average person spends five hours a day watching streaming video alone, that's the ONS figures, five hours a day, the problem is not so much time. It's priority. A check-in text to your friend will take you 10 seconds. A catch-up call will take you about 10 minutes. A meet-up will take just one hour out of your week. Do that, and you’ll still have time to watch people renovate homes you'll never afford. (Laughter) Or get voted off an island. (Laughter)

我们需要把目光转回最重要、最有价值、最被需要的地方。把人放在像素之前,把朋友放在屏幕之前。

英文原文 We need to return our gaze to where it's most important, to where it's most valued, where it's most needed. Putting people before pixels and friends before screens.

当我们拥抱互惠的风险时,革命就开始了。如果你总是付出的一方,那不是友谊,那是慈善。如果你总是接受的一方,那也不是友谊,那是依赖。友谊是一支给予与索取的舞蹈:看见与被看见,倾听与被倾听,了解与被了解。说来有趣,恰恰是这种互惠让它有了风险。

英文原文 And the revolution will begin when we embrace the risk of mutuality. If you are always the giver, that is not friendship. That is charity. And if you are always the receiver, that is not friendship. That's dependence. Friendship is a dance of giving and taking, seeing and being seen, hearing and being heard, knowing and being known. And funnily enough, it's this mutuality that actually makes it risky.

一直做付出的一方可能很容易,它让你不必暴露自己的需求。一直做接受的一方当然也很容易,每个人都围着你、关注你、听你说话、照你想做的去做。这让我们免于友谊所要求的牺牲:他们生病时做饭,他们需要离开、搬家时牺牲周末,他们凌晨两点打来电话时牺牲睡眠。但没有这种相互的脆弱与牺牲,就没有友谊。当我们真正尝到它的滋味时,它能让我们热泪盈眶。

英文原文 It can be easy always being the giver. It saves you from being vulnerable about your own needs. And it's sure easy always being the receiver. When everybody kind of surrounds you and focuses on you and listens to you and does what you want to do. It saves us from the sacrifices that friendship requires. Cooking meals when they're sick, giving up weekends when they need to leave, move house, giving up sleep when they call at 2am. But there is no friendship without this mutual vulnerability and sacrifice. And when we taste it for real, it can bring a tear to our eyes.

Helmut的故事

2018年,我父亲被诊断出癌症。更复杂的是,他还是我母亲的照护者。他们住在澳大利亚,所以在我们设法弄清楚该怎么办的那几天里,压力很大。就在那时,父亲教会里的一位男士走进了我们的生活。他叫Helmut。他不认识我们,我们也不认识他。但他听说了我们的情况,想要帮忙。

英文原文 In 2018, my father was diagnosed with cancer. To complicate matters, he was also my mother's carer. And they lived in Australia, so there were a few stressful days as we tried to work out what to do. That’s when a man from Dad’s church stepped into our lives. His name was Helmut. He didn't know us, we didn't know him. But he'd heard about our situation and he wanted to help.

两天后,他拿着一个记事本出现在前门。他走进来,坐下,开始逐条过他列好的清单。他说:"好,化疗开始后,你们需要有人送饭。我来安排一个做饭轮值表。草坪,谁来打理你们的草坪?没人打理?好,我也来安排。垃圾桶,你们哪天倒垃圾?"他一条条往下列,把一切都安排妥当。

英文原文 Two days later, he appears on the front doorstep with a notepad. He comes in, he sits down. He starts making his way through a list that he's made up. He says, "OK, when the chemotherapy starts, you're going to need somebody to deliver meals to you. I'll arrange a cooking roster. Lawns. Who does your lawns? Nobody does your lawns? OK, I'll organize that as well. Bins. What day do your bins go out?" Down the list he went, sorting everything.

两年里,Helmut每周二和周四开车送我父亲去化疗。两年里,冰箱里总是装满饭菜,草坪被修剪,垃圾桶按时拿出去。感觉就像我们被天使拜访了一样。但尽管这很美好,Helmut和我父母在那一刻所拥有的还不是友谊。那是美好的、牺牲的、改变生命的慈善。我们都要像Helmut一样。

英文原文 For two years, Helmut drove my dad to chemotherapy every Tuesday and Thursday. For two years, the fridge was full of meals, the lawns were mown, the bins went out on time. It felt like we'd been visited by an angel. But as beautiful as that was, what Helmut and my parents had in that moment wasn't yet friendship. Instead, it was beautiful, sacrificial, life-changing charity. Let's all be like Helmut.

但随着时间推移,有些东西改变了。随着一个个周二和周四累积起来,Helmut发现在我父母身上,他有了可以交谈小事与大事的人,可以依靠他们来解决自己的问题,可以在彼此鼓励中共同成长,也可以一起享受。事实上,周二和周四成了他一周中最喜欢的日子。

英文原文 But as time went on, something shifted. As those Tuesdays and Thursdays racked up, Helmut found that in my parents, he had somebody that he could talk to about the little things and the large and depend on to work through his own problems and to grow with as they encouraged each other, and to enjoy. In fact, Tuesdays and Thursdays became his favorite days of the week.

我父亲与癌症的抗争在2021年9月结束。Helmut在场,抬着灵柩。并非所有的慈善关系都能、或者说都应该变成友谊,但这一段做到了。平等、互惠、充实。友谊在停滞的时期给我们所需的改变,在危机的时期给我们所需的延续。

英文原文 My father's battle with cancer ended in September 2021. Helmut was there, carrying the coffin. Not all relationships of charity can or indeed should become friendship, but this one did. Equal, mutual and fulfilling. Friendship gives us the change we need in times of stagnation and the continuity we need in times of crisis.

我梦想有这样一天:关于友谊的歌曲充满我们的歌单,国际友谊日和情人节一样受到庆祝。(笑声)当我们把友谊视为使命,当我们把朋友放在屏幕之前,当我们拥抱互惠的风险时,那一天就会到来。这就是我们改写故事的方式,这就是我们建立一个有凌晨两点朋友的世界的方式。朋友们,这就是革命开始的地方。出发吧。谢谢。(欢呼和掌声)

英文原文 I dream of the day when songs about friendship populate our playlists, and when International Friendship Day is celebrated as much as Valentine's Day. (Laughter) That day will come when we treat friendship as a vocation, when we put friends before screens and when we embrace the risk of mutuality. This is how we'll change the story. This is how we'll build a world of 2am friends. This, my friends, is where the revolution begins. Let's go. Thank you. (Cheers and applause)
