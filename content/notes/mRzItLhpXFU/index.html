我们当时所勾勒的愿景，并不是在追逐现存的既有市场机会，而是预见未来的世界必将充斥着形形色色的机器人——它们会在农田中劳作，在工厂装配线上作业，在物流仓储中穿梭，在千家万户中提供家政服务与养老护理，当然还有穿梭于道路上的自动驾驶汽车。

What an amazing impact. So XPeng, they actually maybe help one brand, you know, to become very very appealing, very very attractive to consumers, but I want to enable and help all the brands, all the cars.

中国车市内卷与极速迭代

尼古拉·坦根: 如今中国汽车市场的竞争极其惨烈残酷。到底是什么在驱动着当下的中国汽车市场？是价格战，还是智能化，抑或是其他因素？

英文原文

Nicolai Tangen: Now the Chinese car market is kind of brutally competitive. What is driving the Chinese car market just now? Is it price, intelligence, just what is it?

余凯: 目前的中国汽车市场确实令人叹为观止，全球任何地方都找不到类似的市场生态。这里活跃着大约 60 到 70 个不同的汽车品牌。仅在今年上半年，全行业就推出了超过 580 款新车型！这无疑是一个极其艰难苛刻的市场环境。

驱动这个市场的核心动力，首先是白热化的极致竞争；而硬币的另一面，则是广大消费者日益旺盛的技术期待与需求。中国消费者对于各种智能化功能充满渴望——自动泊车、全场景自动驾驶、体验炫酷的智能座舱与车载娱乐系统。所有这些要素汇聚在一起，不仅塑造了极度内卷的竞争态势，更催生了惊人的全行业创新活力。

英文原文

Kai Yu: Well the current Chinese car market is really crazy. I think you don't see any market like here. There are you know I don't know how many, probably 60 or to 70 different car brands, and for the first half of this year more than 580 new car models launched. And it's extremely tough market, but I think the market is really driven by competition. This is but on the other hand is also driven by consumer demand. So consumers are really expecting, you know, more and more smart features, you know, automatic parking, autonomous driving and the more fancy infotainment systems. And I think all of those factors driving the market not just the competitive, but also very innovative, very innovative.

尼古拉·坦根: 当中国本土汽车厂商在如此残酷的环境下脱颖而出后，再走向海外市场（比如进军欧洲），在激烈的竞争对比中是不是就像探囊取物一般轻松了？

英文原文

Nicolai Tangen: What what does it do to Chinese manufacturers when you have such strong competition at home that when you get out to let's say Europe is it just like easy peasy to compete relative?

余凯: 对于地平线而言，首先我们从不与任何整车厂商竞争，我们的角色是赋能者与协作者。我们与所有汽车制造商以及产业链上的 Tier 1 供应商（如博世、电装以及海内外主流供应商）建立深厚的战略合作伙伴关系。

我们的经营哲学是广交朋友，与客户及合作伙伴构建长期的信任纽带。这种价值观深植于地平线的企业基因中。因此，我们很少从纯粹零和博弈的竞争视角思考问题，而是始终聚焦于我们能创造怎样的真实价值。

幸运的是，如果审视我们的商业赛道，相较于整车厂白热化的红海竞争，车载计算芯片领域的竞争格局其实并没有那么拥挤。放眼中国市场，智能汽车计算芯片前三大顶级玩家，如今基本已经高度集中收敛为三家：英伟达（Nvidia）、华为（Huawei） 与 地平线（Horizon Robotics）。这个赛道其实并不拥挤。

英文原文

Kai Yu: I think we position ourself not first we are not competing with any car makers. We are actually enabling them. We are helping them. We are building strong partnerships with all the car makers and all the ecosystem partners including the automotive suppliers for example Bosch, Denso and many other global and local suppliers. And our philosophy is really make friends and make a long-term trust with our customers and partners. Essentially, this is fundamentally in the gene of our company. So we don't have too in a sense of we don't have too much culture from the competition perspective but more from, you know, what kind of value we can create.

But luckily if you look at our business I mean fundamentally compared to like those car makers we are not so crowded, right? And if you look at China I think pretty much the three top players for the automotive computing I think namely nowadays I think really consolidated to Nvidia, Huawei and Horizon Robotics. Not very crowded.
