童年记忆与黄油吐司

成为母亲：我成了食物本身

职业压力与感官倦怠

Anna Martin: 随着时间推移，做饭不再仅仅是家庭事务，它成为了你的全职职业。你在《Bon Appétit》的工作获得了巨大的成功，镜头前的你看起来如此自在快乐。但你在新书中提到，在那段时间里，你私下里对食物的感受其实发生了变质？

Original English

Anna Martin: And over time, cooking wasn't just home life, it became your entire career. Your work at Bon Appétit took off in a massive way. You looked so happy and at ease on camera. But in the book, you write that behind the scenes during that era, your personal relationship with food was actually deteriorating?

Carla Lalli Music: 是的。当做饭变成一份极度高压的工作时，它的本质就变了。在测试厨房里，你必须不断开发、反复测试、产出爆款食谱；而在镜头前，你必须始终保持饱满的热情和充沛的精力。每一道菜你都必须去理性解构它：盐分够不够、酸度是否平衡、烹饪步骤是否对读者友好。每天回到家，我已经精疲力竭，完全失去了为自己品尝食物的感官欲望。食物变成了任务清单上的一项项待办事项，我彻底丧失了从吃东西中获取纯粹快乐的能力。

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: Yes. When cooking becomes your full-time, high-pressure job, the nature of it shifts. In the test kitchen, you are constantly developing, testing, producing hit recipes; and on camera, you have to perform and be "on." Every dish is dissected intellectually: Is there enough salt? Is the acid balanced? Are the instructions clear for home cooks? By the time I got home, I was completely depleted. I had zero sensory desire left for food. Food became a to-do list, a set of deliverables, and I completely lost touch with eating for simple joy.

Anna Martin: 你不仅要喂养镜头前的几百万观众，还要回家喂养家人，同时还要担任大厨的角色。这种“全天候喂养他人”的状态持续了多久？

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: 持续了许多年。做饭成了我定义自身价值的唯一支柱，但在这个过程中，我完全把自己抽空了。我一直在问大家想吃什么、孩子们需要什么营养、食谱读者需要什么灵感，但我从没有停下来问过自己一句：“Carla，你现在真正想吃的是什么？”

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: For many years. Cooking had become the primary pillar of my self-worth, but in that process, I emptied myself out. I was always asking what everyone else wanted, what nutrition my kids needed, what the audience was looking for, but I never once paused to ask: "Carla, what do you actually crave right now?"

二十四年婚姻的裂痕与终结

Anna Martin: 与此同时，你的个人生活也迎来了巨震。你与丈夫结束了长达 24 年的婚姻。做出分开的决定对你而言意味着什么？

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: It was a long, painful awakening. We had been together for 24 years, raised two children, and built a life that looked solid from the outside. Admitting that a marriage of a quarter-century is no longer working takes immense courage, because it means dismantling the entire world you built.

Carla Lalli Music: 餐桌变成了掩盖裂痕的舞台。每天准备晚餐变成了维持表面正常的机械仪式。我做饭是为了维持家庭的运转，但饭桌上的沉默和压抑却让人窒息。食物失去了滋味，它只是维系日常运转的燃料。

I cooked to keep the household running, but the silence and emotional tension around the table were suffocating. Food lost all flavor; it was merely functional fuel to keep routine intact.

独居生活与番茄三明治的顿悟

Anna Martin: 离婚之后，你搬进了属于自己的公寓，开始了独自生活。你能回忆一下你第一次独自为自己做饭时的场景吗？

Carla Lalli Music: 那是一种前所未有的陌生感。二十多年来，我第一次走进厨房，不需要考虑任何人的口味、过敏源或时间表。一开始我甚至感到恐慌——冰箱里空空如也，我站在那里想：“我现在该给谁做饭？”然后我意识到：只有我一个人。

Carla Lalli Music: It was totally disorienting. For over two decades, I had never stepped into a kitchen without catering to someone else's preferences, allergies, or schedules. Carla Lalli Music: Because a truly ripe tomato is visceral and sensual! The aroma of the vine, the burst of juice, the tender flesh, that perfect tension of sweet and acid. 食物是一种感受：依循食欲而活

Anna Martin: 这直接切中了你新书的主题——《Food Is a Feeling》。很多传统食谱都在教人“如何正确烹饪”或者“每周备餐指南”，但你的这本书却完全是从情感和身体感受出发的。能谈谈这个理念吗？

Original English

Anna Martin: That gets right to the core of your new book, Food Is a Feeling. So many traditional cookbooks focus on technique or rigid meal prep, but yours is structured around emotional and physical cravings. Tell me about that philosophy.

Carla Lalli Music: 我们被灌输了太多关于饮食的“规矩”：什么时间该吃什么、营养必须如何搭配、碳水是不是太多了。但我们往往忘记了，食欲是身体与潜意识最直接的对话。在经历了漫长的压抑和取悦他人之后，我决定彻底打破这些条条框框。如果我今天渴望吃一碗热气腾腾、拌了大块黄油和酱油的米饭，那它就是今晚最完美的晚餐。你不需要为此感到内疚，顺应食欲是对自己最深层次的关爱。

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: We are conditioned by so many dietary rules: what to eat at certain times, macro balances, worrying about carbs. If tonight I crave a hot bowl of rice with a huge knob of butter and a splash of soy sauce, that is the most perfect dinner. There is no guilt required. Feeding your true cravings is the ultimate form of self-care.

Anna Martin: 听你这么说，这不仅仅关乎做饭，更是一种决策模式的重塑。离婚需要做无数艰难痛苦的理性抉择，而在厨房里，你开始把决策权交还给直觉和身体。

Carla Lalli Music: 没错！在经历了那么多痛苦、悲伤和自我怀疑之后，我问自己：“我为什么还要在日常饮食中继续惩罚自己？”如果某样东西让我感到美味和安慰，我就应该毫无保留地享受它。

Original English

Carla Lalli Music: Exactly! After enduring so much denial, hardship, and heartbreak, I thought: "Why would I continue to punish myself?" If something tastes amazing and brings me comfort, I should eat it with complete abandon and give myself that gift.

重新约会与向他人敞开
