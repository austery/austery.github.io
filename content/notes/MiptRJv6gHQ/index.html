产品类型分类：加装件、智能马桶座圈与一体机

克里斯汀·西里莱特: 道格，市面上的卫洗丽产品种类繁多，你能帮大家梳理一下主要有哪几种类型吗？

Original English

Christine Cyr Clisset: Doug, there are so many different types of bidets on the market. Can you break down the main categories for us?

道格·马奥尼: 大致可以分为三大类：

第一类是冷水加装配件（Bidet Attachment）。这类产品售价通常在 25 到 60 美元 之间。它是一块轻薄的塑料配件，直接夹装在你现有的普通马桶座圈和陶瓷马桶之间。它不需要通电，完全依靠水管自身的水压机械驱动。缺点是只有常温冷水，功能相对单一。

第二类是电子智能马桶盖（Electric Bidet Seat）。售价一般在 200 到 700 美元 之间，比如我们长期推荐的 Toto Washlet C5 或 Coway Bidetmega 200。你拆下原有的马桶盖，换上整个带有电脑控制的座圈。它需要插电，提供恒温加热水流、座圈加热、暖风烘干、前后移动喷洗、脉冲按摩以及无线遥控器等丰富功能。

第三类是一体式智能马桶（Integrated Smart Toilet），例如 Toto Neorest 系列。整套马桶和卫洗丽系统融合为一体，外观极其极简优雅，具备自动开合盖、自动冲水、夜灯、空气净化等全套顶级功能，价格通常在 2,000 美元到数万美元。

Original English

Doug Mahoney: There are basically three main categories:

First, you have bidet attachments. These usually cost between $25 and $60. It's a thin plastic plate that mounts under your existing toilet seat. They are non-electric and operate purely on mechanical water pressure. The tradeoff is they only offer cold/ambient temperature water and basic controls.

Second are electric bidet seats, which cost between $200 and $700, like our long-standing top picks, the Toto Washlet C5 or the Coway Bidetmega 200. You completely replace your toilet seat with a full electronic unit. These require a nearby electrical outlet and give you warm water, heated seats, warm-air dryers, oscillating nozzles, and wireless remote controls.

Third are integrated smart bidet toilets, like the Toto Neorest series. The bidet is seamlessly built into the porcelain toilet bowl. They look sleek and offer auto-opening lids, automatic flushing, deodorizers, and nightlights, but they cost anywhere from $2,000 to well over $10,000.

安装实战：租房客的顾虑与水电改造

罗茜·加林: 作为一名租房者，我最大的担忧是安装过程。安装它需要改造管道吗？如果我搬家退租，能轻松拆下来还原吗？

Original English

Rosie Guerin: As a renter, my biggest worry is the installation. Does it require permanent plumbing changes? And when I move out, can I uninstall it and return the bathroom to its original state?

道格·马奥尼: 这其实是卫洗丽最大的优点之一：它是完全可逆的，不会对房屋造成任何永久性破坏。

安装非常简单，一般只需 15 到 30 分钟：

  1. 关闭马桶水箱下方的角阀供水，并冲水排空水箱。
  2. 拧开原有的进水软管。
  3. 接上包装内自带的金属 三通转接阀（T-valve）。
  4. 将一端重新接回马桶水箱，另一端连接卫洗丽进水管。
  5. 将座圈或配件卡扣固定在马桶螺栓孔上。

当你搬家退租时，只需反向操作，拆下三通阀和卫洗丽，重新装回原来的普通马桶盖即可，房东根本不会察觉。

Original English

Doug Mahoney: That's actually one of the best things about bidets: it is completely reversible and causes zero permanent changes to the plumbing.

Installation takes about 15 to 30 minutes:

  1. Shut off the water supply valve below the toilet tank and flush to empty the tank.
  2. Disconnect the flexible water supply hose.
  3. Screw on the included metal T-valve adapter to the bottom of the tank.
  4. Reconnect the supply hose to one branch, and connect the bidet hose to the other.
  5. Secure the mounting bracket to the seat bolt holes and slide the bidet seat in.

When you move out, you just reverse the steps, put the original seat back on, and your landlord will never know.

凯拉·布莱克威尔: 关于电源问题呢？如果卫生间马桶旁没有插座怎么办？

Original English

Kyra Blackwell: What about the electricity issue? What if you don't have an electrical outlet near the toilet?

道格·马奥尼: 如果你没有插座，你有两个选择： 第一，选择非电动机械款（如基础加装件）。虽然只有常温水，但在春夏秋季体验依然很好。 第二，如果想要恒温热水款，可以请电工在马桶旁加装一个带 GFCI（漏电保护） 的安全插座，费用通常在 150 到 300 美元 左右。很多租客也会使用带有防水保护的安全延长线，从洗手台附近的插座沿踢脚线引过去。

Original English

Doug Mahoney: If you don't have an outlet nearby, you have two main routes: First, go with a non-electric mechanical attachment. You'll only have ambient tap water, but many people find it refreshing and completely fine. Second, if you really want heated water and luxury features, you can hire an electrician to install a GFCI outlet near the toilet, which typically costs $150 to $300. Alternatively, some renters safely route a heavy-duty, moisture-resistant extension cord neatly along baseboards from the sink vanity outlet.

使用体验与纸张节约效益

克里斯汀·西里莱特: 很多人第一次使用卫洗丽时会担心冷水会不会太冰刺激。道格，以你的测试经验来看，冷水款真的可以接受吗？

Original English

Christine Cyr Clisset: A lot of first-time buyers worry that cold water is going to feel shocking or uncomfortable. In your testing experience, Doug, is cold water actually tolerable?

道格·马奥尼: 绝大多数人在试过几次后都会迅速适应。室内的自来水管水温一般在 15-20 摄氏度左右，其实并不会像冰块那样刺骨，反而给人一种清爽振奋的感觉。当然，在寒冷的北方冬季，温水款的舒适度确实无可替代。

Original English

Doug Mahoney: Most people get used to it after just two or three uses. Ambient water in indoor plumbing is usually around 55 to 65 degrees Fahrenheit, so it's refreshing rather than freezing painful. But if you live in a very cold northern climate in winter, a heated model with continuous warm water is definitely worth the upgrade.

凯拉·布莱克威尔: 自从用了卫洗丽，我家的卫生纸消耗量下降了至少 75%。你基本上只需要用一两格纸轻轻沾干水分即可，甚至如果带有暖风烘干功能，连纸都可以省下。

Original English

Kyra Blackwell: Since switching to a bidet, my household toilet paper consumption dropped by at least 75%. You really only need one or two squares to pat dry, or if your model has a warm-air dryer, you barely need any paper at all.

道格·马奥尼: 没错，无论是从个人卫生、皮肤健康、减少下水道堵塞风险，还是环保角度来看，卫洗丽都是一项极小投入却能带来长期巨大收益的升级。

Original English

Doug Mahoney: Exactly. Whether you look at it from personal hygiene, skin health, preventing plumbing clogs, or environmental sustainability, adding a bidet is one of the highest-ROI upgrades you can make to your daily routine.
