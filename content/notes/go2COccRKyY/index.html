美加关税战与地缘信任崩塌：当百年盟友沦为‘美国湖’与吞并威胁的靶心 TVO Today 2026-09-11

领域: geopolitics

分类: geopolitics

主讲人: TVO Today

来源: 查看

发布时间: 2026-09-11

摘要:

本期节目深入探讨了特朗普政府对加拿大实施高额关税、吞并威胁及擅自将安大略湖改名为‘美国湖’所引发的美加关系历史性危机。访谈邀请了地貌政治学者、前总理幕僚与资深时政记者，从地名政治学、跨党派政治博弈、民意情绪激化及历史断层等维度，剖析了两国百年同盟关系的不可逆裂痕与加拿大国家认同的觉醒。

美加贸易谈判破裂与关税战升级

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 长期以来，我们已经习惯了作为美国最亲密的盟友和最大的贸易伙伴之一。然而，唐纳德·特朗普 (Donald Trump) 总统上台了。他多次威胁要吞并加拿大，并坚信我们一直在占美国的便宜。

Original English

Jan Alden: Look, we are used to being one of the United States's closest allies and largest trading partners. Then came President Donald Trump. He's repeatedly threatened to annex Canada and he's convinced we're taking advantage of America.

唐纳德·特朗普 (Donald Trump): 加拿大在贸易上占我们便宜已经占了很久了。

Original English

Donald Trump: Canada's been ripping us off for a long time on trade.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 那么，我们最近的贸易谈判进展顺利吗？正如你可能已经知道的那样，答案绝对是否定的。谈判在最后一刻破裂了。根据卡尼 (Carney) 的说法，美方“要得太多，给得太少”。现在，美国对加拿大加征了更多关税，而加拿大的反制关税也在本周正式生效。

哦，而且这场贸易战还引发了一场围绕一个湖泊名称的口水战，而且这个湖离我们家门口非常近。

Original English

Jan Alden: So, did our recent trade talks go well? As you're probably already aware, that's a definite no. They collapsed at the last minute. And according to Carney, the US quote asked too much and offered too little. Now we've got more US tariffs on Canada and the Canadian counter tariffs that took effect just this week. Oh, and the trade war also triggered a war of words over a lake and it's pretty close to home.

“美国湖”风波与地名政治学

唐纳德·特朗普 (Donald Trump): 我们将立即把安大略湖 (Lake Ontario) 的名字更改为美国湖 (Lake America)。

Original English

Donald Trump: We're going to be changing the name of Lake Ontario effective immediately to Lake America.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 不出所料，安大略省人根本不打算配合。省长道格·福特 (Doug Ford) 更是直截了当地表达了反对。那么，特朗普改名的举动真的重要吗？这一切对我们与美国的关系究竟意味着什么？我们将深入探讨美加关系到底发生了多大改变，以及它是否还有可能恢复。我是简·奥尔登，欢迎收听《The Rundown》。

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 这是特朗普正在使用的更广泛策略的一部分，某种程度上是为了重塑我们对现实的认知。

我叫鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)，是维多利亚大学 (University of Victoria) 的地理学教授，我的研究方向是地名政治学和文化景观研究。

在美国，地理实体命名的常规流程是：存在一个美国地名委员会 (US Board on Geographic Names)。他们设有国内地名委员会和国外地名委员会，专门审议提案。事实上，任何人都可以向美国地名委员会提交提案。该提案会在多个层级经历广泛的咨询程序。如果是跨国地理实体，提案随后还会提交给相关国家的地名委员会——在这种情况下也就是加拿大地名委员会 (Geographical Names Board of Canada)。我们在2009年萨利希海 (Salish Sea) 命名时就看到了这种情况，当时两国在州级、省会及联邦层面展开合作。

Original English

Jan Alden: No surprise, Ontarians aren't willing to play along. Premier Doug Ford literally spelled it out. So, does Trump's name change matter? And what does all this mean for our relationship with the states? We dig into how much it's changed and whether it'll ever recover? I'm Jan Alden. Welcome to the rundown.

Ruben Rose-Redwood: This is part of a broader playbook that Trump is using to kind of reshape our sense of reality in a sense. So my name is Ruben Rose-Redwood and I'm a professor of geography at the University of Victoria and my research looks at the politics of place naming and cultural landscape studies. The conventional process of renaming geographic places in the United States is that there's the US Board on Geographic Names. They have a domestic and a foreign names committees and they consider proposals. Anyone in fact can submit a proposal to the US Board on Geographic Names. It goes through an extensive consultation process at multiple levels. If it's an international geographic feature, it would then go to the geographic names board in this case of Canada as well. We saw this with the naming of the Salish Sea back in 2009 where the two countries worked together both at state, provincial and federal levels.

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 没错。谷歌和苹果都在运营各自的地图应用程序，他们都采纳了“美国湖”这一命名。在美国境内，特朗普有权直接向内政部长下达行政命令，要求其指示地名委员会更改联邦地图上的名称。然而，在国际层面，这一更名是不具有法律效力的。

Original English

Ruben Rose-Redwood: Right. So both Google and Apple, they run their different map applications and they both adopted this Lake America naming. In the United States, Trump does have the authority through executive order to direct the Secretary of the Interior to direct the Board on Geographic Names to make a name change on federal maps. However, internationally, that name change has no standing.

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 是的。正如我在撰写的文章中所指出的那样，我认为这与将墨西哥湾更名为“美洲湾”并没有本质区别。这是一种地缘政治的挑衅姿态。然而，如果这种改名被大型科技公司制度化，并且在日常地图导航中被数亿人使用，它就会逐渐渗透并开始改变大众的日常空间感知。

Original English

Ruben Rose-Redwood: Yes. And so as I said in the article that I wrote, you know, I don't think this is more or less offensive than the change in name to the Gulf of America. It is a geopolitical provocation. But if it gets institutionalized by big tech and used by hundreds of millions of people on a daily basis in mapping apps, it starts to shift people's everyday spatial perception.

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 为什么呢？因为在过去18个月里，我们本有充分的时间来认清一个事实：唐纳德·特朗普显然在利用加拿大对美国数字基础设施和服务器的依赖。自从贸易冲突爆发以来，科技巨头迅速顺从白宫意愿的举动，暴露了我们在关键数字主权上的巨大脆弱性。

Original English

Ruben Rose-Redwood: Why? Because you've had 18 months to deal with the fact that Donald Trump is very clearly abusing their dependence on this US server. Since the trade disputes began, the speed with which tech giants comply with White House directives exposes our critical vulnerabilities in digital sovereignty.

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 没错。在历史上，这种通过看似琐碎或荒谬的象征性争端，揭示出深层实质性危机的先例屡见不鲜。表面上是在争夺一个湖泊的名字，但实质上暴露的是两国地缘政治权力的不对称以及关键基础设施的控制权争夺。

Original English

Ruben Rose-Redwood: Right. So, you know, this would not be the first time in history that a relatively important substantial issue was illuminated by something that seemed symbolic or petty on the surface. It’s about a lake name on the outside, but underneath it exposes asymmetric power and control over critical infrastructure.

鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood): 人们目前对数据中心有什么看法？你可能也看过汉密尔顿的一些相关报道。但总的来说，人们正在逐渐意识到，物理层面的土地、水资源以及数字层面的服务器和数据归属权，正在成为新的地缘战略博弈焦点。

非常感谢。

Original English

Ruben Rose-Redwood: How do people feel about data centers at the moment? Well, I mean you've probably seen some of the reporting from Hamilton as well, right? But generally, people are waking up to the fact that physical land, water resources, and digital server jurisdiction are becoming the frontline of geostrategic leverage. Thank you so much.

加拿大政界与公众的强硬回应

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 加拿大和美国曾是长期的盟友与挚友，拥有深厚的经济、文化和人际纽带。如今，在吞并威胁和贸易制裁的冲击下，两国的同盟信任正在分崩离析。

道格·福特 (Doug Ford): 不，我绝不会向像特朗普总统这样的独裁者低头。他就是一个彻头彻尾的霸凌者。

Original English

Jan Alden: Canada and the States have been longtime friends and allies with close economic, cultural, and personal ties. Now, thanks to annexation threats and trade sanctions, that foundation of trust is fracturing.

Doug Ford: No, I don't respond to a dictator like President Trump. Bully like President Trump.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): “独裁者”、“霸凌者”，政界甚至用上了这样的字眼。香农，你曾在《环球邮报 (Globe and Mail)》上撰写过多篇关于特朗普政府的报道。加拿大政界人士在公开表态上是否采取了恰当的基调？

Original English

Jan Alden: We've got dictator. We've got bully. Shannon, you've written about the Trump administration for the Globe and Mail. Are Canadian politicians taking the right tone in their rhetoric?

香农·普劳德富特 (Shannon Proudfoot): 安德鲁，你曾担任前总理斯蒂芬·哈珀 (Stephen Harper) 的通讯总监。所以在传递信息方面，当面对这种挑衅时，政界需要把握非常微妙的平衡。

Original English

Shannon Proudfoot: Andrew, you were the director of communications for former Prime Minister Stephen Harper. So I need to understand when it comes to, you know, delivering a message against this kind of provocation, it requires a very delicate balance.

安德鲁·麦克杜格尔 (Andrew MacDougall): 我认为首先必须明确的是，你在公开场合说的任何话，其效果都是极其有限的。你面对的是一个完全不受传统外交规矩约束的对手。任何试图通过理性辩论或迎合来改变特朗普立场的做法都是徒劳的。唯一的出路是展现出坚决不可动摇的底线，同时在内部做好长期的抗压准备。

Original English

Andrew MacDougall: Well, I think the first thing you have to do is to realize the limitations of what anything you say can and might do. I think you're dealing with an opponent who doesn't play by conventional diplomatic rules. Trying to reason with or placate Trump is futile. The only viable path is demonstrating absolute firmness while preparing domestic resilience for the long haul.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 正如你所说，加拿大公众目前似乎非常支持卡尼的强硬应对策略。根据安格斯·里德 (Angus Reed) 周二发布的一份民意调查，76% 的受访者表示支持加拿大采取对等反制措施。

Original English

Jan Alden: Well as you said Canadians seem to be on board with Carney's approach. So far an Angus Reed poll that came out Tuesday found that 76% say Canada should stand its ground with counter-tariffs.

安德鲁·麦克杜格尔 (Andrew MacDougall): 这确实让国内各党派处于一个非常罕见的统一战线。对于保守党来说，这其实也是一个微妙的境地，因为在国家受到外部单边威胁时，任何偏离国家团结立场的政党都将付出巨大的政治代价。

Original English

Andrew MacDougall: Oh, I think it definitely does. I mean, it puts all political factions in a rare united stance. It’s actually a very tricky position for the Conservatives, because when the country faces external unilateral threats, breaking ranks with national solidarity carries a devastating political penalty.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 香农，同一份民调也显示，大量民众对物价上涨和失业感到深切恐慌。你认为加拿大人真的做好了承受贸易战长期阵痛的准备吗？

Original English

Jan Alden: Shannon, that same poll did find that a whole lot of people are scared about the rising costs, uh, losing their jobs as well. Do you think Canadians are genuinely prepared for the sustained pain of a full-blown trade war?

香农·普劳德富特 (Shannon Proudfoot): 安德鲁，帮我理解一下：政界人士目前该如何维持公众的支持？当我们审视这些数据时，虽然支持强硬反击的比例很高，但日常生计的焦虑同样真实存在。

Original English

Shannon Proudfoot: Andrew, help me understand here. How do politicians keep Canadians on side right now? When we look at those numbers, the appetite for defiance is huge, but the anxiety over kitchen-table economics is just as real.

安德鲁·麦克杜格尔 (Andrew MacDougall): 是的，我认为关键在于始终牢记宏观大局。正如斯蒂芬所指出的，目前加拿大人最希望看到的是政府展现出捍卫国家主权的强硬姿态，而不是软弱妥协。民众清楚痛苦是不可避免的，但他们绝不接受不战而降。

Original English

Andrew MacDougall: Yeah, I think it's just keeping the greater context in mind. Right now the number one thing Canadians want to see, as Stephen was saying, is a tough posture defending sovereignty. People know economic pain is coming, but they won't tolerate surrender without a fight.

特朗普挑衅与民间认同的反弹

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 到目前为止，特朗普的一系列挑衅实际上反而在促使加拿大人空前团结地反对他。“美国湖”的闹剧以及社交媒体上疯传的一系列争议言论便是明证。

Original English

Jan Alden: I mean, so far Trump has been doing a whole lot to keep Canadians united against him. There's Lake America and there have been a few viral posts and memes poking back at it.

斯蒂芬·马什 (Stephen Marche): 如果我想的话，我也可以变得非常琐碎刻薄。很多政客可能心里会想：我也能用 AI 生成几张恶搞图片在网上反唇相讥。

Original English

Stephen Marche: I can be very petty if I want to. I'm sure politicians probably are like, I can probably get some AI images and fight back with memes on social media.

斯蒂芬·马什 (Stephen Marche): 但当对方沉迷于这种表演式挑衅时，我们必须埋头苦干。我们当前真正的当务之急，是彻底摆脱对这种不可预测势力的依赖，加速重构我们的经济与供应链。

Original English

Stephen Marche: But when he's doing that theater, we need to be working. We need to be real busy right now getting out of the way of these people and insulating our critical dependencies.

历史断层：美加同盟关系的永久性改变

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 斯蒂芬，我想继续请教你。这里的很多人不仅感受到了经济上的冲击，更感受到了人格与尊严上的冒犯。美加两国之间的关系是否已经遭受了不可逆转的破坏？

Original English

Jan Alden: Stephen, I want to stick with you. A lot of people here feel attacked personally, of course, not just economically. Has the US-Canada relationship suffered permanent, structural damage?

斯蒂芬·马什 (Stephen Marche): 我认为答案是肯定的。如果我们回顾历史，唯一能勉强相提并论的时期，或许只有加拿大联邦成立 (Confederation) 之前——也就是19世纪芬尼亚狂徒袭击 (Fenian raids) 和南北战争后美国吞并威胁笼罩的年代。但这百年来建立起来的信任基石，如今已被彻底摧毁。即便未来换了政府，加拿大人也不会再忘记：邻国随时可能将关税和主权威胁作为地缘敲诈的武器。

Original English

Stephen Marche: I don't think so. I mean, the last time you could compare it to—though I'm not sure it's fully comparable—is just before Confederation during the Fenian raids and post-Civil War annexation rhetoric. But the century-old foundation of assumed trust is gone. Even if an administration changes, Canadians will never forget that our neighbor is capable of weaponizing trade and sovereignty overnight.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 安德鲁，如果美国迎来一届新政府，情况会好转吗？

Original English

Jan Alden: Andrew, would a new administration change things?

安德鲁·麦克杜格尔 (Andrew MacDougall): 等一下，也许我会给你一个看似保守的回答：这取决于新政府的具体构成。但严肃地说，我认为必须正视两点：美国政治内部的保护主义和孤立主义已经深植于其两党政治光谱中。特朗普不仅是一个个体，他更代表了美国政治文化的一场深层质变。我们不能把国家安全寄托在华盛顿的选举周期上。

Original English

Andrew MacDougall: Wait a minute. I guess I'll give you the coward's answer: it depends what that administration looks like. But seriously, protectionism and America-first isolationism are now deeply embedded across the US political spectrum. Trump isn't just an anomaly; he accelerated a structural shift. We cannot base our national strategy on hoping for benign Washington election cycles.

美国民意极化与加拿大的国家性格重塑

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 斯蒂芬，根据 YouGov America 的民调，38% 的共和党选民将加拿大视为不友好国家甚至敌人，这创下了历史最高纪录。这种民意层面的敌意意味着什么？

Original English

Jan Alden: Stephen, according to polling from YouGov America, 38% of Republicans view Canada as unfriendly or an enemy to the US, by far the highest level in polling history. What does that hostility mean?

斯蒂芬·马什 (Stephen Marche): 这取决于唐纳德·特朗普想利用这种情绪做什么。在当前的极化环境下，支持者群体完全可以被煽动成特朗普所要求的任何狂热状态。将加拿大塑造成替罪羊和经济对手，已经成为其民粹动员机制的一部分。

Original English

Stephen Marche: Well, it depends what Donald Trump wants to do with them. I mean, they're capable of being whipped into basically any frenzy that he demands of them. Painting Canada as a villain and economic leech has become an established trope for populist mobilization.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 香农，这场贸易战以及伴随而来的一切，是否揭示了我们国家性格中的某些深层特质？

Original English

Jan Alden: Shannon, is this trade war and everything that goes with it revealing anything about our national character?

香农·普劳德富特 (Shannon Proudfoot): 我手头正好在写一篇关于这个主题的深度报道。长期以来，加拿大人总是习惯于在低调与自我调侃中定义自己，我们总觉得自己缺乏某种鲜明的国家神话。但历史一再证明，每当来自南方的外部压力试图强行碾压我们时，一种极具韧性、坚定且不容妥协的加拿大集体认同便会迅速凝聚。这场危机不仅重新划定了边界，更让加拿大人重新看清了我们是谁。

Original English

Shannon Proudfoot: I happen to be working on a story about that right now. We infamously define ourselves by polite understatement and self-deprecation, often feeling we lack an aggressive national myth. But history shows that whenever external pressure from the south attempts to bulldoze us, a fierce, resilient, and unyielding Canadian identity instantly coalesces. This crisis isn't just redefining borders; it's forcing us to remember who we are.

简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden): 时间会给出最终的答案。香农、斯蒂芬、安德鲁，非常感谢三位抽出时间参与我们的讨论。面对这场前所未有的变局，你对美加关系的未来又有何看法？感谢收听本期节目。

Original English

Jan Alden: Time will tell. Shannon, Stephen, Andrew, thank you so much. I really appreciate you taking the time. Have your feelings about the US changed and what do you think lies ahead? Thank you for watching and listening to the rundown.
