新生儿的意识之谜：科学如何揭示婴儿内心世界

关键字 development ethics llm newborn-consciousness

摘要

本文探讨了新生儿意识的科学研究进展，挑战了传统上认为婴儿是被动观察者的观点。通过测量大脑活动，神经科学家发现新生儿和晚期胎儿表现出意识感知的神经信号，包括对意外刺激的反应和注意力网络的切换。这些发现对新生儿手术麻醉和堕胎伦理等领域具有重要的医学和伦理启示，揭示了婴儿从出生甚至更早阶段就已积极感知世界。

踏入新世界：新生儿的感知体验

你在一片全新的世界中醒来。 你的双眼睁开，面对的是明亮而令人困惑的光线。 你的耳边充满了神秘的声音。 你周围的一切都让你感到陌生。 这就是新生儿的真实世界。 那么，作为一个新生儿是怎样的体验呢？ 对于像我这样的哲学家和心理学家来说，这是一个引人入胜的问题。 要知道成年人脑中在想些什么已经够难了。 那么一个新生儿的脑中又会发生什么呢？ 婴儿有意识（Consciousness: 对自身存在和周围世界的感知与体验）吗？ 也就是他们对自己的内心和世界的主观体验。 在成年人中，意识涉及视觉、听觉和思维的体验，以及疼痛、快乐和情绪的感受。 婴儿是否也拥有这种体验和感受，点亮他们的内心世界呢？

英文原文

You wake up in a new world. Your eyes open to bright, confusing lights. Your ears are filled with mysterious sounds. Everything around you feels unfamiliar. This is a reality of a newborn baby. So what is it like to be a newborn? For a philosopher and a psychologist like me, this is a fascinating question. It is hard enough to know what's going on in adults' minds. What could be going on in a newborn baby's mind? Do babies have consciousness? The subjective experience of their mind and the world. In adults, consciousness involves experiences of seeing, hearing and thinking, and feelings of pain, pleasure and emotions. Do babies also have this experience and feelings that light up their inner world?

挑战传统观念：婴儿并非被动观察者

传统的观点认为，新生儿只是被动地观察着混乱不堪的世界，他们可能根本没有意识。 这在今天听起来令人难以置信，但在50年前，医生们在没有麻醉的情况下例行对婴儿进行包皮环切术，因为他们坚信新生儿不成熟的大脑无法感受到疼痛。 从那时起，发展心理学家已经表明，婴儿的能力比我们之前想象的要复杂得多。 但关于婴儿意识的问题仍然悬而未决。 一个问题是，婴儿无法告诉我们他们的感受。 他们无法描述自己的想法。 他们当然也无法参加意识测试。 那么，我们如何才能知道他们内心发生了什么呢？

英文原文

So the traditional view is that newborns are passive observers of overwhelming chaos, and they may not be conscious at all. It sounds unbelievable today, but 50 years ago, doctors routinely performed circumcision without anaesthetic, convinced that newborns' immature brain could not feel pain. Since then, developmental psychologists have shown that infants' abilities are much more complex than we thought before. But the question of infant consciousness has remained open. One problem is that infants cannot tell us how they feel. They cannot describe their thoughts. And they certainly cannot take a consciousness test. So how can we know what's going on inside their minds?

大脑证据：神经信号揭示早期感知

一个答案是测量婴儿的大脑。 在过去的几十年里，意识科学告诉我们很多关于成年人意识的大脑基础。 我们发现，只有当成年人有意识地感知刺激时，某些神经信号才会活跃。 最近，神经科学家在婴儿的大脑中也发现了相同的神经信号。 这些提供了有力的新证据，表明婴儿可能从非常早的年龄就开始积极地体验周围环境。

英文原文

One answer is to measure infants' brains. Over the past few decades, the science of consciousness has told us a lot about the brain basis of consciousness in adults. We found neural signals that are only active when an adult is consciously perceiving a stimulus. Recently, neuroscientists found the same neural signals in infants' brains. These provide powerful new evidence that infants might be actively experiencing their surroundings from a remarkably early age.

奇异刺激范式：P300波的发现

神经科学中一项创新的实验是奇异刺激范式（Audible Paradigm/Oddball Paradigm: 一种通过检测大脑对意外声音序列的反应来衡量意识的实验方法）。 这是一个测试我们的大脑在发生意想不到的事情时如何反应的实验。 我喜欢这个范式，它的工作原理是这样的： 想象一下你反复听到相同的声音序列：哔、哔、哔、嘟。 哔、哔、哔、嘟。 哔、哔、哔、嘟。 突然，这个熟悉的模式被一个不同的序列打断了：哔、哔、哔、哔。 你的大脑会立即检测到这种意外，产生一个可测量的脑信号，称为P300波（P300 wave: 一种与意外刺激反应相关的脑电信号）。 这种对预期声音序列的“奇异”反应只发生在个体有意识时。 深度睡眠中的人没有这种反应。 昏迷中的人也没有。 但新生儿有。 神经科学家Ghislaine Dehaene发现，当婴儿只有几天大时，他们对这种奇异的声音序列表现出相同类型的大脑活动。 这表明，从出生那一刻起，婴儿可能就已经真正在体验有意识的感知和有意识的预期。

英文原文

One innovative experiment in neuroscience is the audible paradigm. This is a test of how our brain reacts when something unexpected happens. I love this paradigm, and here’s how it works. Imagine repeatedly hearing the same sequence of sounds. Beep, beep, beep, boop. Beep, beep, beep, boop. Beep, beep, beep, boop. Suddenly, this familiar pattern is interrupted by a different sequence. Beep, beep, beep, beep. Instantly, your brain detects the surprise, producing a measurable brain signal called the P300 wave. This oddball response to an expected sequence of sounds only happens when an individual is conscious. People in deep sleep don't have it. People in comas don't have it. But newborn babies do. The neuroscientist Ghislaine Dehaene has found that when babies are just a few days old, they show the same type of brain activity in response to this oddball sequence of sounds. What this suggests is that right from birth, infants might be truly experiencing conscious perceptions and conscious expectations.

注意力模式：内外意识的切换

研究人员还通过大脑中的注意力模式来寻找意识的证据。 在有意识的成年人大脑中，当我们把注意力从外部世界切换到内部思想时，不同类型的网络会交替活动。 你知道这是怎么回事。 你现在可能就在这样做。 你可能先专注于说话者一段时间，然后又开始神游。 （笑声） 结果发现，婴儿也会做类似的事情。 神经科学家Lorina Naci最近在新生儿的大脑中观察到了这些网络之间相同类型的交替。 这表明，这种内部和外部意识的焦点切换从出生那一刻起就存在了。 还有来自注意力空白的证据。 当我们的思维高度专注于一件事时，它通常会对紧随其后发生的事情视而不见。 我们称这种现象为注意力瞬盲（Attentional Blink: 当大脑高度专注于某事时，对紧随其后发生的事件暂时失去感知的现象）。 婴儿也会经历这种现象，但速度较慢。 三个月大的婴儿需要将近一整秒的时间才能将注意力从一个视觉线索转移到另一个视觉线索，而成年人则能更快地完成这种转移。 令人惊讶的是，婴儿在发生这种情况时表现出相同类型的大脑反应，这强烈暗示他们正在积极地体验周围环境。

英文原文

Researchers have also looked for consciousness through patterns of attention in the brain. In conscious adults’ brains, different types of networks alternate their activity when we switch our attention between the external world and our internal thoughts. You know how it is. You might be doing this right now. You focus your attention on the speaker for a while, and then you daydream for a while. (Laughter) It turns out that infants do the same sort of thing. The neuroscientist Lorina Naci recently observed the same type of alternation between these networks in newborn brains. This suggests that these switches of focus of internal and external awareness are present right from birth. There is also evidence from gaps in attention. When our mind intensely focuses on one thing, it usually becomes blind to something that happens immediately afterward. We call this phenomenon attentional blink. Infants experience this phenomenon too, but in slow motion. At three months old, infants take near a full second to shift their attention from one visual cue to another, compared to adults that can manage this shift much faster. Amazingly, infants showing the same type of brain response when this happens is strongly hinting they are actively experiencing their environment.

胎儿意识及其伦理与医学启示

研究人员还在早产儿身上发现了相关的大脑模式。 这让你不禁会想，意识是否可能在出生前就开始了？ 这是一个非常重要的问题。 我告诉过大家科学家如何对新生儿进行听觉测试。 他们也对怀孕35周左右的晚期胎儿进行了相同的测试。 结果令人震惊。 胎儿表现出与我们在新生儿身上发现的相同类型的大脑反应。 所以，即使在出生和进入世界之前，婴儿似乎也能够有意识地处理声音，这意味着他们的意识可能在子宫内就已经发展了。 当然，这些结果在科学、医学和伦理方面都具有潜在的意义。 首先，我们现在知道，当我们对新生儿、早产儿或晚期胎儿进行手术时，我们应该给他们麻醉。 我知道你们许多人会想到堕胎的争论。 在这种情况下，我应该强调，我们最有力的证据是，意识需要在大约24周妊娠期（Gestation: 怀孕的整个时期）后出现的大脑结构，而此时堕胎是罕见的。 [新生儿有意识的]新证据可能延伸到妊娠晚期的胎儿，但不会延伸到更早的阶段。 这是一种新的理解，这种新的理解仍在发展中，但可能会改变我们对新生儿的看法。 他们不是等待意识开启的被动生物。 他们是已经感知模式并以有意义的方式与世界互动的微小人类。 随着人类生命的展开，意识也随之展开。 我们的自我意识不断成长和变化。 我们的意识此消彼长，直到有一天它结束。 从我们第一次呼吸的那一刻起，直到我们死亡的那一刻，我们的生命都被意识的火焰照亮。 我们与其他的动物分享这火焰，也许有一天我们也会与机器分享它。 总的来说，我们有意识的头脑照亮了宇宙，尽管每一束火焰最终都会熄灭，但意识之光永不消失。 它在存在的无尽舞蹈中，随着每一个新生命重新点燃。 谢谢大家。 （掌声）

英文原文

Researchers have also found relevant brain patterns in premature infants. Which makes you wonder, could consciousness begin before birth? This is a really important question. I told you all how scientists apply the audible test to newborns. Well, they applied the same test to late-term fetuses around 35 weeks into pregnancy. The results were striking. Fetus shows the same type of brain response as we found in newborns. So even before birth and entering the world, babies seem to be capable of consciously processing sounds, meaning their awareness might develop while they are still in the womb. Of course, these results have potential implications scientifically, medically and ethically. For a start, we now know that when we perform surgery on newborns or premature infants or late-term fetuses, we should give them an anesthetic. I know that many of you will be thinking about the abortion debate. In that context, I should stress that our strongest evidence is that consciousness requires brain structures that emerge after 24 weeks of gestation, a time when abortion is rare. The new evidence [that newborns are conscious] might extend to fetuses in third trimester of gestation, but it doesn't extend to earlier than that. This is a new understanding, and this new understanding is a work in progress, but might change our picture of newborn babies. They are not passive creatures waiting for consciousness to switch on. They are tiny humans already perceiving patterns and interacting with the world in a meaningful way. As human life unfolds, consciousness unfolds with it. Our sense of ourselves grows and changes. Our consciousness waxes and wanes until one day it ends. From the moment we take our first breath to the moment of our deaths, our lives are lit by the flame of awareness. We share this flame with other animals and we might one day share it with machines. Collectively, our conscious minds illuminate the universe, and though each flame eventually fades, the light of consciousness never disappears. It is rekindled with each new life in the endless dance of existence. Thank you. (Applause)
