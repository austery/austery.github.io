他们以为一颗子弹能结束一个人，却没想到点燃了一个时代的火。 迄今为止最震撼的声音，来自 Stephen Miller。 这不仅是一场演讲， 而是一段足以穿越时空、铭刻历史的经典。 “是我们，在建设，是我们，在创造，是我们，在托举人类文明！你以为你可以杀死 Charlie Kirk？你错了—你让他永生！” “你无法想象你们唤醒了什么，你们无法理解，你们唤起了我们每一个人心中的军团！” “我们所捍卫的，是正义，是美德，是崇高。那些煽动针对我们暴力的人—你们拥有什么？你们什么都没有。你们就是邪恶、嫉妒、仇恨。 你们一无所有，你们创造不了任何东西，建造不了任何东西，留不下任何东西。” “你们根本不知道你们唤醒了怎样的巨龙……我们将以怎样的决心走到底。” 🚨 BREAKING - STEPHEN MILLER JUST WENT OFF: "WE are the ones who build, who create, who lift up humanity. You thought you could KILL Charlie Kirk? YOU HAVE MADE HIM IMMORTAL!" "They cannot IMAGINE what they have AWAKENED! They cannot conceive of the army they have arisen in all of us!" "And now millions will carry on his legacy! And we will devote the rest of our lives to FINISHING the causes for which Charlie gave his last measure of devotion." "We stand for what is GOOD, virtuous and NOBLE. And to those trying to incite violence against us? ... What do YOU HAVE? You have NOTHING, you are nothing. You are wickedness, you are jealousy, you are hatred, YOU ARE NOTHING. You can build nothing, produce nothing, create nothing." "You cannot defeat us, slow us, stop us - we will carry Charlie and Erika in our heart every single day and fight that much harder because of WHAT YOU DID TO US." "You have no idea the dragon you awakened...how determined we will be."
