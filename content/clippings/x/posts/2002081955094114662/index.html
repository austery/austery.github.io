The retail edge isn’t timing. It’s structure. Here’s the only framework I trust in “AI audit season” core+tactical. $MU Most people treat earnings and macro like a casino: call the move or confess you’re wrong. That’s not investing. That’s dopamine management. Buy-side doesn’t get paid for being clever. It gets paid for staying solvent through regime shifts. Core is your right to be there. It’s held on the thesis clock, not the candle clock. Tactical is path management. It exists to make sure volatility doesn’t tax you out of a good idea. Two rules. No extra religion. When the tape is strong, I harvest a little certainty. Not a full exit. Not a victory lap. Just enough to lower my emotional cost and stop overtrading. When the tape is weak, I only add if structure is intact. Not “it’s red.” “it’s red and getting absorbed.” That’s the difference between a discount and a knife: A discount drops, then stabilizes. A knife drops, then accelerates and fails to reclaim. And stop worshipping levels like buttons. “Did it break 260?” is the wrong question. The question is: did the market accept above it by the close? Wicks are noise. Closes are verdicts. If you internalize that, you stop donating good positions to the wash cycle. You stop confusing motion with information. AI audit season doesn’t pay you for predicting candles. It pays you for staying on the right side of structure long enough for the thesis to compound.
