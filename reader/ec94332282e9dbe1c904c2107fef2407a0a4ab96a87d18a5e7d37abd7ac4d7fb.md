6

Stand with Jensen.

希望能在独立公正调查、充分自我申辩或全体股东投票决定后再做出免职决定。

As a NVIDIA shareholder for the past seven years, I firmly believe that Jensen Huang’s leadership, vision, and contributions to the company are irreplaceable.

I fully support his return. Any investigation should be fair, independent, and transparent, and Jensen must be given every opportunity to respond to the allegations and defend himself.

Only after a fair investigation and due process should all NVIDIA shareholders have the opportunity to make an informed decision through a shareholder vote.

Jensen has played an extraordinary role in building NVIDIA into what it is today. He deserves a fair hearing, and shareholders deserve a voice in the company’s future.
