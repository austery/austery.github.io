### 产品哲学与消灭公司官僚主义

**主持人**: 除了 Roblox 之外，你是如何思考优秀产品的定义？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: Outside of Roblox, how do you think about great products?

</details>

**David Baszucki**: 我非常推崇“少即是多”的概念。我认为增加按钮和配置非常容易，但去掉所有的按钮和滑块却非常困难。当我们思考优秀的产品时，我喜欢用厨房来做类比。比如，我绝对不会想要一个专门煮热狗的单一功能电器，相比之下，我更喜欢一把精美的刀和一口精美的平底锅。所以我更喜欢简单且高品质的事物，而不是复杂的冗余之物。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**David Baszucki**: I like the notion of less is more. So, I like the notion of telling everyone it's very easy to add knobs and stuff. It's very hard to remove all of the knobs and sliders. It's fun when we think about products like in a kitchen, for example, when we think about great products. I definitely would not like a dedicated appliance to cook hot dogs as opposed to a beautiful knife and a beautiful frying pan. So, I think I like simpler things of higher quality rather than complicated things.

</details>

**主持人**: 在公司运营层面上，你是如何理解“简化”和“去除”的？我的意思是，企业内部似乎很容易堆积起无用的“沉积物”，而**官僚主义（bureaucracy）**则是组织天然的熵增表现。必须有一种反向的力量来清除它。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: How do you think of removing when it comes to the company? I mean, there seems to be like sediment builds up inside the company and bureaucracy is the natural sort of like entropy of an organization. And then there has to be a counterforce almost to get rid of that.

**David Baszucki**: 在 Roblox 内部，我们有时确实会使用“**消灭官僚主义（destroy bureaucracy）**”这个词。我想说的是，如果一个组织不在主动减少官僚主义，那么就没有所谓的中间地带。在很多情况下，如果你觉得现状还不错，只想保持现有的稳定状态，那实际上整个系统就已经在某种程度上开始内爆了。如果人们不去努力改进事物，事物就会退步。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**David Baszucki**: We do use the term destroy bureaucracy inside the company at times. Like I I would say that if you are if one is not reducing bureaucracy, there's no neutral ground. Like I think in a lot of situations where one thinks we're pretty good, like we're just going to let it be in this steady state, that's actually things are imploding in a way. If one is not trying to improve things, it is going backwards.

</details>

When someone's trying to send that email I'm optimizing for myself. ### 决策机制、第一性原理与社区的力量

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**David Baszucki**: Oh, no. I don't think there's a lot of mistakes. I think the typical pattern is um hey, can we make this simpler? Hey, can we look a couple years out and think about what's going to happen with that? That looks a little short-term. Hey, that looks a little complicated. You have two things doing the same thing. Hey, can we take that from a appliance that heats up hot dogs into two separate appliances, a pan and a knife that are both the best in the world.

</details>

**主持人**: 你能稍微展开解释一下你眼中的高效决策和思考的原则吗？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

</details>

**David Baszucki**: 在一个组织内部，许多决策的制定中，更重要的事情其实是明确“谁是这个决定的决策者”，并让他们在某种自由和创造性的空间里去独立做出决定，这往往比搞集体决策、含糊不清的决策要有效得多。所以在 Roblox 内部，你会越来越多地看到我们强调：我们能否明确决策人，并给他们一定的自由度去自己拿主意。我认为这超级强大。
