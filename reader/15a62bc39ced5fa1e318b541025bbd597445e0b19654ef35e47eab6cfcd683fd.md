### Medicaid 工作新规将至，全美医院全力应对

对于享受医疗补助计划（Medicaid）的数百万美国低收入群体中的许多人而言，时间正一分一秒地流逝。明年初，这项公共医疗保险计划的重大新规将正式生效。这些变更是由特朗普总统及国会共和党人倡导的一项重大法案的一部分，新规将要求许多享受 Medicaid 的人遵守冗长的新规定，证明自己处于就业或就学状态，方可保留参保资格。

<summary>Original English</summary>
For many of the millions of lowincome Americans on Medicaid, the clock is ticking. At the start of next year, major new rules for the public health insurance program are set to kick in. The changes are part of the one big beautiful bill championed by President Trump and Republicans in Congress, and they will require many of those on Medicaid to follow lengthy new regulations to prove that they're employed or in school to keep their coverage.
