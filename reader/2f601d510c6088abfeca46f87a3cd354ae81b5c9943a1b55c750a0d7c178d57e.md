### 如何将物理实体“保存到桌面”

**Alan Resnick**: 还没在电脑里！但实际上任何东西都可以被保存到你的桌面上。你唯一需要做的，就是把一个摄像头围着一个桶呈 90 度架设好。然后你要往桶里装满黑色的水，并把你心爱的物品涂成白色。接着这样做，真的很简单。你把物品浸入水中，并在把它拉出来的时候拍下自己，记录它随着时间推移的拓扑结构。你将获得每一个轴向的拓扑数据，包含所有的 axes，axi。一旦你拥有了轴向数据，你唯一需要做的就是对这些轴进行布尔运算。你最终会得到一个非常高画质的 **OBJ 格式文件**，你可以在无限种灯光渲染条件下渲染出极高的画质。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Alan Resnick**: Yet! But literally anything can be saved to your desktop. All you have to do is rig up a camera around a bucket at 90 degrees. You're going to want to fill the bucket with black water and paint your beloved object white. And here's what you do. It's really easy. You submerge the object in the water and film yourself as you pull it up, recording its topology through time. You're going to get the topology of every axis, axes, axi. And once you have the axis data, all you have to do is boolean the axis. You’re going to end up with an OBJ file that’s super high quality, you can render in an infinite quality of lighting conditions.
