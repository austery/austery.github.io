### 赌博还是投资？两种叙事的法理博弈

**娜塔莉·基特罗夫 (Natalie Kitroeff)**: 好，那请带我们理清一下支持州政府监管与支持联邦监管各自的法律论点。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Natalie Kitroeff**: Okay, I think for a lot of people their gut reaction to that is just going to be wait, obviously these are betting apps, right? So, just break down exactly how this argument works, how this isn't actually gambling.

**大卫·亚菲-贝拉尼 (David Yaffe-Bellany)**: 预测市场的结构与传统体育博彩公司截然不同。在传统的体育博彩中，一方是下注的玩家，另一方是设定赔率的**庄家**（the house）。当赌客输钱时，庄家盈利。因此，在平台公司与用户之间，天然存在着一种对立、零和的关系。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**David Yaffe-Bellany**: Prediction markets are structured differently than traditional sports books. So, in traditional sports books you've got the person placing the bets, and then you've got the house which sets the odds. And when the person who's betting loses, the house makes money. So, you're set up in this kind of like oppositional relationship between the company and the user.

</details>

**娜塔莉·基特罗夫 (Natalie Kitroeff)**: 这会在体育博彩的运营方式中滋生出某些掠夺性的不良动机。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Natalie Kitroeff**: And that can create some predatory incentives in how sports books are run.

</details>

**大卫·亚菲-贝拉尼 (David Yaffe-Bellany)**: 但预测市场的架构完全不同。它的设计更像是一个**金融交易所**（financial exchange）：在每一个赌约的两端，坐着的都是不同的真实用户。我并不是在与庄家博弈，我是在与平台上的另一个下注者博弈。如果我出 5 美元押费城老鹰队赢，那么就会有另一个人出 5 美元押芝加哥熊队赢。因此，它是一种点对点（P2P）的互动模式，而不是由一个靠你输钱来盈利的中心化庄家来坐庄。此外，平台只收取交易佣金手续费，无论你是赢是输，平台都只拿固定抽成。所以平台强调：你的输赢与我们的利益并不直接挂钩。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**David Yaffe-Bellany**: The markets are structured differently. They're structured more like a financial exchange where on each side of a bet, you've got a different user. So, I'm not vying against the house, I'm vying against somebody else who's also betting on the platform. So, if I bet $5 that the Eagles will win, someone else is betting $5 that the Bears will win. And so, it's a peer-to-peer interaction as opposed to one that's run by this kind of centralized house that benefits when you lose. And also, the markets take transaction fees.
