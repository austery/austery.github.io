**克洛伊·莎莎·布鲁克斯**: 在你的众多热门设计作品中，你让 **Crocs** 变得极具潮流感。这款鞋被称为 **Crocs Pollex clog**，它是基于你自己的指纹倒模打造的。而我特别喜欢的一点是，起初是 Crocs 主动联系你，询问是否可以给你寄几双鞋，而你却反问：“我们何不真正做一次联名合作呢？”他们答应了。那么，在他们同意之后，你是如何决定具体做成这样的呢？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**CSB**: Among many of your popular designs, you made Crocs cool. And this is called the Crocs Pollex clog, based on your own fingerprint mold. </details>

**萨莱希·本伯里**: 这是一次极大的荣幸，因为他们最初就想促成合作。后来，当我给他们发去一款我当时甚至不知道那是童鞋尺寸的 Crocs 样品时，他们告诉我，他们非常喜欢我对 **Crocs 形态（form）** 的重新思考。因为过去的许多合作者仅仅是在 Crocs 表面换个涂装配色（reskinning），或者加一些智必星（Jibbitz）鞋扣。

而我思考的是形态结构，这源于我的**工业设计背景**。一旦他们看到我对重构形态产生兴趣，他们就询问我是否愿意成为首位从零开始独立设计并创造一双全新 Crocs 的合作设计师。我当时立刻做了一个嘻哈歌手 Birdman 式的兴奋搓手动作，毫不犹豫地答应了。

（观众笑声）

眼前这双鞋就是那次合作的成果。最初，我必须确保自己保留并延续 Crocs 现有的品牌 DNA 元素，确保不会吓跑现有的消费群体；同时，能够与 Crocs 这样的品牌合作本身就是一种荣誉，你得以融入他们的历史传承和品牌基因。因此，我既要维护这些核心底色，又要引入我自己的品牌识别度，将其置于一个全新的视角之下，并借此触达潜在的新受众。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**SB**: It was a great honor because they first wanted to collaborate. And then when I sent them what I didn’t know was a kid’s Croc shoe, they let me know that they loved that I was thinking of the form of the Croc, which a lot of collaborators in the past were simply reskinning the Croc, maybe adding jibbitz. And I was thinking of the form, and that comes from my industrial-design background.

Once they saw that I was interested in manipulating the form, they asked me if I wanted to be the first collaborative designer to create a Croc from scratch, which I immediately did a Birdman hand rub and said yes.

(Laughter)

And so this is the result of that. Initially I had to make sure that I fostered and maintained the existing elements of the Croc DNA and made sure that I didn't scare away the existing consumer and getting the honor of working with a brand like Crocs, you get to work with their heritage and their DNA. So I had to make sure to maintain that and then also introduce my own brand identity, put it through a new lens, and potentially speak to a new audience.

</details>

### 从沙发失业到入主Versace

**克洛伊·莎莎·布鲁克斯**: 这真的很不可思议，因为作为一名球鞋设计师，你走过了一段令人惊叹的历程。你在 2009 年起步于 **Payless**，随后开始与众多知名品牌展开合作，比如 **New Balance**、**Cole Haan** 以及 **Versace** 等等。如今你还运营着自己的品牌 **Spunge**，也就是你脚上穿的这些。

为了让大家更清晰地了解你是如何开拓出属于自己的独特道路的，我很想请你分享一个我个人非常喜爱的故事：当时你正处于失业期，坐在自家沙发上吃着麦片，却不知怎地，通过在 **LinkedIn** 上浏览求职信息，最终成功说服了奢侈品牌 Versace 给你提供了一份梦寐以求的工作。你能为我们讲讲那个故事吗？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**CSB**: You know, it's pretty amazing because you've had a really amazing journey as a footwear designer. You started at Payless in 2009, and then you started doing collaborations with a lot of brands like New Balance, Cole Haan, Versace, to name a few. And now you run your own brand called Spunge, that's what those are.

So to help paint the picture of how you've forged your own path, I'd love for you to share the story that I just love, of you being on your couch, eating cereal in a moment of unemployment and you somehow went from scrolling on LinkedIn to convincing Versace to give you your dream job. Will you tell us that story?

</details>

**萨莱希·本伯里**: 坐在沙发上吃麦片，正是我当时所经历的失业状态的最生动写照。那时我正在利用所有能够用来对外建立联系的工具，包括 LinkedIn、Craigslist，当时甚至可能还用了 Facebook，给任何愿意接收信息的人发送自荐信。

我联系到了 Versace 的一位设计总监，向他提出了一个商业与设计机会。我对他说：运动鞋产业是一个价值数十亿美元的庞大市场，但在传统上，这类消费者并不怎么关注高级时装品牌，或者说他们传统上只关注我们都非常熟悉、甚至此刻大家脚上可能正穿着的那些传统球鞋品牌。

但随后发生了一场大规模的市场启蒙与认知普及——**Raf Simons** 与 **Adidas** 的联名、**Nike** 与 **Comme des Garçons** 的合作等，使得庞大的大众消费群体开始意识到，高级时装屋同样可以打造顶级球鞋。

于是我向他们发出了自荐信。仅仅过了几天，**多娜泰拉·范思哲（Donatella Versace）** 本人就亲自回复了我。她说她非常喜欢我的想法。这照片出现的时机真是太完美了。

她说她希望我飞去米兰，当面向她阐述我认为 Versace 的球鞋业务体系可以做成什么样。几天后我飞到了米兰，向她做了一场结合了商品企划（merchandising）、工业设计与销售市场洞察的完整提案，她彻底爱上了这个方案。

她当时对我说：“我想雇佣你。我希望你立刻搬到米兰来。你愿意做设计顾问吗？”她对于合作形式给出了非常开放和包容的条件。正因为她如此开明，我便顺势提出请求：我能否在洛杉矶保留自己的独立设计工作室，每个月飞往米兰进行工厂视察和参与时装秀发布？她爽快地答应了。

于是我走出会场的大门，就像电影《当幸福来敲门》里的场景一样，在大街上激动得放声大哭——因为我的人生在那一瞬间被彻底改变了。但从本质上讲，这更多体现了**敢于开口请求的力量（the power of asking）**，以及这种勇气所能带给你的无限可能。

（观众掌声）

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**SB**: So, cereal on my couch, that is a description of the image of unemployment that I was experiencing at that time. I was using all of the tools that have been given to us for outreach, LinkedIn, Craigslist, maybe it was even Facebook at the time, and sending messages to whoever would accept them.

I reached out to a Versace design director, and I proposed an opportunity. I said, the sneaker industry is a multibillion-dollar business, and traditionally, that consumer isn't paying attention to fashion brands or traditionally paying attention to the sneaker brands that we're all used to and probably wearing right now.

But then there was this mass education that happened that Raf Simons, Adidas, Nike, Comme des Garçons, and now this massive consumer was aware of the fact that fashion houses can make sneakers. And so I reached out to them, a few days later, Donatella herself responded. She said she loved my ideas. Perfect timing for that photo. She said that she wanted me to come out to Milan and present to her what I thought the sneaker program could be.

I went out to Milan a few days later, presented a combination of merchandising and design and sales insight, and she loved it. And she was like, "I want to hire you. I want you to move to Milan right away. Do you want to consult?" She was really open about what the opportunity could be. Because she was so open, I asked if I could have my own design studio in Los Angeles and travel to Milan monthly for factory visits and shows and she said yes.

So I went outside and did my pursuit of happiness -- cry in the streets -- because my life changed instantly. But really what that was more about was the power of asking and what that can bring you.

(Applause)

</details>

### 用大自然与叙事凝聚社群

**克洛伊·莎莎·布鲁克斯**: 另外这段视频真的太迷人、太有魅力了。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**CSB**: And then this video is just so charming.

</details>

**萨莱希·本伯里**: 那是音乐人 **Thundercat**（史蒂文·布鲁纳）。那是为了与 New Balance 合作的一个名为 **"Sand be the Time"** 的项目拍摄的。我的很多项目都深受个人生活体验的启发。这次的灵感源自我的一次旅行——那是哪儿来着？那是犹他州的粉红沙丘（Coral Pink Sand Dunes）。那段自然风貌基本上构成了这双鞋的故事基石。

对于普通人来说，看这双鞋可能只觉得它是一双粉色的鞋子。但如果结合粉红沙丘的故事背景，并将雪尼尔面料（chenille）的质感与日落晚霞的色彩层次相呼应，你便培育出了一个认同这套美学的社群，你邀请人们走进了你的精神世界，并且完成了一次生动的叙事。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**SB**: That is Thundercat, that's Steven. That was for a New Balance project called "Sand be the Time." A lot of the projects are influenced by experiences. So this was from a trip -- Where was that? That was the pink sand dunes in Utah. And that basically was the foundation of the story for that shoe.

So the average person looking at that shoe might see a pink shoe. But then with the story of the pink sand dunes and relating the execution of the chenille into the colors of the sunset. Now you’ve cultivated a community, you’ve invited people to come into your world and you've told a story.

</details>

**克洛伊·莎莎·布鲁克斯**: 你后来变得非常热爱在大自然中徒步。你开始设计户外鞋履和装备。例如在一次创作中，你将亚利桑那州的羚羊峡谷（Antelope Canyon）转化成了一款名为 **"Peace Be the Journey"** 的联名鞋款，于 2020 年正式发布。尽管那双鞋已经推出了好几年，但至今依然热度不减，甚至直到今天在 **eBay** 等二级转售网站上，依然能稳定以极高的溢价售出。跟我们聊聊，为什么你认为这双鞋能保持如此长久的影响力与吸引力？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**CSB**: You got really into hiking in nature. You started designing outdoor shoes and gear, and in one instance you translated Arizona's Antelope Canyon into a shoe called Peace Be the Journey, which came out in 2020. And that shoe, even though it came out six years ago, is still so popular that it's being sold for markup on resale sites like eBay like, to this day, consistently, for a lot of money. So tell us why you think this shoe is still so popular.

</details>

**萨莱希·本伯里**: 或许是因为我讲述了一个能够让人们产生深刻共鸣的故事。这双鞋的灵感来源于我前往羚羊峡谷的一次旅行——对于那些还没去过的人来说，那真是一个美轮美奂的地方。我的终极目标就是让这双鞋成为那段震撼体验的缩影。

所以当你第一眼看这双鞋时，它是一双橙色的鞋。但随后我将这种橙色与羚羊峡谷岩石的色调相连接，蓝色的“N”字标识代表着湛蓝的天空，而栗色/紫红色调则呈现出落日余晖的渐变。再加上宣传片进一步将这个故事娓娓道来。

如今当人们穿上这双鞋时，当然，他们可能首先觉得这双鞋外形很酷，但随之而来的是，他们会感觉自己属于某个有着共同审美与体验的文化社群。

我记得当我最初为了工作前往中国出差时，我总是会在人群中搜寻穿着 **Supreme** 的人，因为我直觉认为他们大概率会说英语。所以很多时候，视觉符号会成为辨识和连接特定社群的密码。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**SB**: Potentially that I told a story that people could resonate with, this was influenced by a trip to Antelope Canyon, a beautiful place, for those that haven't been. And ultimately the goal was to make this shoe represent that experience.
