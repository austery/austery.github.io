### 政府报告揭露AI聊天机器人的安全风险

<summary>Original English</summary>
[music] Now another critical tech development. This morning, a government investigation released [music] showing how AI chatbots could be used to plan mass casualty attacks. 这份来自政府问责署（GAO）的报告涉及ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini和Grok。其中两款模型生成的结果可用于制造或引爆简易爆炸装置（IED）。在另一项测试中，两款模型生成了有助于将电网作为攻击目标的信息。在某些测试环节中，它们还提供了实施袭击的日期与时间，以及最大限度增加破坏程度的指引。报告并未指明哪些模型通过或未通过具体测试，因为他们不希望其实验被复现。

<summary>Original English</summary>

And in another test, two models generated information that would help target the electrical grid. At some points, they supplied dates and times of when to carry out an attack and guides to maximize damage. The report didn't specify which models passed or failed the specific tests because they didn't want their experiments to be recreated.

### 美国失去麻疹消除状态

公共卫生领域遭遇重大挫折，美国已正式失去其麻疹消除状态。该状态由泛美卫生组织（PAHO）评定，该组织昨天宣布了这一令人担忧的节点。其官方判定标准是该高传染性病毒在一个国家持续传播超过一年且未被中断。在自2025年1月开始的美国近期疫情中，已有10人死亡，数百人住院，其中许多是儿童。
<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>
In a stunning setback for public health, the US has officially lost its measles elimination status. That status is decided by the Pan American Health Organization, which announced the ominous milestone yesterday. Its official metric is when the highly contagious virus has been spreading in a country for more than a year uninterrupted. Over the course of the recent US outbreaks, which started in January 2025, 10 people have died and hundreds of people have been hospitalized, many of them children.
</details>

>> 一些批评人士表示，失去麻疹消除状态是对罗伯特·F·肯尼迪二世（Robert F. Kennedy Jr.）及其卫生政策的一次公投，因为麻疹疫情最严重的两年恰好与他担任卫生部长的任期完全重合。
<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>
>> Some critics say that the loss of the measles elimination status is a referendum on Robert F. Kennedy Jr. and his health policies because the two worst years of measles outbreaks have coincided precisely with his tenure as health secretary.
</details>

>> 我的同事阿普瓦·曼达维利（Apoorva Mandavilli）负责报道公共卫生领域。她说，肯尼迪有时会嘲讽麻疹的威胁，推广未经验证的治疗方法，并削弱公众对儿童疫苗的信心。
<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>
>> My colleague Apoorva Mandavilli reports on public health. She says Kennedy has at times mocked the threat of measles, promoted unproven treatments, and undermined confidence in childhood vaccines.
</details>

>> 肯尼迪先生指出其他国家也失去了麻疹消除状态，在这点上他说得没错。但我交流过的专家指出，疾控中心（CDC）采取的措施，远没有达到人们在面临此类疫情时通常所期望的程度。在这样的情况下，你本会预期看到CDC主任频繁且大声疾呼接种疫苗的重要性，并且会预期看到向社区开展宣传以提高接种率，但我们极少看到这些行动。如果说有什么动作的话，那就是CDC一直在与某些州就如何定义麻疹死亡病例发生争执，并且尚未完全承认各州报告的所有死亡病例。
<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>
>> Mr. Kennedy has pointed out that other countries have also lost their measles elimination status, and he is right about that. But the experts I talk to point out that the CDC is not taking anywhere near the kind of measures you would normally expect it to when there are outbreaks like this. In a situation like this, you would expect to see the CDC director talking often and loudly about the importance of vaccination, and you would expect to see outreaches to communities to increase vaccinations, but we've seen very little of that. If anything, the CDC has been engaging in fights with some states over how to define a measles death and has not yet fully acknowledged all of the deaths that states have reported.

>> 阿普瓦表示，据公共卫生专家称，如果美国想要控制住麻疹，就必须回归基本面。这意味着要恢复疫苗接种率，使麻疹检测更加快捷简便，并摸索如何改善对该疾病的治疗。麻疹在很长一段时间内都非常罕见，以至于许多医生没有诊治经验，因此医院和医疗机构目前正在紧急培训其工作人员。

<summary>Original English</summary>

Measles has been so rare for so long that many doctors have no experience with it, so hospitals and medical organizations are now scrambling to train their staff.
