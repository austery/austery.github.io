### “美国湖”风波与地名政治学

**唐纳德·特朗普 (Donald Trump)**: 我们将立即把**安大略湖 (Lake Ontario)** 的名字更改为**美国湖 (Lake America)**。

<summary>Original English</summary>

**简·奥尔登 (Jan Alden)**: 不出所料，安大略省人根本不打算配合。省长**道格·福特 (Doug Ford)** 更是直截了当地表达了反对。那么，特朗普改名的举动真的重要吗？这一切对我们与美国的关系究竟意味着什么？我们将深入探讨美加关系到底发生了多大改变，以及它是否还有可能恢复。我是简·奥尔登，欢迎收听《The Rundown》。

**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**: 这是特朗普正在使用的更广泛策略的一部分，某种程度上是为了重塑我们对现实的认知。

我叫**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**，是**维多利亚大学 (University of Victoria)** 的地理学教授，我的研究方向是地名政治学和文化景观研究。

在美国，地理实体命名的常规流程是：存在一个**美国地名委员会 (US Board on Geographic Names)**。他们设有国内地名委员会和国外地名委员会，专门审议提案。事实上，任何人都可以向美国地名委员会提交提案。该提案会在多个层级经历广泛的咨询程序。如果是跨国地理实体，提案随后还会提交给相关国家的地名委员会——在这种情况下也就是**加拿大地名委员会 (Geographical Names Board of Canada)**。我们在2009年**萨利希海 (Salish Sea)** 命名时就看到了这种情况，当时两国在州级、省会及联邦层面展开合作。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jan Alden**: No surprise, Ontarians aren't willing to play along. Premier Doug Ford literally spelled it out. So, does Trump's name change matter? And what does all this mean for our relationship with the states? We dig into how much it's changed and whether it'll ever recover? I'm Jan Alden. Welcome to the rundown.

**Ruben Rose-Redwood**: This is part of a broader playbook that Trump is using to kind of reshape our sense of reality in a sense. So my name is Ruben Rose-Redwood and I'm a professor of geography at the University of Victoria and my research looks at the politics of place naming and cultural landscape studies. The conventional process of renaming geographic places in the United States is that there's the US Board on Geographic Names. They have a domestic and a foreign names committees and they consider proposals. Anyone in fact can submit a proposal to the US Board on Geographic Names. It goes through an extensive consultation process at multiple levels. If it's an international geographic feature, it would then go to the geographic names board in this case of Canada as well. We saw this with the naming of the Salish Sea back in 2009 where the two countries worked together both at state, provincial and federal levels.

</details>

**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**: 没错。谷歌和苹果都在运营各自的地图应用程序，他们都采纳了“美国湖”这一命名。在美国境内，特朗普有权直接向内政部长下达行政命令，要求其指示地名委员会更改联邦地图上的名称。然而，在国际层面，这一更名是不具有法律效力的。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Ruben Rose-Redwood**: Right. So both Google and Apple, they run their different map applications and they both adopted this Lake America naming. In the United States, Trump does have the authority through executive order to direct the Secretary of the Interior to direct the Board on Geographic Names to make a name change on federal maps. However, internationally, that name change has no standing.

</details>

**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**: 是的。正如我在撰写的文章中所指出的那样，我认为这与将墨西哥湾更名为“美洲湾”并没有本质区别。这是一种地缘政治的挑衅姿态。然而，如果这种改名被大型科技公司制度化，并且在日常地图导航中被数亿人使用，它就会逐渐渗透并开始改变大众的日常空间感知。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Ruben Rose-Redwood**: Yes. And so as I said in the article that I wrote, you know, I don't think this is more or less offensive than the change in name to the Gulf of America. It is a geopolitical provocation. But if it gets institutionalized by big tech and used by hundreds of millions of people on a daily basis in mapping apps, it starts to shift people's everyday spatial perception.

</details>

**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**: 为什么呢？因为在过去18个月里，我们本有充分的时间来认清一个事实：唐纳德·特朗普显然在利用加拿大对美国数字基础设施和服务器的依赖。自从贸易冲突爆发以来，科技巨头迅速顺从白宫意愿的举动，暴露了我们在关键数字主权上的巨大脆弱性。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Ruben Rose-Redwood**: Why? Because you've had 18 months to deal with the fact that Donald Trump is very clearly abusing their dependence on this US server. Since the trade disputes began, the speed with which tech giants comply with White House directives exposes our critical vulnerabilities in digital sovereignty.

</details>

**鲁本·罗斯-雷德伍德 (Ruben Rose-Redwood)**: 没错。在历史上，这种通过看似琐碎或荒谬的象征性争端，揭示出深层实质性危机的先例屡见不鲜。表面上是在争夺一个湖泊的名字，但实质上暴露的是两国地缘政治权力的不对称以及关键基础设施的控制权争夺。

<details>
