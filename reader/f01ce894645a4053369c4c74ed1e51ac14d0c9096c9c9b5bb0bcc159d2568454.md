### 名誉团长之职与百年勋章传承

**主持人**: 伍冰枝女士，您自2007年起就一直担任该步兵团的名誉团长。我注意到您今天也佩戴了该步兵团的徽章胸针。能请您为我们讲述一下您与这支步兵团之间的深厚渊源吗？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: Madame Clarkson, you have been the colonel-in-chief since 2007, and I see that you have the infantry's pin on as well. Talk us through your relationship with this infantry.

</details>

**伍冰枝**: 好的。我对这枚胸针感到无比自豪，正如你们在这里看到的，它镶嵌着钻石，是帕特里夏公爵夫人轻步兵团的团徽。

而这枚胸针本身有着极其厚重的历史——它的筹款，是由帕特里夏公爵夫人团的第一批官兵在第一次世界大战的战地前线自发发起的。官兵们将这枚胸针赠送给了**帕特里夏公主**（Princess Patricia）。公主后来嫁给了平民**艾伦·拉姆齐**爵士（Sir Alan Ramsay），由此成为帕特里夏夫人（Lady Patricia）。在当年，她曾立下誓言，在战争彻底结束、国家赢得胜利之前绝不成婚。当胜利到来、她大婚之时，官兵们便将这枚胸针作为新婚礼物赠予了她。

后来，这枚珍贵的胸针传给了帕特里夏夫人的教女——**缅甸蒙巴顿女伯爵**（Countess Mountbatten of Burma），而蒙巴顿女伯爵最终又将它郑重交到了我的手中。因此我一直无比珍惜它。这是一枚非同寻常、承载着荣耀的胸针，我深以为傲。在出席团部重大活动，或是任何与我们步兵团相关的场合时，我都一定会佩戴它。

我担任这支步兵团的名誉团长已有近二十年之久，与帕特里夏团建立了极为紧密的联结。在我眼中，他们不仅秉承着一贯的作风“率先奔赴前线”，更是一支极其出色的卓越部队。在第二次世界大战期间，该团忠诚的埃德蒙顿部队（Loyal Edmonton Regiment）是由我丈夫的教父**罗文·科尔曼**（Rowan Coleman）亲自指挥的。因此，我与帕特里夏团之间有着极深的宿缘。这种联系既是职业层面的——在专业军事层面上我对他们怀有极大的钦佩与敬意；同时，这更是一份深厚而真挚的个人情感。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Adrienne Clarkson**: Well, I'm very proud of this pin, which you can see here. It's diamonds, and it's the crest of the Princess Patricia's. And this pin, this very pin, the money for it was raised by Princess Patricia's first in the field in the First World War and given to Princess Patricia, who became Lady Patricia on her marriage to the commoner Sir Alan Ramsay. She vowed that she wouldn't marry till the war was over and that they had won. And then she got this pin from them as her wedding present.

That pin was passed on to Lady Patricia, Countess Mountbatten, and she gave it to me. So I treasure it, and it's quite a wonderful pin, and I'm very proud of it. I always wear it to regimental occasions and anything having to do with our regiment. And I've been colonel-in-chief for 20 years and have a very close relationship with the Princess Patricia's, whom I think are not only first in the field as they always have been, but are a wonderful, wonderful regiment with the Loyal Edmontons, of whom were commanded by my husband's godfather Rowan Coleman in the Second World War. So I have a great connection with the Patricia's and it is both professional—I admire them enormously professionally—and it's also very personal.

</details>

### 军队奉献的真谛与步兵团的历史荣光

**主持人**: 当我们谈论起这样一场重大的灾难时，这对整个加拿大而言究竟意味着什么？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: What does something like this mean for Canada when we talk about a tragedy like this?

</details>

**伍冰枝**: 我认为这包含着两层极其深刻的含义。第一层，是国家必须在官方层面上，向那些随时准备为国家献出宝贵生命的英雄们致以最高敬意的哀悼。事实上，他们当时正处在战术演练之中，却突然遭遇了火灾——虽然具体的事故细节我们尚未完全掌握，但目前看来，他们是被一场在狂风中疯狂蔓延失控的草原灌木野火所围困。

我认为至关重要的是，我们整个国家必须去深思：我们武装部队的官兵们，在日常生活中付出了多少无私奉献的牺牲。他们打从心底明白，自己肩负着保家卫国、捍卫所有国民的使命。

帕特里夏公爵夫人步兵团的座右铭就是“率先奔赴战场”（*First in the Field*）。在历史上的每一个关头，他们永远都是率先挺身而出、奔赴前线。这支步兵团最初是由蒙特利尔著名企业家**汉密尔顿·戈尔特**（**Hamilton Gault**）出资创立的。在战争爆发之初，他向全国发出号召，短短数周之内，热血男儿便从全国各地纷至沓来，汇聚在一起，组成了这支誓要在战场上一马当先的帕特里夏步兵团。

而维多利亚女王的曾孙女、时任加拿大总督的千金帕特里夏公主本人，亲手为步兵团缝制了战旗，士兵们就是扛着这面旗帜奔赴沙场。这一切都赋予了这支部队极其浪漫而厚重的历史传奇色彩。后来，加拿大甚至发行了印有她肖像的一元面值纸币，这枚具有象征意义的一元纸币至今仍装裱在我的办公室墙上。

不仅如此，帕特里夏公主生前还是一位极具天赋的水彩画家。在其父亲担任加拿大总督期间，她在渥太华总督府周围绘制了大量精美的水彩画作。在我担任总督期间，我们还特意将这些画作用作了总督府宴会菜单的封面。

所以对我而言，帕特里夏公主是一个鲜活而真实的存在。再通过她的教女帕特里夏夫人，我与这支军团之间建立起了完整而牢固的传承与纽带。在历史上，唯有极少数的人曾荣膺担任这支步兵团的名誉团长，能够成为其中一员并履行这项职责，我感到无上的荣幸。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Adrienne Clarkson**: Well, I think it's two things. One is it's mourning officially for people who are willing to give their lives for their country. The fact that they were on a training exercise, that they were caught—and we don't know the details, but it seems that they were caught in what would be a prairie brush fire that just took off with the wind. And I think it's important that we think how much of the lives of our armed forces are given in a selfless kind of way. They know that they are defending us.

And the Patricia's motto is "first in the field." They always have been first in the field. They were founded by Hamilton Gault, a wonderful Montreal businessman who put out a call, and several weeks people came from all over the country to be first in the field in the Princess Patricia's. And Princess Patricia herself, the daughter of the Governor General, the great-granddaughter of Queen Victoria, sewed their battle colors and they went into the field with them. It's all a very kind of romantic and historic occasion.

And then, of course, a dollar bill was founded in her name, and I have that dollar bill framed in my office here. And she was a wonderful watercolorist, and while her father was the Governor General, she did a lot of watercolors of the area around Ottawa of the house, and we used them for menu covers when I was Governor General. So Princess Patricia is a very real person to me, and through her goddaughter Lady Patricia, I was really very happy to have that complete connection. So they've only had those very few people to be the colonels-in-chief, and I'm very happy to be that today.

</details>

### 从阿富汗友军误击到今天：国家元首与军人的生死共情

**主持人**: 伍冰枝女士，遗憾的是，您对重大的军事悲剧并不陌生。早在2002年4月您担任加拿大总督期间，加拿大官兵就在阿富汗战场的一起美军战机误击事件（友军误伤，Friendly Fire）中不幸遇难。在遭遇这样的至暗悲剧时刻，您究竟该如何去抚慰整个国家的创伤？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: Now, Madame Clarkson, unfortunately, you're not a stranger to tragedy. In April 2002, while you were Governor General, a group of Canadian soldiers were killed during an accidental friendly fire in Afghanistan. How do you comfort a nation during a time of tragedy like this?

</details>

**伍冰枝**: 首先，你必须坚定地告诉所有人：**他们的牺牲绝不是徒劳的**。

你必须让大家明白，这些军人从自愿签约入伍的那一刻起，就做好了随时舍生忘死、挺身而出保卫他人的准备。在2000年代初期的阿富汗战场上，我们的官兵不幸倒在友军误射的火力之下，那是一场无可挽回的悲剧，是一场极其惨痛的误判事故，在场所有人对此都心知肚明。

我永远无法忘记当年我登上大力神运输机（C-130 Hercules）前往德国的情景。我飞抵德国，走入大力神运输机的机舱，探视那些大难不死的幸存伤员。第二天，我赶赴位于拉姆施泰因（Ramstein）的美军医院，紧紧握住受伤官兵们的手，给他们带去大量的杂志书籍和各类慰问物品，希望能让他们稍微好受一些。

在后来的岁月里，我看着当年重伤的官兵中，有人哪怕历经创痛，伤愈后毅然选择重返军营、再次服役。这让我极其深刻地看到了他们对国家的无限忠诚，看到了他们身上那股坚韧不拔的复原力。他们极其透彻地理解：自己所从事的事业绝非一时兴起的短暂停留，而是一份托付终生、跨越生死的崇高誓约。他们或许找不出华丽的词藻来表达这种信念，而我们作为旁人也无需苛求他们诉诸言语；我们心中唯有一份肃穆与敬畏：因为我们深知，他们是在替我们所有人直面危险，替我们整个国家负重前行。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Adrienne Clarkson**: Well, first of all, you have to say that this is not in vain. You have to say that these people have signed up to put their lives on the line to save others. The fact that our people were in friendly fire on that particular occasion in the early 2000s was a tragedy and a mistake, and everybody realized that.

And I will always remember going into the Hercules in Germany. I flew to Germany and walked into the Hercules plane and saw our people who had survived. And then the next day I saw them at the hospital in Ramstein and held their hands and brought them lots of magazines and things that they would enjoy, and have basically seen some of them who joined up again even though they had been injured at that time. And it showed to me their devotion and their ability to recover, their ability to understand that what they do is not just for the moment, but it's forever. They don't have the words for that, nor do we worry about whether they have the words for that. All we think of is that they are doing it for us and in our place.

</details>

### 总督职责的本质：亲历现场与血脉传承

**主持人**: 加拿大总督的角色在外界看来往往偏向于礼仪性质。我非常希望您能为我们的电视观众和广播听众阐明：当这种不可承受之重的悲剧袭来时，加拿大总督究竟扮演着怎样的核心角色？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Host**: Now, the role of a Governor General is mostly ceremonial, and I'm hoping you can help us understand for our viewers and listeners, when tragedy hits like this, what is the role of the Governor General?

</details>

**伍冰枝**: 我认为，当人们使用“礼仪性”（Ceremonial）这个词时，在听者的感觉里，似乎总督的位置是高高在上、与基层保持距离的，仿佛置身于另一个遥远的世界，永远不需要同苦难发生实质接触。

但事实绝非如此，完全不是这样。在我的人生中，最刻骨铭心的记忆，正是当年登上那架大力神运输机探望我们的负伤官兵；是次日赶赴拉姆施泰因军事医院，抚摸着他们的手臂，与他们面对面推心置腹地长谈。这种面对面、直抵人心的直接关切与接触，正是作为一名名誉团长必须去履行的神圣使命；而对于总督而言，作为全体加拿大武装部队的总司令（Commander-in-Chief），更必须时刻准备好走向前线、践行这种关怀。

我之所以对这份职责有着充分的思想准备，是因为我从小就在这样的军人家庭记忆中长大。我的父亲在二战保卫香港战役中曾荣获英国军功奖章（Military Medal）。他当时作为华裔为英军效力，担任战地通信传令兵（Dispatch Rider）。因此，我是在极具使命感的军事传统熏陶下成长起来的。

在我的成长观念中，军人在国家遭受攻击时毅然奔赴沙场保家卫国，是天经地义、不容置疑的职责所在，历史向来如此。我的父亲生前对军人的这份荣誉与牺牲始终秉持着极度坚定的信念。二十年前他去世时，我将他生前获得的全部军功勋章捐赠给了加拿大战争博物馆。

正因为我伴随着这样的记忆和理解成长，在当年阿富汗友军误击事故发生时，我父亲尚在人世，他完全明白军人走上战场意味着什么，我也完全理解这其中的重大代价。我深知，每一位穿上军装的军人，即便最终没有付出生命的终极代价——就像艾登（Aiden）、肖恩（Shawn）、海登（Hayden）和摩根（Morgan）在此次事故中所不幸遭遇的那样——但他们每一个人在出发前，都已在灵魂深处做好了随时为国捐躯的准备。

这其中包含着两个层面：一个是下定决心时刻准备赴汤蹈火；另一个则是当厄运降临，在现实中真正做出了惨烈的牺牲。我认为我们加拿大军队之所以无比受人尊敬，正是因为每一名官兵都有着这种随时赴难的觉悟。我被他们的崇高精神深深震撼，我将自己身为帕特里夏团名誉团长的身份视作一生的至宝。这支部队拥有无比辉煌的历史，我珍视与官兵们相处的每一个瞬间。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Adrienne Clarkson**: I think when you say ceremonial, you make it sound as though it were arms length, or it was in another place, or it was far away, or you never come in contact with it. That's not the case at all.

My fondest memories are of going into that Hercules and seeing our wounded, and then the next day going to the hospital in Ramstein and touching their arms and being able to speak to them. And always that personal, direct contact is what I think a colonel-in-chief has to do. And certainly the Governor General, as the Commander-in-Chief of all the armed forces, should be prepared to always do that.

I'm very well prepared for it because my father has the Military Medal from Hong Kong. He was a Chinese who worked for the British and was a dispatch rider. And so I've been brought up in a military tradition. It's not to me to question whether somebody would go to war when they are attacked—that just happened. And my father was always very, very keen on that. And I gave his medals to the War Museum when he died 20 years ago.

And to me, I've been brought up with that memory and also with that understanding. And he was still alive when the friendly fire thing happened, and so he understood perfectly what it was about and I understood what it was about. I realize that soldiers, even if they don't make the ultimate sacrifice—which Aiden and Shawn and Hayden and Morgan have—they are prepared to do it.

And so there's two things: there's being prepared to do it, and then perhaps actually doing it. And I think that what we have in our forces is quite wonderful because our people are prepared for it. I'm deeply impressed by them and I treasure my role as colonel-in-chief of the Patricia's. They have a beautiful history and I love all the moments I spend with them.

</details>

<details>
