### Uber：预算四个月用完
2026年4月，Uber的首席技术官悄悄承认：公司已经烧光了全年的AI预算——整个年度AI预算，到4月就没了，12个月的预算四个月就花完了。细节更糟：Uber在2025年12月向工程团队推出了Anthropic的AI编程工具Claude Code。到3月，在公司5,000名工程师中，采用率从32%跃升至84%。其中95%的工程师每月都在使用AI工具，70%的提交代码由AI生成。除薪资之外，单个工程师每月的AI使用成本在500至2,000美元之间。而这并非偶然：Uber主动做了激励。公司建立了内部排行榜，按AI工具使用量给工程团队排名。消耗越多，排名越高；排名越高，越显得出色。12个月的预算四个月就烧光，是因为公司建了一个奖励花钱的系统。这相当于给每个员工发一张公司信用卡，再搞个花钱最多者的排行榜，账单来了却假装惊讶。我是ML，计算机科学博士，我分析AI的发展，以看清炒作背后真正发生的事。现在真正发生的是：AI行业出现了严重的成本问题，连卖这项技术的公司都承认账算不过来。但Uber只是开始。在这个视频里，我会讲Meta如何把AI使用变成竞技运动，微软为什么砍掉自家工程师偏爱的工具，以及Nvidia的CEO和他自己的一位副总裁为何在提出相反的论点。然后我会说说我认为这些算力真正应该去哪里，因为现在整个行业都把它指向了错误的方向。朋友们，这就是荒诞的九层地狱，我们乐在其中。

### 先解释token
在讲发生了什么之前，我需要先解释一个概念，让后面的内容更容易理解，最多15秒。token是AI模型处理的基本数据单位，大致相当于一个词，有时是一个词的一部分。每次你使用AI工具——问问题、让它写代码、运行AI智能体——你都在消耗token，而token是要花钱的。任务越复杂，消耗的token越多。简单的聊天机器人交流可能只用几千个token。执行多步骤编码任务的AI智能体可能烧掉数百万个。

<summary>Original English</summary>

Before I get into what happened, I need to explain one concept that makes the rest of the story legible super quickly, just like 15 seconds tops. A simple chatbot exchange might use a few thousand tokens. An AI agent performing a multi-step coding task can burn millions.

### 单价下降，总量暴增
有意思的地方来了：单个token的成本正在快速下降。Gartner最近的一份报告发现，到2030年，使用前沿AI模型的实际成本——给它发一个提示、拿回结果，顺便说，这也叫运行推理——对提供商来说将比2025年低90%以上。这听起来是好消息，但并不是，因为智能体式AI模型——不只是回答问题，而是自主去做事的那种，比如跨多个文件写代码、订机票，或者在没有人指导每一步的情况下管理工作流——每个任务需要的token是标准聊天机器人的5到30倍。所以单价下降，但单位数量暴增。高盛预测，到2030年，智能体式AI可能推动token消耗增长24倍，达到每月处理120千万亿个token。这是一个众所周知的经济规律，叫杰文斯悖论：当一种资源的使用效率提高时，人们不会少用，而是会多用得多。我在之前的视频里详细讲过这个概念，当时把它用在AI部署与人类就业的关系上。同样的悖论适用于此，只是被消耗的资源不再是工作岗位，而是算力。更便宜的token并不意味着更便宜的AI，而是意味着更多的token。正如Gartner高级总监分析师Will Summer所说，首席产品官不应把商品化token的通缩与前沿推理的民主化混为一谈。这句话承载了非同寻常的分量，我怀疑最需要听到它的人并不在读Gartner的报告。哦，顺便说一下，我刚刚开通了频道会员。视频永远免费。会员和咖啡只是一种支持这项工作的方式，如果你愿意并且有能力的话。没有压力，没有工资，也不会错过什么。链接在下面。

### Meta的“Clodonomics”与Amazon的token最大化

### 把投入当作产出
我想在这里停一下，点明这是什么，因为我认为它的荒诞可能掩盖了分析要点。这些公司在衡量投入——你消耗了多少token——并把它当作产出——你创造了多少价值——的代理指标，而这两者之间没有任何必然关系。这就像奖励销售团队，不是看卖出多少房子，而是看他们开车去开会烧了多少汽油。最佳表现者不是成交最多的人，而是开了最多英里的人。排行榜追踪的是燃料消耗，但没有人在统计收入。作为一个有计算机科学博士学位、在这个领域工作了十多年的人，我可以告诉你，这种模式并不新鲜。工程师们——我是带着很多爱说这话的，因为我也是其中一员——往往会如此迷恋一个系统的复杂性，以至于常常忘了这个系统本来该做什么。这有点像当艺术家。你希望代码漂亮，希望架构优雅，希望文档一尘不染，这些都有价值。在有人来找我麻烦之前我先说，我不是说它们没有价值，但它们次于软件的根本目的：解决问题，把事情做成，能用。token最大化是同一种冲动的组织版本。它在为一个感觉重要的指标做优化——消耗、活动、使用量——却完全脱离了这些消耗是否产生了相应价值这个问题。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

I want to pause here and name what this is because I think the absurdity of it can obscure the analytical point. These companies are measuring input, how many tokens are you consuming, as a proxy for output, how much value are you creating, and those two things have no guaranteed relationship. This is like rewarding your sales team not for how many houses they sold, but for how much petrol they burned driving to meetings. The top performer isn't the one who closed the most deals, is the one who drove the most miles. The leaderboard tracks fuel consumption, but nobody's striking revenue. As somebody with a PhD in computer science who has worked in this field for over a decade now, I can tell you this pattern is not exactly new. Engineers, and I say this with a lot of love because I am one, have a tendency to get so enamored with the complexity of a system that they often lose sight of what the system is supposed to do. It's almost like being an artist. You want the code to be beautiful, you want the architecture to be elegant, you want the documentation to be pristine, and all of those things have value. I'm not saying they don't before somebody comes at me, but they are secondary to the fundamental purpose of software, which is to solve a problem, to do the thing, to work. Token maxing is the organizational version of that same impulse. It's optimizing for a metric that feels important, consumption, activity, usage, while completely decoupling from the question of whether any of that consumption is producing proportional value.

</details>

### 微软：砍掉工程师偏爱的工具
与此同时，微软也在应对它自己版本的这个问题，细节揭示的东西略有不同，但同样有启发。2025年12月，微软在其自家的GitHub Copilot CLI之外，开放了对Anthropic的Claude Code的访问。两者都是在开发者终端中运行、能编写、编辑和管理代码的AI编码工具。大约6个月里，微软工程师并排使用这两个工具。工程师们更喜欢Claude Code。据报道，内部对比显示它在真实工程任务上优于Copilot CLI。正如The Verge的Tom Warren所说，它变得非常受欢迎，也许有点太受欢迎了。5月，微软开始取消Claude Code许可。Experiences and Devices部门——负责Windows、Microsoft 365、Outlook、Teams和Surface的团队——的工程师被要求在6月30日前把Claude Code从工作流中移除，改用Copilot CLI。官方理由是工具链统一。而时间点——6月30日，微软财年的最后一天——表明削减成本至少同样重要。账单差异的具体细节非常技术化。如果你希望我对按席位计费与按API计费的定价及其差别为何重要做一个完整拆解，我可以另做一个视频。但简短版本基本是：运行Claude Code意味着要向Anthropic同时支付每席位许可费和按API费率计算的token成本。把Claude的模型通过Copilot CLI（微软通过GitHub拥有）路由，就消除了许可层，把计费收归内部。它更便宜，钱也留在微软生态内。但成本的说法掩盖了更能说明问题的细节：微软做了一个6个月的实验，它自己的工程师选了竞争对手的产品，而微软的回应是砍掉竞争对手的产品，而不是改进自己的。这是一家公司把平台控制置于工程质量之上。这是否是正确的战略决策，可以讨论；但这是出于成本驱动的决定，则毋庸置疑。再看更广泛的时间点：GitHub Copilot同时在6月1日转向按使用量计费，从固定的高级请求次数转为使用AI credits的token消耗模式。所以，微软恰恰在那个平台切换到炸掉Uber预算的同一种按消耗计价模式的时刻，整合到单一平台上。成本问题没有被解决，只是被换了个标志重新组织。这就是企业层面AI成本危机的真实样子。它不是单一的戏剧性失败，而是上千个采购决策，每一个单独看都合理，合起来却揭示出一个花钱速度快于衡量回报能力的行业。

<summary>Original English</summary>

Meanwhile, Microsoft has been dealing with its own version of this problem, and the details reveal something slightly different but equally instructive. In December 2025, Microsoft opened up access to Anthropic's Claude Code alongside its own GitHub Copilot CLI. Both are AI coding tools that run in a developer's terminal and can write, edit, and manage code. For roughly 6 months, Microsoft engineers run both tools side by side. The engineers preferred Claude Code. Internal comparisons reportedly showed it outperforming Copilot CLI on real engineering tasks. It became, as The Verge's Tom Warren put it, very popular, perhaps a little too popular. In May, Microsoft began canceling Claude Code licenses. Engineers in the Experiences and Devices division, the teams behind Windows, Microsoft 365, Outlook, Teams, and Surface were given until June 30th to remove Claude Code from their workflows and switch to Copilot CLI. The official justification was tool chain unification. The timing, June 30th, the last day of Microsoft fiscal year, suggests cost-cutting was at least equally important. The specifics of the billing difference are very technical. If you'd like me to do a full breakdown of seat-based versus API-based pricing and why the distinction matters, I can make a separate video on that. But the short version is essentially, running cloud code means paying Anthropic both a per-seat license fee and API rate token cost on top. Routing Claude's models through Copilot CLI, which Microsoft owns via GitHub, eliminates the licensing layer and brings the billing in-house. It's cheaper and the money stays inside Microsoft ecosystem. But the cost story obscures the more revealing detail. Microsoft ran a 6-month experiment. Its own engineers chose the competitor's product, and Microsoft responded by killing the competitor's product rather than improving its own. That is a company optimizing for platform control over engineering quality. Whether that's the right strategic decision is debatable. That it's a cost-driven decision is not. And consider the timing more broadly. GitHub Copilot is simultaneously transitioning to usage-based billing on June 1st, moving from flat premium request counts to a token consumption model using AI credits. So, Microsoft is consolidating onto a single platform at exactly the moment that platform is switching to the same consumption-based pricing model that blew up Uber's budget. The cost problem is not being solved. It is being reorganized under a different logo. This is what the AI cost crisis actually looks like at the enterprise level. It is not a single dramatic failure. It is a thousand procurement decisions, each individually rational, that collectively reveal an industry spending faster than it can measure the returns.

### Nvidia：两种相反的说法
然后是Nvidia，这里的矛盾如此明显，几乎不需要评论。Nvidia的CEO黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）在2026年3月说，如果他公司里一位年薪50万美元的工程师没有至少消耗25万美元的AI token，他会深感警觉。他把这说成是生产力的必要条件：token让工程师更有价值。Nvidia应用深度学习副总裁Bryan Catanzaro对Axios说：“对我的团队来说，算力成本远远超过员工成本。”同一家公司，一位高管说多花在token上，另一位说token的成本已经超过了人。顺便说，两人都没错，我认为这正是大多数人在看到这种矛盾时错过的部分，他们以为肯定有人在撒谎、困惑或愚蠢。其实不是。他们的激励结构不同。黄仁勋是一家制造GPU的公司的CEO，而这些GPU处理着全世界所消耗的每一个token。消耗的token越多，需要的GPU越多，Nvidia的收入越多。他的激励是最大化需求。他当然希望工程师烧token。Catanzaro管理的是一份实际的工程预算。他的激励是在成本框架内交付成果。他当然会注意到算力账单超过了工资单。在生活中，这类情形里很少有好人和坏人，只有激励、目标和达成目标的路径。问题不在于黄仁勋错了或Catanzaro错了。问题在于，来自供应链顶端——从消耗中获利最多的公司——的“多用”信号，沿着整个行业一路向下传导。它传到Meta，变成Cloudonomics；传到Amazon，变成在琐碎任务上token最大化；传到Uber，变成在4个月里烧光一年预算的排行榜。消耗的信号源自卖出被消耗之物的人。而在这条链上，没有任何一环在系统性地衡量这些消耗是否产生了相应的价值。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

And then, there is Nvidia, where the contradiction is so clear it almost doesn't need commentary. Jensen Huang, Nvidia's CEO, said in March 2026 that he would be deeply alarmed if a $500,000 engineer at his company was not consuming at least $250,000 in AI tokens. He framed this as a productivity imperative. The tokens make the engineer more valuable. Bryan Catanzaro, Nvidia's vice president of applied deep learning, told Axios, "For my team, the cost of compute is far beyond the cost of the employees." Same company, one executive says spend more on tokens, the other says the tokens already cost more than the people. Neither of them is wrong, by the way, and I think that's the part that most people miss when they look at contradiction like this and assume somebody must be lying or confused or stupid. They're not. They have different incentive structures. Huang is the CEO of a company that manufactures the GPUs that process every token consumed everywhere in the world.
