## 投资驱动型增长模式

现在让我们回到我的研究结果，深入探究日本、韩国和中国的增长奇迹究竟是如何运作的。好吧，首先我们要说，韩国和中国的增长奇迹都深受日本增长奇迹的启发，而日本是通过经济学家迈克尔·佩蒂斯（Michael Pettis）所说的“投资驱动型增长模式”创造出其奇迹般增长的。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

Now let's get back into the results of my research. Digging into how the Japanese, Korean, and Chinese growth miracles actually worked. Okay, let's start by saying that both the Korean and Chinese growth miracles were heavily, heavily inspired by that of Japan, which produced its miracle growth through what economist Michael Pettis calls an investment led growth model.

</details>

简而言之，其理念是，像 19 世纪中叶的日本、20 世纪 60 年代的韩国和 20 世纪 80 年代的中国这样贫穷的农业经济体，之所以能够以奇迹般的速度增长，是因为尽管它们拥有受过相当良好教育的人口（即劳动力），但它们几乎没有现代基础设施和机器等实物资产可供这支受过相当良好教育的劳动力使用。因此，只要它们能够非常迅速地对实物资产进行投资、投资、再投资，它们当然就能以奇迹般的速度增长，至少在它们赶上发达经济体之前是这样。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

In a nutshell. The idea is that poor agricultural economies like Japan in the mid 19th century, Korea in the 1960s, and China in the 1980s could grow at miracle speeds because while they had a fairly well educated population, which is called labor, they had almost no physical assets like modern infrastructure and machines that this fairly well educated workforce could use. So if only they would be able to very rapidly invest, invest, invest in physical assets, then of course they should be able to grow at miracle speed, at least until they catch up with the advanced economies.

</details>

但当然，贫穷国家就是穷。所以即使它们的政府意识到投资实物资本是快速增长的最佳方式，它们通常也没有钱来为所有这些投资提供资金并以奇迹般的速度增长。这就把我们带到了大多数亚洲增长奇迹的肮脏秘密：它们严重操纵本国经济，将投资置于一切之上。你看，就像在战时可以操纵经济以将战争置于一切之上一样，在和平时期也可以操纵经济，将对实物资本的投资置于一切之上。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

But of course, bore countries are poor. So even if their governments realize that investments in physical capital are the best way to grow quickly, they typically do not have the money to finance all of those investments and grow at miracle speed. And this gets us to the dirty secret of most Asian growth miracles. They heavily manipulated their economies to prioritize investment above all else. You see, just like how in times of war, the economy can be manipulated to prioritize war above everything else, the economy can also be manipulated in peacetime to prioritize investments in physical capital above all else.

</details>

但它们究竟是怎么做到的呢？在日韩中投资增长模式中，最相关的四种操纵手段是：第一，迫使储户接受较低的利率，以使制造企业和政府的贷款更便宜。第二，迫使银行将贷款直接投向工厂和政府，并抑制向服务业或消费者等非优先部门发放贷款。第三，压低工资和劳工权利，使公司有更多的资金和动力去投资机器和其他实物资本。第四，优待出口商并使进口商处于不利地位，以获取宝贵的外汇，这些外汇随后可用于例如购买外国机器。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

Number two, forcing banks to direct loans to factories and governments and discourage loans to non prioritize sectors like service businesses or consumers. </details>

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

So what did these manipulations due to the economies of these nations? First and most importantly, as Japan, Korea and China have all shown, the investment led growth model essentially works. It is extraordinarily effective at creating miracle speed economic growth. But as you may expect, this manipulation comes with a lot of side effects, such as number two. It makes ordinary citizens less prosperous than they could have been in the short term. That is, by suppressing wages and interest rates on saving accounts. Ordinary people, of course, have less money to spend. But crucially, rapid economic growth does cause worker wages to rise substantially in the long run. And since in these countries are therefore typically quite happy with the trade

## 出口反弹与模式瓶颈

经济操纵、奇迹增长、投资驱动型增长以及高额贸易顺差——这些都是先是日本增长奇迹、接着是韩国奇迹、然后是中国奇迹的共同特征。然而，如果我们看看出口繁荣的副作用，就会看到日本、中国和韩国之间的第一个真正区别，那就是韩国是一个比日本和中国小得多的国家。因此，当它们的出口激增时，只有日本和中国的贸易操纵遭到了全球范围的强烈抵制，尤其是来自美国的抵制。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

Economic manipulation, miracle growth, investment led growth, and high trade surpluses. These are all things that characterize the Japanese growth miracle first, then the Korean miracle and then the Chinese miracle. Yet if we look at the export boom side effect, we will see the first real difference between Japan, China and Korea, which is that Korea is a significantly smaller country than Japan and China. And therefore, when their exports exploded, only Japanese and Chinese trade manipulations encountered a massive global backlash, especially from the United States.

</details>

就日本而言，这种抵制以 1985 年《广场协议》的形式出现，根据该协议，不仅日本——我必须说，还有德国和法国——同意让本国货币对美元升值，以使全球经济更加公平。有趣的是，中国官方媒体倾向于将《广场协议》描绘成日本停滞的原因。按照他们的逻辑，更坚挺的日元据称迫使日本央行降低利率以刺激经济，这随后催生了房地产泡沫，进而导致了日本化。但我认为，中国自身的经历表明这一诊断是错误的。尽管没有《广场协议》，中国最终也出现了巨大的房地产泡沫。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

In Japan's case, the backlash came in the form of the 1985 Plaza Accord, under which not just Japan, I have to say, but also Germany and France agreed to let the value of their currencies go up versus the dollar to make the global economy more fair. Interestingly, the Chinese state media tends to portray the Plaza Accord as the is the reason that Japan stagnated. According to their logic, a stronger yen supposedly forced the Bank of Japan to lower interest rates to stimulate the economy, which then created the property bubble which led to Japan. But I think China's own experience shows that this diagnosis was wrong. China also ended up with a massive property bubble despite having no Plaza Court.

</details>

因此，与其归咎于出口抵制，我认为中国领导层长期以来忽视的真正问题是，一旦在机器和基础设施上的投资追赶上发达经济体的水平，投资驱动型增长模式就不可避免地会失去动力。毕竟，你能建的桥梁、道路，甚至工厂都是有限的。在此之后，压低当地工资和补贴制造业将成为经济的负担，而不是资产。你看，随着制造业变得越来越先进，由于自动化，它自然会提供更少的就业岗位，因此在像美国这样真正繁荣的发达经济体中，大多数人在服务业工作。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

So instead of blaming the export backlash, I think the real problem that China's leadership ignored for far too long is that the investment led growth model will inevitably run out of steam once investments in machines and infrastructure catch up to the level of advanced economies. After all, you can only build so many bridges, roads, and yes, even factories. After this, the suppression of local wages and subsidies to manufacturing will become a liability to the economy rather than an asset. You see, as the manufacturing sector gets more and more advanced, it will naturally provide fewer jobs due to automation and therefore in advanced economies that are really thriving like the United States. Most people work in the service sector.

</details>

现在，对中国来说的好消息是，实际上，在这个阶段，一种新的增长战略有可能再次释放快速的经济增长。也就是说，如果他们现在干脆取消工资压制并取消对制造企业的补贴，普通人很快就会有更多的钱可以花，并把这些钱花在服务业上，这随后就能推动下一波经济增长。完成从农业到制造业再到全栈经济体的转型，从而避免日本化。

<details><summary>Original English</summary>

Now, the good news for China is that actually, at this point, a new growth strategy can again potentially unlock rapid economic growth. That is, if they now simply removed the wage suppression and removed subsidies from manufacturing firms, ordinary people could quickly have a lot more money to spend and spending that on services which could then power the next wave of economic growth. Completing the transition from agricultural to manufacturing to a full stack economy and thereby avoiding Japan.
