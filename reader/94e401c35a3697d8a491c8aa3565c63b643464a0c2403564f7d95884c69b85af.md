### 自由主义作为社会技术的三大支柱

**Jonathan Rauch**: 我对自由主义的理解与常人略有不同。因为大多数谈论和思考自由主义的人都是政治理论家，他们会告诉你这是一种政治学说——它确实也是。自由主义正如《独立宣言》中所作的最佳表述那样，是一种坚信“人皆生而平等，并享有不可剥夺的权利”的思想。但自由主义同时也是一种构建社会的运作方式。它是一种社会技术（social technology）。它是用来解答人类所面临的最艰难问题的一种方式：你如何去组织大量人口之间的协作，特别是当其中绝大多数人彼此素昧平生的时候？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: I have a slightly unusual take on on liberalism because most people who talk and think about it are political theorists who will tell you it's a political theory and it is. Liberalism is the idea stated best in the Declaration of Independence that all men are created equal and endowed with inalienable rights. But liberalism is also a way to structure societies. It's a social technology. It's a way to answer maybe the most difficult question humans have which is how do you organize cooperation of large numbers of people most of whom will be strangers to each other.

</details>

**Jonathan Rauch**: 自由主义对此给出了独一无二的答案，而且是一个极其有效的答案。作为一种社会技术，作为一种组织人类群体的方式，它的成效如此卓著，以至于在字面意义上彻底重塑了人类物种。这项社会技术的核心理念在于：依靠规则而非统治者来治理社会；依靠合作而非强制力来推进行动；设定所有人平等且地位可互换的前提，而不是建立一套让某些人凭借出身、财富或其他特权来掌权的等级制度。那么，你该如何将这一套构想具象化？落实到现实操作中时，它究竟意味着什么？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: And liberalism gives a unique answer to that and a uniquely effective answer to that. so effective that as a social technology as a way to organize our groups it is literally species transforming the social technology the core idea is you operate societies according to rules not rulers you use cooperation not coercion and you assume equality and the people are interchangeable instead of hierarchy in which some people are by virtue of their birth or their wealth or whatever in charge. So, how do you instantiate that? What does that actually mean when the rubber hits the road?

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: Well, it turns out that there are three great liberal social systems and in some realms they're distinct, but they have the same core idea and they're interacting all the time and we all depend on them every day for everything we hold dear. Peace, prosperity, freedom, and truth. So, the first of those is liberal markets, often called capitalism. That's the idea that you have a big marketplace and people negotiate through mechanisms like prices and we trace that back in its theoretical form to Adam Smith.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: The second big liberal system is liberal democracy. And this in the United States we trace to our founding fathers, the greatest avatars of this idea who built the constitution around it. <details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: And then there's a third system. This is the one that often gets left out, but it's just as liberal and just as important as the other two. I call it liberal science. And that's our system for finding truth and settling disagreements in a way that both keeps the peace and keeps us in touch with reality. And that's the system that says, so how do we settle conflicts over what's true and what's false? </details>

**Jonathan Rauch**: 传统的解决方式是依赖祭司或者政治局，求助于神谕，或者依附于传统习俗以及像《圣经》这样的典籍。过去人们就是这样解决争端的，但效果并不理想。自由科学则主张：“不，我们要采取完全不同的做法。我们将要求人们在既定规则的约束下，展开持续不断的相互批评与思想交锋。”比如，如果有人做了一项实验，那么每个人都必须能够重复这项实验；又比如，任何人都不允许动用武力去强行终止对话。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: Traditional way of doing that, you'd have a priest or a politburo. You'd use an oracle. You'd lean on tradition or a text like a Bible. That's how you'd settle things. Doesn't work very well. Liberal science says, "No, we're going to do something different. We're going to require people to be in a constant exchange of criticism according to rules." Like, if there's an experiment, everyone needs to be able to do it. And like no one can shut down the conversation using force.

</details>

**Jonathan Rauch**: 正是通过这种机制，我们确立了今天所看到的科学界、新闻界以及法律体系。那是一个开放式、基于规则的求真系统，这就是自由科学。我认为它是这三大系统中最重要、最成功的一个，因为它为我们带来了技术突破与知识的飞跃。毫不夸张地说，在当今世界，全球科学界在任何一个清晨所产生的新知识，都比整个人类物种在最初的 20 万年间所积累的还要多。但这三大系统全都在同一套原则下运转：它们依赖规则，具有非人格化的制度特征，并且保障系统内个体的平等地位。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: And through that process, we're going to establish what looks today like science and journalism and law. And that's an open-ended rules-based truth seeking system. That's liberal science. I think it's the most important and successful of the three because that's given us the technological advances and the advances in knowledge. It is literally true that any given morning, our global world of science produces more new knowledge than humanity did in its first 200,000 years. But those three are all working on the same principles, which is they're relying on rules and they're depersonalized and people are equal within those systems.

### 规则的界限、内部争论与去中心化特质

**Jonathan Rauch**: 关于自由市场的一个巨大误解，是人们容易将它与自由意志主义（libertarianism）混为一谈。自由意志主义者确实是自由主义传统的一个分支，但他们这个分支在本质上追求的是将他们所认定的自由与个体自主性最大化。他们极力排斥政府，试图在一切可能的地方把政府干预降到最低。他们心目中的自由资本主义是一种非常放任自流的特殊形态。他们要求绝对最小化的政府干预，并极度强调私有财产权是一项固有的自然权利。他们当中的许多人甚至断言，政府根本无权征税，也无权在狭窄边界之外对财产进行监管。

<summary>Original English</summary>

**Jonathan Rauch**: A big misconception about liberal markets confuses it with libertarianism. And and libertarians are a branch of liberalism, but they're a branch that's really fundamentally they maximize what what they consider freedom, individual autonomy. They want to minimize government at every turn. And their idea of liberal capitalism is a specific type that's it's very laissez-faire. They want absolutely minimal government intervention.
