### 谷歌以千万美元收购精神航空破产数据训练AI
今年春天精神航空（Spirit Airlines）破产时，该公司的资产被挂牌出售。包括其标志性的黄色飞机，但也包括其数据，其中涵盖了员工记录。而现在，这些记录已被谷歌以1000万美元买下。像谷歌这样的公司希望训练其人工智能模型，使其听起来更像人类，特别是像在工作场所中运作的人类。因此，从一家倒闭公司挖掘海量的真实人类数据来训练大语言模型，这个想法相当有吸引力。我的同事丽贝卡·戴维斯·奥布赖恩（Rebecca Davis O'Brien）一直在跟踪这桩交易。她采访的专家表示，这可能是首次有一家大型破产公司的数字资产被这样剥离，用来训练像Gemini或ChatGPT这样的大语言模型。而这可能只是一场数据淘金热的开端。但是，数据被记录在案的人们正在奋起反对。丽贝卡表示，代表前精神航空空乘人员的工会在法庭上提出异议，辩称员工的个人信息可能会被泄露。随后在破产法官批准该出售之前，谷歌同意对其获取的数据做出一定限制，例如不得包含医疗记录或银行记录。我认为撇开破产法的细微差别不谈，在人工智能时代，这是劳动法和劳工议题的一个全新前沿——人们理所当然地担忧，他们的职业数据可能会被转让给最高出价者去训练AI产品。
<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>
[music] When Spirit Airlines went bankrupt this spring, the company's assets went up for sale. Its iconic yellow planes, but also its data, including employee records. And now those records have been bought by Google for $10 million. So companies like Google want to train their artificial intelligence models to sound more like humans and specifically humans operating in a workspace. So the idea of mining or training the LLM on troves of actual human data from a failed company is pretty appealing. My colleague Rebecca Davis O'Brien has been covering the sale. Experts she spoke with say this may be the first time the digital assets of a large bankrupt company have been peeled off like this to train large language models like Gemini or Chat GPT. Google then agreed to some limitations on what data it's getting, like no medical records or bank records before a bankruptcy judge approved the sale. I think setting aside the nuances of bankruptcy law, this is a new frontier for labor law and labor issues in the AI era when people are concerned that rightfully so that their professional data might be signed away to the highest bidder to train AI products. [music]
</details>
