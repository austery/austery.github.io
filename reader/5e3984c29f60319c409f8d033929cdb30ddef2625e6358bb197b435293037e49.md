### BNPL 与传统信用卡的差异

**斯泰西·考利**: 它们与信用卡有几个非常关键的区别。首先是它们的结构：当你使用信用卡时，你拥有一个整体的循环信用额度，比如 5,000 美元。你刷卡消费，下个月账单出来后，你可以选择全额还清，或者只还一部分并为剩余余额支付利息。但使用先买后付时，每笔交易都是一笔**独立的独立贷款**。你买一双鞋，这就是一笔具有明确还款计划的单独贷款；如果你之后又买了件毛衣，那就是另一笔有着自己还款计划的新贷款。

其次是**准入门槛**。信用卡对信用评分要求较高，很多借款人往往无法获批信用卡，或者其信用额度非常有限。而先买后付平台通常不进行严格的传统信用调查，或者只做极轻微的软性信用查询，这让那些没有传统信贷记录或信用分偏低的群体能够极其轻松地借到钱。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Stacy Cowley**: They are different from a credit card in a couple key ways. One is just the structure of them. So, when you use a credit card, you have one big line of credit, let's say $5,000. You make a bunch of charges, you get a bill next month, and you can choose to pay the whole thing off or carry some of it and pay interest on the balance. With a buy now, pay later loan, each transaction is its own individual discrete loan. So you buy the shoes, that's one loan with its own repayment schedule. Then you buy a sweater, that's a different loan with its own repayment schedule. Another difference is who can get these loans. Credit cards typically have higher underwriting standards. So, a lot of borrowers can't qualify for a credit card or can only get one with a very low credit limit. Buy now, pay later lenders generally don't do hard credit checks, and so it's much easier for people with low credit scores or thin credit files to qualify for these loans.

</details>
