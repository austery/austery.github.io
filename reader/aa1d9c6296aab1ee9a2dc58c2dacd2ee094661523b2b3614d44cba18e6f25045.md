### 什么是早发性癌症？

**雷切尔·阿布拉姆斯**: 尼娜，你的报道方向主要是**慢性病**。最近你发表了一系列关于年轻人癌症发病率上升的专题报道。我们今天会深入聊聊你在报道中所发现的情况。但在开始讨论之前，我想先为我们讨论的对象做一个明确的界定。具体来说，当我们谈论年轻人群体中癌症发病率的增长时，我们指的究竟是哪些人？这又涉及哪些情况？

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Rachel Abrams**: Nina, your beat is chronic disease and you recently published a series on the growing rates of cancer among young people and we're going to talk about what you found in your reporting. But before we do that, I just want to define what we're talking about here. Exactly, like who and what are we referring to when we talk about growing rates of cancer among young people?

</details>

**尼娜·阿加瓦尔**: 所谓的**早发性癌症**（Early-onset cancer）通常是指发生在 50 岁以下成年人身上的癌症。所以，这并不是指非常年幼的儿童，但相对于传统的癌症发病年龄来说，这已经是非常年轻的群体了。在历史上，癌症一直被视为一种随着年龄增长而发生的老年性疾病，但研究人员现在已经开始以相当系统的方式记录到，癌症的诊断在年轻成年人中正在不断增加。而且这并不是什么我们偶然听到的零星传言，在过去几年里，一些综合性的学术论文已经明确指出，这绝非一个孤立的个案现象。这是一个在过去几十年中，跨越多种癌症类型、持续发生且不容忽视的规律性趋势。事实上，自 **1990 年**以来，这种发病率的攀升每年都在发生。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Nina Agarwal**: So early onset cancer generally means cancer in adults under 50. So not super young, but young for cancer. Cancer has historically been a disease of aging, but researchers have started to document in a pretty systematic way that cancer diagnoses are increasing in younger adults. And this is something, you know, we've been hearing about kind of in trickles, but you know, in the last couple of years, there have been some comprehensive papers showing that this isn't just an isolated phenomenon. This is a pattern happening across cancers over decades. And it's been happening every year since 1990.

</details>

**雷切尔·阿布拉姆斯**: 而且我们知道，不同类型癌症的发病增长率是不同的。因此，对于某些癌症，其发病率每年会攀升 1% 到 2%；而对于另外一些癌症，或者在特定的年龄组别中，这种年增长率甚至可以高达 6% 左右。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Rachel Abrams**: And you know the rates vary from cancer to cancer. So for some it's annual increases of 1 to 2% a year and for others and in certain age groups the increase could be as much as like 6% per year.

</details>

**尼娜·阿加瓦尔**: 是的。对于年轻人来说，这完全是猝不及防的。我的意思是，在生命的任何阶段，癌症的诊断都是令人震惊且绝非所愿的，但对于许多年轻的成年人来说，这无异于晴天霹雳。它彻底颠覆了他们的生活轨迹，其颠覆的方式与老年人截然不同。因为这一切发生在一生中最具活力的黄金时期，他们正处于组建家庭、立足社会、规划未来，也就是确立自己人生方向的关键阶段。在过去的一年里，我花了相当长的时间去努力理解这群人的真实体验。不仅去探究发生了什么、为什么发生，更去倾听这对那些确诊的患者而言意味着什么。我们收到了数百名读者的来信，向我们倾诉他们面对早发性癌症的亲身经历。在过去的几个月里，我采访了数十名患者，试图拼凑出一幅完整的画面，去展现当一个人在 20 多岁、30 多岁或 40 多岁这样的人生阶段确诊癌症时，它究竟是如何以一种无法逆转的方式，彻底改变了他们余生中所有事物的走向。

<details>
<summary>Original English</summary>

**Nina Agarwal**: Yes. And for younger people it's very unexpected. I mean, I think a cancer diagnosis is unexpected and undesired at any time, but it comes out of left field for so many of these younger adults. And it totally upends their lives in a way that is different from the way it upends the lives of older people because this is happening at a really vital moment in their life when they're still kind of putting down the roots and becoming the adults that they're going to be. And I spent a good part of the last year trying to understand exactly what the experience is like. Not only what's happening and why, but what that means for the people who are getting diagnosed. We had hundreds of people write into us to share their experience of early onset cancer. And I spoke to dozens over months to just get a picture of all of the ways that getting a cancer diagnosis in that time of life, your 20s, your 30s, your 40s, how it changes a trajectory of everything that comes after.

</details>
